# Insomniac

**Insomniac** (formerly **Insomniac Events**) is an American electronic music event promoter and music distributor headquartered in Beverly Hills, California. Founded in 1993 by Pasquale Rotella, the company specializes in the organization of large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festivals and the management of nightlife venues.

## Event Portfolio
Insomniac organizes a diverse range of music festivals tailored to specific electronic subgenres. Its flagship event is the **Electric Daisy Carnival** (EDC), held annually in Las Vegas. Other major festival brands operated by the company include:

* **Beyond Wonderland** and **Nocturnal Wonderland**: General electronic music festivals.
* **bassRush**: Focused on drum and bass and dubstep.
* **bassCon**: Dedicated to hardstyle.
* **dreamState**: Focused on trance music.

The company also oversees other events such as the **Hard** music festival and the **Audiotistic Music Festival**.

## Venue Operations
Insomniac manages a portfolio of nightclubs across several major United States cities. In Los Angeles, the company operates **Exchange L.A.**, the warehouse venue **Factory 93**, and **Create** (managed in partnership with SBE). 

The company expanded its East Coast operations through the 2019 acquisition of **Club Space** in Miami. In 2020, Insomniac acquired **Echostage** and **Soundcheck** in Washington, D.C., along with their operator, **Club Glow**. Through this subsidiary, Insomniac also oversees the **Project GLOW** and **Moonrise** music festivals.

## Music Distribution and Media
In 2014, Insomniac established **Insomniac Records**, a co-branded EDM label launched in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M. By 2018, the company transitioned its A&R and distribution operations to an in-house model under the **Insomniac Music Group**. Additionally, the company operates **Insomniac Radio**.

## Corporate Strategy and Partnerships
Founder Pasquale Rotella has stated that the company prioritizes fan experience over profit margins, citing the community-driven model of Burning Man as an influence. 

In June 2013, Insomniac entered into a "creative partnership" with **Live Nation**, granting the company access to Live Nation's logistical resources while maintaining independent creative control over its events. Rotella specified that this agreement was not an acquisition. During the same period, the company partnered with **Dick Clark Productions** to produce a dance music awards program.
