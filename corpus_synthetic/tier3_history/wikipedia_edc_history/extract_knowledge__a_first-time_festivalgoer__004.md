### Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Global Operations: 2016–2019

Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music festivals held across multiple international territories. The primary flagship event takes place at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Between 2016 and 2019, the Las Vegas event transitioned from June dates to May (beginning in 2018) to mitigate extreme heat and improve attendee safety. In 2018, the organizers introduced on-site camping, RV parking, and earlier opening times to manage traffic congestion. Total attendance for the 2018 Las Vegas event was estimated at 411,400 people.

**North American Regional Events**
*   **EDC Mexico:** Held annually at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City. Events occurred February 27–28 (2016), February 25–26 (2017), February 24–25 (2018), and February 23–24 (2019).
*   **EDC Orlando:** Hosted at Tinker Field, Florida. The event took place November 4–5 (2016), November 10–11 (2017), and November 9–10 (2018). In 2019, the festival duration expanded from two days to three.
*   **EDC New York:** Held May 14–15, 2016, at Citi Field in Queens, NY.

**International Expansions**
*   **EDC UK:** Held in Milton Keynes, England, on July 9, 2016; the 2017 iteration was cancelled.
*   **EDC India:** Held November 12–13, 2016, at the Buddh International Circuit in New Delhi.
*   **EDC Japan:** Held in Chiba at Zozo Marine Stadium and Makuhari Beach Park on May 12–13 (2018) and May 11–12 (2019). The 2018 event experienced equipment failure due to heavy rain.
*   **EDC China:** Debuted April 29–30, 2018, at the Shanghai International Music Park.
*   **EDC Korea:** Held August 31–September 1, 2019, at Seoul Land, Gwacheon.

**Safety and Incident Reports**
Health and security incidents have been recorded during these events. In 2017, Las Vegas saw a doubling of medical calls on opening night due to heat; one fatality occurred involving MDMA and TFMPP consumption combined with heat exposure. In 2019, high winds at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway caused partial stage closures. Miscommunication regarding "taking cover" led some attendees to exit the venue; these individuals were barred from returning due to a strict no-reentry policy. Las Vegas police reported 65 arrests during the 2019 festival.
