**Las Vegas Motor Speedway** is a professional racing complex located in Las Vegas, Nevada. The primary facility is a 1.500-mile (2.414 km) tri-oval intermediate speedway with a seating capacity of 80,000 as of 2023. The venue is owned by Speedway Motorsports, LLC (SMI) and is managed by General Manager Patrick Lindsey.

### History
Following the 1971 closure of Stardust International Raceway, Curly Price, owner of Craig Road Speedway, planned a new road course and drag strip on city-owned land in North Las Vegas. The resulting facility, named the Las Vegas Speedrome, opened in 1972 after preliminary races in 1971, though the site quickly deteriorated.

The complex was revived during the 1980s by drag racer Alex Rodriguez and his son. In 1985, a 0.375-mile (0.604 km) oval was added to the site. In 1989, Richie Clyne, director of the Imperial Palace Hotel's automobile museum, purchased the complex from the city. By the mid-1990s, Clyne, Ralph Engelstad (owner of the Imperial Palace), and Bill Bennett (owner of the Sahara Hotel) collaborated to construct the current 1.500-mile (2.414 km) oval, which was completed in 1996. The facility was acquired by Bruton Smith and SMI in 1998.

### Track Configurations
The complex consists of several distinct racing surfaces:
* **Main Oval:** A 1.500-mile (2.414 km) tri-oval used for major sanctioned events.
* **The Bullring:** A 0.375-mile (0.604 km) oval established in 1985.
* **Clay Oval:** A 0.500-mile (0.805 km) dirt track.
* **Road Courses:** The complex includes a road course with multiple layouts and an infield road course dedicated to sports car racing.

### Event History
The Las Vegas Motor Speedway has served as a host for various high-profile motorsport series, including:
* NASCAR
* IndyCar
* Champ Car
