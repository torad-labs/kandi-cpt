**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is an electronic dance music (EDM) festival produced by the promoter and distributor Insomniac. The flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, is held annually in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. As of 2024, it is the largest EDM festival in North America, recording a total attendance of 525,000 over a three-day period.

The festival's programming spans multiple electronic genres, including house, techno, drum and bass, dubstep, and trance. The site infrastructure consists of eight stages; the largest, Kinetic Field, has a capacity of 70,000 attendees. Beyond musical performances, the venue incorporates three-dimensional superstructures, LED-lit flora and fauna, interactive art installations, and a carnival component consisting of 18 rides and four Ferris wheels.

Operationally, the Las Vegas event expanded to a two-day format in 2009 and a three-day format in 2011. On-site camping was introduced in 2018. The event is supported by a staff of over 5,000 personnel.

While the flagship remains in Nevada, EDC maintains a global presence with current annual events in Orlando (Florida), Mexico City (Mexico), Medellín (Colombia), Suzhou (China), Phuket (Thailand), and Seoul (South Korea). Former iterations were hosted in cities including Los Angeles, New York City, London (Milton Keynes), São Paulo, and New Delhi.

The next scheduled flagship event, **EDC Las Vegas**, is set for **May 15–17, 2026**.
