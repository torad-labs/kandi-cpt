Friend: Yo! I'm finally pulling the trigger on EDC 2026. Since you've been to like, a dozen of these, what's the deal with the promoter? Is it still just Pasquale?

Vet: Pretty much. Pasquale Rotella started Insomniac back in '93, and he's still the face of it. They're based out of Beverly Hills, but they've basically built an empire. It's not just EDC; they run the whole ecosystem now.

Friend: I've seen some other names on the flyers. Are those different companies or just other festivals?

Vet: They're all Insomniac divisions. If you're into the heavier side, you'll see Bassrush for the dubstep/DnB crowd and Basscon if you want that hardstyle energy. If you're looking for the trance vibes, that's all under Dreamstate.

Friend: Nice. I feel like I see their branding everywhere lately, even at clubs.

Vet: That's because they've been buying up venues. They've got Create, Exchange L.A., and Factory 93 in LA. They also snatched up Club Space in Miami a few years back, and they own Echostage and Soundcheck in D.C. through their subsidiary, Club Glow.

Friend: Damn, they're really taking over. Do they actually care about the music, or is it just a corporate cash grab now?

Vet: Honestly, Rotella still pushes the "fan experience" angle. He's always talking about how you don't need massive arena acts to make a festival work—he actually cites Burning Man as an inspiration for letting the people create the vibe while the organizers just provide the space.

Friend: That's cool. I'm mostly worried about the music side—do they have their own label or just book everyone?

Vet: They've got Insomniac Records. They started it with Interscope back in 2014, but by 2018 they moved everything in-house to the Insomniac Music Group. They handle all their own A&R and distribution now.

Friend: Got it. I heard they partnered with Live Nation a while back. Does that mean they lost creative control?

Vet: Nah, that was a "creative partnership" back in 2013. Rotella was very clear that it wasn't an acquisition. They get Live Nation's massive resources for logistics, but Insomniac keeps total control over the actual show.

Friend: Sweet. So for 2026, where should I spend most of my time? Give me a game plan.

Vet: Listen, you gotta hit kineticFIELD on Friday night for the main stage madness. Then, spend your Saturday diving into the underground vibes at CIRCUITGROUND. If you want that pure euphoria, save your Sunday for the trance sets over at the Dreamstate stage. Trust me, that's the only way to do it.
