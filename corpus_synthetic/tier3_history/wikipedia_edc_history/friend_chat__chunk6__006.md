Friend: I'm thinking about finally hitting EDC in 2026! I know it's a huge brand now, but has it always been this massive?

Vet: Not even close! It started way back in '91 in Chino, California, with only about 3,000 to 3,500 people. It was super underground back then—they even had a few years in the 90s where it wasn't organized at all.

Friend: That's wild. When did it actually start becoming a "destination" thing?

Vet: The shift happened around 2011. That was the first year they hit the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Once it landed there, it exploded. By 2019 and 2021, they were hitting 450,000 people, and the 2023/2024 events peaked at 525,000.
