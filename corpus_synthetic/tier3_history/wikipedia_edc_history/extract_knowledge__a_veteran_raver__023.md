The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) transitioned its flagship event to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in 2011. This relocation from Los Angeles followed a series of controversies involving underage drug use, multiple injuries, and the death of a 15-year-old attendee.

Since the 2011 move, the festival has been held annually at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway venue.
