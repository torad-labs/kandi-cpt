Q: Who considered the track revived after "seven years of relative silence" by April?
A: Review-Journal writer Mike Henle.

Q: Which official considered revoking the complex's operating permit due to criticism regarding violence, drug use, and underage drinking?
A: Commissioner Ron Lurie.

Q: When did Price lose his appeal regarding the complex?
A: February 1980.

Q: On what date did the partnership give up the complex due to issues with lighting, timing systems, and maintenance effort?
A: November 5.

Q: Which two businessmen signed a 10-year lease with the city the day after the partnership gave up the complex?
A: Terry Ainsworth and Harold Ellis.

Q: Who replaced Terry Ainsworth as a partner at the complex after the city appointment?
A: Alex Rodriguez and his son, Alex Rodriguez Jr.

Q: How much did Alex Rodriguez invest in improvements and repaving the track during his first few months?
A: $75,000.

Q: Why did Van Norman give up ownership of the Craig Road Speedway to First Interstate Bank in 1982?
A: To satisfy debts from his family business.

Q: In what year did LVIS opt to host stock car racing events to revive the local stock car racing community?
A: 1984.

Q: What was the length of the new oval built in 1985, and what materials were used to build it?
A: The oval was 0.333 mi (0.536 km) and was built using donations of old Caesars Palace Grand Prix barriers.

Q: By 1987, how much had Rodriguez invested into the complex, and which organization honored it as one of the best facilities in the United States?
A: Rodriguez invested over $500,000, and the facility was honored by the NHRA.

Q: Which organization helped the complex announce plans to repave the road course in 1987?
A: The Sports Car Club of America.
