### Insomniac Events Overview

Insomniac Events is a promoter and producer of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals and club events. The organization's longest-running brand is Nocturnal Wonderland, which served as the company's first public event. Its primary global brand, Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC), currently operates in Las Vegas, Mexico, Orlando, and Portugal; previous editions in the UK, India, Puerto Rico, and Brazil have been discontinued. Other active event brands include Beyond Wonderland, Dreamstate, Escape Halloween, Countdown New Year’s Eve, and Daytrip. Insomniac also maintains a partial collaboration with Madison House under the Anschutz Entertainment Group for Electric Forest.

### Insomniac Music Group and Labels

In May 2014, Insomniac established Insomniac Records in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M to sign both emerging and established producers; early signings included Arty, Bingo Players, and Chris Lake. In December 2018, the company formed the Insomniac Music Group to manage A&R and distribution for its internal imprints. These imprints include Basscon Records, Bassrush Records, Discovery Project, Dreamstate Records, Gud Vibrations, HARD Recordings, In / Rotation, Lost in Dreams, and What To Do.

### Media and Digital Expansion

Insomniac has expanded its media presence through various radio partnerships. This includes the launch of Insomniac Radio on SiriusXM in 2018 and a partnership with Dash Radio in May 2019. During the COVID-19 pandemic, the organization transitioned to virtual programming via "Insomniac Rewind" streams on YouTube and Twitch, as well as physical drive-in experiences including "Park 'N Rave" at the NOS Events Center and "Electric Mile" at Santa Anita Park. In March 2022, the company partnered with Unity Technologies to develop a persistent online world for virtual concerts.

### Acquisitions and Partnerships

Insomniac has diversified its portfolio through strategic acquisitions, including a majority stake in Miami's Club Space nightclub in July 2019. In June 2019, Insomniac acquired a controlling stake in Soundslinger, LLC, the organizer of the Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival. However, as of April 2025, Soundslinger announced that Insomniac is no longer involved with the Okeechobee festival.
