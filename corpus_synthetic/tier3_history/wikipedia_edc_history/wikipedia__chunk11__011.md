# Insomniac Events

**Insomniac Events** is an American promoter of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals and club events. The organization is responsible for a diverse portfolio of large-scale productions, ranging from destination cruises and boutique regional gatherings to massive urban festivals.

### Event Portfolio

Insomniac operates several recurring event series and standalone festivals across North America.

#### Large-Scale Festivals
*   **Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC):** The organization's flagship event, featuring multiple themed stages and large-scale art installations.
*   **Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival:** A multi-genre event held in Florida that integrates music with nature and arts.
*   **Middlelands:** A regional festival focused on the Midwestern United States.

#### Specialized and Boutique Series
*   **Crush:** A series of regional events hosted in major metropolitan hubs, including Southern California, San Francisco, Phoenix, and Dallas.
*   **White Wonderland:** A winter-themed event typically characterized by a specific dress code for attendees.
*   **Bay Nites:** A series of events targeted at the Northern California region.
*   **Together as One** and **We Are NRG:** Additional branded event series produced by the organization.

#### Destination and Niche Events
*   **Holy Ship!:** A recurring music cruise featuring electronic artists and themed parties.
*   **Project Z:** A specialized event series within the Insomniac portfolio.

### Production and Logistics

#### Stage Design
Insomniac utilizes a specific naming convention for its production environments, often employing camelCase for its stage designations to distinguish between different musical genres and atmospheric themes.

#### Logistics and Safety
The organization manages the deployment of large-scale infrastructure, including sound systems, lighting rigs, and crowd control measures. Safety protocols typically include dedicated medical tents and hydration stations to manage attendee wellness in high-density environments.
