Q: Where is the Las Vegas Motor Speedway located?
A: The Las Vegas Motor Speedway is located in Las Vegas, Nevada, in-between Interstate 15 and Las Vegas Boulevard.

Q: What was the stated capacity of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway as of 2023 according to the Las Vegas Sun?
A: The stated capacity was 80,000.

Q: How many acres of land did the track complex occupy as of 2015 according to the Las Vegas Review-Journal?
A: The complex occupied over 1,200 acres of land.

Q: What is the name of the redesigned, interactive garage built during the 2006 renovations?
A: The interactive garage is known as the Neon Garage.

Q: What is the current name of the three-story, 60,000 square-foot media center located in the track's infield?
A: The media center is currently known as the LocaliQ Digital Center.

Q: Why was the plan to build a 127-unit condominium tower overlooking the track's first turn cancelled?
A: The plan saw opposition from Nellis Air Force Base and United States Air Force officials due to safety risks regarding regular flight training and drills near the track.

Q: When did the 0.500 mi (0.805 km) clay dirt oval open?
A: The clay dirt oval opened in November 1996.

Q: What is the name and length of the oval track that was modified in 2000?
A: The track is named the "Bullring" and is 0.375 mi (0.604 km) long.

Q: When did "The Strip" drag strip open and how many lanes was it expanded to in 2017?
A: "The Strip" opened in April 2000 and was expanded to four lanes in 2017.

Q: On what date did the Las Vegas Review-Journal report that Carl "Curly" Price aimed to lease land to build a drag strip, road course, and oval?
A: August 14, 1970.
