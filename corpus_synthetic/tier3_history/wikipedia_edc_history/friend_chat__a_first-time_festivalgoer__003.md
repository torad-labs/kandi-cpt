Friend: I’m finally doing it! I'm hitting EDC 2026. Since it's my first time, I'm nervous. Does this thing actually get as crazy as people say?

Vet: Oh, absolutely. It’s a massive operation. Back in 2012, they had 320,000 people show up, which was a 30% jump in attendance. It's a city within a city.

Friend: 320k?! That sounds overwhelming. Is it always smooth sailing with a crowd that big?

Vet: Not always. You have to stay alert. In 2012, a guy from Florida actually died while leaving the Motor Speedway because he'd been drinking and smoking and got hit by a truck. And in 2015, a UC Irvine grad passed away due to MDMA—it causes dehydration and spikes your body temp. Please, just drink tons of water and look out for your crew.

Friend: Yikes, noted. Water is key. What about the weather? I heard Vegas can be unpredictable.

Vet: It can be wild. In 2012, 30 MPH winds hit around 8 p.m. on the second night. They had to shut everything down by 1 a.m. and send 90,000 people to the bleachers. 

Friend: Wait, did the music just stop? What happened to the DJs?

Vet: Mostly, yeah, but Markus Schulz and Steve Aoki actually did impromptu sets on the Insomniac Wide Awake art car for a bit before officials shut that down too for safety. To make it up to everyone, Insomniac let the Saturday crowd come back on Sunday for free.

Friend: That's a cool save. I've seen photos of the stages—are they really as huge as they look?

Vet: Even bigger. In 2014, Insomniac actually set the record for the largest structural stage in North America. By the time that event wrapped, they were seeing about 134,000 people per day, with all 345,000 tickets sold out by June 18th.

Friend: I'm definitely aiming for kineticFIELD, but are there other cool themes?

Vet: For sure. Back in 2015, the main stage had this awesome blinking owl as the background theme. It’s all about that visual immersion.

Friend: Is Vegas the only place to experience this, or are there other versions?

Vet: It started in Vegas, but it’s gone global. They’ve done EDC Orlando at Tinker Field and EDC New York at MetLife Stadium—which drew 45,000 people a day. They've even expanded to London, San Juan, Brazil, and Mexico City.

Friend: I didn't realize it was a worldwide thing. Is there any way to see what the vibe is like before I go?

Vet: Definitely check out the documentary "Under the Electric Sky." It premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival and gives you a great look at the magic of the event.
