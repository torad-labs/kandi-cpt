Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music festivals produced by Insomniac Events. The flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, is held at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. The 2026 iteration follows a history of logistical evolutions, including the adoption of a May date to mitigate heat-related safety issues and the implementation of air-conditioned camping options.

The festival is characterized by large-scale production, featuring immersive stages and drone shows. The main stage has historically required significant capital investment, with one iteration costing approximately $3 million. The event typically spans three days and attracts hundreds of thousands of attendees; for example, the 2024 event recorded 525,000 attendees.

Beyond the Las Vegas event, the EDC brand operates several international and satellite festivals, including:
*   **EDC Mexico:** An annual event based in Mexico.
*   **EDC Orlando:** A regional event held in Florida.
*   **EDSea:** A festival cruise operating in the Caribbean.
*   **International Debuts:** The brand has expanded into markets including Japan, China, and Thailand.

The festival's operational history includes challenges such as weather-related evacuations due to high winds and regulatory conflicts that led to its relocation from the Los Angeles Coliseum to Las Vegas in 2011.
