**Origins and Early Years**
The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) was established after Philip Blaine granted the use of the name to Pasquale Rotella, CEO of Insomniac Events, in 1995. Following the creation of the Organic '96 event, Insomniac debuted EDC in 1997 at the Shrine Expo Hall in Los Angeles, California. In 1999, the event was hosted at Lake Dolores Waterpark in Newberry Springs, California.

**Expansion and Venue Transitions (2000–2006)**
In 2000, EDC took place at the International Agri-Center in Tulare, California, drawing 24,000 attendees; the venue subsequently terminated its contract due to noise complaints. The 2001 event at Hansen Dam in Southern California introduced multiple stages: the Merry Go Round, Fun House, Clown Alley, Confusin' & Amuzin' Mirror Maze, Bassrush Arena, and Cosmic Healing Temple. That same year, the first EDC Texas was held at Thunderhill Raceway Park in Austin.

In 2002, the California event moved to the Queen Mary Events Park in Long Beach. A planned event at the Travis County Exposition Center in Austin was canceled shortly before the date, resulting in a make-up show with a modified lineup one week later. From 2003 to 2006, EDC was hosted at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino, California.

**Growth and Diversification (2007–2009)**
EDC returned to Los Angeles in 2007 and 2008, utilizing the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum and Exposition Park. On June 28, 2008, the event was held at the Coliseum, while a separate EDC Colorado event occurred on June 14 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora.

In 2009, EDC expanded to a two-day format on June 26–27, with attendance reaching approximately 55,000 on Friday and 99,000 on Saturday. The 2009 event occupied the southern half of Exposition Park. Concurrent regional editions included EDC Colorado on June 13, headlined by Paul Van Dyk and Infected Mushroom, and the inaugural EDC Puerto Rico on August 14 at the Arena Fairgrounds in San Juan.
