Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music festivals held globally. In 2016, EDC Las Vegas took place at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway from June 17–19, featuring a performance by DJ Marshmello and DJ Tiësto. Other 2016 installments included EDC Mexico (Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez, February 27–28), EDC New York (Citi Field, May 14–15), EDC UK (Milton Keynes, July 9), EDC Orlando (Tinker Field, November 4–5), and EDC India (Buddh International Circuit, November 12–13).

The 2017 EDC Las Vegas event at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway saw over 135,000 attendees on the opening night. This event was marked by a significant increase in heat-related medical calls and the death of a 34-year-old male attributed to heat exposure and the ingestion of MDMA and TFMPP. Other 2017 events included EDC Mexico (February 25–26) and EDC Orlando (November 10–11); the scheduled EDC UK event in Milton Keynes was cancelled.

In 2018, EDC Las Vegas was rescheduled from June to May 18–20 to provide cooler weather conditions. The event introduced on-site camping and adjusted opening times to mitigate traffic, drawing an estimated 411,400 attendees over three days. The 2018 circuit also included EDC Orlando (November 9–10), EDC Mexico (February 24–25), EDC Japan (Chiba, May 12–13), and the debut of EDC China at the Shanghai International Music Park (April 29–30).

The 2019 EDC Las Vegas event occurred from May 17–19. High winds on the second day led to stage closures; emergency instructions to "take cover in your car" caused some attendees to mistakenly exit the venue, where they were barred from returning due to a strict no-reentry policy. Las Vegas police recorded 65 arrests during the festival. Additional 2019 events included EDC Mexico (February 23–24), EDC Japan (May 11–12), and EDC Korea (Seoul Land, August 31–September 1). EDC Orlando expanded its duration from two days to three days at Tinker Field.
