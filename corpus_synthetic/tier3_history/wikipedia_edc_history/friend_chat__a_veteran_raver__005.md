Friend: Yo, I’m trying to get my head around the EDC 2026 vibes. Since you've been to basically every Insomniac event, do you know if they're sticking with the updated layout from 2024?

Vet: For sure. In 2024 they shifted a few stages around specifically to open up the dance floors and fix the crowd flow. If you're hitting 2026, expect those wider lanes and more breathing room near the big stages.

Friend: Nice. I remember things got weird with the dates a few years back. What actually happened with the 2020 run?

Vet: Total chaos. Mexico City hit the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez from Feb 28 to March 2, but the Vegas side was a mess. They originally had May 15–17 slated, then pushed it to October 2–4 because of COVID. They did a virtual stream on Twitch and YouTube for the May dates, but by August, they just cancelled the October one entirely.

Friend: Wait, did they change the grounds at the Speedway during that time too?

Vet: Yeah, Rotella mentioned they were utilizing more of the grounds because the speedway ripped out a ton of RV hookups. It actually made a bunch of areas usable for the festival that were blocked off before.

Friend: Did 2021 almost get cancelled too? I remember some drama with the county.

Vet: Oh man, yeah. It was supposed to be May 21–23, but Clark County wouldn't let it happen until 60% of residents were vaxxed. Pasquale basically said the vaccination rate was out of his control and he wasn't taking the risk. They ended up pivoting to October 22–24.

Friend: I missed EDC Orlando a while back—was that the one where they dropped the new lineup in June?

Vet: Exactly. June 2024 was the big reveal for Orlando. It was a heavy mix of the usual legends and some newer emerging talent.

Friend: I saw Thailand just started up. When did that debut?

Vet: That was brand new for 2025. It went down January 17–19 at Boat Avenue Lakefront in Phuket.

Friend: I heard some bad news about that one, though. Was it actually sketchy?

Vet: Unfortunately, yeah. It was a bit of a tragedy—two foreign tourists died in suspected drug-related incidents, and three others got arrested for possession/use. Definitely a reminder to keep it safe.

Friend: For sure. So for 2026, I'm guessing we're still talking kineticFIELD and the usual suspects?

Vet: You bet. Whether it's kineticFIELD or circuitGROUNDS, the layout changes from '24 are the biggest thing to keep in mind for your navigation. Just stick to the plan and you're golden.
