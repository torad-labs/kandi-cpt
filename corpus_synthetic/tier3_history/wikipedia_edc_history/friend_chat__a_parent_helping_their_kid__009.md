Friend: My kid is dying to go to EDC 2026, but I have no idea what I'm getting into. Who actually runs this thing?

Vet: It’s all handled by a company called Insomniac. They were started back in '93 by a guy named Pasquale Rotella. They're basically the gold standard for electronic music festivals in the US.

Friend: Is EDC the only thing they do? I want to know if this is a one-off or a professional operation.

Vet: Oh, they're huge. Besides EDC, they run Beyond Wonderland and Nocturnal Wonderland. Depending on what your kid likes, they also have Bassrush for dubstep, Basscon for hardstyle, and Dreamstate for trance.

Friend: I saw some mentions of nightclubs too. Do they just do festivals?

Vet: Nope, they own a bunch of venues. In LA, they've got Create, Exchange L.A., and Factory 93. They also bought Club Space in Miami, plus Echostage and Soundcheck in D.C. through a subsidiary called Club Glow.

Friend: That sounds like a massive corporate machine. Is it just about making money?

Vet: Actually, Rotella is pretty vocal about prioritizing the "fan experience" over just chasing profits. He’s even praised Burning Man for being "by the people, for the people." He believes you don't need arena-filling superstars to make a festival work.

Friend: Okay, but I'm worried about the quality. Who ensures the production is actually good?

Vet: They have a creative partnership with Live Nation. Insomniac keeps total creative control, but they use Live Nation’s massive resources to build those crazy stages.

Friend: Speaking of stages, what should I tell my kid to look out for? I keep seeing weird names online.

Vet: You'll see them written in camelCase. Tell them to check out kineticFIELD for the main vibes, and keep an eye on the other themed stages. Since they have their own label, Insomniac Music Group, they often book a mix of global stars and their own signed artists.

Friend: I'm still a bit nervous. Is there a way to vet them more before I buy tickets?

Vet: Definitely. Check out the official Insomniac website. They've been around since the 90s and are headquartered in Beverly Hills, so they aren't some fly-by-night operation. It's a very well-oiled machine.
