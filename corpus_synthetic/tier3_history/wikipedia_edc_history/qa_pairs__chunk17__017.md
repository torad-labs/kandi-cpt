Q: On what date did the city of Las Vegas consider revoking Price's lease?
A: September 17.

Q: What were the stated reasons for the city of Las Vegas considering the revocation of Price's lease?
A: Price owed approximately $3,400 on monthly payments, there were increased cases of vandalism to the track and machinery, and residents were stealing bleachers.

Q: Why did Price forfeit his lease on December 16?
A: He failed to stockpile 35,000 cubic yards of gravel.

Q: When was Price's lease officially terminated after he failed to maintain the gravel stockpile required by his contract?
A: July 1976.

Q: Which judge approved the termination of Price's lease and ordered him to vacate the land?
A: Clark County District judge Howard Babcock.

Q: How much was Price ordered to pay for unpaid rent and failure to maintain his contract?
A: $22,140.

Q: To whom did the city of Las Vegas temporarily lease the complex in November after Price confirmed his decision to appeal to the Supreme Court of Nevada?
A: Show & Go Ltd.

Q: For what period was the track temporarily leased to car clubs just before the end of 1977?
A: The first three months of 1978.

Q: How much per month did Bob Van Norman offer to lease the complex in February 1978?
A: $300 a month.

Q: What race featuring movie stars took place at the complex in June, despite a lack of spectators?
A: The Silverbird Race of Champions.

Q: What company was formed by five businessmen, including Van Norman and Horten, to bid on temporary ownership of the track?
A: Las Vegas Recreation, Inc.

Q: On what date did the city's commission unanimously approve the lease to Las Vegas Recreation, Inc.?
A: February 21.
