Insomniac Care manages the charitable contributions of the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC). The organization donates a percentage of ticket sales from each flagship festival to national charities and community organizations. Since 2011, these efforts have raised over two million dollars. Beneficiaries include the Boys and Girls Club of Southern Nevada, the Nevada Childhood Cancer Foundation, the Las Vegas Academy for Arts, the Injured Police Officers Fund, Families for Effective Autism Treatment, and Rock the Vote. EDC Las Vegas has previously utilized the fundraising platform Surreal to solicit donations through sweepstakes for activities and helicopter rides with DJs.

In media, the 2013 EDC Las Vegas event was the subject of the documentary *Under the Electric Sky*, which premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival.

The festival has received several industry nominations and rankings. EDC was nominated for "Best Music Event" at the International Dance Music Awards annually from 2010 to 2016, and for "Best Festival" in 2019 and 2020. According to *DJ Magazine's* "Top 100 Festivals" list, EDC ranked 3rd in 2022, 4th in 2023, and 2nd in 2024.
