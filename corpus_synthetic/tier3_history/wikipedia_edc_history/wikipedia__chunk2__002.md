# Electric Daisy Carnival (2010–2011)

The **Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a series of electronic dance music festivals produced by Insomniac Events. Between 2010 and 2011, the event expanded into a global brand with multiple regional iterations, though this period was marked by significant safety controversies and a relocation of its flagship event.

## 2010 Events

### EDC Los Angeles
The 2010 edition of EDC Los Angeles recorded an attendance of approximately 185,000 people. Ticket pricing began at $60, with some attendees paying in excess of $100.

### Regional Editions
Following the Los Angeles event, Insomniac held several other regional festivals in 2010:
* **EDC Colorado:** Held June 12 at the Arapahoe County Fairground in Aurora.
* **EDC Dallas:** Held June 19 at Fair Park in Dallas, with an attendance of 11,000.
* **EDC Puerto Rico:** Held August 28 at the Estadio Sixto Escobar in Puerta de Tierra, San Juan.

## 2011 Events

### EDC Las Vegas
Due to controversies surrounding the previous venue in Los Angeles, the flagship festival was relocated to Las Vegas, Nevada, in 2011. The event took place from June 24–26 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, with a reported attendance of 230,000 people over three days.

### Regional Editions
* **EDC Orlando:** Held May 27–28 at Tinker Field and the grounds adjacent to the Florida Citrus Bowl. Attendance was approximately 12,000 on Friday and 20,000 on Saturday.
* **EDC Colorado:** Held June 11 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora.
* **EDC Dallas:** Held June 18 at Fair Park with an estimated 25,000 attendees.
* **EDC Puerto Rico:** Held August 27 at the Estadio Sixto Escobar in San Juan.

## Safety and Logistics

### 2010 Los Angeles Incidents
The 2010 Los Angeles event faced criticism from promoters and local authorities after underage individuals under the age of 16 gained entry. A crowd stampede resulted in the hospitalization of more than 100 attendees. Additionally, a 15-year-old attendee, Sasha Rodriguez, died following the ingestion of MDMA.

In response, the City of Los Angeles suspended all remaining scheduled events for 2010 and future dates pending the implementation of new security provisions. These mandates included:
* A minimum age requirement of 18 years for all attendees.
* The mandatory hiring of on-site medical doctors.

### 2011 Dallas Incidents
The 2011 event in Dallas was impacted by extreme weather, with temperatures exceeding 110 degrees Fahrenheit. This resulted in dozens of hospitalizations and at least one fatality. Consequently, this was the final EDC event hosted in Dallas.
