**Las Vegas Motor Speedway** (LVMS) is a racing complex located at 7000 North Las Vegas Boulevard in Las Vegas, Nevada. Known by the moniker "America's Racing Showplace," the facility has a seating capacity of 80,000 and is currently owned by Speedway Motorsports, which acquired the venue in 1999.

### History
Construction of the initial track complex began on February 24, 1971, with an initial investment of US$1 million. The complex officially opened on May 16, 1971. Over several decades, the venue operated under various names, including the Las Vegas International Speedrome (1971–1990), Las Vegas International Speedway (1990–1992), Las Vegas Speedway (1992), and Las Vegas Speedway Park (1993–1996). The main tri-oval track was developed with a construction cost of US$72 million.

### Stages and Configurations
The complex features multiple racing configurations to accommodate different disciplines of motorsport:

*   **Tri-Oval:** The primary asphalt circuit is 1.500 miles (2.414 km) in length. It features 20° banking in the turns and 9° banking on both the frontstretch and backstretch. Prior to 2006, the turn banking was 12°. A quarter-mile oval was added to the frontstretch in 2006.
*   **The Bullring Oval:** A shorter oval circuit measuring 0.375 miles (0.604 km), operational since 1985.
*   **Road Course Complex:** Established in 1996, the infield contains three distinct road course layouts:
    *   **Long Road Course:** 2.499 miles (4.022 km).
    *   **Medium Road Course:** 2.150 miles (3.460 km).
    *   **Short Road Course:** 1.938 miles (3.119 km).

The venue has also utilized "roval" (road course/oval hybrid) configurations for specific events.

### Lineup of Major Events
The speedway hosts a variety of professional racing series. Current major events include:
*   **NASCAR Cup Series:** Including the Pennzoil 400 (since 1998) and the South Point 400 (since 2018).
*   **NASCAR O'Reilly Auto Parts Series:** Including The LiUNA! (since 1997) and the Focused Health 302 (since 2018).
*   **Formula D:** Hosted from 2008–2012, with a scheduled return in 2026.

Former notable events include the IZOD IndyCar World Championship (1996–2000, 2011), the Grand Prix of Las Vegas (American Le Mans Series, 1997–2000), the Champ Car World Series (2004–2005), and the Indy Autonomous Challenge (2022–2025).
