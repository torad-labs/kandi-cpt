EDC Las Vegas, organized by Insomniac, is North America's largest EDM festival, featuring house, techno, drum and bass, and dubstep. The next event is scheduled for May 15–17, 2026, at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. 

The festival incorporates 3D superstructures, LED flora/fauna, interactive art, carnival performers, 18 rides, and four Ferris wheels. Since 2018, an on-site camping experience has been available. Ticket purchasing is available via mobile apps.

The event utilizes eight stages: kineticFIELD (capacity 70,000), cosmicMEADOW, circuitGROUNDS, neonGARDEN, bassPOD, wasteLAND, quantumVALLEY, and stereoBLOOM. 

Historically, EDC began in 1991 in Chino, California. It expanded to a two-day event in 2009 and a three-day event in 2011. In 2017, it won Festival of the Year at the Electronic Music Awards. 2024 attendance totaled 525,000 over three days. Beyond Las Vegas, EDC operates annually in Orlando, China, and Mexico, with former events in Puerto Rico, UK, Brazil, Japan, and India.
