### Philanthropy and Community Outreach
Insomniac Care, the charitable arm of the festival organizer, allocates a percentage of ticket sales from flagship events to community organizations and national charities. Since 2011, these initiatives have raised over two million dollars. The organization has utilized Surreal, a fundraising platform, to facilitate donations via attendee sweepstakes for experiences such as helicopter rides with DJs. Recipient organizations include the Nevada Childhood Cancer Foundation, the Boys and Girls Club of Southern Nevada, The Las Vegas Academy for Arts, the Injured Police Officers Fund, Families for Effective Autism Treatment, and Rock the Vote.

### Media and Documentation
The 2013 EDC Las Vegas event was the subject of the documentary film *Under the Electric Sky*. The film, which focuses on the community and experiences of festival attendees, premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival.

### Industry Recognition and Rankings
Electric Daisy Carnival has received multiple nominations for "Best Music Event" or "Best Festival" at the International Dance Music Awards from 2010 through 2016, and again in 2019 and 2020. In 2018, award winners were selected by the Winter Music Conference; public voting resumed in 2019.

The festival is consistently ranked by *DJ Magazine* in its "Top 100 Festivals" list. In 2022, the event was ranked 3rd; it placed 4th in 2023 and 2nd in 2024.
