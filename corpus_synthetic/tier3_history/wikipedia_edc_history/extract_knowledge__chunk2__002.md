The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) experienced a significant operational shift between 2010 and 2011 following safety failures in Los Angeles. The EDC Los Angeles event drew approximately 185,000 attendees, with ticket prices ranging from $60 to over $100. The event faced criticism from promoters and local authorities due to the admission of minors under the age of 16 and a crowd stampede that resulted in over 100 hospitalizations. A 15-year-old attendee, Sasha Rodriguez, died following the ingestion of MDMA.

In response to these incidents, the city of Los Angeles suspended all remaining 2010 events and future bookings pending the implementation of new safety provisions. These mandates required attendees to be at least 18 years of age and the employment of on-site medical doctors. Other 2010 regional events included EDC Colorado on June 12 at the Arapahoe County Fairground in Aurora, EDC Dallas on June 19 at Fair Park (11,000 attendees), and EDC Puerto Rico on August 28 at the Estadio Sixto Escobar in San Juan.

In 2011, the flagship festival relocated to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in Nevada, where it ran from June 24–26 with a reported attendance of 230,000 over three days. Other 2011 regional iterations included:

*   **EDC Orlando:** Held May 27–28 at Tinker Field and the Florida Citrus Bowl grounds, with approximately 12,000 attendees on Friday and 20,000 on Saturday.
*   **EDC Colorado:** Held June 11 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds.
*   **EDC Dallas:** Held June 18 at Fair Park with approximately 25,000 attendees. Due to temperatures exceeding 110 degrees, which caused dozens of hospitalizations and one death, this was the final EDC event held in Dallas.
*   **EDC Puerto Rico:** Held August 27 at the Estadio Sixto Escobar.
