# Insomniac Events

**Insomniac Events** is an American promoter and organizer of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals and club events. The company manages a diverse portfolio of large-scale festivals, boutique experiences, and recurring event series across North America and internationally.

## Event Portfolio

### Major Festivals
Insomniac produces several flagship festivals characterized by high production values and immersive environments. These include:
* **Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC):** The company's primary brand, with major installments in Las Vegas and various international locations.
* **Middlelands:** A regional festival held in the Midwestern United States.
* **Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival:** A multi-genre event integrating music, art, and camping.

### Event Series and Boutique Experiences
The company operates several themed event series and smaller-scale productions:
* **Crush:** A regional series with activations in Southern California, San Francisco, Phoenix, and Dallas.
* **White Wonderland:** A seasonal winter-themed event.
* **Bay Nites:** A series of events focused on the Northern California market.
* **Together as One:** A collaborative event series.
* **We Are NRG:** A themed production focused on high-energy dance music.
* **Project Z:** A specialized event concept.

### Specialized Logistics
Insomniac manages niche event formats, including **Holy Ship!**, a cruise-based music festival that integrates performance stages with maritime travel.

## Production and Stage Design

The company utilizes a standardized approach to stage architecture and thematic design to maintain brand consistency across its events. Stage names and designators typically follow a camelCase naming convention (e.g., `circuitGrounds`, `kineticField`, `electricSky`).
