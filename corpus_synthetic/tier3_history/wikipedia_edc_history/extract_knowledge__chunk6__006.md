Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music festivals. For the 2026 calendar year, three events are currently planned:

**EDC Thailand** is scheduled to take place from January 16 to 18 at the Boat Avenue Lakefront in Phuket, Thailand.

**EDC Las Vegas** is scheduled for May 15 to 17 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in North Las Vegas, Nevada.

**EDC Colombia** is scheduled for October 10 to 11 at the Estadio Atanasio Girardot in Medellín, Colombia.
