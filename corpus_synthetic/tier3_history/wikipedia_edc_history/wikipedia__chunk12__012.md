**Insomniac Events** is an American promoter and organizer of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals and club events. Founded by Pasquale Rotella, the company is best known for producing the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) and managing a diverse portfolio of music brands, venues, and media outlets.

### Corporate History and Partnerships
In 2013, Insomniac entered into a creative partnership with Live Nation, which subsequently acquired a 50% stake in the company. The organization has expanded its operations beyond event promotion into music publishing and distribution through the establishment of the Insomniac Music Group and a label imprint launched in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M.

In recent years, the company has ventured into digital technology, partnering with Unity in 2022 to develop a dance-music metaverse. During the COVID-19 pandemic, Insomniac pivoted to virtual programming, launching the "Rave-A-Thon" series of livestreams for brands including HARD Summer and Countdown.

### Event Portfolio
Insomniac manages a wide array of festival brands catering to various subgenres of electronic music:

*   **Mainstream & Hybrid:** Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC), Beyond Wonderland, and Day Trip.
*   **Genre-Specific:** Basscon (bass music), Dreamstate (trance), and Lost In Dreams (future bass and melodic dubstep).
*   **Underground & Niche:** Factory 93, Forbidden Kingdom, and Project Z.
*   **Themed & Boutique:** Apocalypse Zombieland, BOO!, and the Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival.
*   **Other Productions:** Middlelands, Bay Nites, Crush, We Are NRG, and Holy Ship!.

### Venues and Logistics
The company maintains a significant footprint of physical nightlife venues. In Los Angeles, it has operated Create and Exchange L.A. Its national expansion includes the acquisition of Club Glow, Soundcheck, and Echostage in Washington D.C., as well as becoming the majority owner of Club Space in Miami. In Orlando, the company debuted the Vanguard venue.

To accommodate attendee logistics at large-scale festivals, Insomniac has integrated "vanlife" trends and specialized camping arrangements to manage the movement and housing of large crowds.

### Media and Broadcasting
Insomniac operates several media channels to promote its artists and events:
*   **Radio:** The company operates Insomniac Radio, which has partnered with Dash Radio and SiriusXM. This includes the launch of Night Owl Radio on SiriusXM, which replaced the "Electric Area" channel.
*   **Audiovisual:** The company has produced documentary content and mixed compilations, such as *Under the Electric Sky* and *Electric Daisy Carnival, Vol. 1*.

### Safety and Innovation
In response to public health mandates in 2020, Insomniac implemented socially distanced event models, such as the "Electric Mile," an audiovisual experience designed to minimize crowd density while maintaining event operations.
