Friend: I'm finally doing it! I'm going to EDC 2026. I know it's at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, but I've never been there. Is it a weird spot for a party?

Vet: Not at all, it's actually legendary for festivals. It's a massive complex. Fun fact: it originally opened back in '96 for IndyCar practice. It's got a huge history of high-speed racing, which is why there's so much room for the stages.

Friend: Wait, is it just one big track? Where do the stages actually go?

Vet: It's a giant facility. The main party happens across the grounds, but the "Bullring"—which is their smaller oval—is a key part of the layout. You'll be spending most of your time at kineticFIELD for the big headliners on Friday and Saturday.

Friend: I've heard the venue is huge. Will I be walking forever?

Vet: It is massive. Back in 2006, they did a huge renovation to increase capacity to 141,000 people. That's why the scale of the event is so wild. My advice? Wear comfortable shoes because you'll be trekking between the main stages and the smaller ones like circuitGROUNDS.

Friend: How did EDC even end up at a race track?

Vet: It happened in 2011. EDC had some controversy at its old spot in Los Angeles, so they moved the flagship event to LVMS. It's been the home of the festival ever since.

Friend: Is there anything else at the venue besides the main track?

Vet: Yeah, there's a whole complex. They have a drag strip that was actually expanded to four lanes a few years ago. You won't see it while you're dancing at neonGARDENS, but it's part of why the venue is so versatile.

Friend: I'm a bit nervous about the crowd. Is it safe?

Vet: Totally. Just follow the flow. The venue is designed for huge crowds—they've been hosting NASCAR Cup Series weekends for decades, so they know how to handle 100k+ people.

Friend: Which stage should I hit first on Friday?

Vet: Definitely start at kineticFIELD to get the energy up, then migrate over to bassCanyon for the heavier stuff. Since you're a first-timer, just remember that the scale of the Speedway is a bit overwhelming at first, but you'll love it.

Friend: Thanks for the tips! I can't wait to see the lights on that track.

Vet: You're gonna love it. Just remember: hydrate, wear a wristband, and I'll see you at the Speedway!
