Q: Where is the Las Vegas Motor Speedway located?
A: Las Vegas, Nevada.

Q: What is the length of the tri-oval intermediate speedway at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: 1.500 mi (2.414 km).

Q: What was the capacity of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway as of 2023?
A: 80,000.

Q: Who owns the Las Vegas Motor Speedway facility?
A: Speedway Motorsports, LLC (SMI).

Q: Who is the track general manager of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: Patrick Lindsey.

Q: What are the lengths of the two oval tracks adjacent to the main track at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: 0.375 mi (0.604 km) and 0.500 mi (0.805 km).

Q: What is the name of the 0.375 mi (0.604 km) oval added in 1985?
A: The Bullring.

Q: In what year was the 1.500 mi (2.414 km) oval at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway completed?
A: 1996.
