Q: Who granted Pasquale Rotella the use of the name Electric Daisy Carnival in 1995?
A: Philip Blaine.

Q: Which event did Philip Blaine and Pasquale Rotella create between 1995 and the first EDC?
A: Organic '96.

Q: Where did Insomniac debut Electric Daisy Carnival in 1997?
A: The Shrine Expo Hall in Los Angeles, California.

Q: Where was Electric Daisy Carnival held in 1999?
A: Lake Dolores Waterpark in Newberry Springs, California.

Q: How many attendees did the 2000 Electric Daisy Carnival at the International Agri-Center attract?
A: 24,000.

Q: Which stages were featured at the 2001 Electric Daisy Carnival at Hansen Dam?
A: The Merry Go Round, the Fun House, Clown Alley, the Confusin' & Amuzin' Mirror Maze, Bassrush Arena and Cosmic Healing Temple.

Q: Where was the first EDC Texas held in 2001?
A: Thunderhill Raceway Park in Austin, Texas.

Q: Where was EDC 2002 held in California?
A: Queen Mary Events Park in Long Beach, California.

Q: Where was Electric Daisy Carnival held from 2003 to 2006?
A: The NOS Events Center in San Bernardino, California.

Q: Where was Electric Daisy Carnival held in 2007?
A: The Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum in Los Angeles' Exposition Park.

Q: When and where did the EDC Colorado event take place in 2008?
A: June 14 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora.

Q: How many attendees were present on Friday and Saturday of the two-day California event held June 26–27?
A: Approximately 55,000 attendees on Friday and approximately 99,000 on Saturday.

Q: Who headlined the June 13 EDC Colorado event at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in 2009?
A: Infected Mushroom and Paul Van Dyk.

Q: When and where was the first EDC Puerto Rico held?
A: August 14, 2009, at the Arena Fairgrounds in San Juan.
