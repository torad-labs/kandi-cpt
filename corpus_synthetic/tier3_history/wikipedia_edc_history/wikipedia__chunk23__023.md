### Non-NASCAR Racing Events
The Las Vegas Motor Speedway's half-mile dirt track served as a venue for the World of Outlaws Sprint Car Series across two periods: 1996–2006 and 2009–2019. High Limit Racing is scheduled to compete at the facility in 2025, coinciding with the NASCAR Cup Series weekend.

Between 2014 and 2016, the venue hosted the Red Bull Air Race World Championship. The 2014 event was abandoned due to adverse weather, with final standings determined by qualifying speeds. A successful event was held in 2015; however, the 2016 event was cancelled entirely due to high winds. In 2020, the outer road course was utilized for Roborace Season Beta.

### Festivals and Public Events
Since 2011, the speedway has been the flagship location for the Electric Daisy Carnival. The event relocated to the venue from Los Angeles following safety concerns and controversies regarding underage drug use at the previous site, which had resulted in multiple injuries and the death of a 15-year-old.

The venue has hosted various other specialized events, including:
* **Sports:** The World Long Drive Championship in 2013.
* **Culinary:** The Las Vegas branch of the FoodieLand Night Market, an annual food festival held since 2023.
* **Academic:** A graduation ceremony for Faith Lutheran High School in 2020, conducted in response to the COVID-19 pandemic.

### Driving Programs
The facility hosts several professional driving experiences. Since 2012, former Italian Formula Three driver Enrico Bertaggia has operated the Dream Racing program, allowing members of the public to drive exotic vehicles on the track's road course. Additionally, the venue hosts Driver's Edge, a specialized driving education course.
