**Insomniac Events and Corporate Structure**

Insomniac is the organizing entity behind a diverse portfolio of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals and events. The organization's longest-running festival brand is Nocturnal Wonderland, which served as the company's first public event. Other active event brands include Beyond Wonderland, Dreamstate, Escape Halloween (formerly Escape From Wonderland), and Project GLOW. 

The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a primary festival brand with active iterations in Las Vegas, Mexico, Orlando, and Portugal. Previous iterations in the United Kingdom, India, Puerto Rico, and Brazil have been discontinued. Insomniac also maintains a partial collaboration with Madison House under the Anschutz Entertainment Group for the Electric Forest festival.

**Insomniac Music Group and Label Operations**

In May 2014, Insomniac established Insomniac Records in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M to sign both emerging and established producers; Arty was the inaugural signee, followed by Chris Lake and Bingo Players. In December 2018, the company formed the Insomniac Music Group to provide distribution and A&R services for its in-house imprints. 

Current imprints under the Insomniac Music Group include:
*   Basscon Records
*   Bassrush Records
*   Discovery Project
*   Dreamstate Records
*   Gud Vibrations
*   HARD Recordings
*   In / Rotation
*   Lost in Dreams (a label focused on future bass and melodic dubstep)
*   What To Do

**Digital Integration and Brand Expansion**

Insomniac has expanded into digital media and virtual environments. This includes the launch of "Insomniac Rewind" on YouTube and Twitch, and a 2022 partnership with Unity Technologies to develop a persistent online world for virtual concerts. The company has also utilized radio platforms, transitioning from SiriusXM's Electric Area to Insomniac Radio and subsequently partnering with Dash Radio in May 2019.

In terms of physical venues and acquisitions, Insomniac acquired a majority stake in Miami's Club Space in July 2019. While the company previously held a controlling stake in Soundslinger, LLC (the organizer of the Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival), Soundslinger announced in April 2025 that Insomniac was no longer involved with the festival.
