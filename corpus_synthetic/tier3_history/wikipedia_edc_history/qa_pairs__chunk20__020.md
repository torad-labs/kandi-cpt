Q: On what date did Clyne announce plans to build a $65-70 million, 100,000-seat facility near the Las Vegas Speedway Park?
A: November 30.

Q: What was the scheduled opening date for the facility announced by Clyne on November 30?
A: April 1, 1996.

Q: Where was the track officially confirmed to be built in January 1995?
A: Along Interstate 15 on a 1,100 acre plot of land.

Q: What additional feature was included in the January 1995 plans along with the track?
A: A 40-acre industrial park.

Q: When was the 200 miles (320 km) race to christen the track scheduled to be held?
A: September 15, 1996.

Q: Who was named the general manager of the track in January 1996?
A: Ray Wilkings.

Q: How much revenue was the track projected to bring to the Las Vegas economy by the end of February 1996?
A: Over $890 million.

Q: When did Clyne buy out the Las Vegas Speedway Park from Juberg?
A: April.

Q: According to Bruton Smith, who funded the majority of the project?
A: Ralph Engelstad.

Q: Which Sahara Hotel owner joined as a part owner of the track in September?
A: Bill Bennett.
