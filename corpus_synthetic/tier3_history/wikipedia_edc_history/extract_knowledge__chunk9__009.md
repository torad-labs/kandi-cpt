Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is the flagship electronic music festival organized by Insomniac, an American event promoter and music distributor founded in 1993 by Pasquale Rotella. Headquartered in Beverly Hills, California, Insomniac operates as a private entity specializing in electronic dance music (EDM).

In addition to EDC, the promoter manages several genre-specific event brands: Bassrush (drum and bass and dubstep), Basscon (hardstyle), and Dreamstate (trance), as well as the Beyond Wonderland and Nocturnal Wonderland festivals. The organization also maintains a music distribution arm, Insomniac Music Group, which evolved from Insomniac Records, a label established in 2014 through a partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M.

Insomniac's operational portfolio includes the management of multiple nightclubs. In Los Angeles, it operates Exchange L.A., Factory 93, and Create (in partnership with SBE). Through the acquisition of Club Glow, Insomniac manages Echostage and Soundcheck in Washington, D.C., as well as the Project GLOW and Moonrise music festivals. The company also acquired Club Space in Miami in 2019.

Strategically, Insomniac maintains a creative partnership with Live Nation to access corporate resources while retaining creative control. The promoter has also partnered with Dick Clark Productions to produce dance music awards programming.
