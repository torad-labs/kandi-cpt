Friend: I'm trying to map out my 2026 travel for a feature piece. What's the deal with EDC in 2026? Is it just Vegas?

Vet: Definitely not just Vegas, though that's the crown jewel. You've got Thailand kicking things off in January from the 16th to the 18th at Boat Avenue Lakefront in Phuket.

Friend: Thailand sounds wild. What about the main event?

Vet: EDC Las Vegas is locked in for May 15–17 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. If you're going, you've gotta hit kineticFIELD on Friday night for the headliner—it's the only way to experience the scale of that place.

Friend: I saw something about a new location. Is that actually happening?

Vet: Yeah, the big news for 2026 is EDC Colombia. It's planned for October 10–11 at Estadio Atanasio Girardot in Medellín. It'll be a huge vibe shift from the Speedway.

Friend: Man, the scale of this thing is insane now. Was it always this massive?

Vet: Not even close. Back in '91, it started in Chino, California, with only about 3,000 to 3,500 people. It was basically a warehouse party compared to the 525,000 people we saw at the Speedway in 2023 and 2024.

Friend: That's a crazy jump. Did they just jump straight to the Motor Speedway?

Vet: Nah, they bounced around a lot. They did the Shrine Auditorium in '97, Lake Dolores in '99, and spent a huge chunk of the early 2000s—like 2003 through 2006—at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino.

Friend: I remember seeing something about them going global. When did they actually start hitting other countries?

Vet: They dabbled early with Puerto Rico in 2009, but the real expansion hit around 2011. That's when they launched EDC Orlando at Tinker Field and started hitting places like Mexico, the UK, and eventually Brazil in 2015.

Friend: Did they ever try Asia?

Vet: Oh yeah, they went hard there. They did China in 2018 and Korea in 2019. They're actually returning to Korea in 2025 at the Inspire Arena before the 2026 run kicks off.

Friend: Any other weird experiments I should know about for my article?

Vet: If you want a quirk, look up EDSea. They literally put the festival on a cruise ship—the Norwegian Joy. They've had runs in 2023, 2024, and they've got another one planned for November 2025.

Friend: Last question—is it always this consistent, or do they actually miss dates?

Vet: They've had a few hiccups. They didn't organize the event at all in '94, '96, and '98. More recently, the pandemic wiped out Vegas in 2020, and Typhoon Bebinca actually forced them to cancel EDC China in 2024. But they always bounce back.
