**Electric Daisy Carnival** (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac Events. The flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, is one of the largest dance music festivals in the world, known for its large-scale production, pyrotechnics, and immersive art installations.

### Overview
EDC began as a single-day event in California in 1997 and has since expanded into a global brand with editions in Mexico, Orlando, and other international locations. The festival is characterized by its "Under the Electric Sky" theme, featuring neon aesthetics and elaborate stage designs. In 2024, the Las Vegas edition reported 525,000 attendees.

### History and Evolution
The festival's origins trace back to 1996 and 1997 in California. Early iterations faced various challenges, including noise complaints and venue disputes in Tulare. In the mid-2000s, the event grew in scale and popularity, eventually moving to the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum.

Following a period of controversy and conflict at the Coliseum, including a ban on new raves and reports of attendee fatalities related to substance use, Insomniac relocated the flagship event to Las Vegas in 2011. The move to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway allowed for significant expansion in production and capacity. The event has since become a major economic driver for Clark County; for example, the 2013 event reportedly generated $278 million for the region.

The brand has expanded internationally with the introduction of EDC Mexico in 2014, as well as events in the United Kingdom, Brazil, Japan, and China. In 2023, Insomniac launched EDSea, a festival cruise centered in the Caribbean.

### Stages and Production
EDC is noted for its high production value, utilizing drone shows, fireworks, and complex lighting. The main stage often sets records for size and assembly in North America, with some iterations costing upwards of $3 million to construct.

The festival layout typically includes several themed stages, such as:
* **kineticTHEATRE**
* **circuitGROUNDS**
* **bassPOD**
* **pyramID**

### Lineup
The festival features a broad spectrum of electronic music, ranging from house and techno to dubstep and trance. Headliners have historically included artists such as Tiësto, Armin van Buuren, Martin Garrix, and Zedd. Recent editions have seen the debut of artists such as Four Tet and Peggy Gou, as well as surprise performances from acts like Snoop Dogg and Grimes.

### Logistics and Safety
Due to its scale and the nature of the environment, EDC has implemented various logistical and safety measures:
* **Climate Adaptation:** In 2018, Insomniac shifted the festival dates to May and introduced air-conditioned campers to mitigate heat-related health risks.
* **Emergency Response:** The festival employs extensive medical teams. However, the event has faced challenges with attendee fatalities linked to heat and drug use.
* **Weather Contingencies:** The event has been subject to weather-related disruptions, including the evacuation of attendees due to high winds in 2012 and 2019.
* **COVID-19 Impact:** The 2020 Las Vegas event was postponed and eventually canceled due to the virus outbreak, leading to the creation of a digital alternative.

### Recognition
EDC has been recognized globally within the electronic music community, frequently appearing in *DJ Mag*'s rankings of the top festivals in the world and receiving nominations for the International Dance Music Awards (IDMA).
