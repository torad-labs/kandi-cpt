### Electric Daisy Carnival: Historical Overview and Operational Shifts

The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a large-scale electronic dance music festival. In 2010, the event was hosted in Los Angeles with an attendance of approximately 185,000. Ticket prices for this event ranged from $60 to over $100. The 2010 Los Angeles event resulted in significant regulatory scrutiny following a crowd stampede that hospitalized over 100 individuals and the death of a 15-year-old attendee, Sasha Rodriguez, attributed to the use of MDMA, a psychoactive drug.

Due to these safety failures and the unauthorized entry of minors under the age of 16, the City of Los Angeles suspended all remaining 2010 events and future scheduling. New security provisions were mandated, requiring all attendees to be at least 18 years of age and the presence of on-site medical doctors. Other 2010 satellite events included EDC Colorado (June 12 at Arapahoe County Fairground, Aurora), EDC Dallas (June 19 at Fair Park, with 11,000 attendees), and EDC Puerto Rico (August 28 at Estadio Sixto Escobar, San Juan).

In 2011, the flagship festival relocated to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in Nevada, running from June 24–26. This three-day event drew an estimated 230,000 attendees. Other 2011 regional events included:
*   **EDC Orlando:** May 27–28 at Tinker Field and the Florida Citrus Bowl grounds, with 12,000 attendees on Friday and 20,000 on Saturday.
*   **EDC Colorado:** June 11 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds.
*   **EDC Dallas:** June 18 at Fair Park, with approximately 25,000 attendees. High temperatures exceeding 110 degrees caused dozens of hospitalizations and one fatality, ending the festival's presence in Dallas.
*   **EDC Puerto Rico:** August 27 at the Estadio Sixto Escobar.

Notable performers at subsequent iterations include Michael Calfan, who appeared at EDC Las Vegas in 2016.
