**Artists & Performances**

**Venue & Locations**
* EDC Las Vegas is hosted at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway.
* EDC Mexico is held at Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City.
* EDC New York took place at Citi Field in Queens.
* EDC UK was hosted at Milton Keynes in England.
* EDC Orlando is held at Tinker Field.
* EDC India took place at Buddh International Circuit in New Delhi.
* EDC Japan is held at Zozo Marine Stadium and Makuhari Beach Park.
* EDC China debuted at the Shanghai International Music Park.
* EDC Korea was held at Seoul Land in Gwacheon.

**Operations Policies**
* EDC Las Vegas offers on-site camping for attendees.
* Rentable campers and RV parking are available at the Las Vegas venue.
* Las Vegas opening times were shifted earlier to reduce traffic congestion.
* EDC Las Vegas enforces a strict no-reentry policy.
* EDC Orlando expanded its duration to three days in 2019.
* EDC Las Vegas moved its schedule from June to May for cooler weather.

**Safety & Health**
* Heat-related health incidents increased significantly during the 2017 event.
* High winds can cause immediate stage closures for safety.
* Attendees are instructed to take cover in cars during severe weather alerts.
* MDMA and TFMPP combined with heat exposure can be fatal.
* Heavy rains can cause technical equipment issues at outdoor venues.
* Local police conduct arrests for DUIs and felonies during the festival.

**Wayfinding & Logistics**
* Safety announcements are broadcast via on-site screens.
* Audio messages are used to direct crowds during emergencies.
* Attendees should verify stage closures before exiting the venue.
