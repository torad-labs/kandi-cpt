Friend: Yo! I'm finally trying to plan for EDC 2026. Since you've been to basically every rave ever, can you help me get my head around how Insomniac actually works?

Vet: Haha, I got you. Just keep in mind that Insomniac is way more than just a festival promoter now; they're a full-blown media and music empire.

Friend: Really? I thought they just did the big parties. Do they actually run records too?

Vet: Oh yeah. They started Insomniac Records back in 2014 with Interscope. Arty was actually the first one signed, then they brought in guys like Chris Lake and Bingo Players. Now they have the Insomniac Music Group which handles A&R for a ton of imprints like Basscon Records, Dreamstate Records, and Gud Vibrations.

Friend: That's wild. So if I'm looking at the EDC lineup for 2026, are there specific stages I should prioritize?

Vet: For sure. You have to hit kineticFIELD for the main stage energy, but if you want that melodic vibe, head to the Dreamstate stage. If you're into the heavier stuff, look for the Basscon or Bassrush areas. They even have a newer brand called Lost in Dreams specifically for future bass and melodic dubstep.

Friend: I heard they do other festivals too. Is it just EDC?

Vet: Not even close. They do Beyond Wonderland, Escape Halloween, and Nocturnal Wonderland—which is actually their longest-running brand and the first public event they ever did. They also do Countdown for NYE and have a partial collab with Madison House for Electric Forest.

Friend: What about the smaller stuff? I saw something about a "Park 'N Rave" thing online.

Vet: That was a COVID-era move. When they couldn't do big festivals, they did those drive-in shows at the NOS Events Center and a "drive-through" thing called Electric Mile at Santa Anita Park. They also did "Insomniac Rewind" streams on YouTube and Twitch.

Friend: Do they have a radio station? I want some new mixes for the drive to Vegas.

Vet: Definitely. They've jumped around a bit—started with Night Owl Radio on SiriusXM's Electric Area, then launched Insomniac Radio, and eventually partnered with Dash Radio as their flagship station in 2019.

Friend: I remember seeing something about them owning clubs and other festivals, like Okeechobee?

Vet: They did! They bought a majority stake in Club Space in Miami and a controlling stake in Soundslinger for Okeechobee back in 2019. But actually, as of April 2025, Soundslinger announced that Insomniac isn't involved with Okeechobee anymore.

Friend: Last question—I saw some tech news about them doing something with Unity. Are we going to be raving in VR by 2026?

Vet: It's possible! In 2022, they partnered with Unity Technologies to build a "persistent" online world for virtual concerts. So, while we're hitting the Las Vegas desert for EDC 2026, there might be a digital version happening at the same time.
