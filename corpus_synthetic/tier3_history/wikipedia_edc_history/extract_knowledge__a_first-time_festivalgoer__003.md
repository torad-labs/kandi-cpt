**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas**
EDC Las Vegas is a multi-day electronic dance music festival held at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. In 2012 (June 8–10), the event saw 320,000 attendees. The second night of the 2012 festival concluded early at 1:00 a.m. due to winds reaching 30 MPH. During this weather event, DJs Markus Schulz and Steve Aoki performed brief sets on the "Insomniac Wide Awake" art car, a mobile stage, before it was closed for safety.

In 2014 (June 20–22), Insomniac Events sold 345,000 tickets, averaging 134,000 attendees per day. The 2014 production featured the largest structural stage in North America. The 2015 event took place June 19–21 and featured a blinking owl theme on the main stage.

**Regional and International Expansions**
Insomniac Events operates satellite EDC festivals globally. In the United States, these have included:
*   **EDC Orlando:** Held at Tinker Field.
*   **EDC New York:** Held at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey. In 2012, this event drew 45,000 attendees per day.
*   **EDC Chicago:** Established in 2013.

International iterations include:
*   **EDC Mexico:** First held March 15–16, 2014; later held at Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City.
*   **EDC Puerto Rico:** Held at Estadio Sixto Escobar.
*   **EDC UK:** Held at the National Bowl in Milton Keynes.
*   **EDC Brazil:** First held December 4–5, 2015, at Autódromo José Carlos Pace in São Paulo.

**Incident Reports and Media**
Festival safety records include a 2012 fatality involving a 31-year-old male struck by a truck while exiting the Las Vegas Motor Speedway on June 11. In 2015, a UC Irvine graduate died during the second night of the Las Vegas event; the cause of death was ruled as related to MDMA, which can cause dehydration and hyperthermia. 

In 2014, a documentary titled *Under the Electric Sky* premiered at the Sundance Film Festival, detailing the festival's operations.
