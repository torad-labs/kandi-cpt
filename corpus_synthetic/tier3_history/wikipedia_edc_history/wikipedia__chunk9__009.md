**Insomniac** (formerly **Insomniac Events**) is an American electronic music event promoter, music distributor, and venue operator headquartered in Beverly Hills, California. Founded in 1993 by Pasquale Rotella, the company specializes in the production of large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festivals and the management of nightclubs across the United States.

### Overview
Insomniac is characterized by a corporate philosophy that emphasizes fan experience and immersive production over traditional ticket-sales models. Founder Pasquale Rotella has cited the community-driven nature of events such as Burning Man as an influence on the organization's approach to providing open spaces for attendee creativity. 

In June 2013, the company entered into a creative partnership with Live Nation, granting Insomniac access to Live Nation's operational resources while maintaining independent creative control over its events. Additionally, the company partnered with Dick Clark Productions to produce a dance music awards program.

### Event Portfolio
Insomniac organizes a diverse range of festivals and event brands tailored to specific sub-genres of electronic music:

*   **Flagship Events:** The company's primary event is the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC), with its largest iteration held annually in Las Vegas. Other major festivals include Beyond Wonderland and Nocturnal Wonderland.
*   **Genre-Specific Brands:**
    *   **Bassrush:** Focused on drum and bass and dubstep.
    *   **Basscon:** Dedicated to hardstyle.
    *   **Dreamstate:** Focused on trance music.
*   **Additional Festivals:** The company's portfolio includes the Hard music festival and the Audiotistic Music Festival.

### Venue Operations
The company operates several nightclubs and warehouse venues across major U.S. metropolitan areas:

*   **Los Angeles:** Insomniac manages Exchange L.A., the warehouse venue Factory 93, and Create (operated in partnership with SBE).
*   **Miami:** The company acquired Club Space in 2019.
*   **Washington, D.C.:** In 2020, Insomniac acquired the operator Club Glow, which oversees the Echostage and Soundcheck nightclubs, as well as the Project GLOW and Moonrise Music festivals.

### Music Distribution
In 2014, Insomniac launched Insomniac Records, a co-branded EDM record label established in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M. By 2018, the company transitioned its A&R and distribution operations in-house under the entity Insomniac Music Group. The company also operates Insomniac Radio.
