**EDC Mexico and Las Vegas (2020–2021)**
EDC Mexico took place February 28–March 2, 2020, at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City. In Las Vegas, the 2020 event was rescheduled from May 15–17 to October 2–4 due to the COVID-19 pandemic; a virtual version was streamed on Twitch and YouTube during the May dates before the October event was cancelled in August 2020. The 2021 EDC Las Vegas event, originally set for May 21–23, was moved to October 22–24 because Clark County required a 60% resident vaccination rate before allowing large-scale gatherings.

**Recent Developments (2024–2025)**
In 2024, EDC implemented structural changes to stage placement to increase dance floor size and improve crowd flow. The EDC Orlando lineup was announced in June 2024.

EDC Thailand debuted from January 17–19, 2025, at Boat Avenue Lakefront in Phuket. This inaugural event was marked by the deaths of two foreign tourists in suspected drug-related incidents and the arrest of three individuals for drug possession or use.
