# Electric Daisy Carnival (2012–2015)

The **Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a series of electronic dance music festivals produced by Insomniac Events. Between 2012 and 2015, the event expanded its footprint through a flagship annual gathering in Las Vegas and various satellite events across North America, Europe, and South America.

## Las Vegas Events

### 2012
The 2012 festival took place from June 8 to 10 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Attendance increased by 30% over previous years, totaling 320,000 attendees. 

During the second night, high winds reaching 30 mph began around 8:00 p.m., forcing the event to shut down early at 1:00 a.m. Approximately 90,000 fans were relocated to the speedway's bleachers. DJs Markus Schulz and Steve Aoki performed brief, impromptu sets on the `insomniacWideAwake` art car, a mobile stage, before officials closed the unit due to forecasts that dangerous winds would persist until 5:00 a.m. To compensate for the cancellation, Insomniac permitted Saturday pass holders to return on Sunday.

### 2013–2014
The 2013 event was held June 21–23. The 2014 festival occurred June 20–22 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Insomniac announced that all 345,000 tickets had been sold by June 18, 2014, with the event averaging approximately 134,000 attendees per day. The 2014 production featured what was cited as the largest structural stage in North America.

### 2015
The 2015 festival took place June 19–21 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, featuring a blinking owl as the primary background theme for the main stage.

## Regional and International Events

### North America
*   **New York:** Held at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey. Despite the venue's location, the event was branded as EDC New York due to its proximity to New York City. The 2012 edition (May 18–20) drew 45,000 attendees per day. Events were also held in 2013, 2014, and 2015 (May 23–24).
*   **Orlando:** Held at Tinker Field. The 2012 event took place November 9–10, coinciding with the "Dancegiving" Dayglow event in Fort Lauderdale. Subsequent events were held in 2013 and 2014, and on November 6–7, 2015.
*   **Other Locations:** Events were held in Chicago in 2013 and San Juan, Puerto Rico, from 2013 through 2015. The San Juan event was held at Estadio Sixto Escobar; the February 21–22, 2015, date marked its sixth occurrence.

### International
*   **Mexico:** The first EDC Mexico was held March 15–16, 2014. The second event took place February 28–March 1, 2015, at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City.
*   **United Kingdom:** Events were held in London in 2013 and at the National Bowl in Milton Keynes in 2014 and 2015 (July 17).
*   **Brazil:** The first EDC Brazil was held December 4–5, 2015, at the Autódromo José Carlos Pace in São Paulo.

## Logistics and Media
In 2014, the festival was the subject of a documentary film titled *Under the Electric Sky*, which premiered at the Sundance Film Festival.
