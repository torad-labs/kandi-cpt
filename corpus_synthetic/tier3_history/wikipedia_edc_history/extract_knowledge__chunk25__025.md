Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is the flagship electronic music festival organized by Insomniac, an American event promoter and music distributor founded in 1993 by Pasquale Rotella. Headquartered in Beverly Hills, California, Insomniac operates as a private company specializing in electronic music events and venue management.

Insomniac manages a diverse portfolio of music brands, including Beyond Wonderland, Nocturnal Wonderland, Bassrush (drum and bass and dubstep), Basscon (hardstyle), and Dreamstate (trance). The organization also operates the Insomniac Music Group, which internalized its A&R and distribution operations in 2018 following a 2014 partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M to launch Insomniac Records.

The promoter's operational reach extends to nightclub management. In Los Angeles, Insomniac manages Exchange L.A., Factory 93, and Create (in partnership with SBE). Its acquisitions include Club Space in Miami (2019) and the Washington, D.C.-based operator Club Glow (2020), which manages the nightclubs Echostage and Soundcheck, as well as the Moonrise and Project GLOW music festivals.

In June 2013, Insomniac established a creative partnership with Live Nation to access corporate resources while maintaining creative control over its events. Additionally, the promoter partnered with Dick Clark Productions to produce a dance music awards program.
