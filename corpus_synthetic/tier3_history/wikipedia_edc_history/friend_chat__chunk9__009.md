Friend: Yo! I’m thinking about finally hitting EDC 2026. Since you've been to like a million of these, what's the deal with the people running it?

Vet: You're talking about Insomniac. They're the legends behind the whole thing. Pasquale Rotella started the company back in '93 in Beverly Hills, and he's basically the face of the operation.

Friend: Are they just a one-festival company or do they do other stuff too?

Vet: Oh, they're huge. EDC is the flagship, but they run Beyond Wonderland and Nocturnal Wonderland. Depending on what you like, they have specific brands too—Bassrush for the dubstep/DnB heads, Basscon for hardstyle, and Dreamstate if you're into trance.

Friend: That's a lot of brands. Do they actually own any clubs, or just the festivals?

Vet: They've got a massive footprint. In LA, they manage Create, Exchange, and this warehouse spot called Factory 93. They also bought Club Space in Miami, and they own Echostage and Soundcheck in D.C. through their subsidiary, Club Glow.

Friend: Nice. I noticed some of the music on the posters is really specific. Do they have their own label?

Vet: They do. They started Insomniac Records back in 2014 with Interscope, but by 2018 they brought everything in-house under the Insomniac Music Group.

Friend: I've heard people say the vibe is different from other corporate festivals. Is that true?

Vet: Definitely. Pasquale always pushes the "fan experience" over just chasing profits. He’s actually praised Burning Man for being "by the people, for the people," and he tries to bring that same energy to EDC. He doesn't think you just need "arena-filling" acts to make a festival work; it's about the community.

Friend: Speaking of acts, where should I be hanging out for the best music?

Vet: It depends on the day! If you want the main energy, you gotta hit kineticFIELD. But if you're looking for those specific brand vibes I mentioned, keep an eye on the stages dedicated to Basscon or Dreamstate.

Friend: I saw something about Live Nation online—did they buy Insomniac out?

Vet: Nah, not an acquisition. They did a "creative partnership" back in 2013. Insomniac gets the resources of Live Nation, but Pasquale keeps total creative control over how the festivals actually look and feel.

Friend: Got it. I'm sold. Let's start planning for 2026!
