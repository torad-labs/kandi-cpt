Friend: Hey! I'm thinking about finally hitting EDC 2026. I know it's huge, but who actually puts the whole thing together?

Vet: That would be Insomniac. They're the powerhouse behind the whole scene. It was started back in '93 by Pasquale Rotella—he's basically the face of the company.

Friend: Oh, so they only do EDC?

Vet: Not even close. They run a ton of other stuff. If you like drum and bass or dubstep, they do Bassrush. For the hardstyle fans, there's Basscon, and if you're into trance, you've gotta check out Dreamstate. They also do Beyond Wonderland and Nocturnal Wonderland.

Friend: That's a lot of ground to cover. Do they just do festivals, or do they own clubs too?

Vet: They're all over the club scene. In LA, they manage Create, Exchange L.A., and this underground spot called Factory 93. They also picked up Club Space in Miami a few years back, and they own Echostage and Soundcheck in D.C. through their subsidiary, Club Glow.

Friend: I've seen some labels on the flyers. Do they run the music side too?

Vet: Yeah, they started Insomniac Records back in 2014 with Interscope, but by 2018 they brought everything in-house under the Insomniac Music Group for A&R and distribution.

Friend: I've heard the vibe is different from other corporate festivals. Is that true?

Vet: Definitely. Pasquale always says they prioritize the fan experience over just chasing ticket profits. He’s actually praised Burning Man for being "by the people, for the people," and he believes you don't always need those massive arena-filling acts to make a festival work.

Friend: Interesting. Do they do everything themselves or partner with the big guys?

Vet: They've got a "creative partnership" with Live Nation. It gives them access to a ton of resources, but the key is that Insomniac keeps total creative control over the events. It wasn't an acquisition, just a strategic deal.

Friend: So for 2026, if I want the full experience, where should I be?

Vet: You've gotta hit the main stages. I'm telling you right now, you'll spend your Friday night at kineticFIELD for the big energy, Saturday is for exploring the madness at circuitGROUNDS, and Sunday you wrap it all up at EDC World.

Friend: Sounds intense. I'm in!
