Q: How many people attended EDC Los Angeles?
A: About 185,000 people.

Q: What were the ticket prices for EDC Los Angeles?
A: Tickets started at $60, with some attendees paying over $100.

Q: Who was the 15-year-old attendee who died after taking MDMA at EDC Los Angeles?
A: Sasha Rodriguez.

Q: What new security and safety provisions were implemented by the city of Los Angeles following the 2010 events?
A: The provisions included the hiring of on-site doctors and a requirement that attendees be over 18 years of age.

Q: When and where was EDC Colorado held in 2010?
A: It was held on June 12 at the Arapahoe County Fairground in Aurora.

Q: How many people attended EDC Dallas on June 19, 2010, at Fair Park?
A: 11,000 people.

Q: When and where was EDC Puerto Rico held in 2010?
A: It was held on August 28, 2010, at the Estadio Sixto Escobar at Puerta de Tierra, San Juan.

Q: When and where was the flagship EDC festival held in 2011?
A: It was held at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway from June 24–26.

Q: How many people reportedly attended the 3-day EDC Las Vegas festival in 2011?
A: 230,000 people.

Q: What was the attendance for EDC Orlando 2011 on Friday and Saturday?
A: Approximately 12,000 people on Friday and 20,000 people on Saturday.

Q: Why was the June 18, 2011, EDC Dallas event at Fair Park the last EDC held in Dallas?
A: Temperatures above 110 degrees led to dozens of hospitalizations and at least one death.
