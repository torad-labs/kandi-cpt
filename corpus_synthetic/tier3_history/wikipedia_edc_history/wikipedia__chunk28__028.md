**Insomniac Events** is an American promoter and event production company specializing in electronic dance music (EDM). Founded by Pasquale Rotella, the company is best known for producing the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) and managing a diverse portfolio of music festivals, nightclub venues, and record labels. In 2013, the company entered into a creative partnership with Live Nation, which subsequently acquired a 50% stake in the organization.

### Event Portfolio
Insomniac produces a wide array of themed festivals and event series catering to various subgenres of electronic music:

*   **Large-Scale Festivals:** The company's flagship event is the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC). Other major productions include Beyond Wonderland, Countdown, and the Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival.
*   **Genre-Specific Brands:** Insomniac operates several specialized brands, including Basscon (bass music), Dreamstate (trance), Lost In Dreams (future bass and melodic dubstep), and Factory 93, an underground brand launched in 2016 focusing on warehouse-style events.
*   **Specialized Series:** The company's portfolio includes themed events such as Apocalypse Zombieland, Day Trip, Forbidden Kingdom, and the BOO Halloween series. Other notable productions include Middlelands, Project Z, and the Holy Ship! cruise.

### Venues and Logistics
Insomniac has expanded its operations from event promotion into the ownership and management of physical venues and hospitality:

*   **Club Ownership:** The company owns or maintains majority stakes in several high-profile venues, including Club Space Miami, and Washington D.C.-based venues Echostage and Soundcheck. In Los Angeles, the company has been involved with venues such as Create and Exchange L.A.
*   **Innovation and Technology:** In 2022, Insomniac partnered with the 3D tech platform Unity to develop a dance-music metaverse. During the COVID-19 pandemic, the company pivoted to virtual formats, launching the "Rave-A-Thon" livestream series and socially distanced experiences such as the "Electric Mile."
*   **Accommodations:** The company has integrated "Vanlife" culture into its festival logistics to accommodate mobile attendees.

### Media and Music Groups
The organization maintains a presence in music distribution and broadcasting:

*   **Insomniac Music Group:** This division provides label management and distribution services. The company has previously launched imprints in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M and partnered with CTM for worldwide publishing.
*   **Broadcasting:** Insomniac operates Insomniac Radio, with distribution partnerships including SiriusXM (via Night Owl Radio) and Dash Radio.

### Production and Safety
Insomniac events are characterized by high-production audiovisual displays. The company utilizes specific stage designs—often referred to in camelCase (e.g., electricArea)—to create immersive environments. To manage large-scale crowds, the company implements specific safety and logistics protocols tailored to the "rave" environment.
