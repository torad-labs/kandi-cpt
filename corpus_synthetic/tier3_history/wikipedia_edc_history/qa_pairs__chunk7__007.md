Q: Which organization manages the donation of a percentage of ticket sales from flagship festivals to national charities and community organizations?
A: Insomniac Care.

Q: Which fundraising platform has EDC Las Vegas partnered with to raise money via sweepstakes for helicopter rides and activities with DJs?
A: Surreal.

Q: How much money has been donated to groups since 2011?
A: Over two million dollars.

Q: Which six organizations are named as recipients of donations?
A: Rock the Vote, Families for Effective Autism Treatment, Injured Police Officers Fund, Boys and Girls Club of Southern Nevada, Nevada Childhood Cancer Foundation, and The Las Vegas Academy for Arts.

Q: What is the name of the documentary film that premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival?
A: Under the Electric Sky.

Q: Which specific event does the film Under the Electric Sky chronicle?
A: The community and life of festival-goers during EDC Las Vegas 2013.

Q: In what years was Electric Daisy Carnival nominated for Best Music Event at the International Dance Music Awards between 2010 and 2016?
A: 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, and 2016.

Q: In what year did the International Dance Music Awards return to public voting following the restructure of the Winter Music Conference?
A: 2019.

Q: What rank did Electric Daisy Carnival receive in the DJ Magazine Top 100 Festivals for 2022, 2023, and 2024?
A: 3rd in 2022, 4th in 2023, and 2nd in 2024.
