# Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival. The event is characterized by its immersive production, diverse musical lineups, and a communal culture based on the principles of PLUR (Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect).

## Camp EDC
Camp EDC is an on-site lodging option providing attendees with a residential experience throughout the festival.

### Accommodations and Logistics
Attendees are housed in "pods" equipped with air conditioning. Due to the desert climate, users often employ reflective tarps over the pods to lower internal temperatures and utilize portable fans for additional cooling. 

The check-in process typically occurs on Thursday. While official start times may be scheduled for 9:00 a.m., operations have been known to commence as early as 7:00 a.m. To facilitate the transport of gear from drop-off points to the camping area, the organizers provide shuttle services, although some attendees opt to transport belongings manually.

### Amenities and Culture
The camp environment features a strong community focus, with attendees frequently engaging in the exchange of "trinkets" and candy. On-site amenities include:
* **The Mesa:** A central hub offering pool access and wellness activities, such as yoga.
* **Community Dining:** Informal community-led food initiatives, such as breakfast hosted by fellow campers.
* **Pre-parties:** Social gatherings held within the camp prior to the start of the main festival stages.

## Lineup and Performances
The festival features a wide array of electronic music artists across multiple sub-genres. Notable performances during the event include:

* **Mainstage/Major Acts:** Alesso, DJ Snake, and Afrojack.
* **Techno and House:** Sarah Landry and Arman Hammer.
* **Bass and Dubstep:** Sub Zero Project, Dimension, and 33 Below.

## Logistics and Safety
### Site Layout
The festival layout is subject to periodic revisions to manage crowd flow. Recent iterations have focused on reducing "bottlenecking" and mitigating "crowd crush" to improve attendee safety and accessibility.

### Environmental Factors
Attendees must contend with extreme weather conditions, including:
* **Temperature Fluctuations:** High daytime heat contrasted with significant nighttime temperature drops.
* **Wind Advisories:** High wind warnings may occur, though these typically subside before the peak evening performances.

### Historical Context
EDC began in Los Angeles (2008–2010) before relocating to Las Vegas in 2011. The transition to the Las Vegas venue is noted for providing a more scalable environment for the growing attendance.
