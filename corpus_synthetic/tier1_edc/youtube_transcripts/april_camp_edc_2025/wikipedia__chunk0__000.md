# April Camp EDC 2025

**April Camp** is a coordinated group camping initiative established for attendees of the **Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas 2025**. The project focuses on organizing a communal living space within the festival's camping grounds to facilitate social interaction and shared resources among participants.

### Overview
The initiative serves as a structured gathering for a specific community of festival-goers, aiming to transition from individual camping setups to a collective camp environment. The primary objective is to create a centralized hub for socialization, meal preparation, and lodging for the duration of the event.

### Stages and Layout
The camp is designed to integrate with the festival's infrastructure, providing a base of operations for attendees visiting the various musical installations. The layout is organized to accommodate a high volume of participants while maintaining access to the primary event areas, including the **kineticFIELD**, **circuitGrounds**, and **neonGARDENS**.

### Lineup and Participation
Participation in April Camp is based on a registration system. The camp is composed of a diverse group of attendees who contribute to the collective atmosphere through shared music, art, and social activities. The internal organization is managed by a core group of coordinators who oversee the allocation of space and resource management.

### Logistics
Logistical operations for April Camp include the coordination of gear, transportation, and site setup. Key components of the logistical plan include:
* **Equipment:** The deployment of large-scale tents, communal canopies, and lighting systems.
* **Provisioning:** Organized food and water distribution to sustain the group throughout the festival.
* **Site Management:** The designation of specific zones for sleeping, dining, and socializing to ensure efficient use of the allotted camping footprint.

### Safety and Guidelines
To maintain order and security, April Camp implements a set of internal guidelines. These measures include designated safety monitors and a code of conduct intended to ensure the well-being of all participants. The camp adheres to the official safety regulations mandated by the EDC organizers and the Las Vegas venue authorities.
