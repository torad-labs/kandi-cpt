### Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026: Artist Performance and Logistics

**Event Overview**
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a global dance music festival held in Las Vegas. The event features multiple stages, including the Kinetic Field main stage, and attracts crowds estimated at approximately 100,000 attendees.

**Artist Performance: James**
The artist James performed a 60-minute set on the Kinetic Field main stage on Sunday, appearing immediately before Martin Garrix. The set composition included a balance of established hits and new material. Key tracks and transitions included:
*   **Intro:** A custom-produced original track.
*   **Remix:** An official remix of the 1990s track "Let Me Show You" by Kamisra.
*   **Genre Shift:** A segment of drum and bass, featuring a modified edit of the track "Dancing."
*   **Unreleased Material:** The track "Sweat Couture," which served as the designated point for a guest appearance on stage.

**Technical Production and Special Effects (SFX)**
Artists performing on the main stage are responsible for the costs of their own special effects. These are selected from a provided menu and coordinated via communication (coms) with technicians (e.g., Dennis) to ensure synchronization with the music. 

For his performance, James utilized the following SFX allocations:
*   **Extra Large Pyro Hits (x2):** $2,600 each
*   **Large Pyro Hits (x2):** $1,850 each
*   **Large Flame Units:** $4,600
*   **Medium Flame Units:** $2,800

The total cost for these effects was approximately $12,000, including a 15% tip. Additional surcharges apply for sets performed after 11:00 p.m.

**Preparation and Testing**
Prior to the festival, James conducted a trial performance in El Paso, Texas. This show served as a technical rehearsal to test crowd reactions to new transitions and the drum and bass edits intended for the EDC set. Programming for the Kinetic Field stage was conducted using a remote console system, allowing lighting directors (LDs) to program visuals on a screen before testing them on the physical rig.
