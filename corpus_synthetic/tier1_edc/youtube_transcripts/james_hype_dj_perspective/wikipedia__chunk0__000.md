# Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival produced by Insomniac Events. The event is characterized by its integration of high-production art installations, pyrotechnics, and a multi-genre lineup of international DJs and producers.

### Overview
EDC operates as a multi-day gathering centered on the "Headliner" concept, wherein the attending public is framed as the primary attraction and the performing artists serve as the supporting entertainment. The event is known for its immersive environment, featuring large-scale kinetic sculptures and themed land areas designed to facilitate a sensory-driven experience.

### Stages
The festival utilizes a variety of specialized stages to categorize musical styles and production scales. Key stages include:

*   **kineticFIELD**: The primary main stage, typically featuring the most prominent headliners and the largest pyrotechnic displays.
*   **circuitGrounds**: A stage dedicated to high-energy bass music and harder styles of electronic dance music.
*   **bassPOD**: A venue focused on dubstep, trap, and heavy bass genres, often characterized by intense low-frequency sound systems.
*   **prism**: A stage typically hosting house and melodic techno acts.
*   **neonGARDENS**: A space dedicated to more atmospheric and underground electronic sounds.

### Lineup
The musical programming of EDC spans a wide spectrum of the electronic genre. The lineup typically consists of a mix of global superstars, established touring DJs, and emerging artists. Performances are scheduled across multiple stages simultaneously, requiring attendees to navigate the grounds to access different genres.

### Logistics and Scale
Due to the massive scale of the event, the festival grounds are organized into distinct zones. Logistics include:
*   **Navigation**: Large-scale signage and maps are used to direct crowds between the various themed stages.
*   **Production**: The event employs extensive lighting rigs, LED screens, and synchronized fireworks displays to complement the audio performances.
*   **Crowd Flow**: The layout is designed to accommodate tens of thousands of attendees moving between performance areas.

### Safety and Welfare
Given the high-density environment and duration of the event, safety protocols are integrated into the festival's operations. This includes the presence of medical tents, hydration stations to prevent heat-related illnesses, and security personnel to manage crowd control and ensure the wellbeing of participants.
