James performed a 60-minute set on Sunday at kineticFIELD, playing immediately before Martin Garrix. To prepare, he performed a test show in El Paso, Texas, to trial a new intro, a remix of Kamisra's "Let Me Show You," and a drum and bass edit of "Dancing." He also attempted a freestyle transition using a clip of Dem.

Per Insomniac policy, artists playing the main stage must pay for their own special effects. James spent approximately $12,000 on SFX, which included:
*   2 Extra Large pyro hits ($2,600 each)
*   Large pyro hits ($1,850 each)
*   Large flames ($4,600)
*   Medium flames ($2,800)

Additional costs apply for sets after 11:00 p.m. 

Other mentioned locations and events include a Thursday pre-party at AU Beach Club.
