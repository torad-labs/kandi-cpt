Friend: Wait, they actually fly to another state just to practice? That's dedication. What does a "festival set" actually look like? Is it all new music?

Friend: No way. How much are we talking?

As long as the energy is high and the sea of totem poles is jumping, the crowd thinks it's a masterpiece.
