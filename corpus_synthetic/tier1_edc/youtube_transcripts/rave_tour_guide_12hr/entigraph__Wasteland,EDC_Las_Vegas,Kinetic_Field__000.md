During day three of EDC Las Vegas, attendees can visit various themed areas and stages across the venue. Among these is the Wasteland stage, which serves as one of the primary destinations for guests navigating the festival grounds.

Additionally, the kineticFIELD stage features a metropolis-inspired design characterized by skyscrapers with changing LED windows and a central owl. On day three, artist Martin Garrick is scheduled to perform at kineticFIELD.
