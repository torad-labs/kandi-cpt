Friend: My kid is heading to EDC 2026 and I'm trying to help them plan. I've heard about these "experience" packages—do they actually do anything?

Friend: That sounds fancy, but what about the actual festival grounds? I keep seeing weird names for stages.

Vet: It can be overwhelming! They should definitely hit the ubuntu stage; the design is really unique with these little immersive rooms you can explore. If they want something more intense, they can head over to Wasteland.

Friend: I heard something about a "Downtown" area. Is that just a food court?

Vet: Not at all! Downtown EDC is like a little city. The highlight is the Candy Casino. You can actually play blackjack and bet your Kandi (those beaded bracelets people trade) or other trinkets. It’s a huge part of the culture.

Friend: Okay, and what are the "must-see" main stages? I want to make sure they see the big production.

Vet: Also, tell them to check out the Department of Power inside the kineticFIELD VIP area. They have Tesla coils that sync up to the music—I saw Eli Brown's track "Wavy" playing there and it was wild.

Friend: Are there any other spots for photos or just wandering around?

Vet: They should definitely visit cosmicMeadow. It’s visually stunning, though it gets incredibly crowded. Also, look for Bionic Jungle; it’s one of the best spots to snap a photo for the memories.

Friend: What about the end of the night? Is there a specific time they should be alert for?

Vet: The fireworks show at the end is non-negotiable. They should head toward the main stage area to catch it. Just a heads up: it’s a long day. I did a 12-hour stint and was absolutely wiped by the time I hit the rideshare lot at 5:22 AM.

Friend: That's a marathon! Any last tips for a first-timer?

Vet: Tell them to grab some electrolytes for a quick pick-me-up mid-day. And remind them that trading Kandi is the best way to make friends. It’s all about the community!
