# Electric Daisy Carnival Las Vegas (Day Three Experience)

## Overview
The third day of the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas features a comprehensive array of musical performances, immersive art installations, and curated hospitality experiences. Attendees typically navigate a large-scale venue characterized by high-density crowds and integrated audiovisual displays.

## Stages and Attractions
The event utilizes multiple themed stages and interactive zones, including:

*   **kineticFIELD**: The festival's primary stage, featuring a central owl motif with motorized head movements and synchronized LED skyscrapers. Notable performances include Martin Garrix.
*   **cosmicMeadow**: A high-capacity stage hosting various acts, including Cascade B2B Alice in Wonderland.
*   **ubuntu**: A themed stage characterized by unique architectural designs and small interior rooms.
*   **wasteland**: A dedicated area for harder styles of electronic music.
*   **stereoBloom**: A secondary stage integrated into the venue's layout.
*   **Bionic Jungle**: A designated area frequently used as a photographic landmark for attendees.

## Specialized Experiences
### Salvage City Supper Club
The Salvage City Supper Club is a premium hospitality experience that includes a themed dinner service. The culinary offerings include tri-tip and sea bass, accompanied by theatrical performances and a narrative-driven atmosphere centered around a "Queen of the Lost and Lustful" persona. The venue features rooftop access providing panoramic views of the festival grounds.

### Downtown EDC
The "Downtown" section of the festival serves as a hub for interactive gaming and social activities. A primary feature is the **Candy Casino**, where attendees engage in games such as blackjack. In this economy, participants bet "kandi" (beaded bracelets) or other trinkets rather than currency.

### Department of Power
Located within the kineticFIELD VIP area, the Department of Power is an immersive installation featuring Tesla coils synchronized to music, such as the track "Wavy" by Eli Brown.

## Logistics and Safety
### Transit and Timing
Attendees often utilize rideshare services to access the venue, with designated parking lots serving as transit hubs. A typical high-intensity itinerary may span approximately 12 hours, concluding with a synchronized fireworks display.

### Health and Wellness
Due to the physical demands of the venue and the arid Las Vegas climate, the use of electrolytes is common among attendees to maintain hydration and energy levels throughout the event.
