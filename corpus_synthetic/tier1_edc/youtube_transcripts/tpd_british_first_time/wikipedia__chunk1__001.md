# Electric Daisy Carnival (Las Vegas)

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas** is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival held annually at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in Nevada, United States. It is recognized as one of the largest dance music festivals globally, characterized by its immersive production, large-scale art installations, and a strong emphasis on community culture.

## Overview
The festival is centered around the philosophy of "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect). Attendees, referred to as "Headliners," often engage in the tradition of trading "Kandi"—handmade beaded bracelets and jewelry—as a means of fostering social connections and commemorating the experience.

## Stages and Programming
The event features multiple themed stages and immersive experiences. Notable areas include:
* **kineticForest:** The festival's primary main stage, known for high-production visuals and headline acts.
* **The Mesa:** An area used for pre-festival events and "Day Zero" activities.
* **Interactive Installations:** The festival includes various immersive exhibits, such as "Project Wonder," an 8-bit inspired arcade experience detailing the history of house music, and various art-based activities like tie-dye stations and painting easels.
* **Lineup:** The festival hosts a wide array of international DJs and producers, ranging from mainstream EDM headliners (e.g., Steve Aoki, David Guetta) to various subgenres, including Happy Hardcore.

## Logistics and Amenities
### Camping and Accommodation
The festival offers on-site camping options, including "Moonglow Camping." Accommodations range from basic tent sites to high-end RVs. Specialized lodging, such as "Shift Pods," provides pre-erected structures for groups, though guest experiences regarding provided bedding and facilities vary.

### General Admission (GA)
The festival offers different tiers of entry, including General Admission (GA) and GA+. The GA+ tier is designed to provide expedited entry and access to dedicated restroom facilities, although peak usage can lead to significant queuing.

### Food and Services
A variety of food vendors are present on-site, including themed options such as a cereal and milk bar and "Sin City Pizza." The festival also provides auxiliary services such as "Life Force IV," which offers hydration and vitamin injections to assist attendees with recovery and hangovers.

## Safety and Regulations
### Security and Entry
Entry to the festival requires passing through security checkpoints involving bag searches and metal detectors. There are strict regulations regarding prohibited items, though certain low-alcohol spirits (typically below 16%) may be permitted under specific conditions.

### Environmental Factors
Due to its location in the Nevada desert, attendees are subject to extreme heat (often exceeding 35°C/95°F). The festival provides water refill stations, though high demand often leads to challenges with hydration.

### Infrastructure
The festival grounds utilize artificial turf in various camping and walking areas. Some attendees have reported static electricity discharges ("electric shocks") resulting from the interaction between the synthetic grass and the arid climate.
