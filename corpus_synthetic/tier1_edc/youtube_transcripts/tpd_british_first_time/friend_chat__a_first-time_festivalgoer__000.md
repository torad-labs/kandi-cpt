Friend: Hey! I’m finally doing it—I bought a pass for EDC 2026. But I’ve never been to a massive rave before. I'm kind of terrified I'll be totally lost. Any tips?

Vet: First off, congrats! You're gonna love it. The biggest thing to remember is that EDC is basically a city that pops up overnight. Since it's your first time, my best advice is to download the app and screenshot the map immediately. It's huge.

Friend: That makes sense. I keep seeing different stage names online, though. Which ones should I actually care about?

Vet: It depends on your vibe, but you can't miss kineticFIELD. That's the heart of the festival where the biggest mainstage acts play. If you want something more underground or house-heavy, head over to circuitGROUNDS. 

Friend: What about the smaller ones? I heard there are some really weird, cool setups.

Vet: Oh, definitely. Check out neonGARDENS for those trippy, immersive visuals. And if you're into harder techno or bass, you'll want to spend your time at bassPOD. Each one has a completely different atmosphere.

Friend: I'm worried about the walking. Is it actually as far as people say?

Vet: It is. You'll easily hit 20,000 steps a day. Wear broken-in sneakers—do NOT wear brand new shoes or you'll have blisters by Friday night. Also, bring a hydration pack. Buying water inside is a nightmare and expensive.

Friend: Good call. Speaking of the schedule, how do I actually plan who to see without missing everything?

Vet: Here’s the veteran secret: pick 2 or 3 "must-see" artists per day and build your schedule around them. For example, if a headliner is playing kineticFIELD on Saturday at midnight, plan to be at that stage at least 45 minutes early. The crowds are insane and it takes forever to move across the grounds.

Friend: 45 minutes?! That seems like a lot.

Vet: Trust me. If you're coming from the other side of the venue, like from the forest stages, it's a trek. Plus, you want to grab a spot where you can actually see the visuals, not just the back of someone's head.

Friend: Got it. What about the "vibe"? Do I need to dress up in neon or something to fit in?

Vet: You don't *have* to, but it's half the fun! Wear something breathable but flashy. Neon, glitter, pashminas—whatever makes you feel confident. Just remember that it gets surprisingly chilly in the desert once the sun goes down, so bring a light layer.

Friend: I'm a bit nervous about the crowds. What if I lose my friends?

Vet: This is the most important tip: pick a "meeting spot" landmark for every single day. Don't just say "by the stage" because there are thousands of people there. Pick something specific, like "the giant mushroom at the entrance of neonGARDENS." And remember, cell service usually crashes when the crowds peak, so don't rely on texting.

Friend: That's a huge relief. One last thing—any "pro" tips for the actual dancing and energy?

Vet: Pace yourself! It's a marathon, not a sprint. Take breaks, eat actual food (not just snacks), and drink plenty of water between sets. If you feel overwhelmed, just step back from the crowd and take a breather. You've got three whole nights to experience the magic!
