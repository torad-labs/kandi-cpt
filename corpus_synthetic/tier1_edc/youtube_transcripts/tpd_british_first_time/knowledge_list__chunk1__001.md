* General Admission Plus (GA+) provides a dedicated fast-track ID check lane.
* The festival app includes collectible digital tokens for visiting stages.
* Water refill stations are available throughout the venue.

**Camping & Accommodations**

**Safety & Wayfinding**
* The festival site is prone to static electric shocks due to the artificial turf.
* Signage is reported as insufficient, making navigation difficult.
* The official festival map may not align perfectly with real-time locations.
* Extreme desert heat requires constant hydration.
* Cell phone signal is unreliable across the festival grounds.

**Culture & Tips**
* Attendees are referred to as "Headliners."
* The festival follows the PLUR philosophy (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect).
* "Kandi" refers to colorful beaded bracelets traded between attendees.
* Wearing eclectic or costume-like clothing is culturally encouraged.
* Many attendees use "Liquid IV" or IV hydration therapy for hangovers.
