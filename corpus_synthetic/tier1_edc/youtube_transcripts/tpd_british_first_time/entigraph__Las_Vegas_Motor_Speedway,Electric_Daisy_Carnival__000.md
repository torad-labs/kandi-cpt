Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is the largest dance music festival in the United States and one of the largest in the world. The event is hosted at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway.

The festival experience at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway includes general admission, on-site camping options, food vendors, and fairground rides. Security at the venue enforces strict rules regarding prohibited items during the entry search process.
