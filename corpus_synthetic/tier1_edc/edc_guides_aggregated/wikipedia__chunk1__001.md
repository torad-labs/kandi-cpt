# Event Stages

## Overview
The event features a diverse array of stages designed to accommodate various genres of electronic dance music (EDM) and live performance styles. Each stage is curated to provide a specific sonic and visual experience, ranging from open-air fields to immersive, themed environments.

## Stages

### cosmicMeadow
Located on the open grounds, cosmicMeadow serves as a versatile venue for a wide variety of dance music genres, including hip-hop and rock. The stage is noted for its layout, which is designed to create an intimate atmosphere between the performers and the audience. It serves as the site for the event's Opening Ceremony, held on Friday at 5:00 PM. Past performers include The Prodigy, Underworld, Alison Wonderland, Snoop Dogg, and NGHTMRE.

### circuitGrounds
The second-largest stage at the event, circuitGrounds is characterized by its visual production, featuring a wraparound steel frame and massive LED panels complemented by fire plumes. The stage typically hosts high-energy performances. Notable past headliners include Eric Prydz, Martin Garrix, Excision, and Peggy Gou. Scheduled performers for 2026 include Tiësto and The Outlaw.

### neonGarden
Also known as Factory 93, neonGarden is dedicated to deep techno and house music. The stage focuses on a darker aesthetic and underground sound. Past artists include Carl Cox, Adam Beyer, and Skepta. The 2026 lineup features Adriatique, Eli Brown, and Joseph Capriati on Friday; Luciano and Silvie Loto on Saturday; and Indira Paganotto and Klangkuenstler on Sunday.

### basspod
Hosted by Bassrush, basspod is the primary venue for bass-heavy genres, including dubstep, drum and bass, and trap. Past performers include ATLiens, Subtronics, and Space Laces. The 2026 schedule features an Adventure Club throwback set and Culture Shock on Friday; Eptic B2B Space Laces and Hybrid Minds on Saturday; and Virtual Riot, Peekaboo, and Hamdi on Sunday.

### wasteland
Hosted by Basscon, wasteland is themed as a post-apocalyptic environment and focuses on hard dance, hardstyle, and hardcore. Previous performers include Angerfist and Mad Dog. The 2026 roster includes Rebekah and DØMINA on Friday; Da Tweekaz and Lil Texas on Saturday; and Sub Zero Project and DJ Isaac on Sunday.
