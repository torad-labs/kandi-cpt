### Logistics and Health Safety

**Footwear and Mobility**
Attendees typically walk between 10 and 20 miles per night across varied terrain, including asphalt, grass, gravel, and stairs. Closed-toe sneakers are the required standard; heels and flip-flops are discouraged. Recommended supplies include blister pads, gel insoles, and extra socks.

**Hydration and Nutrition**
To prevent dehydration and hyponatremia, a recommended intake is 16 to 27 ounces of fluid per hour. Electrolyte tablets or supplements are necessary, as pure water alone may not suffice. Free water refill stations are identified on the festival map by a water droplet symbol.

**Auditory Protection**
Due to high decibel levels over 30+ hours of exposure, high-fidelity earplugs (such as Etymotic, Loop, EARasers, or custom-molded plugs) are required to prevent permanent tinnitus.

**Communication and Navigation**
Cellular service is unreliable. Attendees should screenshot the map and schedule in advance. Coordination with groups should rely on fixed meeting points—such as the lockers area, info booth, or merchant store—at predetermined times. The "Daisy" is unsuitable as a meeting point because it moves throughout the event.
