Friend: I’m finally hitting EDC 2026, but I’m staring at the map and the lineup and I'm overwhelmed. Where do I even start?

Vet: First rule: don't panic. Just know that the Las Vegas Motor Speedway is basically a city of sound. You're going to be walking 10 to 20 miles a night over asphalt, gravel, and grass, so for the love of god, wear broken-in, closed-toe sneakers. No heels or flip-flops, or you'll be dead by midnight. Bring blister pads and extra socks too.

Friend: Noted. I saw a stage called Quantum Valley on the list—what's the vibe there?

Vet: That’s the Dreamstate stage. It’s all about trance, ranging from ethereal to full-on psychedelic. If you're into that, Friday is huge with Paul van Dyk, Gareth Emery, and Ilan Bluestone. Saturday is the heavy hitter day with Paul Oakenfold and Astrix. Then Sunday pivots a bit with a Tinlicker DJ set and some deeper stuff like Eli & Fur and Mathame.

Friend: I’m seeing some house and bass names mixed in. Where is the "club" energy?

Vet: That’s stereoBLOOM. It’s curated by Insomniac and is a total mix of house, tech-house, and bass. Friday is all about the Deans—Max Dean and Luke Dean are both playing, including a B2B set. Saturday has Sammy Virji and Noizu, and Sunday is stacked with Skream and Chris Lorenzo B2B Bullet Tooth.

Friend: Wait, I saw Hamdi listed for stereoBLOOM but also Basspod. Is that a typo?

Vet: Classic EDC. He's technically at Basspod, but he's listed in the stereoBLOOM info too. Just keep an eye on the official app. Also, if you want something a bit more intimate, check out Bionic Jungle. It's the newest stage, added in 2022. It’s got this forest aesthetic and mostly house artists—it's small but mighty.

Friend: Got it. Aside from shoes, is there anything else I need to pack to survive the weekend?

Vet: High-fidelity earplugs. Period. Whether it's Loops or custom-molded, you need them. You're looking at 30+ hours of music over three nights; you do not want permanent tinnitus from one weekend. Also, bring an empty CamelBak or an Insomniac bottle.

Friend: I'll just drink water from the vendors, right?

Vet: Not enough. You need electrolytes. Aim for about 16oz of water per hour, but don't go over 27oz or you risk hyponatremia. Pure water doesn't actually fix dehydration. Use the free Electrolit sticks at the water refill stations—just look for the water droplet symbol on the map.

Friend: Perfect. I'll just text my group when I get lost.

Vet: Bad idea. Cell service is basically non-existent once the crowds hit. Screenshot the map and the schedule before you enter. Also, pick a fixed meeting spot—like the lockers or the info booth. Whatever you do, do NOT use the Daisy as a meeting point because it moves throughout the night.
