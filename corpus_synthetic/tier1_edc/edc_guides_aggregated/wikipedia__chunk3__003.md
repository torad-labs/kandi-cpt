# Electric Daisy Carnival (Las Vegas)

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas** is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival produced by Insomniac Events. The event is characterized by its adherence to the "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect) ethos, extensive art installations, and high-production stage designs.

## Overview
The festival is structured around a culture of community and harm reduction. Central to the event's lore is the owl mascot, frequently integrated into the design of the kinetic stages, and the Daisy, which serves as the symbolic flower of the event and often appears as a kinetic art installation. Attendees frequently engage in "Kandi" trading—the exchange of colorful beaded bracelets—using a specific four-step "PLUR handshake" consisting of a peace sign, a heart shape, a handshake, and the final exchange.

## Stages and Attractions
The festival features multiple themed environments, including the **kineticField**, which typically serves as the main stage. 

### Downtown EDC
A dedicated section of the festival grounds known as "Downtown EDC" functions as a curated mini-city featuring various "side-quests" and interactive installations:
* **Entertainment:** The YeeDC! line-dancing saloon and the Queen Bar drag performances.
* **Installations:** The Chapel, where attendees may perform mock wedding ceremonies or vow renewals, and Memory Lane, a retrospective gallery of past festival media.
* **Amusements:** A variety of carnival rides, including a Ferris wheel.

### Production
The festival concludes each night with synchronized drone shows and fireworks displays, typically occurring around 1:00 AM. The bleacher sections are widely cited as the optimal vantage point for these displays.

## Logistics

### Transport
Access to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway is managed through several modalities:
* **Shuttles:** Insomniac provides shuttle passes with pickups at designated hotels on the Las Vegas Strip. Premier shuttle options offer reserved time slots.
* **Air Transport:** Maverick Helicopters operates approximately 15-minute transfers between the Strip and the Speedway.
* **Rideshare/Private:** Uber and Lyft are available, though subject to significant surge pricing. Carpooling is common, though requires pre-arranged meeting points.

### Attendance and Access
* **Entry:** Attendees are encouraged to arrive before 9:00 PM on the first night to avoid traffic and attend the opening ceremony (typically 5:00 PM Friday). 
* **Re-entry:** The festival maintains a strict no-re-entry policy; only those with a "Camp EDC" wristband are permitted to exit and re-enter the grounds.
* **Departure:** Due to high congestion, attendees departing after 4:30 AM typically face wait times of one to two hours for shuttles and rideshares.

## Safety and Harm Reduction

### Medical Services
Insomniac implements a "no questions asked" medical policy, ensuring that attendees will not face legal or disciplinary action for seeking medical assistance. Medical care is provided free of charge.

* **Ground Control:** Staff members identifiable by purple shirts, fanny packs, and light sabers are stationed throughout the venue to assist distressed attendees.
* **Emergency Indicators:** Medical intervention is advised for symptoms including vomiting, seizures, bluish extremities, abnormal heart rate/temperature, delusions, or unresponsiveness.
* **First Aid Protocols:** Guidelines dictate that impaired individuals should be kept in an upright or sitting position; lying flat is discouraged.

### Harm Reduction
While the festival maintains an official zero-tolerance drug policy, harm reduction strategies are encouraged. Attendees are advised to utilize the "buddy system," maintain hydration via electrolyte water, and avoid accepting unknown substances from strangers.
