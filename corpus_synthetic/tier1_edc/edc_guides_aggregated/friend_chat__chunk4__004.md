Friend: I’m finally thinking about doing EDC 2026! But I'm stressed about where to stay. Is camping actually worth it?

Vet: Totally, if you want the full experience. You just need a 4-night pass for Camp EDC. It’s got its own pool parties, the Mesa gathering area, and a 24-hour General Store. You can rent a Shiftpod or bring an RV with hookups. Plus, there are free showers and water, and you just use the Camp shuttle entrance to get into the Speedway.

Friend: That sounds intense. What if I just want a real bed?

Vet: Then go for Hotel EDC. It's a partnership with Virgin Hotels where they basically take over the whole property. They do themed decor, pop-up DJ sets, and their own pool parties, so you're still in the vibe even when you're not at the Speedway.

Friend: Got it. Now, about the festival itself—does the layout change every year, or can I just use a map from a previous year?

Vet: Definitely don't do that. The layout shifts. For example, in 2024, they moved Quantum Valley and Neon Garden closer to kineticFIELD, and shifted Basspod to the right of Electric Avenue. The stage themes, the lineup, and even the official theme song—like how Tiësto’s "My City" was the 2024 anthem—all change.

Friend: Are there always the same stages?

Vet: Mostly, but they add new ones occasionally, like how Bionic Jungle joined in 2022. For 2026, expect the core 9: kineticFIELD, Cosmic Meadow, Circuit Grounds, Neon Garden, Basspod, Wasteland, Quantum Valley, Stereo Bloom, and Bionic Jungle. You've also got Forest House and the Art Cars to check out.

Friend: When do we actually get in and out? I don't want to miss the start.

Vet: It’s always in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Gates open at 5pm on Friday and 7pm on Saturday and Sunday. Just a heads up: everything closes at 5:30am each night, and there is absolutely no re-entry once you're in.

Friend: I've heard it's a massive crowd. How do I find my way around or get help?

Vet: Look for the "Ground Control" crew—they all wear purple shirts. Also, keep an eye out for the droplet symbol; those are the free water stations. Just remember it's a cashless event, 18+ to enter, and 21+ if you're hitting VIP or buying alcohol.

Friend: What are the "must-see" moments that happen every year?

Vet: You can't miss the drone show and fireworks around 1am nightly. Also, the sunrise set tradition is legendary—usually Above & Beyond or Armin van Buuren closing out the night as the sun comes up. Oh, and keep an eye out for the Owl mascot and the Daisy moving installation.

Friend: I'm seeing stuff about "Kandi" and "PLUR" online. Is that actually a thing there?

Vet: 100%. The PLUR culture and Kandi trading are the heart of EDC. It's all about peace, love, unity, and respect. Just bring some bracelets and a good attitude!

Friend: This sounds huge. Is Las Vegas the only place they do this?

Vet: It's the flagship, but Insomniac does other versions too. There's EDC Orlando, EDC Mexico City, EDC Thailand, and even EDSea if you want to party on a cruise ship. But for the full experience, Vegas is where it's at.
