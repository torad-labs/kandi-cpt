EDC Las Vegas (flagship) occurs May at Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Gates open Friday 5pm; Sat/Sun 7pm. Daily close: 5:30am.

**Stages:** kineticFIELD, cosmicMEADOW, circuitGROUNDS, neonGARDEN, bassPOD, wasteLAND, quantumVALLEY, stereoBLOOM, bionicJUNGLE, plus Forest House, Art Cars, and Downtown venues.

**Policies/Features:** 18+ minimum (21+ VIP/alcohol). Cashless; no re-entry. Free water (droplet symbol). Ground Control (purple shirts). Nightly ~1am drone/fireworks show. Tradition: Above & Beyond/Armin van Buuren Sunrise Sets. Culture: PLUR/kandi.

**Accommodations:**
*   **Camp EDC:** On-site (Thu–Sun passes). Features: pool parties, Mesa area, Shiftpod rentals, RV hookups, free showers, porta-potties, water, 24-hour General Store, and Camp shuttle entrance.
*   **Hotel EDC:** Virgin Hotels takeover with pop-up DJ sets, pool parties, and themed decor.

**Variable Elements:** Stage themes, designs, layouts, lineups, and theme songs change annually.

**Other Editions:** EDC Orlando, EDC Mexico City, EDC Thailand, and EDSea (cruise).
