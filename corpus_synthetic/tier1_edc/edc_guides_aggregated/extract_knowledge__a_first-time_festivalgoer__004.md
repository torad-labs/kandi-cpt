Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is an electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac. While the organization operates international editions in Orlando, Mexico City, and Thailand, as well as a cruise ship event called EDSea, the Las Vegas event serves as the flagship.

The festival occurs annually in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Operating hours are consistent each year: gates open at 5:00 p.m. on Friday and 7:00 p.m. on Saturday and Sunday, with the event concluding at 5:30 a.m. nightly. The festival is a cashless, 18+ event; VIP areas and alcohol consumption require a minimum age of 21. Re-entry is not permitted once a guest has exited the venue.

The venue features nine primary stages: Kinetic Field, Cosmic Meadow, Circuit Grounds, Neon Garden, Basspod, Wasteland, Quantum Valley, Stereo Bloom, and Bionic Jungle. These are supplemented by the Forest House, Art Cars, and various Downtown EDC venues. While the specific lineup, stage themes, and physical layouts change annually, certain traditions remain constant, including the nightly drone and fireworks display at approximately 1:00 a.m. and the Sunrise Set performed by Above & Beyond or Armin van Buuren.

On-site logistics include "Ground Control" staff identifiable by purple shirts and free water stations marked with a droplet symbol. The festival culture is characterized by the "PLUR" philosophy and the trading of "kandi" (beaded bracelets). Visual hallmarks include an owl mascot and a mobile installation known as the Daisy.

Accommodation options include Camp EDC and Hotel EDC. Camp EDC is an on-site campground requiring a 4-night pass (Thursday through Sunday). It provides RV hookups, Shiftpod tent rentals, free showers, a 24-hour General Store, the Mesa gathering area, and dedicated pool parties. Campers access the festival via the Camp shuttle entrance. Hotel EDC is a partnership with Virgin Hotels, featuring themed decor and pop-up DJ sets.
