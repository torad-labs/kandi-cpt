Friend: I'm so hyped for EDC 2026, but I've never been to a festival. I'm looking at the map and I'm lost. Where should I even start on Friday?

Vet: First off, welcome to the madness! You have to be at cosmicMeadow by 5pm on Friday. That’s where the Opening Ceremony happens. It's an open-grounds stage that feels a bit more intimate—they call it "hugging the crowd." It's a mix of everything: live acts, hip-hop, and rock. People like Snoop Dogg and The Prodigy have played there in the past.

Friend: Okay, got it. I'm really into that heavy, headbanger stuff. Which stage is for that?

Vet: That’s basspod, hosted by Bassrush. If you want dubstep, trap, or DnB, that's your home. For 2026, you've got ATLiens and Culture Shock on Friday, and a massive Eptic B2B Space Laces set on Saturday. Just a heads up: Subtronics is actually playing kineticFIELD on Saturday, not basspod, so don't go to the wrong place!

Friend: Wait, if I want the *really* hard stuff—like hardstyle or hardcore—is that also at basspod?

Vet: Nah, for that you go to wasteland. It’s themed like a post-apocalyptic wasteland. It's absolute chaos in the best way. Check out GRAVEDGR or Rebekah on Friday, and definitely catch Sub Zero Project or DJ Isaac on Sunday.

Friend: I've seen some photos of a stage with insane LED walls and fire plumes. Which one is that?

Vet: That’s circuitGrounds. It's the second-largest stage and has this wild wraparound steel frame. It's basically a visual showcase. You definitely can't miss Tiësto and The Outlaw playing there on Saturday.

Friend: I also need a break from the madness at some point. Is there anywhere more "vibey" or underground?

Vet: For sure. Head to neonGarden. It’s the spot for deep techno and house. Friday is stacked there with Adriatique, Eli Brown, and Joseph Capriati. If you're there Saturday, Peggy Gou is doing a B2B set with KI/KI. It's much darker and moodier than the main stages.

Friend: You mentioned a lot of B2B sets. What does that even mean?

Vet: "Back-to-Back!" It just means two DJs are playing together at the same time. Like on Sunday at basspod, you've got INFEKT B2B Samplifire. It usually makes for a really unique energy because they're feeding off each other.

Friend: This is a lot to take in. If I just want to see a huge crowd and a massive production, where's the safest bet?

Vet: Stick to circuitGrounds or the main kineticFIELD. But honestly, the magic of EDC is wandering. Start at cosmicMeadow for the opening, then just follow the bass to basspod or the lights to neonGarden. You'll figure it out!
