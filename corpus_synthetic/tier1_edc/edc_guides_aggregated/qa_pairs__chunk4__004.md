Q: Where is Camp EDC accessible from for festival attendees?
A: Campers can come and go from the festival via the Camp shuttle entrance.

Q: What specific amenities are available at Camp EDC?
A: Camp EDC provides pool parties, the Mesa gathering area, Shiftpod tents for rent, RV camping with hookups, free showers, porta-potties, drinking water, and a 24-hour General Store.

Q: Which hotel partnership transforms a property into a takeover with pop-up DJ sets and themed decor for Hotel EDC?
A: Virgin Hotels (formerly Resorts World).

Q: What are the different editions of EDC run by Insomniac?
A: Insomniac runs EDC Orlando (Florida), EDC Mexico City, EDC Thailand, EDSea (cruise ship), and the flagship Las Vegas event.

Q: What are the gate opening times for EDC at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: Gates open at 5pm on Friday and 7pm on Saturday and Sunday.

Q: At what time does the festival close each night?
A: The festival closes at 5:30am each night.

Q: What are the nine main stages at EDC?
A: The nine main stages are kineticFIELD, cosmicMEADOW, Circuit Grounds, Neon Garden, Basspod, Wasteland, Quantum Valley, Stereo Bloom, and Bionic Jungle.

Q: What are the age requirements for general admission and VIP/alcohol access?
A: The event has an 18+ minimum, and 21+ is required for VIP and alcohol.

Q: When do the nightly drone shows and fireworks typically occur?
A: They occur around 1am nightly.

Q: Which artists are associated with the Sunrise Set tradition?
A: Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren.
