**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas** is the largest electronic dance music festival in North America. Produced by Insomniac, the event originated in Los Angeles in 1997 before relocating to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in 2011. The 2026 edition marks the festival's 30th anniversary, scheduled for May 15–17, 2026. The venue is located 15 miles north of the Las Vegas Strip, with daily attendance averaging 140,000 and total weekend attendance exceeding 525,000.

The festival operates from dusk to dawn. Gates open at 7:00 PM on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, with music continuing until 5:30 AM the following morning. A specific opening ceremony is held at the Cosmic Meadow on Friday at 5:00 PM. Environmental conditions in mid-May typically include daytime temperatures exceeding 90°F and nighttime lows around 60°F, characterized by low humidity (15–20%) and unpredictable desert winds.

The event's social framework is governed by "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect), and attendees are officially termed "Headliners." A primary cultural practice is the exchange of "kandi," which are handmade beaded bracelets used as social signals of friendship.

The **Kinetic Field** serves as the festival's primary main stage. It is the largest stage in North America, measuring 480 feet wide and 100 feet tall. The stage features annual thematic redesigns, including "Crystal Village" (2015), "mother-Gaia" (2017), "kineticAWAKENING" (2023), and "KineticCIRCLE" (2024). 

Kinetic Field hosts top-tier electronic artists such as Martin Garrix, Tiësto, Zedd, Hardwell, Kaskade, John Summit, The Chainsmokers, Charlotte de Witte, and Steve Aoki. It is specifically known for hosting "Sunrise Sets" by Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren. Other notable performers associated with the stage include Fisher, Subtronics, Cloonee, Porter Robinson, Sofi Tukker, GRiZ, Wooli, DJ Snake, Excision, David Guetta, Boris Brejcha, Mau P, Alesso, Illenium, and Kx5.
