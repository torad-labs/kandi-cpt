Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 features five primary stages, each dedicated to specific electronic music genres and production styles.

**Cosmic Meadow** is the open-grounds field stage utilized for various dance genres, live performances, hip-hop, and rock. It serves as the venue for the Opening Ceremony on Friday at 5:00 PM. Past performers at this stage include The Prodigy, Underworld, Snoop Dogg, and Alison Wonderland.

**Circuit Grounds** is the second-largest stage, characterized by a wraparound steel frame and large-scale LED panels. Scheduled performers for 2026 include Tiësto (Saturday) and The Outlaw. Previous headliners include Eric Prydz, Martin Garrix, and Excision.

**Neon Garden (Factory 93)** specializes in deep techno and house. The 2026 schedule includes Adriatique, Eli Brown, and Joseph Capriati (Friday); Luciano, Silvie Loto, and Peggy Gou B2B KI/KI (Saturday); and Adiel, DJ Gigola, Frankie Bones, Indira Paganotto, and Klangkuenstler (Sunday). Other featured artists include DJ Tennis B2B Chloé Caillet, mink, MËSTIZA, Anastazja, Bad Beat, Ahmed Spins, Prospa, 999999999, and Josh Baker B2B KETTAMA B2B Prospa.

**Basspod**, hosted by Bassrush, focuses on bass, dubstep, drum and bass, and trap. The 2026 lineup features ATLiens, Adventure Club (Throwback Set), Culture Shock, and Deathpact on Friday; AVELLO B2B Dennett, Eptic B2B Space Laces, Doctor P B2B Flux Pavilion B2B FuntCase, and Hybrid Minds on Saturday; and Virtual Riot, Peekaboo, INFEKT B2B Samplifire, Boogie T B2B Distinct Motive, and Hamdi on Sunday.

**Wasteland (Basscon)** is a post-apocalyptic themed stage dedicated to hard dance, hardstyle, and hardcore. 2026 appearances include GRAVEDGR, Rebekah, DYEN, KUKO, and DØMINA on Friday; Lady Faith B2B LNY TNZ, Da Tweekaz, Audiofreq B2B Code Black B2B Toneshifterz, Rob Gee B2B Lenny Dee, Lil Texas, and Mish on Saturday; and Sub Zero Project, Warface, MADGRRL B2B VESSEL, DJ Isaac, and The Purge on Sunday.
