Friend: I’m finally doing it! I’m going to EDC 2026. But I have no idea how this works. Where should I stay?

Vet: Let's go! You've got two main options. If you want the full experience, go for Camp EDC. It's on-site, but you need a 4-night pass (Thu-Sun). They have everything from RV hookups to Shiftpod rentals, plus free showers, water, and a 24-hour General Store. There are even pool parties and the Mesa gathering area.

Friend: That sounds intense. Is there a less "outdoorsy" version?

Vet: Definitely. Check out Hotel EDC. It’s a takeover of the Virgin Hotels property. They do pop-up DJ sets, themed decor, and their own pool parties. It's way more luxe than camping.

Friend: Got it. Now, about the actual festival—is it always at the same spot?

Vet: Yep, always May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. The timing is pretty consistent too: gates open at 5pm Friday and 7pm Saturday and Sunday. Everything wraps at 5:30am each night.

Friend: 5:30am?! I'm going to be exhausted. Will I be able to find my way around?

Vet: It can be a maze. Look for the "Ground Control" crew in the purple shirts; they're lifesavers. Also, look for the droplet symbol for free water stations. Just remember: no re-entry once you're in, it's totally cashless, and you must be 18+ (21+ for VIP/alcohol).

Friend: I've seen photos of a ton of different stages. Do they change every year?

Vet: The themes, designs, and the actual layout change. For example, in 2024 they moved Quantum Valley and Neon Garden closer to kineticFIELD, and shifted Basspod to the right of Electric Avenue. They even add new ones sometimes, like how Bionic Jungle joined the mix in 2022.

Friend: Which stages should I actually look for?

Vet: There are 9 main ones: kineticFIELD, Cosmic Meadow, Circuit Grounds, Neon Garden, Basspod, Wasteland, Quantum Valley, Stereo Bloom, and Bionic Jungle. Don't forget to check out Forest House and the Art Cars too.

Friend: Any "must-see" moments I should plan for?

Vet: Absolutely. Every night around 1am, there's a massive drone show and fireworks. Also, keep an eye out for the Owl mascot and the Daisy moving installation. And for the big finale, the Above & Beyond or Armin van Buuren sunrise sets are a legendary tradition you can't miss.

Friend: I keep hearing about "Kandi" and "PLUR." What is that?

Vet: That's the heart of the culture! PLUR stands for Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect. Kandi are those colorful beaded bracelets people trade. Just be open and friendly, and people will start swapping bracelets with you.

Friend: This sounds huge. Is Las Vegas the only place they do this?

Vet: It's the flagship, but Insomniac does others too. There's EDC Orlando, EDC Mexico City, EDC Thailand, and even EDSea, which is on a cruise ship! But for your first time, Vegas is the gold standard.
