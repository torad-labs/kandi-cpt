**Safety & Policy**
Medical care is free and "no questions asked"; seeking help will not result in trouble. Contact Ground Control (purple shirts, fanny packs, light sabers) or medical tents for symptoms: vomiting, seizures, pale/blue skin/lips, abnormal heart rate/breathing/temperature, severe headache, delusions, non-reactive pupils, confusion, or unresponsiveness. Keep impaired persons sitting upright; avoid coffee or cold showers for alcohol poisoning. Official drug policy is zero-tolerance; harm reduction includes electrolyte water and immediate medical intervention.
