Friend: I’m finally doing EDC 2026! I'm hyped but honestly a little overwhelmed. Where do I even start with the lineup?

Vet: First off, welcome to the madness! It’s a blast, but you gotta have a game plan. What kind of music are you looking for?

Friend: I love trance and that more "ethereal" vibe. Is there a specific spot for that?

Vet: Definitely. You want to spend a lot of time at Quantum Valley. It’s the Dreamstate stage, and they do everything from psychedelic to melodic. Friday is stacked there with Paul van Dyk, Gareth Emery, and Ilan Bluestone. If you're there Saturday, you can't miss Paul Oakenfold and Astrix.

Friend: Oh, nice. What about something a bit more housey or bass-heavy?

Vet: For that, hit up stereoBLOOM. It's curated by Insomniac and is a total mix of house and tech-house. Friday has Wax Motif and Max Dean, but Saturday is huge with Noizu, CID, and Sammy Virji. Sunday is also great—look out for Skream and Chris Lorenzo B2B Bullet Tooth.

Friend: I saw something about a "Bionic Jungle" stage? What's the deal with that one?

Vet: That's the newest addition, added back in 2022. It's got this cool forest aesthetic and mostly runs house artists. It’s a "small but mighty" vibe—really lets you unleash your inner animal. MALUGI was a huge anchor there recently, and it's just continuing to grow.

Friend: Got it. Aside from the music, what do I actually need to pack? I was thinking of some cute boots.

Vet: Stop right there. Comfortable, closed-toe sneakers are non-negotiable. You’ll be walking 10-20 miles a night across asphalt, gravel, and grass. Break them in *before* you go, and bring blister pads and gel insoles. Leave the heels and flip-flops at home or you'll be miserable by Friday night.

Friend: Yikes, I didn't realize it was that much walking. What about staying hydrated? Just buy water inside?

Vet: You can, but bring an empty CamelBak or an Insomniac bottle and hit the free refill stations (look for the water droplet symbol on the map). Pro tip: don't just drink plain water. Use electrolyte tablets or the free Electrolit sticks at the stations. Aim for about 16 oz per hour—don't go over 27 oz or you risk hyponatremia.

Friend: I didn't know that. Anything else I'm forgetting?

Vet: Two big things. First: high-fidelity earplugs. Whether it's Loops or Etymotics, get some. You're exposing your ears to 30+ hours of massive sound; tinnitus is real. Second: assume your phone won't work. Cell service is basically non-existent.

Friend: Wait, no phone? How am I supposed to find my friends if we get split up?

Vet: You have to pick a fixed meeting spot and a specific time—like the lockers area or the info booth. Whatever you do, don't use the Daisy as a meeting point because it moves! Also, screenshot the map and the schedule before you enter the gates so you aren't scrolling through a dead signal.
