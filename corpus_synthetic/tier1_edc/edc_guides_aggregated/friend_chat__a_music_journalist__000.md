If you see people trading beaded bracelets, that’s "kandi," and it's the fastest way to make friends.

Friend: I saw the dates are May 15-17. Is it just a standard festival schedule?

Vet: Not exactly. It’s a "dusk to dawn" operation. Gates open at 7pm Friday through Sunday, and the music doesn't stop until 5:30am the next morning. If you're there Friday, get there early for the opening ceremony at cosmicMEADOW at 5pm.

Vet: It’s a trip. You’ll deal with 90°F+ days and then it drops to 60°F at night. The humidity is crazy low—like 15-20%—and the desert wind just rips through the Speedway. Bring a light jacket for the sunrise, or you'll freeze.

Friend: Got it. Now, the stages. I keep seeing "kineticFIELD" mentioned. Is that just the main stage?

Vet: Exactly. kineticFIELD is the crown jewel—it's the largest stage in North America, 480 feet wide and 100 feet tall. Every year it has a new theme. In 2023 it was kineticAWAKENING with that massive "Celestial" figure, and 2024 was the KineticCIRCLE with those stained-glass windows. It’s where the absolute giants play.

Friend: Who usually plays there? Like, who are the "must-sees" for that stage?

Vet: Think the biggest names in the game: Martin Garrix, Tiësto, Zedd, and Charlotte de Witte. You’ll see guys like John Summit or Kaskade there, too. But the real magic is the Sunrise Sets—Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren are the legends who usually handle the transition from night to morning at kineticFIELD.

Friend: That sounds insane. Are there other stages I should be tracking, or is everything just a variation of the main one?

Vet: Oh, they're all different. For example, cosmicMEADOW is way more chill and atmospheric compared to the chaos of the main stage. The whole layout is designed to be a journey, so you'll be trekking a few miles a day across the Speedway.

Vet: Just remember the Speedway is about 15 miles north of the Strip, so don't rely on a quick Uber. Pack way more water than you think you need because of that dry air, and just lean into the weirdness. It's a 30th-anniversary party, man—go all out.
