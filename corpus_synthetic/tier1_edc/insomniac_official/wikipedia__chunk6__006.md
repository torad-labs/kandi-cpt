# Electric Daisy Carnival Las Vegas (Policy and Logistics)

## Overview
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is an electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac. The event adheres to the "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect) ethos, emphasizing community kindness, hydration, and the "buddy system" for attendee safety.

## Event Schedule
The event takes place over three consecutive days in May. On Friday, May 15, the opening ceremony commences at the cosmicMeadow stage at 5:00 p.m., with the full grounds opening at 7:00 p.m. On Saturday, May 16, and Sunday, May 17, the grounds open at 7:00 p.m. Each night of the festival concludes at 5:30 a.m. the following morning.

## Ticketing and Admission
Entry is restricted to holders of 3-day passes, with the exception of VIP guests purchasing single-day bottle service. Ticket purchases are limited to a maximum of four per person and are processed exclusively via credit or debit cards.

Admission requirements include:
* **Age Restrictions:** All attendees must be at least 18 years old. Access to the VIP Enhanced Experience and the purchase of alcohol require a minimum age of 21.
* **Identification:** Valid government-issued identification is required for entry and alcohol purchases. Expired IDs are only accepted if accompanied by DMV renewal documentation. Valid forms of identification include passports (individually) or foreign driver's licenses (accompanied by a passport photocopy). Consular IDs are not permitted.
* **Re-entry:** The event maintains a strict no re-entry policy. Entry is permitted up until one hour before the event's conclusion for those holding valid, unscanned tickets.

## Logistics and Media
### Information Systems
Event details, including the festival map, set times, and lineup updates, are disseminated via the Insomniac mobile application, the official EDC Las Vegas website, social media, and email updates. Additionally, printed festival guides are distributed to attendees upon entry.

### Camera Policy
The use of professional cameras is prohibited. Photo passes are not required for handheld video devices under six inches or standard cameras with fixed, non-detachable lenses. Insomniac does not issue media or photo credentials to independent photographers.

### Parking
Overnight sleeping in the parking lot is prohibited. Vehicles remaining overnight are subject to towing at the owner's expense.
