Friend: Yo, I’m trying to map out my weekend for EDC 2026. Saturday looks absolutely insane—who are the absolute must-sees for the day?

Vet: It’s a heavy day. If you want the big energy, you’ve gotta hit Tiësto, Hardwell, and Kaskade. But for me, the real move is The Prodigy; they’re going to tear the place apart.

Friend: I saw a few "In The Round" sets on the list. Which ones are actually worth the trek across the grounds?

Vet: Definitely catch BUNT. and VTSS. Those 360-degree setups are built for that kind of energy, especially with VTSS bringing that industrial vibe.

Friend: I’m really into the melodic/trance side of things. Who’s playing the sunrise slots on Saturday?

Vet: You’re in luck. Above & Beyond and BOLO both have Sunrise Sets scheduled. There is nothing like hearing A&B as the sun comes up over the desert—it's a spiritual experience.

Friend: What about the bass side? I see Subtronics and Excision usually dominate, but who's the move this time?

Vet: Subtronics is obviously a lock, but keep an eye on the Eptic B2B Space Laces set. Also, the Doctor P B2B Flux Pavilion B2B FuntCase trio is going to be pure chaos.

Friend: I’ve been digging more techno and house lately. Anyone standing out in those genres?

Vet: For sure. John Summit and Peggy Gou (who is doing a B2B with KI/KI) are the big names, but if you want something grittier, check out Boys Noize or Hannah Laing.

Friend: I’m trying to figure out where these acts actually land. Where should I expect the heavy hitters and the underground stuff to be?

Vet: The mainstage energy always centers at kineticFIELD, but for the harder stuff, keep your eyes on circuitGROUNDS. If you're looking for that weird, experimental house or techno, you'll probably find the likes of VTSS or MCR-T over at bassPOD or neonGARDENS.

Friend: Any wildcards or weird B2Bs I should keep on my radar?

Vet: The Josh Baker B2B KETTAMA B2B Prospa set is going to be a journey. Also, keep an eye out for the Rob Gee B2B Lenny Dee collab if you want some old-school hardcore energy.

Friend: Last question—is it worth staying late into Sunday morning?

Vet: Always. Between the sunrise sets and the way the crowd peaks at 4 AM, you're missing the best part of the festival if you leave early. Just make sure you've got your hydration sorted.
