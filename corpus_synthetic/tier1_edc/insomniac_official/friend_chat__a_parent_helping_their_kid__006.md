Friend: My kid is heading to EDC 2026 and I'm trying to help them prep. Do you know when things actually start?

Vet: For sure! Friday, May 15th is the big kickoff. The opening ceremony at Cosmic Meadow starts at 5:00pm, but the rest of the grounds don't open until 7:00pm. Saturday and Sunday just open at 7:00pm. Every night runs until 5:30am.

Friend: That's a long night! Can they pop out to the hotel for a nap or a change of clothes and come back in?

Vet: Nope, definitely not. Re-entry isn't allowed at all. Once they're in, they're in. The only thing to know is that as long as they have a valid unscanned ticket, they can enter up until one hour before the event ends.

Friend: Got it. I'm looking at tickets now—are there single-day options?

Vet: Not for general admission; it's 3-day passes only. The only way to get a single day is if you're paying for VIP Bottle Service. Also, keep in mind there's a 4-ticket limit per person and they only take cards—no cash.

Friend: Okay, what about age requirements? My kid is 18, but they have some older friends going too.

Vet: 18 is the minimum to get in the door. However, if they want the VIP Enhanced Experience or want to buy alcohol, they have to be 21.

Friend: I'm worried about the ID situation. What if their license is slightly expired or they're using a foreign ID?

Vet: They'll definitely need a valid ID to get in. Expired ones only work if they have DMV renewal papers. For foreign IDs, a passport works on its own, or a foreign driver's license paired with a photocopy of the passport. Just a heads up: Consular IDs aren't accepted.

Friend: My kid wants to bring a camera to document the trip. Do they need a special permit?

Vet: As long as it's a standard camera with a fixed lens (non-detachable) or a handheld video device under 6 inches, they're fine. No photo pass needed. But no professional gear allowed—Insomniac doesn't give out media credentials to independent photographers.

Friend: How do they keep track of who is playing where? Is there a physical map?

Vet: They get a printed guide at entry, but the Insomniac app is the way to go. It has the map, set times for stages like kineticFIELD and circuitGROUNDS, and lineup updates. They can also check the website or social media.

Friend: What about parking? Can they just crash in the car if they're too tired to drive back?

Vet: Absolutely not. No overnight sleeping in the parking lot. If security catches a car left overnight, it'll be towed at the owner's expense.

Friend: Any last pieces of "pro" advice for a first-timer?

Vet: Tell them to use the buddy system, stay hydrated, and wear the most comfortable shoes they own. Most importantly, remind them to be kind to everyone. It's all about PLUR—Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect.
