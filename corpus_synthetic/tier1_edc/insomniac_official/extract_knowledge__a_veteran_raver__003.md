Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 takes place from Friday, May 15, through Sunday, May 17. The festival utilizes several primary stages, including Kinetic Field, Circuit Grounds, and Cosmic Meadow.

**Kinetic Field Programming**
Friday's lineup features The Chainsmokers, Charlotte de Witte, Porter Robinson (DJ Set), and Sofi Tukker. Saturday's schedule includes Hardwell, John Summit, Subtronics, Kaskade, and Steve Aoki. Sunday's schedule features Martin Garrix, Zedd, Fisher, Cloonee, and a B2B set by GRiZ and Wooli.

**Cosmic Meadow and Circuit Grounds**
Underworld performs at Cosmic Meadow on Friday, May 15. The Prodigy performs at Cosmic Meadow on Saturday, May 16. Sunday's Cosmic Meadow roster includes Alison Wonderland, Seven Lions, and a "Wholesome Riddim Set" by San Holo. Tiësto is scheduled for Circuit Grounds on Saturday, May 16.

**Sunrise Sets**
Kinetic Field hosts two specific sunrise sets: Above & Beyond on Saturday, May 16, and Armin van Buuren on Sunday, May 17.

**Sunday Full Roster**
The Sunday lineup consists of a diverse array of artists across genres.
