Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 features several specialized stages and installations. Quantum Valley is the dedicated area for Dreamstate, which focuses on the trance genre, ranging from ethereal melodies to psychedelic styles. Stereo Bloom, curated by Insomniac Music Group, hosts a variety of house, tech house, and bass music. Bionic Jungle is a new addition to the event, featuring a roster of house artists.

The festival grounds include the Speedway, where mobile sound systems known as Art Cars operate. These vehicles broadcast various forms of dance music and may host unplanned DJ sets. Notable Art Cars include the BeatBox, Picnic Playtime, and Metaphoenix.

The Friday, May 15 lineup consists of a wide array of electronic artists, including Porter Robinson (DJ Set), Paul van Dyk, Charlotte de Witte, Peggy Gou, Fisher, and The Chainsmokers. Other performers scheduled for Friday include Adriatique, ATLiens, Cosmic Gate, Darude, Eli Brown, I Hate Models, Joseph Capriati, Kai Wachi, MEDUZA, and Sofi Tukker. The day's roster also features collaborative sets, such as DJ Tennis B2B Chloé Caillet and Walker & Royce B2B VNSSA.
