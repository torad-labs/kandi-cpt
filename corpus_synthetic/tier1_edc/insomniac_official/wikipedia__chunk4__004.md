# Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)

## Overview
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a large-scale electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac. The event features multiple themed stages and curated lineups across various subgenres of electronic music, supported by comprehensive onsite lodging and safety infrastructure.

## Stages and Lineups

### neonGarden
The neonGarden stage features a rotation of house and techno artists. Friday's lineup includes Adriatique, Peggy Gou, Eli Brown, and Joseph Capriati. Saturday's performances feature Peggy Gou B2B with KI/KI, DJ Gigola B2B with MCR-T, mink, Luciano, and Silvie Loto. Sunday's schedule includes Frankie Bones, DJ Gigola, Adiel, Indira Paganotto, and Klangkuenstler.

### quantumValley
The quantumValley stage is dedicated to trance and progressive music. Friday's performers include Cosmic Gate, Gareth Emery, and Paul van Dyk. Saturday's lineup consists of Andrew Rayel, Astrix, and Paul Oakenfold. Above & Beyond performs a set at quantumValley in addition to a separate Kinetic Sunrise appearance. Tinlicker is scheduled for a DJ set on Sunday.

### basspod
The basspod stage focuses on bass music and dubstep. The lineup includes Subtronics, GRiZ B2B Wooli, Sub Focus, Peekaboo, Virtual Riot, Hybrid Minds, and Culture Shock. Additionally, the stage features a "throwback set" by Adventure Club, a B2B performance by Eptic and Space Laces, and a collaborative set featuring Doctor P, Flux Pavilion, and FuntCase.

### wasteland
The wasteland stage hosts hardstyle and hard techno. Friday's performances include GRAVEDGR and Rebekah. Saturday's lineup features Lady Faith B2B LNY TNZ, Da Tweekaz, Rob Gee B2B Lenny Dee, and a B2B set by Audiofreq, Code Black, and Toneshifterz. Sunday's schedule includes Sub Zero Project and Warface.

## Logistics

### Camp EDC
Camp EDC provides onsite camping accommodations. Passes are sold exclusively as four-night packages covering Thursday through Sunday; single-night options are unavailable. 

*   **Amenities:** The campground includes complimentary showers, portable toilets, and drinking water. A General Store operates for the duration of the event. ADA camping is available across all tiers.
*   **RV Facilities:** RV camping is available with paid options for power hookups, water refills, and pump-out services. Rental or preset RVs are not provided; guests must bring their own vehicles.
*   **Transportation:** Vehicles are prohibited from driving into the campground proper, though gear transport assistance is provided from the parking area. Separate parking passes for group members are available for $95 (plus taxes and fees).
*   **Regulations:** Due to heat-related health risks, sleeping in cars or oversized vans is prohibited.

### Cooking and Prohibited Items
Small portable propane stoves and grills are permitted, with a limit of 4 lbs of propane per group. Butane devices are prohibited. Steel knives and other utensils classified as weapons are banned from the campground.

## Safety and Medical

### Ground Control
Ground Control is the designated Insomniac safety team. Personnel are identified by purple shirts, fanny packs, and light sabers. They serve as general assistants and welfare checkers for festival attendees.

### Medical Services
Medical care is provided free of charge at designated tents throughout the festival grounds. Insomniac Health & Safety officers patrol the venue to identify individuals in need of assistance. First aid services cover a range of needs, from minor ailments such as blisters and nausea to critical medical emergencies. The organization maintains a policy that attendees will not face disciplinary action or legal repercussions for seeking medical assistance.
