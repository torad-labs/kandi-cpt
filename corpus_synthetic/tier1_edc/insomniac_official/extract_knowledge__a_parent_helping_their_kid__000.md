**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas 2026**

Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas 2026 is a North American electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac Events. The event takes place from Friday, May 15, through Sunday, May 17, 2026, at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in the Nevada desert. The grounds are open nightly from 7:00 p.m. to 5:30 a.m. The festival's opening ceremony begins Friday, May 15, at 5:00 p.m. at the Cosmic Meadow stage.

**Attendance and Logistics**
The minimum age for attendance is 18. Access to Bottle Service and the VIP Enhanced Experience is restricted to individuals aged 21 and older. The event is cashless; accepted payment methods include credit, debit, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay, and Android Pay. On-site cash-to-prepaid card exchanges are available. Re-entry is prohibited once a guest has entered the festival grounds for the night. The nearest transit hub is Harry Reid International Airport (LAS).

**Facilities and Safety**
On-site lodging is provided via Camp EDC, accessible through four-night camping passes. To mitigate risks of dehydration and heat exhaustion, free water refill stations—sponsored by Electrolit and identified by water-droplet symbols on the map—are located throughout the venue. Safety personnel, known as the Ground Control team, are identifiable by purple shirts.

**Stage Programming**
The festival features nine main stages, including:
*   **Kinetic Field:** The primary main stage with a capacity of approximately 70,000 attendees, hosting major headliners.
*   **Cosmic Meadow:** An open-grounds stage used for the opening ceremony and a mix of big-room and experimental acts.
*   **Circuit Grounds:** A stage characterized by LED walls and fire pyrotechnics.
*   **Neon Garden:** A venue curated by Factory 93 specializing in deep house, techno, and minimalism.
*   **Basspod:** A dedicated area for dubstep, bass house, trap, and drum and bass.
*   **Wasteland:** A stage operated by Basscon focusing on hard dance, hardcore, and hardstyle.
*   **Quantum Valley:** One of the nine primary performance areas.
