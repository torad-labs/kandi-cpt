**Camp EDC**
4-night passes (Thu-Sun); no single nights. Includes free showers, water, porta-potties, and General Store. Staffed 24/7 by Ground Control, medical, and security.
*   **Cooking**: Small portable propane stoves/grills permitted (max 4lb tank/group). No butane or steel knives/weapon-utensils.
*   **Vehicles**: No sleeping in cars/oversize vans (heat risk). No driving into campground; gear assistance provided from parking.
*   **Services**: ADA camping available all tiers. RVs (BYO) have power, water refill, and pump-outs for fees. Extra parking passes: $95 + tax/fees.

**Safety/Medical**
Ground Control (purple shirts/light sabers) provide assistance. Free medical care at tents/via roaming officers; no penalties for seeking help.
