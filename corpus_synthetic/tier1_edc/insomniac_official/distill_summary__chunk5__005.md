# Chunk: 5

**Medical & Safety:** Escalate to medical for vomiting, seizures, pale/bluish skin, abnormal heart rate/breathing, abnormal body temperature, delusions/hallucinations/paranoia, non-reactive pupils, confusion/stupor, coma, or inability to be roused. Keep intoxicated persons awake and upright; avoid binge drinking. Use the buddy system with set meeting points and staff assistance.

**Hydration:** Free UV-sterilized water and Electrolit hydration sticks available at stations (water droplet symbol on map). Prohibited: sealed water. Permitted: empty CamelBaks, reusable bottles, or empty beverage containers.

**Amenities:** 
*   **Food/Drink:** Cashless (Credit, Debit, Apple/Android Pay). Vegan/vegetarian options available via stands and roaming vendors.
*   **Alcohol:** 21+ with ID; limited service hours.
*   **Shopping:** Insomniac Shop sells apparel (T-shirts, jackets, backpacks, sweatshirts, water bottles, accessories). General Shop sells essentials (flip-flops, eye drops, ring pops).
*   **Services:** Lockers provide unlimited access and universal cell phone chargers. Additional charging stations, info booths, and Lost & Found are located throughout the venue per the festival map.
