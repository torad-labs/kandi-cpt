Q: What time does the opening ceremony begin at cosmicMEADOW on Friday, May 15?
A: The opening ceremony begins at 5:00pm.

Q: What are the full grounds opening times for May 15, May 16, and May 17?
A: The full grounds open at 7:00pm on all three days.

Q: What time do the festivities end each night?
A: All three nights run until 5:30am the following morning.

Q: What is the policy regarding re-entry to the event?
A: Ins-and-outs are not allowed.

Q: Until when can a person enter the event with a valid unscanned ticket?
A: You may enter until one hour before the end of the event.

Q: What ticket types are available for general entry, and what is the maximum number of tickets per person?
A: Only 3-day passes are available for general entry, with a maximum of 4 tickets per person.

Q: What are the age requirements for entry, alcohol, and the VIP Enhanced Experience?
A: You must be 18 or older to enter and 21 or older for alcohol and the VIP Enhanced Experience.

Q: Which types of cameras do not require a photo pass?
A: Standard cameras with fixed non-detachable lenses and handheld video devices under 6 inches do not require photo passes.

Q: What is the policy for cars left overnight in the parking lot?
A: Overnight sleeping is not permitted and cars left overnight will be towed at the owner's expense.

Q: Which forms of identification are accepted for entry or alcohol purchases?
A: Accepted identification includes valid IDs, expired IDs with DMV renewal documentation, foreign government driver's licenses with a photocopy of a passport, and foreign government passports.

Q: Which type of identification is specifically not accepted?
A: Consular IDs are not accepted.

Q: Where can attendees find set times and the festival schedule?
A: Set times are available on the Insomniac app, the EDC website, social media, email updates, and the printed festival guide given at entry.
