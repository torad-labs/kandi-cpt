# Electric Daisy Carnival Las Vegas 2026

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas 2026** is a scheduled electronic dance music (EDM) festival produced by Insomniac Events. Taking place from Friday, May 15, to Sunday, May 17, 2026, the event is held at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in Nevada. It is recognized as the largest EDM festival in North America.

## Overview
The festival grounds are open daily from 7:00 p.m. to 5:30 a.m. The event commences with an opening ceremony held at the cosmicMeadow stage on May 15 at 5:00 p.m. Admission is restricted to individuals aged 18 and older, while the VIP Enhanced Experience and Bottle Service areas are restricted to those aged 21 and older.

## Stages
The festival features nine primary stages, supplemented by art cars and smaller performance areas.

*   **kineticField**: The festival's primary main stage, designed to accommodate approximately 70,000 attendees and host the event's headlining artists.
*   **cosmicMeadow**: An open-grounds stage used for the opening ceremony and a mix of big-room and experimental programming.
*   **circuitGrounds**: A high-energy stage characterized by the use of large-scale LED walls and pyrotechnics.
*   **neonGarden**: A stage curated by Factory 93 focusing on deep house, techno, and minimalist electronic music.
*   **bassPod**: A stage dedicated to the Bassrush collective, featuring dubstep, bass house, drum and bass, and trap.
*   **wasteland**: A stage curated by Basscon focusing on hard dance, hardcore, and hardstyle.
*   **quantumValley**: One of the festival's nine main stages.

## Logistics
### Transport and Accommodation
The nearest aviation hub is Harry Reid International Airport (LAS). On-site lodging is available via Camp EDC, which requires a four-night camping pass for access.

### Financials and Access
EDC Las Vegas is a cashless event. Accepted payment methods include credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay, and Android Pay; cash may be exchanged for prepaid cards on-site. The festival enforces a strict no re-entry policy; attendees who exit the grounds are not permitted to return for that calendar day.

## Safety and Health
Due to the festival's location in the Nevada desert, organizers highlight risks associated with dehydration and heat exhaustion. To mitigate these risks, free water refill stations, presented by Electrolit and identified by water-droplet symbols on the official map, are provided throughout the venue. Attendees are advised to wear comfortable footwear, specifically sneakers rather than sandals. On-site safety and attendee assistance are provided by the Ground Control team, identifiable by their purple shirts.
