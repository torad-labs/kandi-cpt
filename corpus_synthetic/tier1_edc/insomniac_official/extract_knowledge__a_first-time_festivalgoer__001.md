Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 features several specialized stages and mobile installations. **Quantum Valley** is the designated area for the Dreamstate brand, focusing on the trance genre, including ethereal and psychedelic styles. **Stereo Bloom**, curated by Insomniac Music Group, hosts a variety of genres including house, tech house, and bass music. **Bionic Jungle** is a new, smaller-scale stage dedicated to house music.

The festival grounds include the **Speedway**, where mobile sound systems known as **Art Cars** operate. These vehicles broadcast various forms of dance music and occasionally host special DJ sets. Specific notable art cars include the BeatBox, Picnic Playtime, and Metaphoenix.

The schedule for Friday, May 15, includes a diverse lineup of artists across multiple electronic subgenres. Notable performers include Porter Robinson (DJ Set), The Chainsmokers, Charlotte de Witte, Fisher, Peggy Gou, Paul van Dyk, and MEDUZA. The day's roster also features specialized sets such as Adventure Club (Throwback Set) and ARMNHMR presents Passport. Additional artists performing on Friday include Adriatique, ATLiens, Cosmic Gate, Darude, Eli Brown, I Hate Models, Kai Wachi, and Wooli.
