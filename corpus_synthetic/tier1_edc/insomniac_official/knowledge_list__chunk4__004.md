**Camp EDC Policies**
* Camp EDC offers 4-night camping passes.
* Single-night camping is unavailable.
* Free showers and porta-potties are provided.
* Drinking water is available at the campground.
* The General Store remains open for the duration of the event.
* Ground Control, medical, and security staff are on-site 24 hours a day.
* Small portable propane stoves and grills are allowed.
* Propane tanks are limited to 4 lbs per group.
* Butane devices are prohibited.
* Steel knives and weapon-like utensils are prohibited.
* Sleeping in cars or oversize vans is forbidden.
* Cars cannot drive into the campground proper.
* Gear transport assistance is available from the parking area.
* ADA camping is available in all tiers.
* RV camping includes power hookups and water refills.
* RV pump-outs are available for an extra charge.
* Attendees must provide their own RVs.
* Extra parking passes cost $95 plus taxes and fees.

**Safety & Wayfinding**
* Ground Control members wear purple shirts and fanny packs.
* Ground Control members carry light sabers.
* Medical staff are located in tents across the grounds.
* Medical care is provided free of charge.
* Insomniac Health & Safety officers roam the venue.
* First aid tents treat nausea, lightheadedness, and blisters.
* Seeking medical help will not result in trouble.
