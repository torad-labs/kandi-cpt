Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 is scheduled for May 15, 16, and 17. On Friday, May 15, the opening ceremony commences at 5:00 p.m. at Cosmic Meadow, with full grounds opening at 7:00 p.m. On Saturday and Sunday, the grounds open at 7:00 p.m. All three event days conclude at 5:30 a.m. the following morning.

Entry is restricted to individuals aged 18 and older, with a minimum age of 21 required for alcohol consumption and the VIP Enhanced Experience. Admission requires a 3-day pass, though single-day access is available via VIP Bottle Service. Ticket purchases are limited to four per person and must be made using credit or debit cards. Entry is permitted up to one hour before the event's conclusion with a valid, unscanned ticket; however, re-entry is prohibited.

Identification requirements for entry and alcohol purchases include valid government IDs. Expired IDs are accepted only when accompanied by DMV renewal documentation. Foreign government passports are accepted independently, and foreign government driver's licenses are accepted if accompanied by a passport photocopy. Consular IDs are not permitted.

The festival's camera policy prohibits professional equipment. Photo passes are not required for handheld video devices under six inches or cameras with fixed, non-detachable lenses. Insomniac does not issue media or photo credentials to independent photographers. Regarding transportation, overnight sleeping in the parking lot is prohibited, and vehicles left overnight are subject to towing.

Event information, including maps, schedules, set times, and lineup updates, is disseminated via the Insomniac app, the official EDC Las Vegas website, social media, email updates, and printed guides distributed at the entrance.
