Friend: I'm finally doing EDC 2026! I'm looking at the lineup now—who should I hit up at Neon Garden?

Vet: That stage is stacked. If you're there Friday, you gotta see Adriatique, Eli Brown, and Joseph Capriati. For the weekend, Peggy Gou is playing solo Friday and then doing a B2B with KI/KI on Saturday. Sunday is the move for Frankie Bones, Adiel, Indira Paganotto, and Klangkuenstler.

Friend: Nice. What about the other days for Neon Garden?

Vet: Saturday is huge too. You've got mink, Luciano, and Silvie Loto. Oh, and DJ Gigola is playing solo Sunday, but she's B2B with MCR-T on Saturday.

Friend: I really want some trance and bass stuff. Where do I go for that?

Vet: For trance, head to quantumValley. Friday is the big day with Cosmic Gate, Gareth Emery, and Paul van Dyk. Saturday has Andrew Rayel, Astrix, and Paul Oakenfold. Also, Above & Beyond is doing a quantumValley set on top of their Kinetic Sunrise slot, and Tinlicker is playing a DJ set Sunday. For the heavy stuff, basspod is where it's at. You'll see Subtronics, GRiZ B2B Wooli, Sub Focus, Peekaboo, Virtual Riot, and Hybrid Minds. Plus, some classics like an Adventure Club throwback set, Eptic B2B Space Laces, and a massive Doctor P B2B Flux Pavilion B2B FuntCase. Culture Shock is on there too.

Friend: I've heard the hardstyle stage is insane. What's the vibe at wasteland?

Vet: It's pure energy. Friday you've got GRAVEDGR and Rebekah. Saturday is wild with Lady Faith B2B LNY TNZ, Da Tweekaz, Rob Gee B2B Lenny Dee, and a trio set with Audiofreq B2B Code Black B2B Toneshifterz. Then wrap up Sunday with Sub Zero Project and Warface.

Friend: I'm thinking about camping to save on hotels. How does Camp EDC work?

Vet: It's pretty straightforward, but you have to commit. They only sell 4-night passes (Thursday–Sunday); you can't just do a single night. It's got everything: free showers, water, porta-potties, and a General Store that's open the whole time. Ground Control, medical, and security are there 24/7 too.

Friend: Can I just sleep in my car if I get tired? And can I cook some food?

Vet: Definitely do NOT sleep in your car or an oversize van—it's banned because of the heat risks. As for food, small propane stoves and grills are fine, but keep the tanks under 4 lbs per group. Just remember: no butane devices and no steel knives or weapons. Also, you can't drive your car into the actual campground, but they'll help you move your gear from the parking lot.

Friend: What if I have a big group? Can we all bring cars?

Vet: You can buy extra parking passes for people arriving separately, but they're $95 plus taxes and fees. Also, if you have an RV, you can bring your own for an extra charge to get power, water refills, and pump-outs. Just a heads up: they don't rent them out, so you have to provide your own. (And don't worry, ADA camping is available in all tiers).

Friend: I get a bit anxious at big crowds. Is there anyone I can talk to if I feel overwhelmed?

Vet: Totally. Look for Ground Control—they're the ones in the purple shirts with fanny packs and light sabers. They're literally there to keep you hydrated and happy. If it's something more serious, just find a medical tent or an Insomniac Health & Safety officer.

Friend: I'm a little worried about the "medical" part. Will I get in trouble if I need help for something... festival-related?

Vet: Not at all.
