### Medical Protocols and Emergency Criteria
Insomniac establishes specific escalation criteria for transporting attendees from basic care (shade and water) to professional medical services. Immediate medical intervention is required for individuals exhibiting vomiting, seizures, pale or bluish skin pigmentation, abnormal heart rate or respiratory patterns, or abnormal body temperature. Further indicators for medical escalation include delusions, hallucinations, paranoia, non-reactive pupils, confusion, stupor, coma, or an inability to be roused.

Regarding alcohol impairment, guidelines state that intoxicated individuals should not be responsible for others. To mitigate the risks of binge drinking and alcohol poisoning, the protocol mandates keeping intoxicated persons awake and in an upright sitting position; coffee, cold showers, and sleep are noted as ineffective treatments for alcohol poisoning.

### Hydration and Water Access
Water refill stations, sponsored by Electrolit, are distributed throughout the venue and identified by a water droplet symbol on the festival map. These stations provide filtered water treated via UV sterilization and distribute free Electrolit hydration sticks. 

The venue prohibits the entry of sealed water containers. Permitted items include empty CamelBaks, empty reusable water bottles (including official Insomniac-branded merchandise), and other empty beverage containers.

### Venue Amenities and Commerce
Electric Daisy Carnival is a cashless event, accepting credit, debit, Apple Pay, and Android Pay. Food and beverage services consist of fixed stands and roaming vendors, with available vegan and vegetarian options. Alcohol service is restricted to attendees aged 21 and older with valid identification.

The Insomniac Shop provides apparel, artist collections, and accessories. The Insomniac General Shop sells utilitarian items, such as flip-flops and eye drops. On-site infrastructure includes lockers with universal cell phone chargers, standalone battery charging stations, information booths, and Lost & Found services.
