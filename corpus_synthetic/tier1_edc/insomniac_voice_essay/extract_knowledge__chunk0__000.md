Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is an electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac. The event is characterized by large-scale lighting installations, bass-heavy musical performances, and a culture of elaborate costuming.

Attendees are advised to review official festival policies regarding prohibited items prior to arrival. Recommended personal supplies for the Nevada climate include sunscreen, hats, sunglasses, and sealed lip balm.

Logistical preparations for the event include the advance booking of hotel accommodations to ensure lodging. Transportation options to and from the venue must be arranged in advance to facilitate movement within the Las Vegas area.
