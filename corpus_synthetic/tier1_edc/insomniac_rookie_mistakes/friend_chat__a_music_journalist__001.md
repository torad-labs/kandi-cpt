Friend: I’m finally hitting EDC 2026, but I’m low-key terrified of the Las Vegas heat. Any pro tips for not collapsing?

Vet: First rule: hydration is non-negotiable. You’re in the desert, dancing and drinking for three days straight—your body is basically running on overtime. Don't be like me and end up dizzy and laid out on the grass because I forgot to drink H2O. Also, eat a real sandwich; you need the calories to survive the weekend.

Friend: Fair enough. I'll pack the electrolytes. I'm planning on filming everything for my feed, though. Any specific spots I should hit for the best content?

Vet: Honestly? Put the phone down. Post a few clips, but don't let Instagram distract you from what's happening right in front of you. This only happens once a year. Just bang your head and be in the moment—the feed will still be there on Monday.

Friend: I get that. I'm mostly worried about getting lost or losing my group in that crowd. How do you handle that?

Vet: We’re all family under the Electric Sky, but you’ve gotta stick with your crew. If someone gets separated, don't just assume they're fine. Go find them. It's a team effort, and honestly, hunting for your lost friend is a great way to meet new people.

Friend: Speaking of the venue, is it actually worth wandering away from the main stages?

Vet: Absolutely. Don't be a sucker and just camp out at kineticFIELD or circuitGROUNDS. The Speedway is massive and there is something to discover around every single corner.

Friend: Like what? Give me some concrete targets for exploring.

Vet: You have to find El Pulpo—it’s this insane fire-breathing installation in the Downtown area. Also, keep an eye out for the art cars roaming the Speedway and make sure you catch the Parade where all the Insomniac characters march through the venue.

Friend: That sounds wild. Anything else I should do besides just watching the DJs?

Vet: Lean into the chaos. Join a conga line, buy a stranger a drink, write on someone's arm, or even get married if the mood strikes. There's even a spot to mail postcards to friends who couldn't make it. Just explore everything EDC has to offer.
