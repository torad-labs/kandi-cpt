Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a large-scale electronic dance music festival held in the Las Vegas desert, where temperatures can reach 95°F. Attendees are advised to wear breathable, layered clothing rather than black garments to manage heat exposure.

Physical health and safety protocols include the use of earplugs to prevent long-term tinnitus and the wearing of broken-in footwear to avoid blisters caused by new sneakers, sandals, or heels. Nutritional requirements for the event include eating substantial meals prior to the festival and consuming breakfast in the late morning for recovery. Hydration should include electrolytes; relying solely on pure water may lead to physical exhaustion, known as "bonking."

Logistical planning is required to navigate the venue and schedule. It is recommended to limit scheduled performances to approximately three sets per night to avoid missing content. To avoid delays, attendees are advised against departing for the venue at 5:30 AM, as this often results in two-hour shuttle lines. 

Communication and safety strategies involve establishing a specific meeting point that is not "the Daisy," as relying on cellular phones or generic landmarks is often ineffective. For substance safety, the organization DanceSafe provides legal reagent kits and fentanyl test strips for testing substances.
