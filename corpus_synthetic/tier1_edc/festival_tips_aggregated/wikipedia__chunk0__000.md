# Electric Daisy Carnival Las Vegas (Attendance and Logistics)

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas** is a large-scale electronic dance music festival held annually at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Due to the event's scale, environmental conditions, and duration, experienced attendees ("veterans") emphasize specific strategic preparations regarding physical health, navigation, and scheduling.

## Overview
EDC Las Vegas is characterized by its massive footprint, featuring over 240 artists performing across nine primary stages and eight secondary venues. The festival operates over three consecutive nights, with each event lasting approximately 10 hours. Because of the spatial distribution of performances, attendees are advised to prioritize a limited number of "must-see" sets (typically 3–5 per night) rather than attempting to visit every venue.

## Stages and Navigation
The festival grounds consist of a mix of rough concrete paving and unpaved sections. The distance covered by attendees often ranges from 10 to 20 miles per day. Navigation is managed via the official festival map, which identifies key landmarks and essential services, such as water stations indicated by a water-droplet symbol.

## Logistics and Preparation

### Footwear and Physical Care
Proper footwear is cited as the most critical element of festival preparation. Recommended attire consists of comfortable, broken-in sneakers; sandals, flip-flops, and high heels are discouraged due to the terrain and mileage. To prevent injury and fatigue, attendees often utilize blister pads, gel insoles, and additional socks. 

### Scheduling and Recovery
To mitigate exhaustion and "post-festival burnout," a common travel itinerary involves arriving on Thursday for acclimation and departing on Tuesday. This allows for a dedicated recovery day following the final event. Attendees are encouraged to pace their energy expenditure across the three nights to avoid premature fatigue.

### Hearing Protection
To prevent tinnitus and permanent hearing loss caused by high-decibel environments, the use of ear protection is recommended. High-fidelity earplugs (such as those from Etymotic, EARasers, or Loop) are preferred over standard foam plugs, as they reduce volume by 15–25 dB while preserving audio clarity.

## Health and Safety

### Hydration and Electrolytes
Hydration is managed through a combination of water and electrolytes to prevent both dehydration and hyponatremia (water intoxication). The recommended baseline intake is 16 oz (480 mL) per hour, with a suggested maximum of 27 oz (800 mL) per hour. 

Attendees typically utilize reusable containers, such as CamelBaks or official Insomniac water bottles, which can be refilled at free water stations. Electrolyte supplementation via products such as Powerade, Gatorade, Pedialyte, or Electrolit is considered essential for maintaining physiological balance.

### Medical Support
First aid tents are stationed throughout the venue to provide emergency medical assistance and bottled water for those in distress.

### Group Coordination
Due to the high density of crowds and the scale of the Speedway, the "buddy system" is standard practice. This involves establishing specific, physical meeting points for groups to reunite in the event of cellular network failure or separation.
