**Artists & Programming**
* Over 240 artists perform across the festival.
* There are 9 main stages.
* There are 8 secondary venues.
* Attendees should prioritize 3-5 must-see sets per night.

**Stage Descriptions**
* The Speedway consists mostly of rough concrete.
* Some sections of the Speedway are unpaved.
* All stages use camelCase naming conventions per Insomniac standards.

**Operations Policies**
* Empty CamelBaks are permitted.
* Insomniac water bottles are available at merch booths.
* Free water stations are marked with a water-droplet symbol on the map.
* Bottled water is available inside first aid tents for emergencies.

**Safety Knowledge**
* Wear broken-in sneakers to avoid foot injuries.
* Avoid wearing sandals, flip-flops, or heels.
* Bring blister pads and gel insoles for foot care.
* Drink 16 oz of fluid per hour as a baseline.
* Limit water intake to 27 oz per hour to prevent hyponatremia.
* Use electrolytes like Pedialyte or Gatorade to supplement water.
* Use high-fidelity earplugs to prevent tinnitus.
* High-fidelity plugs reduce volume by 15-25 dB.
* Use a buddy system with designated meeting points.

**Wayfinding & Logistics**
* Expect to walk 10-20 miles per day.
* The festival grounds are expansive.
* Running between stages consumes significant time and energy.
* Arrive on Thursday to acclimate to the heat.
* Stay through Monday to recover before flying home.

**Veteran Strategy**
* Pace energy across three 10-hour nights.
* Some veterans remain sober on night one to orient themselves.
* Take full days off between nights for recovery.
* Avoid burnout from pre-festival parties in Las Vegas.
* Train with long walks the week before the event.
