# Electric Daisy Carnival (Las Vegas)

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a large-scale electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac Events. The Las Vegas iteration is hosted at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, utilizing both the infield and the exterior areas of the NASCAR racing circuit.

## Layout and Logistics
The festival grounds are organized around a central hub known as Downtown EDC, which features carnival rides and a Ferris wheel. The Ferris wheel serves as both a navigational landmark and a vantage point for viewing the event's fireworks displays. 

Navigational landmarks include the venue's raised bleachers, which indicate the direction of the exit. While the "Daisy" art installation is a prominent feature, it is considered an unreliable meeting point due to its movement throughout the event. Fixed reference points for attendees include the lockers area, merchant stores, and information booths. 

Due to high crowd density and signal interference, cellular service is frequently unreliable. Attendees are encouraged to utilize the official Insomniac application for real-time maps and scheduling. For on-site assistance, "Ground Control" staff (identifiable by purple shirts) and medical personnel provide support; medical services are provided free of charge to attendees.

## Stage Lineup
The festival features several thematic stages, each with a distinct visual identity:

*   **kineticField:** The primary main stage, characterized by a large-scale mascot face or owl.
*   **circuitGrounds:** Notable for its extensive LED walls and pyrotechnic fire plumes.
*   **cosmicMeadow:** An open-grounds stage frequently used for the festival's opening ceremonies.
*   **neonGarden:** A more enclosed environment with a darker aesthetic.
*   **wasteland:** Defined by a post-apocalyptic visual theme.

## Attendee Preparation and Safety

### Health and Wellness
Due to the arid climate of the Nevada desert, hydration and electrolyte loading are critical. Attendees are advised to maintain hydration levels prior to and during the event. High-fidelity earplugs are recommended to prevent hearing damage from high-decibel sound systems. 

Medical and physical precautions include:
*   **Foot Care:** The use of broken-in footwear is emphasized to prevent blisters. Recommended supplies include gel insoles, blister pads, and anti-chafe products (e.g., BodyGlide).
*   **Skin Protection:** Sunscreen is necessary for post-dawn hours, while lip balm is used to protect against windburn.
*   **Temperature Regulation:** Layered clothing is advised, as temperatures can drop to approximately 50°F (10°C) by 4:00 AM, despite the heat generated by crowds and speaker arrays.

### Equipment and Supplies
Recommended gear for attendees includes:
*   **Power and Connectivity:** Portable power banks for mobile devices and phone cases with wrist lanyards.
*   **Hygiene:** Wet wipes and hand fans for crowd-dense areas.
*   **Security:** Small, festival-compliant daybags or fanny packs, along with a photocopy of government identification kept separate from the original document.
*   **Social Customs:** The exchange of "kandi" (beaded bracelets) serves as a primary social signal and friendship marker within the community. Totems are frequently used as visual markers to locate groups.
