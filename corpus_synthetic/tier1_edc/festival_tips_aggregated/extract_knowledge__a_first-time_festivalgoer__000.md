### Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas: Operational Guidelines

**Physical Preparation and Footwear**
The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) takes place at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, a venue characterized by rough concrete and unpaved sections. Attendees typically walk between 10 and 20 miles per day. To prevent injury and fatigue, veterans recommend broken-in sneakers; sandals, flip-flops, and high heels are discouraged. Recommended foot care supplies include blister pads, gel insoles, bandages, and extra socks.

**Health and Hydration**
Hydration requirements are established at a baseline of 16 oz (480 mL) per hour, with a maximum cap of 27 oz (800 mL) per hour to prevent hyponatremia. Effective hydration requires a combination of water and electrolytes (e.g., Pedialyte, Gatorade, or Electrolit). Free water stations, identified by a water-droplet symbol on the official map, provide refills for CamelBaks and approved Insomniac water bottles. Emergency bottled water is available at first aid tents.

**Auditory Protection**
To prevent tinnitus, the use of ear protection is advised. High-fidelity earplugs (such as Loop, EARasers, or Etymotic) are preferred over foam plugs, as they reduce volume by 15–25 dB while maintaining sound clarity.

**Scheduling and Pacing**
The festival consists of three nights, each lasting approximately 10 hours. Due to the scale of the event—featuring over 240 artists across nine main stages and eight secondary venues—it is mathematically impossible to attend every performance. Attendees are advised to select 3–5 priority sets per night. 

To manage exhaustion, a recommended travel itinerary involves arriving on Thursday to acclimate to the climate and departing on Tuesday to allow for post-festival recovery.

**Logistics and Safety**
Due to the size of the grounds and the density of crowds, the "buddy system" is the standard for group safety. This includes establishing concrete, physical meeting points rather than relying solely on mobile communication.
