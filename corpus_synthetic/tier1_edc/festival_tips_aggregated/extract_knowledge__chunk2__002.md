**Venue and Navigation**
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is hosted at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, a NASCAR facility. The event layout encompasses both the infield and the areas outside the track. The primary entrance provides direct access to Kinetic Field, the main stage characterized by a large mascot face/owl. Other stages radiate from this central point, including Cosmic Meadow (site of the opening ceremony), Circuit Grounds (featuring LED walls and fire plumes), Neon Garden (an enclosed environment), and Wasteland (defined by a post-apocalyptic aesthetic).

Wayfinding relies on fixed landmarks. The raised bleachers serve as the primary indicator for the venue exit. Downtown EDC, located between the main stages, contains the Ferris wheel and carnival rides. While the Daisy art installation is mobile and unreliable as a meeting point, the Lockers area, merchant store, and information booths serve as stable reference points.

**Equipment and Attire**
Recommended footwear consists of broken-in sneakers to prevent injury. Clothing should be layered to accommodate temperature fluctuations, as desert nights can drop to 50°F by 4:00 AM, while areas near speakers remain warm. Essential gear includes high-fidelity earplugs, power banks, and festival-approved small daybags or fanny packs. Sun protection (sunscreen, hats, and shades) is necessary for post-dawn hours, and sealed lip balm is recommended for wind protection.

Health and hygiene supplies include wet wipes, anti-chafe products (e.g., BodyGlide), and foot care kits containing blister pads, bandages, and gel insoles. Attendees are advised to use electrolyte loading the day prior to the event and to maintain hydration throughout.

**Logistics and Safety**
The Insomniac app provides the official map and set times. Due to unreliable cellular service, attendees are encouraged to establish a buddy system and fixed meeting points. Ground Control staff, identified by purple shirts, provide guidance, and medical services are available free of charge. 

Administrative preparations include carrying a photocopy of identification and a small amount of cash for prepaid card exchanges. Social customs include "kandi" trading as a signal of friendship and the use of totems as visible meeting markers. Veteran attendees suggest arriving early on the first night and departing by 4:30 AM to manage pacing.
