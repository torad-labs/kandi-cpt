### Health and Physiological Maintenance

Attendees must manage hydration and caloric intake to avoid physical exhaustion. Consuming pure water without electrolytes can lead to "bonking" (sudden depletion of glycogen stores and electrolyte imbalance). It is recommended to consume a substantial breakfast in the late morning to facilitate bodily recovery before subsequent evening events.

Protective gear is essential for long-term health. Earplugs are required to prevent tinnitus. Regarding attire, breathable layered clothing is preferred over dark colors, such as black, to mitigate heat absorption in desert temperatures that can reach 95°F. Footwear should be broken-in prior to the event; new sneakers, sandals, or heels may cause blisters or injury.

### Logistics and Navigation

Communication during the festival is unreliable due to cellular network congestion. Attendees should establish a concrete meeting plan and a specific physical meeting location. "The Daisy" is considered an ineffective meeting spot due to high crowd density.

Transportation timing is critical. Departing the venue at 5:30 AM often results in shuttle wait times of up to two hours.

### Scheduling and Substance Safety

To avoid burnout and ensure a positive experience across all three nights, attendees are advised to limit their itinerary to approximately three primary sets per evening. Over-scheduling or excessive physical exertion on the first night can lead to missing subsequent days of the event. Additionally, excessive partying in Las Vegas on Wednesday and Thursday may compromise stamina for the festival.

For those utilizing substances, testing is advised. DanceSafe provides legal reagent kits and fentanyl test strips for this purpose.
