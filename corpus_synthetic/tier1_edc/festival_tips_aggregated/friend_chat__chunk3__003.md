Friend: I’m finally doing EDC 2026! I’m hyped, but I have no idea how to actually survive the weekend. Any tips?

Vet: First off, congrats! Just remember you won't get everything perfect your first time, and that's okay. The biggest mistake rookies make is going way too hard on night one and completely missing nights two and three. Pace yourself.

Friend: That makes sense. I'm already planning my outfits—I've got some killer black gear and some brand new platform sneakers.

Vet: Stop right there. Do not wear all black in 95°F desert heat; you'll bake. Go with breathable, layered clothing instead. Also, please don't wear brand new shoes or heels. Your feet will be destroyed. Break them in now or stick to comfortable sneakers.

Friend: Good call. I'll stick to water for hydration, right?

Vet: Water is key, but pure water alone can actually lead to "bonking" because you're flushing your salts. Bring electrolytes. Also, don't forget high-fidelity earplugs. If you skip them, you'll likely wake up with tinnitus that lasts a week.

Friend: I didn't think about the ears. What about the schedule? I want to see as much as possible.

Vet: Don't try to catch 15 sets a night; you'll end up seeing none of them properly. Pick 3 "must-see" sets—like a headliner at kineticFIELD or a deep dive at CIRCUITGROUNDS—and let the rest happen organically.

Friend: I'll definitely do that. I'm also planning to hit the clubs on Wednesday and Thursday before the fest starts.

Vet: Be careful with that. Pre-partying too hard in Vegas mid-week is a recipe for burnout. Also, make sure you're eating plenty before the festival starts, and don't skip breakfast when you wake up late in the morning. Your body needs those calories to recover before you head back out.

Friend: Got it. For meeting up with my group, we'll just use our phones or meet at the Daisy, right?

Vet: Absolute rookie move. Cell service is a nightmare, and "the Daisy" is way too crowded to be a reliable meeting spot. Pick a specific, weird landmark and agree on it beforehand.

Friend: True. Last thing—what about the "supplies" side of things?

Vet: Safety first. Don't trust anything you didn't get from a trusted source, and definitely test your substances. DanceSafe sells reagent kits and fentanyl test strips that are legal in most states. Better safe than sorry.

Friend: Thanks for the heads up. I'm planning to head out at 5:30am on the final day to beat the crowd.

Vet: Bad idea. If you leave at 5:30am, you're going to get stuck in a brutal two-hour shuttle line. Plan your exit more strategically. Just keep it simple, take care of your feet, and PLUR!
