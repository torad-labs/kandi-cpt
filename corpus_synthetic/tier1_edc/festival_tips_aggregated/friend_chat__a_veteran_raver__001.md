Friend: Yo! I'm finally locking in my trip for EDC 2026. I've been to a few shows, but the Speedway is a beast. Any pro tips for survival?

Vet: Let's get it! First rule: assume your phone is a brick. Cell service is basically non-existent, and SMS can be delayed by hours. Even Snap Maps are a coin toss. You have to plan your life offline.

Friend: Damn, okay. So how do we actually find each other if the group splits?

Vet: Do NOT say "meet me by the daisy"—that thing moves. Pick a fixed, unmistakable spot. I always tell my crew to use the lockers area because it's well-lit and contained. Set a check-in time every 1-2 hours and keep that spot the same for all three nights.

Friend: Good call. What if I actually get lost and can't find anyone?

Vet: Don't panic. It's a rave; people are generally welcoming and will help you out. As long as you have your shuttle bracelet, you have a guaranteed ride back to your hotel, so you're never truly stranded.

Friend: Speaking of the shuttle, is it worth it or should I just Uber?

Vet: Shuttle is the only way to go if you're staying on the Strip. You buy the pass beforehand and keep that bracelet on your wrist all weekend. It picks up at 10 specific hotels and runs all night. It's your fail-safe.

Friend: Got it. What's the move for the bathrooms? I'm dreading those porta-potty lines.

Vet: Easy. Everyone crowds the front of the rows. Just walk to the very back of the array—the back-row stalls are almost always empty or have way shorter waits. Also, bring your own TP and hand sanitizer; Insomniac's supply usually vanishes halfway through night one. Wet wipes are a lifesaver.

Friend: I definitely want to catch a sunrise set. Which night is the best bet?

Vet: You have to do it at kineticFIELD at least once. Whether it's Above & Beyond or Armin van Buuren, it's a core memory. Go for it on Night 2. If you do it Night 3, you're hitting Monday morning Vegas traffic on the way out, which is a nightmare.

Friend: I'm planning to stay until the end most nights. Is the exit that bad?

Vet: If you stay until 5:30am, be prepared to spend 1-2 hours just waiting in the shuttle line. If you aren't feeling the endgame, peace out before 4:30am to beat the brutal rush.

Friend: I'm bringing some kandi and a costume. Anything else I should know about the vibe?

Vet: Just stick to the PLUR code. Be mindful of crowd flow—don't sit in the middle of the walkway. Be polite, compliment the wild costumes, and keep an eye out for anyone who looks unwell. If you've got extra kandi, trade it! It's the best way to make new friends.
