### Logistics and Communications
Cellular service at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway is unreliable, frequently resulting in significant delays for SMS messages and inconsistent functionality for location-sharing applications such as Snapchat. Attendees are advised to establish fixed meeting points and scheduled check-in times every one to two hours. The lockers area is recommended as a primary meeting location due to its lighting and contained layout.

### Transportation
The EDC shuttle service provides transport between the venue and 10 designated hotels on the Las Vegas Strip. Access requires a pre-purchased shuttle pass, which is provided as a wristband. While shuttles operate throughout the night, significant congestion occurs after 4:30 AM. Attendees departing at the close of the event (approximately 5:30 AM) may experience shuttle wait times of one to two hours.

### Venue Facilities and Scheduling
Porta-potty arrays typically experience shorter wait times in the rear rows. Supplies of toilet paper and hand sanitizer provided by the event organizer, Insomniac, generally deplete by the midpoint of the first night. 

The Kinetic Field hosts "sunrise sets" featuring artists such as Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren. Due to Monday morning traffic patterns in Las Vegas, the second night is identified as the optimal time for these sets compared to the third night.

### Social Conduct and Etiquette
The festival adheres to an unofficial code of conduct known as PLUR (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect). Social customs include the trading of "kandi" (beaded bracelets) and the offering of compliments regarding costumes. Attendees are encouraged to maintain crowd flow by avoiding sitting in walkways and monitoring the well-being of other guests.
