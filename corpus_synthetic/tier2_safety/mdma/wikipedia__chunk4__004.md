# Electric Daisy Carnival (Safety and Medical)

## Overview
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) employs a multi-tiered medical and safety infrastructure designed to manage heat-related illness, substance impairment, and psychological distress. The event utilizes a combination of professional medical staff, designated first-aid stations, and a peer-support network known as Ground Control.

## Medical Response Tiers
Medical assistance is categorized by location and severity of the incident:

### On-Site Festival Grounds
For incidents occurring within the main festival area, attendees are directed to Ground Control personnel or first-aid tents. These stations provide rapid intervention and are designed to manage medical issues without immediate law enforcement involvement.

### Camp EDC and Off-Grounds
For emergencies occurring within the Camp EDC camping areas or outside the venue perimeter, the event utilizes a 24-hour medical post. In cases of critical escalation, emergency services (911) are contacted for transport and advanced life support.

## Impairment Management Protocols
The management of impaired attendees is based on the presentation of symptoms:

*   **Hyperthermia and Dehydration:** Individuals exhibiting sweating and coherence are treated with shade, seated rest, water, and electrolytes.
*   **Psychological Distress:** Attendees experiencing anxiety or panic are moved to quieter, cooler environments. Protocol emphasizes reassurance over logical argumentation.
*   **Physical Exhaustion:** Individuals exhibiting instability are provided with seated rest, hydration, electrolytes, and food, provided they are not vomiting.
*   **Interpersonal Conflict:** Conflicts occurring during peak substance impairment are managed by separating the involved parties and delaying major decision-making.

### Contraindicated Actions
Certain common interventions are discouraged due to health risks:
*   **Caffeine:** Strong coffee is deemed ineffective for impairment.
*   **Cold Showers:** These are avoided to prevent cold shock in individuals with elevated heart rates.
*   **Physical Exertion:** "Walking off" impairment is discouraged as it may exacerbate heat-related stress.
*   **Unmonitored Sleep:** Leaving an impaired individual to sleep without supervision is prohibited due to the risk of airway compromise.
*   **Peer Decision-Making:** Medical decisions should not be made by other impaired attendees.

## Positioning and Airway Safety
To prevent aspiration during unconsciousness or severe impairment, specific positioning is mandated:
*   **Upright Seating:** Impaired individuals who cannot stand should remain seated upright rather than lying flat on their backs to prevent choking during vomiting.
*   **Recovery Position:** If a patient must lie down, they are placed on their side with the top knee bent and the head tilted to maintain a clear airway.

## Logistics and Infrastructure
The festival layout incorporates various thematic zones and stages, including **kineticFIELD**, **circuitGROUNDS**, and **neonGARDENS**, with medical assets distributed to ensure response times are minimized across the grounds.
