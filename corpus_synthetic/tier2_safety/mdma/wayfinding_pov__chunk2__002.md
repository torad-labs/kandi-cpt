*(Sound of distant bass and crowds cheering. The narrator is walking, breathing heavily, voice slightly raised to be heard over the music.)*

"Alright, welcome to EDC 2026. I’ve been doing this for years, so let me get you oriented. Follow me. 

Right now, we’re walking from **kineticFIELD** east toward **cosmicMEADOW**. As we head this way, you’ll see the merchant area on your left and the carnival rides on your right. While we walk, let’s talk about the 'street ecstasy' situation, because it's a minefield. 

See those people passing out pills? Just remember: 'ecstasy' is just a street term for anything represented as MDMA. Those pressed pills are notoriously unreliable. They could be mixed with caffeine, ephedrine, amphetamines, MDA, MDE, DXM, or even DOB. Some don't have any psychoactive content at all.

Now, look ahead—we're passing the **circuitGROUNDS** area. You can hear the heavy bass from the artists playing here. If you’re planning on using, you need to find the DanceSafe booth. They sell the reagent kits and fentanyl test strips you need. But a veteran tip: remember the 'great MDMA shortage' from 2009 to 2017? We had pills that were 90% PMMA and 10% MDMA. Because PMMA doesn't react with Marquis reagent, the small amount of MDMA made them 'pass.' Reagents are red flags, not green lights. A pass doesn't mean it's pure.

*(The narrator stops as they reach a crossroads near the center of the grounds)*

Okay, we're almost at **cosmicMEADOW** now. Before we hit the stage, we need to talk safety. If you're on MAOIs—like Nardil, Parnate, or if you've done Ayahuasca—do NOT take MDMA. And if you're on the protease inhibitor Ritonavir, it could be life-threatening. 

Avoid mixing it with strong stimulants like coke or meth, or heavy alcohol—you'll overheat and dehydrate way too fast in this desert heat. And be careful with other serotonergics like 5-HTP or tramadol. Now, about SSRIs: usually, they just block the MDMA from working. Whatever you do, don't try to 'break through' the blunting by taking massive doses; that's where it gets dangerous.

*(Walking again, passing a hydration station)*

Quick check on your health. If you've got heart issues, high blood pressure, a history of stroke, glaucoma, or liver/kidney disorders, you're at a much higher risk. Same goes for hypoglycemia or a family history of psychotic disorders. Also, keep in mind that about 1-10% of us are 'slow metabolizers'—low P450 2D6 enzyme levels. If that's you, you're way more sensitive and need a much lower dose.

*(The narrator arrives at the entrance of cosmicMEADOW, the music swelling)*

We're here. One last thing before we dive into the set: plan for the aftermath. When the peak wears off, some people hit a 'crash'—that's often just the sadness of the experience ending. Don't redose just to stave off the crash; it only makes it worse. 

And look at your calendar for next week. You need two days: one for the peak and one full recovery day. Then, be prepared for the 'week-after blues.' For the next 5 to 7 days, you might feel irritable or antisocial. You might even have a meltdown over something tiny. It happens to some of us, not all of us, but just be ready for it.

Alright, let's get in there. Enjoy the music."
