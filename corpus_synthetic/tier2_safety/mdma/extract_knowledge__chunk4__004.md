### First Aid and Impairment Management at EDC

**Symptom-Specific Interventions**
Immediate care for attendees varies by presentation. Individuals experiencing heat-related stress (hot, sweaty, but coherent) require shade, electrolyte-enhanced water, and seated rest. Those presenting with anxiety or panic while remaining oriented should be moved to a cooler, quieter environment for reassurance; logical argumentation is discouraged. Physical exhaustion or instability (wobbliness) is treated with seated rest, hydration, electrolytes, and food, provided the individual can maintain ingestion. Interpersonal conflicts occurring during peak impairment are managed by separating the parties, providing water, and deferring major decision-making.

**Contraindicated Actions**
Certain common interventions are identified as ineffective or hazardous for impaired individuals. The administration of strong coffee and the use of cold showers—which risk inducing cold shock in those with elevated heart rates—are not recommended. "Walking off" symptoms may exacerbate heat-related issues. Unmonitored sleep is dangerous due to potential airway compromise. Additionally, decision-making should not be delegated to other impaired individuals.

**Patient Positioning**
Individuals too impaired to stand must be kept in an upright seated position. Lying flat on the back is contraindicated due to the risk of aspiration if the individual vomits while unconscious. If a prone position is necessary, the recovery position must be used: the individual is placed on their side with the top knee bent and the head tilted to maintain a clear airway.

**Emergency Protocols**
Medical escalation protocols depend on the attendee's location. At Camp EDC or off-grounds, individuals should call 911 or proceed to the 24-hour Camp EDC medical post. Within the festival grounds, the primary points of contact are Ground Control and first-aid tents. These onsite resources are prioritized over 911 for faster response times and the avoidance of law enforcement involvement.
