Q: When does post-MDMA depression typically start and how long does it usually last?
A: It often starts on the second day after the experience and lasts up to 5 days.

Q: What is the minimum amount of time that must pass after the last MDMA dose before taking 5-HTP?
A: A minimum of 8 hours.

Q: What is the risk of taking 5-HTP before or during an MDMA roll?
A: It could induce mild to moderate serotonin syndrome.

Q: According to the text, what is the most reliable mitigation for post-roll blues?
A: Spacing your rolls.

Q: How does using MDMA two days in a row affect the experience?
A: It is likely to lead to a greatly diminished experience the second day.

Q: What are the reported cognitive and emotional consequences for heavy/frequent MDMA users in some studies?
A: Reductions in memory and increases in depression and anxiety.

Q: Is MDMA physically dependence-forming?
A: No, it is not physically dependence-forming, though it has the potential to be psychologically addicting.

Q: Which physical symptoms indicate a friend needs immediate medical attention according to Insomniac's escalation criteria?
A: Vomiting, seizures, pale skin or bluish tinge to lips/fingertips, abnormal heart rate or breathing, abnormal body temperature, severe headache, delusions, hallucinations, paranoia, pupils that don't react to light changes, confusion, stupor, coma, or being unable to be roused.

Q: Who can a festival attendee contact for immediate medical help besides first-aid tents?
A: Ground Control (purple shirts) or any Insomniac staff.

Q: What is the explicit festival policy regarding seeking medical help?
A: "You will not get in trouble for seeking medical help."
