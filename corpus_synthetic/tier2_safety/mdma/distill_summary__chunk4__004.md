**Impaired Friend Care:**
*   **Hot/Sweaty:** Shade, electrolytes, water, seated rest.
*   **Anxious/Panicked:** Quiet, cool space; reassure without using logic/argument.
*   **Exhausted/Wobbly:** Seated rest, water, electrolytes, food (if tolerated).
*   **Conflict:** Separate parties, provide water, listen; avoid major decisions.

**Ineffective/Dangerous Interventions:** Strong coffee, cold showers (risk of cold shock), "walking it off," unmonitored sleep (airway risk), or allowing other impaired persons to decide care.

**Positioning:** Keep upright. Never lie flat on back due to vomiting/asphyxiation risk. Use recovery position (side, top knee bent, head tilted) if lying down is necessary.

**Emergency Contact:**
*   **Camp EDC/Off-grounds:** Call 911 or visit 24-hour Camp EDC medical post.
*   **Festival Grounds:** Contact Ground Control or first-aid tents for fast, equipped, non-police assistance.
