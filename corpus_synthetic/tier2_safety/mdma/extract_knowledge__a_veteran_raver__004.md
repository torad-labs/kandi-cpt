### Substance Impairment and Heat Management

Management of impaired individuals varies by symptom presentation. For those experiencing hyperthermia (hot/sweaty) while remaining conscious and coherent, protocol requires immediate relocation to shade, seated rest, and the administration of water and electrolytes. Individuals experiencing anxiety or panic while remaining oriented should be moved to a cooler, quieter environment; reassurance is prioritized over logical argumentation. Physical exhaustion or instability requires seated rest, hydration, electrolytes, and food, provided the individual can maintain oral intake. In cases of interpersonal conflict during peak substance effects, parties should be separated and hydrated; definitive decision-making should be deferred.

### Contraindicated Interventions

Certain common responses to impairment are ineffective or hazardous. Caffeine consumption and "walking off" the effects do not mitigate impairment and may exacerbate cardiovascular stress or heat-related illness. Cold showers are contraindicated due to the risk of cold shock in individuals with elevated heart rates. Unmonitored sleep is dangerous if the airway becomes compromised. Furthermore, decision-making should not be delegated to other impaired individuals.

### Positioning and Airway Safety

Individuals unable to stand or walk must be maintained in an upright seated position. Lying flat on the back (supine) is prohibited due to the risk of aspiration during vomiting, a primary cause of fatality. If a prone position is necessary, the recovery position must be used: the individual is placed on their side with the top knee bent and the head tilted to ensure an open airway.

### Emergency Protocols

Medical escalation protocols differ by location. At Camp EDC or off-grounds, emergencies require a 911 call or transport to the 24-hour Camp EDC medical post. Within the festival grounds, the primary points of care are Ground Control and first-aid tents. These stations provide rapid intervention and medical equipment without involving law enforcement.
