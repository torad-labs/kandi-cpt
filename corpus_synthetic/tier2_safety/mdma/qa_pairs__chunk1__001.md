A: It generally takes 20-60 minutes, although it can take as long as 2 hours depending on how recently the person has eaten.

A: Peak effects typically last 90-120 minutes.

Q: What is the total duration of the primary effects of MDMA?
A: The total primary effects duration is 3-5 hours.

Q: How long is the additional residual period after MDMA's primary effects fade?
A: The residual period lasts 2-6 hours.

Q: What is considered a standard oral dose of MDMA?
A: A standard oral dose is between 80 and 125 mg.

Q: What is the general range of MDMA contained in most good-quality pressed pills?
A: They generally contain 80-120 mg.

A: A single redose of one-third to one-half of the original dose should be taken around 90 minutes after the first dose.

A: Space rolls by a minimum of 3-6 months.

A: A few weeks.

Q: What may a severe, splitting headache on MDMA signify?

Q: What are the two deadly hydration extremes associated with MDMA use?
A: Too little water leads to hypernatremia (dehydration crisis) and too much water leads to hyponatremia (water poisoning/sodium dilution).
