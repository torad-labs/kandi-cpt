Hydration management at Electric Daisy Carnival 2026 requires the balance of water and electrolytes to prevent hyponatremia, a condition also known as "water poisoning" caused by excessive water intake without sufficient salt. To mitigate this risk, the festival provides Electrolit hydration sticks at designated water stations.

Recommended fluid intake is capped at 800 mL (27 oz) per hour, with an ideal target of 480 mL (16 oz) per hour mixed with electrolytes.

Protocols for managing over-stimulation, overheating, or substance overdose include moving the affected individual to a shaded area, ensuring they are seated, and administering water and electrolytes. Medical escalation is indicated by the presence of vomiting, seizures, bluish skin, abnormal heart rate or breathing, abnormal body temperature, delusions, hallucinations, or unresponsiveness. In such cases, individuals are directed to the nearest first-aid tent or to signal Ground Control personnel.
