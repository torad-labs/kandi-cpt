Friend: I'm so hyped for EDC 2026, but since it's my first time, I'm a bit nervous about the substances side of things. I bought some test strips—do those actually work?

Vet: They're a great start! Just remember that strip testing (immunoassay) is basically for "yes/no" answers. Like, if you use a fentanyl strip, it'll tell you if fentanyl or its analogs are present. If you use an amphetamine strip, it’ll react to anything in that class, including MDMA or meth.

Friend: Oh okay, so as long as I use them, I'll know exactly what's in my stuff?

Vet: Not exactly. Strips can't tell you the exact purity, the potency, or if there are inactive cuts like baking soda. They also can't confirm multiple drugs in one mixed sample. Only a professional lab can do that. If you want a real deep dive, check out mail-in services like Transparency Testing or browse the historical data on DrugsData.

Friend: Got it. What about weed or mushrooms? Can I use those same strips?

Vet: Nope. Those tools are for synthetic compounds. Organic plant matter or fungi are almost impossible to test with at-home kits. For mushrooms, the most important thing is identifying the species so you don't eat something poisonous—I usually suggest checking communities like The Shroomery for that. As for weed, lab-confirmed fentanyl contamination is super rare, but be careful with head-shop products since they can have pesticides or heavy metals.

Friend: That's good to know. I'm planning on hitting the big stages, but are there any drug combos I should absolutely avoid?

Vet: Definitely. Avoid mixing alcohol with ketamine, GHB, or opioids because of respiratory depression. Also, if you're on SSRIs or MAOIs, be extremely careful with MDMA; you risk serotonin syndrome, and SSRIs actually blunt the MDMA effect, which leads some people to dangerously redose.

Friend: I was thinking about mixing a bit of everything to keep the energy up for the late-night sets. Is that a bad idea?

Vet: Especially at EDC, yes. Mixing cocaine and alcohol creates cocaethylene in your liver, which is way harder on your heart. Even worse is mixing cocaine with MDMA. When you combine two stimulants with the desert heat and dancing at kineticFIELD or circuitGROUNDS, you're looking at a massive risk for hypertension and hyperthermia. 

Friend: I didn't even think about the heat. Is it really that different from a club?

Vet: Huge difference. The Vegas desert is a force multiplier. Since the temp often stays above 80°F well into the morning, anything that raises your body temp or suppresses your thirst—like stimulants or alcohol—pushes you toward heatstroke way faster. 

Friend: Man, I'm glad I asked. I'll make sure to stay hydrated. Anything else?

Vet: Just watch your sleep. If you're pushing through multiple days on stimulants without sleeping, your risk of psychosis spikes sharply after 48 hours of wakefulness. Get some actual rest between the madness!
