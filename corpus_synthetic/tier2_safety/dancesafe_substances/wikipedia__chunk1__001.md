# Lysergic Acid Diethylamide (LSD)

## Overview
Lysergic acid diethylamide (LSD) is a potent psychedelic substance known for inducing significant alterations in perception, mood, and cognitive processes. While frequently associated with visual distortions, the primary effects often involve a fundamental shift in the perception of the self and the surrounding environment. Familiar locations may appear alien, and users may experience difficulty maintaining conversations or interpreting the emotional states of others. These experiences are frequently accompanied by heightened emotional sensitivity, which may result in cathartic, transformative, or distressing psychological states.

### Visual Effects
Visual experiences vary among users, ranging from mild distortions of color and shape to comprehensive alterations in the appearance of people and spaces. Despite common media portrayals, "true hallucinations"—the perception of objects or entities that are entirely absent—are reported to be rare.

## Administration and Dosage
LSD is most commonly administered orally. The substance is typically distributed via "blotter" (small pieces of absorbent paper), liquid drops, gel tabs, or infused candies.

Due to its extreme potency, a standard dose typically ranges between 100 and 200 micrograms (mcg). A single unit of blotter or one drop of liquid generally contains between 80 and 150 mcg, though concentrations vary. Precise dosage cannot be determined without laboratory analysis, as street-level approximations are often unreliable.

## Safety and Harm Reduction
The psychological outcome of LSD use is heavily influenced by "set" (the user's internal mental state, mood, and expectations) and "setting" (the physical and social environment). Supportive environments and positive mental states are associated with a lower risk of adverse experiences.

### Psychological Risks
LSD can induce extreme anxiety or panic. Individuals with a personal or family history of psychotic or mood disorders are considered to be at a higher risk for psychological instability. Additionally, some users may develop Hallucinogen Persisting Perception Disorder (HPPD), a condition characterized by long-lasting perceptual disturbances.

### Usage Precautions
*   **Onset:** Users are advised to wait at least three hours before administering additional doses to avoid overlapping effects.
*   **Identification:** Pure LSD is generally tasteless. The presence of a bitter or metallic taste may indicate the presence of other substances.

# Cocaine

## Overview
Cocaine is a stimulant alkaloid derived from the leaves of the *Erythroxylon coca* plant. It is characterized by a bitter taste and a local anesthetic (numbing) effect. The subjective effects include increased confidence, alertness, euphoria, and talkativeness. While its effects are similar to those of amphetamines, cocaine has a significantly shorter duration of action.

## Administration and Dosage
Cocaine is primarily consumed via two methods:
*   **Insufflation:** Powder cocaine is typically snorted in small amounts ("bumps" or "lines"). A typical dose ranges from 30 to 70 mg. Effects manifest within minutes, peak between 15 and 30 minutes, and last for a total of 30 to 60 minutes.
*   **Inhalation:** Cocaine in "crack" form is smoked, with typical doses ranging from 15 to 50 mg.

Due to the rapid offset of its effects, users frequently develop a tolerance, leading to an increase in dosage over the course of a single session or over long-term use.
