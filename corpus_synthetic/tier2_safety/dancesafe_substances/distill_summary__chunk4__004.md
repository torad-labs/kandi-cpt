# Chunk: 4

Avoid hyponatremia ("water poisoning") by avoiding excessive plain water intake. Limit consumption to <800 mL (27 oz)/hour; target 480 mL (16 oz)/hour mixed with electrolytes. Utilize Electrolit hydration sticks provided at water stations.

For over-stimulation, overheating, or substance over-consumption: move person to shade, seat them, and provide water/electrolytes. Monitor for escalation signs: vomiting, seizures, bluish skin, abnormal heart rate/breathing, abnormal temperature, delusions/hallucinations, or unresponsiveness. If escalation occurs, go to the nearest first-aid tent or alert Ground Control. Medical assistance is provided without penalty.
