Location
Location of Sparkling Skylands in
Kanto
.
The
Sparkling Skylands
(Japanese:
キラキラうきしまの街
Sparkling Floating Island Town
) is the final unlockable area in
Pokémon Pokopia
, only unlockable after befriending
Kyogre
in
Withered Wasteland
,
Raikou
in
Bleak Beach
, and
Volcanion
in
Rocky Ridges
. It is what remains of
Saffron City
after a series of catastrophic
weather
anomalies ravaged the
world
and forced
humans
to take refuge in space.
Ditto
arrives in the Sparkling Skylands through the
gate
from either Bleak Beach or Rocky Ridges, accompanied by
Professor Tangrowth
.
The Sparkling Skylands is connected to Rocky Ridges to the west, Withered Wasteland to the east, and Bleak Beach to the south through gates. The gate to Withered Wasteland is blocked from the Sparkling Skylands' side, making it inaccessible from Withered Wasteland until this blockage has been removed.

Places of interest
Skyland Pokémon Center
The Skyland
Pokémon Center
is initially a pile of rubble on a stone tile platform, having been torn down by extreme
weather
. It is eventually rebuilt by Ditto and a group of other Pokémon, after which a
note
can be found on the floor inside, alongside a counter with a
cash register
, a
3D printer
, a healing machine, and some furniture.
Tinkmaster's tower
Tinkmaster
has built a tower of junk left behind by humans, with a room at the top.
Ditto
meets Tinkmaster outside this room after Tinkmaster is sent flying out through the doors by an explosion inside.
Big building
The big building sitting on one of the islands is the ruins of the
Silph Co.
building. Tinkmaster will ask Ditto to help her rebuild this building.
High-rise ruins
To the east, just infront of the
gate
to
Withered Wasteland
, is a neighborhood of ruined high-rise buildings.
Gardevoir
is also found here.
Underground office
An underground office appears in the Sparkling Skylands after
every available Pokémon
have been registered in Ditto's
Pokédex
. Inside is a
printer
that lets Ditto print out a
diploma
. Unlocking this office also allows Ditto to learn the
recipes
for the
shutter
,
card reader
, and printer.
Demographics
While there are no longer any
humans
living here, the Sparkling Skylands is inhabited by a handful of Pokémon before
Ditto
starts making new
habitats
.
Not counting Ditto and
Professor Tangrowth
, the Sparkling Skylands is initially inhabited by six Pokémon:
Rookidee
Dragonite
Tinkmaster
Gardevoir
Poliwrath
Gyarados

Hacker's confessions #6
Diary Entries
"In order to escape the disaster, humanity would
temporarily take refuge in space while Pokémon
would be safely stored in a computer system.
This was the general overview of the plan.
But no computer system will keep running forever.
The foundation planned to send a select few
members back to the planet at regular intervals to
perform ongoing maintenance.
But for humanity to survive in space for any length of
time, it would take a tremendous amount of energy.
I calculated that even a slight deviation from the
original plan would result in insufficient energy to
return, leaving humankind stranded in space for
dozens, or even hundreds, of years.
If that happened, the essential system protecting the
Pokémon would eventually cease to function.
So I decided to add a little touch of my own to the
system’s programming.
First, it will continuously check the weather data
across the globe in search of environments that can
prove even slightly habitable to Pokémon.
Next, if maintenance is not performed for a long
period of time and the system begins to approach
its limits, it will release the Pokémon most suited
to those environments.
Of course, ideally humans will return someday and
live alongside Pokémon once more.
But on the off chance humanity never returns from
space...I think releasing the Pokémon into a wild
world rather than leaving them asleep in a computer
would be a happier outcome.
It may sound strange coming from the program’s
creator, but I hope it never has to run."

Poké Life Dev Diary #5
Magazines
"In a most unfortunate turn of events,
Poké Life
never
received a chance to be put to use.
The drastic deterioration of the global environment
continues, and the plan to evacuate all of humanity
into space has finally been put into action.
Efforts have begun to gather as many Pokémon as
possible from all over the world and send them into
the
Pokémon Center
system for safekeeping.
I was unable to make my dream a reality, but there is
at least one silver lining in all of this.
Even after we humans leave this planet, the Pokémon
Center system will remain active in order to keep all
of the Pokémon inside safe.
The system will also keep a record of environmental
data around each Pokémon Center. The framework
we developed for Poké Life will be used for this.
The recorded data will be analyzed by research teams
set to return from space at regular intervals.
They say this is how we will determine when it’s safe
for all of humanity to return to the planet together.
My time here is short. I must soon go to space myself.
My new dream will be of the day when humans and
Pokémon can once again live together on this planet."
