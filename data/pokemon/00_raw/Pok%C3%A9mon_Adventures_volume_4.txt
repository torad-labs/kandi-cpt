Blurb
Pokémon trainer Red goes off on a training challenge and...
never comes back!
But a tired and tattered Pikachu manages to return home by himself. A mysterious young trainer in yellow befriends Pikachu, and together they set out to find our missing hero!
And watch out for Team Rocket, Yellow Caballero... Could they be behind Red's mysterious disappearance?

Trivia

Errors
Yellow says that
Pika
is weak against
Water-type
Pokémon, even though
Electric-types
like Pikachu are strong against Water Pokémon.
In the
Brazilian Portuguese
translation,
Silph Co.
is called
Sylph Co.
on the back cover.
VIZ Media first edition
Experience points are incorrectly named "Number."
VIZ Media second edition
On the "main characters" page, all of the
Gym Leaders
are all labelled incorrectly. Brock is identified as Lt. Surge, Misty is identified as Koga,  Erika is identified as Sabrina and
Blaine
is identified as Giovanni.
The same page also has
Green
's
Blastoise
wrongly translated as
Squirtle
.
Translation edits
In the VIZ Media first edition as well as
The Best of Pokémon Adventures: Yellow
,
Cloystered
is split into two chapters - "Cloystered" and "Raging Rattata".
In the VIZ Media second edition the
Jynx
on the back cover was altered from its original, changing its original black design to the current purple one. This is likely due to the
controversy surrounding the character
.
This change was also reproduced in more recent Japanese reprints, although with a different shading.
