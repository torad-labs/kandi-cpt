Information
Mythical Island as seen on
Aerodactyl ex
Mythical Island
is the first themed booster pack released through
Pokémon Trading Card Game Pocket
. With a tropical island setting, this expansion focuses on
Mythical Pokémon
such as
Mew
and
Celebi
, which appear as
Pokémon ex
. More characters from
Generation I
also make their card debuts in this expansion. In total, Mythical Island contains 86 cards, including five new Pokémon ex and five new Trainer cards; it also marks the first appearance of Pokémon from Generations
II
and
IV
in the game.
Many of the expansion's
Themed Collections
allude to locations around the island, and several of the new illustrations featured within them—including
Joltik
,
Stonjourner
,
Weezing
, and
Chatot
—feature cameos from Mew, as does the immersive illustration for
Celebi ex
.
Mythical Island was released worldwide on December 17, 2024.
Setting
The island can be seen from above in
Aerodactyl ex
's special illustration rare. Areas called Untouched Forest, Tranquil Lake, Scorching Wilderness, Forgotten Ruins, and Tree of Repose are depicted in the cards that make up their respective Themed Collections as well as in the cover art unlocked as a reward for completing them. There may also be an unnamed settlement as depicted in the illustration rares of
Dedenne
and
Marshadow
.
