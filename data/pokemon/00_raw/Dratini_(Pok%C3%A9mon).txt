Biology
A Dratini shedding its skin
Dratini is a
reptilian
Pokémon
with a blue serpentine body and a white underside. It has white, three-pronged fins on the sides of its head and a white bump on its forehead, which is its horn growing. Above its round, white snout are oval, purple eyes.
Dratini's life energy is constantly building so it is always growing, and can reach lengths of over six feet (2 meters). As it grows, it sheds its skin regularly; it hides behind a rapid waterfall during the process to protect itself, since the new skin is soft in texture. The shed skin can be tanned and used to create luxury boots. Dratini has been referred as the "Mirage Pokémon", because its existence was originally thought to be nothing more than a myth. Dratini was first discovered by a fisherman who spent 10 hours attempting to capture it. A small colony has also been found underwater, further confirming its existence. Dratini live
near or in bodies of water
with a rapid flow, such as the pools at the bottom of waterfalls. It lives a quiet life underwater, eating any food that sinks down to the bottom.
As mentioned in
Pokémon Sleep
, Dratini's sleeping habits used to be a mystery due to how rare it was.
[
1
]
However, in recent years, Dratini's sleep styles have been discovered and well documented. This is due to trainers who have obtained Dratini and observed its sleeping habits. The four different sleep styles discovered are called
Coiled Sleep
,
Wriggly Sleep
,
Uncoiled Sleep
, and
Atop-Belly Sleep
.
In
Generation II
, Dratini and its evolved forms were the
only known Pokémon
capable of learning the move
Outrage
, but other Pokémon could still inherit it via
breeding
.

Origin
Dratini appears to be based on
sea serpents
or
ryu
, traditional
Japanese dragons
or
Chinese dragons
who bring good luck. Its constant growth is similar to that of snakes, who grow as long as they live. Additionally, its reclusive nature, aquatic habitat, and the fact that, for a long time, it was considered a myth are reminiscent of the
Loch Ness Monster
.
Dratini may also be based on the Imoogi, a lesser dragon from Korean folklore. An Imoogi is a giant serpent seen as a “dragon in training,” usually living in rivers, caves, and lakes. Before reaching its final form, it must undergo a long period of purification and trials, surviving for a thousand years. During this time, it observes the sky and waits for a falling jewel. If it obtains a Yeouiju (a mystical orb), it can finally become a benevolent celestial dragon (Yong), capable of flying through the heavens and summoning storms. Imoogi are intelligent beings capable of sensing and understanding human emotions.
