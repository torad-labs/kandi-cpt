Location of Rocky Ridges in
Kanto
.
Rocky Ridges
(Japanese:
ゴツゴツやまの街
Rugged Mountain Town
) is one of two areas in
Pokémon Pokopia
that can be unlocked after meeting
Kyogre
in
Withered Wasteland
, the other being
Bleak Beach
. It is what remains of
Pewter City
after a series of catastrophic
weather
anomalies ravaged the
world
and forced
humans
to take refuge in space.
Ditto
arrives in Rocky Ridges through the
gate
from Withered Wasteland, accompanied by
Professor Tangrowth
.
Rocky Ridges is connected to the
Sparkling Skylands
to the east, and
Withered Wasteland
to the south-east through gates.

Places of interest
Ridge Pokémon Center
The Ridge
Pokémon Center
is initially a pile of rubble on a stone platform, having been torn down by extreme
weather
. It is eventually rebuilt by Ditto and a group of other Pokémon, after which a
note
can be found on the floor inside, alongside a counter with a
cash register
, a
3D printer
, a healing machine, and some furniture.
Kitchen
Chef Dente
is first met in a storage room connected to a kitchen, buried under the hill across from the Pokémon Center, after rescuing her from a hanging
barrel
. In order to save Chef Dente,
Ditto
learns how to
cook
meals to power up its
moves
.
DJ Rotom's room
DJ Rotom
is first met after Ditto investigates a stereo speaker system in a room buried in the hill directly east of the Pokémon Center. This room is an extension of a tunnel in the abandoned mines underneath Rocky Ridges, this section leading to the ruins of the
Pewter Museum of Science
.
Pewter Museum of Science
The ruins of the Pewter Museum of Science can be found in the northern part of Rocky Ridges, either by
breaking
through the walls blocking access through the mines, or by climbing over the
ash
covered hills. Ditto can meet
Gimmighoul
here, with the
collect
allowing Ditto to trade
Rainbow Feathers
,
Silver Feathers
, or
appraised
large lost relics
for other items.
Demographics
While there are no longer any
humans
living here, Rocky Ridges is inhabited by a handful of Pokémon before
Ditto
starts making new
habitats
.
Not counting Ditto and
Professor Tangrowth
, Rocky Ridges is initially inhabited by two Pokémon:
Chef Dente
Gimmighoul
Additionally, some Pokémon appear without their habitat as Ditto completes requests:
DJ Rotom
