Although not much is known about him, it was revealed that Akihabara created the Pokémon Transfer System. He has blue hair and wears glasses with spirals on the lenses. He is also a little bit eccentric and is thought to be slightly insane. He created two
Porygon

Akihabara loaned his other Porygon to Ash and his friends when they went to the computer world in order to find and stop Team Rocket and return Akihabara's stolen Porygon prototype, Porygon Zero. Ash and friends used this Porygon to ride in the computer world, likewise to how Team Rocket used Porygon Zero to get around. Porygon battled Porygon Zero as a distraction so Ash and co. could remove the virus keeping the Poké Balls from transferring. They were all forced to flee the scene when a vaccine program was installed by a technician hired by Nurse Joy. After Team Rocket was taken out by the vaccine's laser blast, Porygon went back and saved them. However, Porygon was much slower with the added weight, they all managed to get out of the computer world just before the vaccine destroyed the portal between the two worlds. Both Porygon and Porygon Zero were returned to Akihabara.
Porygon's known moves are
Conversion
and
Tackle
