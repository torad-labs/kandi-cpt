Unlike the historic
Naranja Academy
S
/
Uva Academy
V
, Blueberry Academy was established in recent years somewhere in
Unova
's ocean, being accessed through an undersea tunnel. The academy's curriculum puts a particular emphasis on
Pokémon battling
, with all the students and staff members specializing in
Double Battles
. Additionally, most of its building is located under the ocean, including the classrooms and the famous
Terarium
. The school uses its own currency, referred to as
Blueberry Points
, or BP for short.
Following
Briar
's research into the
Terastal phenomenon
in
Paldea
and
Kitakami
, she created the Terarium Core, a huge glass orb hanging in the ceilling of the Terarium. Filled with Paldean soil and water from Kitakami's
Crystal Pool
, it spreads
Terastal energy
across the academy, enabling students with
Tera Orbs
and certain
wild Pokémon
there to use the Terastal phenomenon. After the main story of
The Indigo Disk
has been completed, Briar upgrades the Terarium Core with crystals collected from the
Area Zero Underdepths
, causing wild
Stellar
Tera Type Pokémon to start appearing in the Terarium.
The player is offered into the academy as an exchange student by
Cyrano
.

The
League Club

League Club

BB League
(Japanese:
ブルベリーグ

and

A challenger must clear an Elite Four member's
Elite Trial
before being allowed to challenge them. Despite being an exchange student, the player is granted the right to challenge the BB League after a majority vote in support from the Elite Four and Champion, which also lets them bypass the lower ranks of the League and become immediately eligible to attempt the Elite Four's trials.

Origin
Blueberry Academy may be inspired by the Dragon Palace from the Japanese folktale
Urashima Tarō
. In the tale, the Dragon Palace is described as being underwater and having a garden where scenery from all four seasons could be seen at once.
[
2
]
Similarly, Blueberry Academy is located partially underwater with the Terarium biomes representing each of the four seasons. This is shown by the presence of all four forms of
Sawsbuck
within the Terarium, one per each biome (Spring: Savanna, Summer: Coastal, Autumn: Canyon, Winter: Polar).

But, to Urashima, more wonderful than the palace was the garden that surrounded it. Here was to be seen at one time the scenery of the four different seasons; the beauties of summer and winter, spring and autumn, were displayed to the wondering visitor at once.

First, when he looked to the east, the plum and cherry trees were seen in full bloom, the nightingales sang in the pink avenues, and butterflies flitted from flower to flower.

Looking to the south all the trees were green in the fullness of summer, and the day cicala and the night cricket chirruped loudly.

Looking to the west the autumn maples were ablaze like a sunset sky, and the chrysanthemums were in perfection.

Looking to the north the change made Urashima start, for the ground was silver white with snow, and trees and bamboos were also covered with snow and the pond was thick with ice.
