6★ EX

Trainer Lodge

6★ EX

Leaf also forms a sync pair with
Blastoise

6★ EX
since it first released, though this pair lacks an EX style.

Trivia
Leaf & Eevee were the first
sync pair
whose
sync move
is a
status move
.
Leaf & Eevee were the first non-
Alolan
sync pair (excluding the
player character
with
Solgaleo
) whose sync move is based on a
Z-Move
.
The name of Leaf's
Trainer move
is a reference to the title of
Pokémon Let's Go, Eevee!
.
Sygna Suit Leaf & Venusaur were the first fair-exclusive
Grass-type
sync pair.
Sygna Suit Leaf & Venusaur are one of only three sync pairs whose debut scout did not begin at 06:00 UTC, the others being
Nemona  & Pawmot
and
Sygna Suit Giovanni (Alt.) & Guzzlord
.
Leaf is the only Trainer in
Pokémon Masters EX
to use a Z-Move,
Mega Evolution
, and
Dynamax
.
Leaf was the one who gave
Copycat
her
Poké Doll
, as revealed in a conversation in the Pokémon Center.
In
Red
's
sync pair story
, Leaf mentions that she tried and failed to catch the
Snorlax
in
Kanto
that Red later caught.
Leaf & Eevee were the last 5★ sync pair in the general sync pair scout pool to have their sync grid expanded, excluding sync pairs released after April 5, 2021, the date of Leaf & Eevee's sync grid expansion.
Leaf's appearance in the
Victory Road
could be a reference to the
player character
becoming
Champion
in
Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen
.
All versions of Leaf use special voice-over lines upon entering a team that contains certain Trainers (regardless of their partner Pokémon) and victory with them. Those Trainers include
Red
(but not his 1996 version),
Elaine
,
Chase
,
Blue
, and
Professor Oak
.
In her Champion outfit's sync pair story, Leaf reveals that she caught her Moltres in Kanto.
Leaf's Clefable may have been originally caught at
Mt. Moon
as a
Clefairy
. In the
special event
Metronome Day
, Leaf says she used to search all over Mt. Moon in Kanto, in hopes of finding a Clefairy.
Origin
Leaf's design is based on her appearance in
Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen
. Leaf's Eevee may be based on the Eevee that can be obtained in
Celadon Mansion
. Eevee's
Dex
entry comes from
Pokémon Ultra Sun
.
Leaf's
Pokémon Adventures counterpart
is depicted with a Clefable. In addition,
Green
, a character similar to Leaf, uses a Clefable in
Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!
. Clefable's Dex entry comes from Pokémon Ultra Sun.
Blastoise is the final
Evolution
of
Squirtle
, one of the
first partner Pokémon
of
Kanto
in the
core series
games. Leaf's Pokémon Adventures counterpart is depicted with a
Blastoise
. In addition, Green, a character similar to Leaf, uses a Blastoise as her signature Pokémon in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!. Blastoise's Dex entry comes from
Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire
.
Venusaur is the final Evolution of
Bulbasaur
, one of the first partner Pokémon of Kanto in the core series games. Venusaur's Dex entry comes from Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire.
Moltres is a
Legendary Pokémon
that can be caught at
Mt. Ember
in Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen. In addition, Leaf's
Pokémon Adventures
counterpart is depicted with a
Moltres
. Moltres's Dex entry comes from Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire.
