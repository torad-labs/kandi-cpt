In the core series games
In the core series games, the player and their rival will begin their journey in their hometown, venturing onto the first routes from there, encountering the
Pokémon Professor
, and getting their
first partner Pokémon
. After entering the
Hall of Fame
, the player will start again from their hometown when the game is next loaded.

Pokémon the Series
Hometowns in
Pokémon the Series
are more diverse than in the games. Main characters and their rivals are not always limited to the towns specified in the games. Like many of the other settlements in the series, the hometowns are significantly larger than their game counterparts.
Kanto
Pallet Town
is the hometown to both
Ash
and
Gary
. Other residents include
Professor Oak
,
Delia
, and
Tracey
.
Pewter City
is the hometown of
Brock
,
Forrest
, their parents
Lola
and
Flint
, and
eight other siblings
.
Solidad
is also a resident of Pewter City.
Cerulean City
is the hometown of
Misty
and
her sisters
Daisy
,
Violet
, and
Lily
.
Vermilion City
is the hometown of
Goh
and
Chloe
.
Frodomar City
is a
Pokémon the Series
-exclusive location that is the hometown of
Ritchie
. It was mentioned only in the English dub.
Johto
New Bark Town
is the hometown of
Casey
,
Jimmy
,
Marina
,
Vincent
and
Lyra
.
Professor Elm
is also a resident there.
Ecruteak City
is the hometown of
Sakura
.
Silver Rock Isle
is a
Pokémon the Series
-exclusive location that is the hometown of
Robert
.
Hoenn
Littleroot Town
is the hometown of
Harrison
. Other notable residents include
Professor Birch
.
Petalburg City
is the hometown of
May
and
Max
, their
mother
Caroline
and their father
Norman
, the Gym Leader there.
Slateport City
is the hometown of
Harley
.
Mauville City
is the hometown of
Tyson
. Other notable residents would be the Gym Leader,
Wattson
, and his assistant,
Watt
.
Verdanturf Town
is the hometown of
Morrison
and
Timmy Grimm
.
Lilycove City
is the hometown of
Kelly
and
Katie
.
LaRousse City
is a
Pokémon the Series
-exclusive location that is the hometown of May's rival,
Drew
.
Sinnoh
Tower Tycoon
Palmer and
Top Coordinator
Johanna, two notable people of Twinleaf Town

is the hometown of
Dawn
, her mother
Johanna
, her rival
Kenny
,
Barry
, and his father
Palmer
. Other residents include
Izzy, Cara
, and

.
