Vermilion Forest
(Japanese:
クチバの森
Kuchiba Forest
) is an
animated series
-exclusive
forest
in the
Kanto
region
. It is located outside
Vermilion City
.
Ash
and
Goh
first visited Vermilion Forest in
Working My Way Back to Mew!
, when Goh was keen to catch more Pokémon and fill the
Pokédex
. Whilst there, Ash and Goh encountered a variety of Wild Pokémon with Goh managing to successfully capture all of Kanto's
Bug
-types.
Ash and Goh visited Vermilion Forest once more in
Trade, Borrow, and Steal!
, where they hoped to find a
Pinsir
that Goh could
trade
to
Kricketina Kylie
in exchange for her
Heracross
. After an encounter with
Team Rocket
, Goh was able to catch a Pinsir that he later traded for Heracross.
