Roshan City
(Japanese:
ローシャン
Rōshan
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in
Unova
. It is one of the settings of
Kyurem VS. The Sword of Justice
.
Roshan City is a sprawling city accessible by train. Underneath the city are disused subway tunnels, and one of the old stations has been transformed into a museum for old transportation devices.
A train track that leads to Roshan City connects it with
Windy Station
and probably other locations as well.
Ash
and
his friends
took the train to Roshan City and on their way met an injured
Keldeo
. When they arrived in Roshan City, they immediately took it to the city's
Pokémon Center
. The
Nurse Joy
there told them more about Keldeo and the
Swords of Justice
. After Keldeo was healed, Ash,
Iris
,
Cilan
took Keldeo outside and ate dinner with it.
Shortly afterwards, they were attacked by a group of
Cryogonal
, which were sent by
Kyurem
. A wild chase followed, continuing through the city's disused subway tunnels, until Ash and his friends managed to escape the city with a blimp from Roshan City's museum of old transportation devices.

Roshan City is modeled after various locations in
Tokyo
, such as the
special wards
of
Shinjuku
and
Shinagawa
along with the waterfront district of
Tennōzu Isle
[
1
]
. Director
Kunihiko Yuyama
had expressed a desire to incorporate reality into the film in light of the
2011 Tōhoku earthquake
, leading to Japan being chosen as a basis for the setting.
