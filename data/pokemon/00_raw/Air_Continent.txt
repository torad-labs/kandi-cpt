The
Air Continent
(Japanese:
風の大陸
Wind Continent
) is a continent of the
Pokémon world
in the
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series
. It was introduced in
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team
. There are two known settlements on the continent:
Pokémon Square
and
Baram Town
. The continent was not named until its appearance in
Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon
.
It is the setting of
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team
and
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Rescue Team DX
. It is the second continent explored in Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon, and is first visited during the main story when
Archen
accidentally buys a ticket to the Air Continent.
