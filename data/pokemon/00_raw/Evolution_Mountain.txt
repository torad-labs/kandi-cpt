Evolution Mountain
(Japanese:
進化山
Evolution Mountain
) is an
animated series
-exclusive mountain located in between the
Safari Zone
and
Cinnabar Island
. At its base lies
Stone Town
. Evolution Mountain is famous for the
Evolution stones
that can be found there.
