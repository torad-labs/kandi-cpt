The
Explorers
(Japanese:
エクスプローラーズ
Explorers
) are the main antagonists in
Pokémon Horizons: The Series
up to the
Rising Again
arc, led by
Gibeon
and, following his death,
Spinel
. Initially, the Explorers referred to a trio of century-old adventurers—
Lucius
,
Rystal
, and Gibeon—whose goal was to find
Laqua
, but underwent a major restructure by Gibeon, turning it into a global
villainous organization
whose goal is to obtain, monopolize, and extract the powers of the mysterious
Laquium
found at Laqua and use it for the benefit of
humans
and
Pokémon
. Under Spinel's tenure, the goal of the Explorers shifted towards global domination from the shadows via media manipulation and public reliance on his Laquium-based
Strong Sphere
project.
The Explorers have a strong connection to
Exceed
, a company founded by Gibeon that is used as a front to conduct research on Laquium.

History
Pre-series
The original Explorers:
Rystal
,
Lucius
, and
Gibeon
100 years prior to the events of
Pokémon Horizons: The Series
, the group known as Explorers was founded by
Lucius
,
Gibeon
, and
Rystal
as they journeyed together to find the Pokémon paradise,
Laqua
.
Eventually, the trio arrived at their destination and, musing over how such a bountiful environment was created, decided to venture in further deep. In the deepest parts of Laqua, they found a cluster of
Laquium
, a highly energetic substance in the form of pink crystals that was forcing the surrounding vegetation to overgrow, leaving the land barren. Lucius and Rystal immediately recognized the danger it posed and decided to leave Laqua, but Gibeon insisted on meddling with it and had his
White Zygarde
dig the cluster out, causing it to activate and begin growing uncontrollably. Despite the efforts of Zygarde and Lucius's
Six Heroes
, the Laquium kept on spreading and caused a fissure to open under Gibeon, swallowing him swole and leaving him presumed dead. At this moment,
Pagogo
, Rystal's
Terapagos
, transformed into its
Stellar Form
, shared its
power
with the Six Heroes, and sealed the Laquium, along with Lucius, within a giant energy barrier. As a result of this, Lucius got trapped in Laqua, Pagogo reverted into a dormant state, and the Six Heroes got scattered around the
world
, with Rystal being the only one to escape unscathed, holding onto the promise of protecting the future of
her and Lucius's daughter
.
Unbeknownst to everyone, however, Gibeon managed to survive due to Laquium's power and, three days later, stumbled out of Laqua and into
Pericarp Town
, where he had his injuries treated by one of the villagers. With Laquium extending his lifespan, Gibeon lived for the next hundred years, during which he established a base of operations near
Mount Pericarp
and reformed the Explorers into its modern-day iteration, aiming to obtain Pagogo and the Six Heroes in order to undo the sealing barrier and extract the crystals directly from the source. He also founded
Exceed
, through which he was able to study Laquium's properties.
At some point following the Explorers' restructure,
Hamber
joined them in gratitude for Gibeon's guidance during his time as an aimless adventurer. Now Gibeon's second-in-command and family butler, Hamber became responsible for recruiting and coaching the new generals of the organization:
Amethio
,
Coral
,
Sidian
, and
Chalce
, in that order. The remaining general,
Spinel
, was a brilliant Exceed researcher that was personally selected by Gibeon.
A year prior to the events of the series, Hamber approached
Diana
, his former traveling companion, and offered to aid her in her adventures, but this was actually an attempt to obtain her pendant, which was Pagogo in its dormant form. She, however, saw through his lies and escaped. Later, Hamber discovered that the pendant had been passed down to Diana's granddaughter,
Liko
, and assigned Amethio and his subordinates,
Zirc
and
Onia
, to retrieve it.

, Indi and Rubina with three other agents used the Super Strong Sphere on five of the Six Heroes to battle Liko, Roy, and Ult, as this was Spinel's plan to take down the Rising Volt Tacklers by using their allies against them. In the
next episode
, despite the Heroes knocking out most of Liko, Roy, Ult, and Amethio's Pokémon with help from Coral and Sidian, they ended up retreating after the effects of the Super Strong Spheres worn off on the five Pokémon.
In
HZ124
, the Explorers were confronted by the younger members of the Rising Volt Tacklers and Amethio in an attempt to rescue the remaining Six Heroes. However, Spinel planned for it as he set up a trap for them when they get inside their HQ. In the
next episode
, the Explorers lose the remaining Six Heroes when the younger members and Amethio free them from their control. After the battle, Indi and Rubina informed Spinel that the recordings were complete, leaving him satisfied despite losing the Six Heroes as he pulled up a screen. At the same time, Chalce rolled her eyes, appearing to disapprove of his methods, while Coral and Sidian started doubting the Strong Sphere. Spinel would later use the recordings to frame the Rising Volt Tacklers again, claiming that they had broken into their facility and stole the remaining Six Heroes that were being cared by them. This was also Spinel's plan to force the Rising Volt Tacklers to go into hiding and promoting the Strong Sphere.
In
HZ130
, the Explorers released the Strong Spheres to the general public. Chalce informed Spinel that Naranja Academy had banned their usage, which he attributed to the Rising Volt Tacklers. Worried about their cooperation with Amethio, Spinel ordered Chalce, Coral, and Sidian to go on a night patrol around Mount Pericarp, reassuring her that he would fulfill her wish. During the patrol, they were confronted by Hamber, who asked them to rebel against Spinel, explaining that the Strong Spheres would destroy the bond between them and their Pokémon. They refused to listen and battled him. Hamber revealed his third Pokémon,
Bloodmoon
Ursaluna
, which defeated Glalie and Garganacl. Chalce used Mega Evolution on her Medicham to defeat the Pokémon. As Chalce ordered agents to apprehend him, Ursaluna created a cloud of dust, allowing Hamber to escape. Deeming him no longer a threat, they resumed their patrol, while Hamber, hidden nearby, felt he had done all he could and left the rest to Amethio.
In
HZ133
, the Explorers got word from Spinel that the Rising Volt Tacklers have return to Laqua with their allies, as he send the agents to stop them. Indi and Rubina reveal during their battle against Dot,
Nemona
, and
Penny
that they're aware of the Rising Volt Tacklers' plan that requires all of the 18 Tera Types. Through Hamber's help, Indi and Rubina were defeated as they retreated in order to get ready for the next stage of their plan. Meanwhile, Coral, Sidian and Chalce had a three on three battle against Roy, Ult and Friede, in which the former two admitted their dislike of the Strong Spheres, with all three ultimately losing the battle in the
following episode
. Seeing Liko, Diana, and
Ann
with Amethio, Onia, and Zirc, Spinel had his upgraded Laquium-activating machine cause the wild Pokémon who still lived in Laqua go berserk. Through the group's efforts, the Laquium-activating machine were destroyed with Zygarde, Zirc, and Onia taking the wild Pokémon somewhere safe. After the Rising Volt Tacklers and their allies regroup, Spinel with Indi and Rubina make their appearances.
In
HZ135
, Spinel ordered Indi and Rubina to stop the Rising Volt Tacklers, being able to take down Penny's
Sylveon
, seemingly stopping their plan. At this point, the other three generals arrived, with Chalce realizing that Spinel had no intentions of honoring her request while Sidian stated that the Strong Spheres had no future with Pokémon. Coral had her Scream Tail substitute Sylveon. Admitting that he had not trusted them either and disappointed that they didn't agree with his views, Spinel voluntarily merged himself with the core, reinforcing its resistance from the purification attempt.
In
HZ136
, the Rising Volt Tacklers were able to force Spinel out of the core. Desperate to keep it intact, he forced Umbreon to fight Liko and her Meowscarada, only to be beaten and the core purified. Spinel tried to recall Umbreon, but she had lost all trust in him and abandoned him, leading to him chasing after her in vain. In the aftermath, the truth was exposed and Spinel was jailed for his crimes, while Cervantis took leadership of the Explorers and regained control of Exceed. All of the remaining generals have left the Explorers. Coral and Sidian travel together while stil being famous, Hamber left on a journey with Diana, and Chalce resumes her teaching career.
