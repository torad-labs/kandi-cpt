Leaf Forest
(Japanese:
リーフの森
Leaf Forest
) is an
animated series
-exclusive
forest
in
Kanto
, featured in
The March of the Exeggutor Squad
. It is located between the
Safari Zone
and
Cinnabar Island
. It has a large population of
Exeggutor
. The area functions similar to a
Leaf Stone
, causing some species of
Grass-type
Pokémon to
evolve
without warning.
