The
Mist Continent
(Japanese:
霧の大陸
Mist Continent
) is a continent of the
Pokémon world
in the
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series
. It was introduced in
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity
. There are three known settlements on the continent:
Post Town
,
Pokémon Paradise
, and
Noe Town
. The continent was not named until its appearance in
Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon
.
It is the setting of Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity. In Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon, it can be visited at any time on the
Lapras Travel Liner
after purchasing the
Mist Pass
.
