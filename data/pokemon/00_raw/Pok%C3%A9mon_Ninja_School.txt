The
Pokémon Ninja School
(Japanese:
忍者スクール
Ninja School
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in the
Kanto
region. It is located between
Lavender Town
and
Fuchsia City
.
Angela
is one of the teachers here. There are many students here, including
Evian
.
Ash
and
his friends
went to the Pokémon Ninja School in order to train themselves in
jujutsu
.
Brock
caught a
Bonsly
there.
It is similar to the
Pokémon Jujitsu Academy
in
Johto
