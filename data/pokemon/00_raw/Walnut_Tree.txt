Walnut Tree
(Japanese:
クルミの木
Walnut Tree
) is a bakery located in
Kanto
in the world of
Magical Pokémon Journey
.
Walnut
works there with his parents.
While it is primarily known for its bread, Walnut Tree also sells confectionery and other treats as well.
Apricot
is a frequent customer of the store, attracting
Walnut
's attention, even though he is initially unaware of her identity.
