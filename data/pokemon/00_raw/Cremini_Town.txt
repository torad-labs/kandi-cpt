Cremini Town
(Japanese:
シキミタウン
Shikimi Town
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location found in
Kanto
. It appeared in
Three Jynx and a Baby!
. The
Battle Tower
, led by
Anabel
, can be found just outside of the town.
Ash
and
his friends
visited Cremini Town in
Three Jynx and a Baby!.
May
saw a
Smoochum
and was about to hold her until the group discovered that Smoochum was being protected by a trio of overprotective
Jynx
. While the group decided to leave Smoochum and the Jynx alone,
Team Rocket
made plans to steal Smoochum. Ash's group was able to save Smoochum and gain the three Jynx's trust. The Smoochum subsequently evolved and became a fourth Jynx. Ash and his friends made plans to rest at Cremini Town until the next day, when they headed to the Battle Tower.
