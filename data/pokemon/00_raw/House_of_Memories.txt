The
House of Memories
(Japanese:
たましいのいえ
Soul House
), named
Soul House
in
Generation II
, is a small building located in
Lavender Town
in the region of
Kanto
during the events of the
Generation II
games and
Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver
. It is located in the southeast portion of Lavender Town, where the
Poké Mart
was during the events of the
Generation I
games and
Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen
.
The House of Memories was built to hold graves from the
Pokémon Tower
when it was converted to the Kanto Radio Tower. There are many chambers within the building, but they cannot be accessed by anyone other than
Mr. Fuji
. There are no
wild Pokémon
or
Trainers
found within the House of Memories.
