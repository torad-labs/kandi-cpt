Sable City
(Japanese:
サビシティ
Sabi City
) is a small
animated series
-exclusive town located in
Kanto
, very close to
Pewter City
.
In
The Ole' Berate and Switch!
, Sable City held a one-day Pokémon Tournament, in which
Ash
entered to train for his upcoming match at the
Battle Pyramid
. Ash participated with his
Corphish
, who helped him win against
Howie
and
Oriba
. However,
Max
accidentally discovered that
Team Rocket
's
Butch
and
Cassidy
had created the event as a scheme to steal all the competitors' Pokémon and give them to
the Boss
. Ash and
his friends
joined forces with the other Trainers to defeat the plot, while the
Team Rocket trio
helped and decided to get their revenge on rivals Butch and Cassidy.
