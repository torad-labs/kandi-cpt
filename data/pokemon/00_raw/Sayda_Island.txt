Sayda Island
(Japanese:
サイダ島
Sayda Island
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in
Kanto
.
Sayda Island is a heavily forested area, with a rocky, mountainous portion that is notable for being where
Crystal
found the Fossilized
Aerodactyl
Egg. This Egg, along with a sample of
Old Amber
, was used by
Gary
and Crystal's sister,
Dora
, to revive an Aerodactyl, which broke free of the lab.
Sayda Island also has fruit trees, the fruit of which Aerodactyl has been depicted to enjoy eating.

Sayda Lab
Sayda Lab
The Sayda Lab is a specialized
Fossil
research facility lead by
Dora
. Following his journey through the
Johto
region
,
Gary
landed a position at Sayda Lab and went on to revive an
Aerodactyl
, its first resurrected Pokémon, from DNA recovered from a fossilized
Egg
and
Old Amber
.
The facility also houses an extensive collection of fossilized remains and a large display with life-size reconstructions of all the
Kanto
Fossil Pokémon.
