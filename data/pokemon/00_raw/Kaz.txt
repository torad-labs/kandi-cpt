Kaz
Kaz
(Japanese:
カズ
Kaz
) is a
character of the day
who appeared in
Showdown at Dark City
. He is the Leader of the
Kaz Gym
in
Dark City
.
Kaz's goal is to get his Gym recognized as the only official
Gym
in Dark City in order to make money. He, therefore, has a bitter rivalry with the other would-be Leader
Yas
and his
Yas Gym
. To this end, the two Leaders and their pupils have been battling constantly, much to the detriment of the residents of Dark City. During a confrontation between the two Gyms, Kaz sends his
Electabuzz
against Yas'
Scyther
but
Ash
,
his friends
, and a group of local children spilled ketchup on him, Yas, and
Team Rocket
. As both Scyther and Electabuzz have a strong aversion to the color red, these two battling Pokémon turned on their Trainers.
After their Pokémon run into each other, the two Leaders go after Ash,
Misty
,
Brock
, and the three kids who helped them.
Pikachu
then fends them off with a
Thunder
. His dreams of wealth through leading an official Gym are dashed when a disguised
Nurse Joy
, acting as a member of the
Pokémon Inspection Agency
, reveals herself and declares both him and his rival unfit to be Gym Leaders.
