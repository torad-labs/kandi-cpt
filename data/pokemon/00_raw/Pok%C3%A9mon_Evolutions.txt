Pokémon Evolutions
(Japanese:
Pokémon Evolutions
Pokémon Evolutions
) is an
animated
miniseries that showcases some moments from the first eight
generations
of the
core series
Pokémon games
. It consists of eight episodes, each approximately 7–11 minutes in length, with one dedicated to each core series
region
, presented in reverse order of their introduction. The series premiered worldwide on September 9, 2021, with episodes being released sporadically over the course of the remainder of the year.

This is the first animated media to be scored by
Ed Goldfarb
in all languages.
Much like in
Pokémon Origins
,
Pokémon Generations
, and
Pokémon: Twilight Wings
, all Pokémon seen in Evolutions make realistic animal sounds instead of just saying their names, like in the
TV series
.
Each episode features two
Pikachu
wearing the hats of the
player characters
of the region they feature in.
The Show
is the first time in any media that
Lugia
is depicted perching on the
Brass Tower
.
