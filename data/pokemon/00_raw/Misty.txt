Appearance
Misty is a young girl with a fair complexion. In almost all of her appearance, her orange hair has a single large pigtail on the left side of her head, and is spikier on the right side.
Pokémon Red, Green and Blue
Misty wears a two-piece swimsuit with sandals. They are rendered black in-game due to color palette limitations, but the official artwork by Ken Sugimori depicts her swimsuit as white with a large red stripe, and her sandals as red with white buttons.
Pokémon Yellow
In Yellow, her outfit is instead based on her anime counterpart, comprising of a yellow crop top, blue shorts (rendered black in-game due to palette limitations), and red sneakers.
Pokémon Gold, Silver, Crystal, HeartGold and SoulSilver
Misty's hair has grown longer, and ends with two hook-shaped bangs on either side. In Pokémon Stadium 2 only, her hair is depicted as pink.
Her outfit consists of a white one-piece swimsuit, a light-blue open jacket, and white slide sandals.
Pokémon Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!
Misty wears a white crop top with red accents, blue shorts with wave-like light blue designs, and red and white sneakers with blue accents.

Trophy information
Misty's the gym leader of Cerulean City and a real tomboy. She loves swimming and all things aquatic, so she makes a point of collecting Starmie, Staryu, and other Water-type Pokémon. This is common among gym leaders: typically, the Pokémon kept by Trainers tend to reflect that person's personality and preferences.
