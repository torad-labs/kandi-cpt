The
Mystery Continent
(Japanese:
謎の大陸
Mystery Continent
) is a continent of the
Pokémon world
in the
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series
. It was introduced in
Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon
. Once any of the dungeons located there have been unlocked, it can be visited at any time via the
Lapras Travel Liner
.
