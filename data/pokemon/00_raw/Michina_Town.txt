Michina Town
(Japanese:
ミチーナ
Michina
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in the
Sinnoh
region
and the setting of
Arceus and the Jewel of Life
. It is a riverside town surrounded by flourishing nature. Most of the town is agricultural, with numerous fields growing crops such as corn and watermelon. The town's centerpiece is the Michina Ruins.
Thousands of years ago, Michina Town was a desolate place, which was nothing but a wasteland. But the
Jewel of Life
's power that
Arceus
gave
Damos
made the town's soil rich and fertile. Then Damos constructed a shrine at the peak of a mountain in order to praise Arceus's kindness.

Places of interest
Michina Ruins
Michina Ruins
The Michina Ruins are the remnants of the shrine made by Damos, which are located at the peak of a huge, rocky mountain in Michina. Close to the ruins is a beautiful lake, as well as multiple waterfalls that cascade down the mountainside.
The ruins are guarded by
Sheena
and
Kevin
. The
Time-Space Axis
can be found in the basement of the ruins; it is used by Sheena and Kevin to monitor disruptions in the time-space continuum. The basement is also the location where the
Jewel of Life
is safeguarded.
