Etymology and design concept
Etymology
Kitakami may be named after areas named "
Kitakami
" (北上) in the
Tōhoku region
of northern Japan, which includes
Kitakami Mountains
(北上山地), Kitakami River Basin (北上盆地),
Kitakami River
(北上川), and
Kitakami City
(北上市). The name "Kitakami" is formed from the words 北
kita
(north) and 上
kami
(up).
The name may also be derived from 神
kami
, a term that encompasses a wide spectrum of mythological and spiritual entities as well as natural phenomena that are venerated in traditional
Shinto
religion.
Design concept
Main article:
Pokémon world in relation to the real world → Kitakami
Kitakami may be inspired by the prefectures of
Iwate
,
Akita
, and
Aomori
in the
Tōhoku region
, particularly the
Towada-Hachimantai National Park
and neighboring areas on the borders of the three prefectures. The aforementioned region is particularly well known for its rural atmosphere as well as its mountains and rice paddies. Notably, the Aomori Prefecture is the largest producer of apples in Japan.
History
Prehistory
Spoiler warning:
this article may contain major plot or ending details.
Hundreds of years prior to the events of
Pokémon Scarlet and Violet

Ursaluna

At some point, a man and an ogre named

As time passed, rumors about the duo and their masks began spreading further, even to distant lands, eventually they got to the ears of an old couple, who requested their Pokémon,
Pecharunt

Okidogi

Munkidori

They went into the duo's home while both were away, but the man found about it and engaged into a battle with them, being left heavily injured but was strong enough to protect at least one of the masks, while the other three were taken. Once Ogerpon returned to its home, it found it in ruins and its friend missing, with the Teal Mask left on the floor. It then donned its mask on and ran down into the village looking for its friend, only to find the four greedy Pokémon gloating over the stolen masks. It madly attacked them and after a battle, the three are slayed while the leader, Pecharunt managed to survive by hiding within its shell while being sent flying deep into the forest. The villagers around were scared of the ogre after seeing what it had done and assumed it was an evil ogre, while the three deceased Pokémon were heroes who sacrificed themselves for them. Ogerpon is left very wounded, but weakly returns to its home very saddened.
After witnessing the battle between the ogre and the greedy Pokémon, the villagers, assuming the three Pokémon were heroes and the ogre was a villain, buried the trio with care and built a monument atop it. They named the deceased trio the
Loyal Three

Loyalty Plaza

Over the years as the mask-maker passed down Ogerpon's true story to his descendants, soon the story was passed down to

Geography
Kitakami is a landlocked region that is mostly mountainous, as a large mountain comprises most of the region, though some rivers are present at the top of the mountain and around it on the east side, some even leading outside of the region. There is a town at the base of the mountain, being the only town in Kitakami.
Kitakami is described as being far away from Paldea, where Florian/Juliana lives, to the degree of a plane being required to travel there.
Demographics
At least 55 people live in Kitakami. This count excludes Kieran, Carmine, and walking NPCs, but does include people on and near Loyalty Plaza, as well as those immediately south of Kitakami Hall. Most of the people there are naturally born citizens there, like Ogerpon's mask-maker, though rarely do they see tourists. Apart from farming, a significant amount of income comes from tourism. Most of the residents of Kitakami do not appreciate this (with both Trainers and main characters referring to those not from Kitakami as "outsiders"). Though following the events of
The Teal Mask
, many warm up to them.

Origin
Kitakami features multiple cultural elements that are typical in Japan, such as
traditional festivals
,
yatai
food stalls,
Jizō
stone statues, and traditional lanterns made of
stone
and
paper
. Locals have also been shown to be wearing
jinbei
, a traditional form of loungewear.
The Pokémon that made its debut in the area appears to be inspired by various aspects of Japanese culture and native animals, such as
Poltchageist
and
Sinistcha
(
matcha
),
Ogerpon
(
oni
and
Noh
mask),
Dipplin
(
candy apple
served during Japanese festivals),
Okidogi
(
Shiba Inu
or
Akita
),
Munkidori
(
Japanese macaque
),
Fezandipiti
(

, official bird of Akita Prefecture), and
Pecharunt
(
mochi
). In addition, the Loyal Three and Pecharunt mirrors the Japanese folklore of
Momotarō
with its three animal companions (dog, monkey, and pheasant) battling against the monstrous
oni
.
Bloodmoon
Ursaluna
's

mentioning that "It crossed the sea and drifted ashore in a new land" may allude to the
Tsugaru Strait
that separates Tōhoku and Hokkaido (original model for

Hisui
