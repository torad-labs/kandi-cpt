Mt. Spirit
(Japanese:
神山
Mt.
Kami
) is a location exclusive to
The Electric Tale of Pikachu
manga
. It is a tall mountain on the outskirts of
Fuchsia City
. The flag on its peak is the finishing point for the
Fuchsia Free-For-All Race
.
Mt. Spirit is covered in steep cliffs and is blanketed in thick fog all year round, making traversing it dangerous.
