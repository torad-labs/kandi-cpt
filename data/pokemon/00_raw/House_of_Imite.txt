The
House of Imite
(Japanese:
モノマネハウス
Mimic House
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in
Kanto
, found in-between the
Safari Zone
and
Cinnabar Island
. It appeared in
Ditto's Mysterious Mansion
.
Home to
Duplica
and her
Ditto
, the House of Imite is where Duplica keeps all of her disguises.
Ash
and
his friends
stumbled across it while seeking shelter from a storm. They found what appeared to be a replica of Ash waiting inside, but it turned out to be Duplica imitating him. Eventually, Duplica formally introduced herself and explained to them who she was and what she did.
The House of Imite is also where Duplica and her Ditto perform shows for audiences. People come from all around to watch Duplica and her Ditto
Transform
together into Pokémon and people. However, for a while, people stopped coming because her Ditto couldn't transform all the way. Eventually, with the help of
Team Rocket
, it learned how to Transform completely. Satisfied, Duplica reopened the House for audiences once again.
