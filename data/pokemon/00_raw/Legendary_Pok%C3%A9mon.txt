Generation V

Special Pokémon

game mascots

In some uncommon cases, the term "Legendary Pokémon" is used to refer to Mythical Pokémon even after the Generation V games, such as in a painting of
Arceus
in the
Lilycove Museum
from
Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire

Mew
and
Shaymin

Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS/Wii U
