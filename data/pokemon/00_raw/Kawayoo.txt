Artwork by kawayoo
Kawaguchi Yoohei
(Japanese:
川口洋平
), born 1974, is a freelance digital illustrator who has provided artwork for the Pokémon series. He began work with the
Pokémon Trading Card Game
, illustrating the
Walrein
,
Hippopotas
, and
Nidorino
included in the
Rising Rivals
expansion. He is often credited by his nickname "
kawayoo
" (
川洋
) in his work.
Youhei's style is very distinctive, brought about from his creative approach and dedication to each one of his illustrations. He creates subjects from scratch by using a graphic tablet in image editing software and applying hundreds of strokes to define base shapes, outlines, backgrounds and final details. These result in often surreal, heavy, multi-layered images reminiscent of oil paintings. His images often focus on muscle tone, expression or contrast and have themes influenced by cyberpunk or anime. The Pokémon he has portrayed are often very detailed, vibrant and in dynamic positions, among which include the
Pokémon
cards, which he provided all the artwork for (with the exception of
LV.X
variants).
Youhei drew the promotional artwork, which includes booster pack art and theme deck art, for every expansion in the HeartGold & SoulSilver series. Outside the TCG, he illustrated maps and other promotional artwork for
Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire
and every
Generation VII
core series
game.
