Palette Town
(Japanese:
まっさらな街
Brand New Town
) is an area in
Pokémon Pokopia
, accessible from the beginning. It is a group of three barren islands with no major story content, though going here is required in order to complete the
Pokédex
.
Ditto
arrives in Palette Town by crossing the bridge from
Withered Wasteland
. This bridge is the only direct connection between Palette Town and the other areas in the game.

Places of interest
Pokémon Center
The
Pokémon Center
is initially a pile of rubble on a wooden platform, having been torn down by extreme
weather
. It is eventually rebuilt by Ditto and a group of other Pokémon, after which a
note
can be found on the floor inside, alongside a counter with a
cash register
, a
3D printer
, a healing machine, and some furniture.
Mysterious pedestals
Each island has a
mysterious pedestal
carrying a yellow
Poké Ball
. These Poké Balls contain kits for structures that must be built to meet and befriend the
legendary birds
:
The
Freezing Chambers kit
can be found on the eastern island. Building this structure will allow
Ditto
to meet
Articuno
.
The
Abandoned Power Plant kit
can be found on the southern island. Building this structure will allow
Ditto
to meet
Zapdos
.
The
Altar of Flame kit
can be found on the northern island. Building this structure will allow
Ditto
to meet
Moltres
.
Demographics
While there are no longer any
humans
living here, Palette Town is inhabited by an
Eevee
before
Ditto
arrives and starts making new
habitats
.

Description
Palette Town
These islands don't have a story to pursue, but they are vast and undeveloped, so you can build them up as you see fit! You can also invite others to play via Link Play to help you develop an island, or you can all visit a
Dream Island
together.
You can also bring Pokémon from other towns to Palette Town. There are also some Pokémon who you can only find there! Work together with other players and Pokémon to create a fun and lively island!
