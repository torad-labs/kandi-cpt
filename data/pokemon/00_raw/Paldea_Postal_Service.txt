The
Paldea Postal Service
is the postal service of the
Paldea
region, which appeared in
Dragonite and the Special Delivery
. It is the workplace of Hana and a Dragonite known for it's efficient work. Just like a real-life post office, the building delivers packages and letters all over the Pokémon world.
