Yas
Yas
(Japanese:
ヤス
Yas
) is a
character of the day
who appeared in
Showdown at Dark City
. He is the Leader of the
Yas Gym
in
Dark City
.
The Yas Gym is in competition with the
Kaz Gym
to become the official
Gym
of Dark City so that he can earn money. After
Ash
refused to join his Gym, he tells his recruit to attack Ash with their
Pokémon
, in order to prevent him siding with the Kaz Gym against him. Later, the two Gyms rendezvous, and he sends his
Scyther
out again. During the fight of his Scyther against
Kaz
's
Electabuzz
, Ash,
his friends
, and a group of local children spill ketchup on him, Kaz, and
Team Rocket
. As both Scyther and Electabuzz have a strong aversion to the color red, these two battling Pokémon turned on their Trainers.
After their Pokémon run into each other, the two Leaders go after Ash,
Misty
,
Brock
, and the three kids who helped them.
Pikachu
then fends them off with a
Thunder
. When
Nurse Joy
, acting as an official of the
Pokémon Inspection Agency
, tells them that Ash, who is known by them as Tom Ato, will be their teacher, Ash tells them to restore the town, which they eventually do.

Scyther
is Yas's main Pokémon. Like the other Pokémon of its species and Electabuzz, Scyther doesn't like the color red. It was first seen when a female recruit of the Yas Gym brings Ash and company to the Yas Gym. Yas tells Ash to prove his strength and commands his Scyther to fight
Pikachu
. Sending its scythes out, it attacks the ketchup bottle Pikachu was holding, sending the ketchup at its eyes. With its eyes covered, Scyther then attacks its Trainer in a frenzy due to seeing everything as red, resulting in Yas having to return it to its
Poké Ball
. Later, when the two Gyms fight again, he sends Scyther back out. It, when surrounded, uses its Swords Dance attack and slices a building with
Team Rocket
on it. When its Trainer is covered with ketchup, Scyther starts to attack him again. After Ash, Misty, and Brock think they have had enough, they spill ketchup on Scyther and
Electabuzz
as well. Upon seeing their reflection in the other's eyes, they run into each other. At the end of the episode, Scyther and Electabuzz chase Team Rocket whom are now covered in red paint as well.
Scyther's known moves are
Swords Dance
and
Cut
.
