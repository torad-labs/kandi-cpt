The
Sand Continent
(Japanese:
砂の大陸
Sand Continent
) is a continent of the
Pokémon world
in the
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series
. It was introduced in
Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon
.
Sahra Town
is the only known settlement on the continent. It can be visited at any time on the
Lapras Travel Liner
after purchasing the
Sand Pass
.
