Forina
(Japanese:
ファウンス
Fauns
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location and one of the settings for
Jirachi: Wish Maker
. It is characterized by scrubs and
pillar-like rock formations
. The area's mountainous terrain makes it difficult to access even on foot.
The caverns beneath the valley are where the
Mythical Pokémon
Jirachi
hibernates for a millennium between the periods where it is awakened by the
Millennium Comet
for seven days. The power that Jirachi absorbs from the comet is said to maintain Forina's natural beauty.
Butler
and
Diane
first visited the area and obtained Jirachi in the hope of restoring the
Legendary Pokémon
Groudon
from a
fossil
. Butler somewhat succeeded, with Jirachi's power managing to unleash
Meta Groudon
. However, it began absorbing energy from Forina's landscape with its tentacles, consuming people and Pokémon alike. After a long
battle
, Jirachi used
Doom Desire
to destroy the Meta Groudon. As a result,
Ash
,
his friends
,
Team Rocket
, Diane, Butler, and Forina's Pokémon were revitalized.
While Jirachi returned to its crystalline slumber, Diane and Butler decided to stay in Forina. In the ending credits of
Lucario and the Mystery of Mew
,
Kidd Summers
visited Forina and met up with Butler and Diane.
According to promotional posters, it is located just southwest of
Hoenn
's
Mt. Chimney
and northwest of
Lavaridge Town
.

Origin
Main article:
Pokémon world in relation to the real world → In the movies
Forina is based on
Wulingyuan
, a scenic area and a UNESCO World Heritage Site located in the province of
Hunan
in
China
. The site is dominated by thousands of
quartzite
sandstone
pillars several hundred meters high that are spread across the forested landscape.
The production notes in the official Japanese movie pamphlet reveals that Director
Yuyama
found his inspiration for the setting after seeing a photo of Wulingyuan on a magazine cover. He noted that the tall rock formations would provide a sense of scale for the aerial battle, along with the mysterious atmosphere surrounding the site would make it an ideal spot for Jirachi to hibernate.
