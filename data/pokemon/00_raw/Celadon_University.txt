Celadon University
(Japanese:
タマムシ大学
Tamamushi
University
) is a university located in
Celadon City
. Celadon University is a special university which only children of primary school age can attend.
From March 1998 to February 1999, Shogakukan Learning Magazines ran a
promotional campaign
in which readers could enroll in the fictional university, and complete various exams to receive prizes. The university's crest—as shown in the commemorative items—consists of a Magikarp,
Gyarados
, and the Kanji 学
gaku
(meaning "to learn").

In the games
While Celadon University does not appear in the games, it is mentioned in one of the
Human Records
in
Pokémon Pokopia
. A research team from the university conducted a study into
Pidgeot
's eyesight, allowing them to discover a specific strand of DNA that impacts eyesight.
During the
Tamamushi University campaign
, 30 players were awarded a special
Magikarp
that knows
Dragon Rage
, based on their performance in the Professor Exam.
In animation
Pokémon the Series
Pokémon the Series: Gold and Silver
Celadon University is referred to several times in
Pokémon the Series: Gold and Silver
. In
Chikorita's Big Upset
,
Nurse Joy
mentioned that she studied a degree in Pokémon psychology at Celadon University. In
Fossil Fools
, it was revealed that
Professor Oak
used to teach there, and
Foster
was one of his top students.
In the manga
Following the Magikarp giveaway, the University has appeared in two manga series:
Pokémon: Yeah! I Got Pokémon!
and
Pokémon Adventures
. In both manga, the appearance of the building is closely based on the artwork from the Student ID Card and the Magikarp with Dragon Rage is referenced in some way.
Celadon University in
Pokémon Adventures
Pokémon Adventures
Yellow
arc
Celadon University was first mentioned in
Sea Sea Seadra
, where
Bill
said he'd be taking his report on
Viridian Forest
's ecology there and proposing it to the university once it was finished.
Celadon University made its physical debut in
Whacked by Marowak
, where
Erika
was seen giving a lecture about the Dragon Rage Magikarp. She was offered a full-time job at the university by Professor Matsumoto, but she declined because of her
Gym Leader
duties.
Celadon University in
Pokémon: Yeah! I Got Pokémon!
Pokémon: Yeah! I Got Pokémon!
Shu
visited Celadon University in
The Challenge at Celadon University!
. To enter the university building, one must answer an exam question.
The university has a large campus, consisting of a swimming pool, Chemical Research Laboratory, Pokémon Pharmaceutical Faculty, Pokémon Art faculty, a Pokémon Environmental Study Faculty, and the University Hospital, where Shu saved a Magikarp that knew Dragon Rage from a
Machamp
. A hospital worker awarded him with a university crest badge for his deed.
