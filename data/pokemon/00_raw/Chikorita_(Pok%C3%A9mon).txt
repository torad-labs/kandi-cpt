Biology
Chikorita is a small, quadrupedal
Pokémon
. It has a pale green body, a large head in proportion to the rest of its body, a pair of large eyes with red irises, and a stubby tail. Each of its short legs end in a single white nail. There are dark green buds around its neck and a large, green leaf on top of its head.
Chikorita has a gentle personality
[
1
]
and is docile by nature. The leaf on its head emits a pleasant, sweet aroma, which can soothe even battling Pokémon. Chikorita also uses it to sense the temperature and humidity of the area around itself, checking for a nice, warm place for it to sunbathe or, as mentioned in
Pokémon Sleep
, to sleep in.
[
2
]
Chikorita can be found in
grasslands
, but it is rare to come by and is generally only found under the ownership of a
Trainer
due to its status as a
first partner Pokémon
. It has been recently seen living in the
Terarium
of
Blueberry Academy
.
