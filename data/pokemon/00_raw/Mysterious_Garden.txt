The
Mysterious Garden
(Japanese:
ふしぎのはなぞの
Mysterious Flower Garden
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in
Kanto
that appeared in
Bulbasaur's Mysterious Garden
. It's a location where
Bulbasaur
from all over the
world
are said to gather every year for a festival to
evolve
into
Ivysaur
en masse.
History
When
Ash's Bulbasaur
started showing signs of evolving, a group of
wild
Bulbasaur forcibly brought it to the Mysterious Garden, prompting
Ash
and
his friends
to follow. A
Venusaur
appeared from a giant tree and
cried out
as all the wild Bulbasaur evolved into Ivysaur. However, Ash's Bulbasaur refused to evolve, angering Venusaur, which tried to hit it with
Vine Whip
, but Ash took the blow and defended Bulbasaur's right to stay unevolved if it so chose.
Team Rocket
subsequently appeared and tried to capture all of the Ivysaur, but Ash's Bulbasaur managed to save them by using its newly learned
Solar Beam
, after which the villainous trio was sent
blasting off
by a collective Vine Whip from all the Bulbasaur evolutionary line members. Accepting Bulbasaur's choice, Venusaur and the Ivysaur then let it leave the Mysterious Garden without evolving alongside Ash and his friends.
