Lucario Kingdom
(Japanese:
ルカリオの
王国
Lucario Kingdom
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in
Sinnoh
.
Riolu
and
Lucario
have been revered as guardians for generations and are said to use their
aura
powers to protect the kingdom.
Prior to the events of
Pokémon Ranger and the Kidnapped Riolu! (Part 1)
, a group of
Pokémon hunters
appeared in the Lucario Kingdom and stole the rare
Aura Sphere Riolu
from its
Trainer
. Riolu managed to escape two different groups of people interested in its power before the
Pokémon Rangers
stepped in to return Riolu to the Lucario Kingdom.
Ash
and
his friends
assisted
Kellyn
, and by the end of
Pokémon Ranger and the Kidnapped Riolu! (Part 2)
, Riolu was reunited with its Trainer.
