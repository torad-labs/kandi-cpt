The Game Store.
The
Game Store
is the official setting for the plot in the
Pokémon Trading Card Game Online
's
Tutorials
and
Trainer Challenge
, which introduces new players to the
Pokémon Trading Card Game
generally and the Pokémon Trading Card Game Online specifically. It is owned by
Ella
's parents, and is run by Ella and
Zach
. Players are provided three
Starter decks
to begin the game. The Game Store also hosts Pokémon card game tournaments, represented in the game as three leagues in the Trainer Challenge. Not much is really known about the shop, other than the fact that the Pokémon Trading Card Game is played in it, and Pokémon cards are
sold
in it. Ella mentions that Pokémon games are their favorite, implying that they sell other types of games as well.
While the tutorials are important in helping new players ease into the game, experienced players can adjust the game's Options to disable the New User Experience in order to bypass it and instead focus on other aspects of the game that interest them or visit the tutorials at any other time.

Trivia
Up to early 2013, many players from the Trainer Challenge featured cards from the
Stormfront
expansion in their decklists, which were never available for players and later removed from the game's servers.
A higher league, fourth "Diamond League" existed in the Trainer Challenge prior to the February 7, 2014 update.
The tutorials feature unique 20 and 40-card deck matches with two and four-Prize cards respectively.
