Pokémon Master Journeys: The Series
is the twenty-fourth
season
of the
dubbed
version of the
Pokémon animated series
. It is the second season of
Pokémon Journeys: The Series
. The season follows
Ash
and
Goh
as they continue traveling around the
world
, with Ash facing stronger and stronger opponents on his quest to climb to the top of the
Pokémon World Coronation Series
rankings.
In
the United States
, episodes were added quarterly on
Netflix
, beginning on September 10, 2021 with the first twelve episodes, starting from
To Train, or Not to Train!
and concluding on May 26, 2022 with the last fifteen episodes, ending with
Showdown at the Gates of Warp!
. In
Canada
, it debuted on June 12, 2021 on Teletoon. In
South Africa
, it debuted on August 3, 2021 on
e.tv
. In
the United Kingdom
, it debuted on September 1, 2021 on
POP
.
It is preceded by
Pokémon Journeys: The Series
, and succeeded by
Pokémon Ultimate Journeys: The Series
.

Blurb
It’s a big, big world, but you know where to find Ash and Goh—battling and catching Pokémon from Kanto to Galar! With Pikachu, Cinderace, and their other Pokémon beside them, our heroes continue to travel all over the world to research Pokémon for Cerise Laboratory. Along the way, Ash climbs higher in the World Coronation Series rankings, and Goh adds more Pokémon to his Pokédex in his quest to catch Mew. Meanwhile, Chloe takes her first steps as a Pokémon Trainer when she meets a mysterious Eevee. New friends, returning rivals, and exciting adventures await—as the journey continues!
