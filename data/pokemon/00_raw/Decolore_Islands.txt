The
Decolore Islands
(Japanese:
デコロラ諸島
Decolora Archipelago
) is an
animated series
-exclusive
region
consisting of a large chain of islands. It is located between
Unova
and
Kanto
.
It is inhabited by
Pokémon
that can be found in Kanto,
Johto
,
Hoenn
,
Sinnoh
, and Unova. The archipelago is sparsely populated, having only some coastal towns and many nature preserves. The region first appeared in
Farewell, Unova! Setting Sail For New Adventures!
when
Ash
,
Iris
, and
Cilan
decide to visit the Decolore Islands in order to travel together from
Nuvema Town
to
Vermilion City
.

Trivia
This is the second archipelago
region
that
Ash
has visited, the first being the
Orange Islands
. However, unlike the Orange Islands, there is neither any type of
League
known to exist in the Decolore Islands nor any regional
Professor
.
This is also the only region in which neither Ash nor any other main character has
caught
a new Pokémon.
This is also the only archipelago to not include a
villainous team
.
Decolora was used as the
OT
of Japanese
Jirachi
event distribution via Wi-Fi in 2013, a reference to
Jirachi
's appearance in
Searching for a Wish!
.
