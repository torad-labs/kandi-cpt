Rota
(Japanese:
ロータの町
Rōta Town
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in northern
Kanto
and one of the settings of
Lucario and the Mystery of Mew
. In ancient times, Rota was an independent kingdom. As a result, the town has a medieval theme, resembling historically preserved medieval cities in Europe.
Rota hosts an annual festival to decided the "Hero of the Year", which requires attendees to wear royal clothing and includes a royal ball as well as a tournament where
Trainers
engage in
battles
to see who will be the year's
Aura Guardian
. The tournament was entered by
Ash
and
Kidd Summers
.
Cameran Palace
lies north of the town, over a lake, and is connected to the town by a long bridge. It is a great medieval-style castle where
Queen Ilene
lives, and where
Queen Rin
,
Sir Aaron
, and his
Lucario
lived in ancient times.
The
Tree of Beginning
can be found a short distance from Rota, along with the site of the biggest battle of the century.
Rota and the Tree of Beginning are located in a mountain range north of
Pewter City
and
Mt. Moon
.
