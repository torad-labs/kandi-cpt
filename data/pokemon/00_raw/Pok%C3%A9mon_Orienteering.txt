(Japanese:
ポケモンオリエンテーリング
Pokémon Orienteering
) is an
animated series
-exclusive
orienteering
competition held in
Potpourri Island
,
Kanto
. It was featured in
Off the Unbeaten Path

as part of

Competition
Potpourri Island's map
At the beginning of the Pokémon Orienteering competition, all of the contestants receive a map of
Potpourri Island
and a
compass
. Together with a partner
Pokémon
,
Trainers
must navigate their way around the island and attempt to find five checkpoints, which are located in places that are difficult to reach. At each checkpoint, their map must be stamped with a Pokémon symbol.
The object of the competition is to get Trainers and their Pokémon to work together. The first team to get all of the stamps on their map and get back to the
Pokémon Center
is declared the winner. The winners are presented with a golden medal called the Pokémon Teamwork Medal. They also receive a sample of Potpourri Island's famous
fruit
.
During the competition,
Jessie
and
Meowth
—who was disguised as a
Sneasel
—had trouble finding the checkpoints and gave up, letting
James
and his
Mime Jr.
collect all of the stamps so they would benefit from the sample of fruits as well. They decided to slow down the other contestants by setting pitfall traps. In the end,
Nurse Joy
announced James and his Mime Jr. as the big winners. James happily accepted the medal, but gave away the fruit to the rest of the contestants, excluding Jessie and Meowth.
Checkpoints
The
Lunatone
stamp is located at Lunatone Rock, next to a giant Lunatone statue.
The
Remoraid
stamp is located at Rainbow Falls.
The
Pineco
stamp can be found next to a 300-year-old giant tree.
The
Kingler
stamp can be found on a cliff overlooking the ocean.
The
Skarmory
stamp is encountered on a pillar of land between two bridges.
