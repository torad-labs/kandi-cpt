Bleak Beach
(Japanese:
ドンヨリうみべの街
Overcast Seaside Town
) is one of two areas in
Pokémon Pokopia
that can be unlocked after meeting
Kyogre
in
Withered Wasteland
, the other being
Rocky Ridges
. It is what remains of
Vermilion City
after a series of catastrophic
weather
anomalies ravaged the
world
and forced
humans
to take refuge in space.
Ditto
arrives in Bleak Beach through the
gate
from Withered Wasteland, accompanied by
Professor Tangrowth
.
Bleak Beach is connected to the
Sparkling Skylands
to the north, and
Withered Wasteland
to the north-east through gates.

Places of interest
Beach Pokémon Center
The Beach
Pokémon Center
is initially a pile of rubble on a brick platform, having been torn down by extreme
weather
. It is eventually rebuilt by Ditto and a group of other Pokémon, after which a
note
can be found on the floor inside, alongside a counter with a
cash register
, a
3D printer
, a healing machine, and some furniture.
Gazebo
Smearguru
can be found by a gazebo on the eastern part of the mainland's shore.
Charging station
The
charging station
is located in the middle of Bleak Beach. Once powered, it allows
Peakychu
to charge itself in order to utilize its
Illuminate
specialty
and brighten up the area.
Mosslax
Mosslax
is initially asleep in a cave near the charging station hidden behind
cracked sandstone
.
Trubbish
will ask Ditto about loud noises coming from this cave, leading Ditto to break down the wall of cracked sandstone. After brightening up the area,
Raikou
summons a bolt of lightning before appearing, breaking open the roof of the cave and hitting Mosslax directly.
S.S. Anne
A ruined
S.S. Anne
is located on an islet in the southern part of the area. Trubbish asks Ditto to go here to investigate, after which
Happiny
can be found on the second deck.
Demographics
While there are no longer any
humans
living here, Bleak Beach is inhabited by a handful of Pokémon before
Ditto
starts making new
habitats
.
Not counting Ditto and
Professor Tangrowth
, Bleak Beach is initially inhabited by three Pokémon:
Meowth
Smearguru
Mosslax
Additionally, some Pokémon appear without their habitat as Ditto completes requests:
Peakychu
Zorua
Happiny
