In the core series games
Will is the first member of the
Indigo League
Elite Four
in the
Generation II
and
Generation IV
games, filling in the spot previously filled by
Lorelei
. He achieved his Elite Four position fairly recently as a result of
training
around the
world
with his
Psychic-type
Pokémon and he has a strong ambition to become the strongest trainer in the world.
Appearance
Will is a tall, slender man with straight, shoulder-length purple hair that curves inward at the ends, parted to the side and covering his right eye. He wears a black domino mask that conceals the area around his eyes. His visible eye is dark and narrow.
In
Generation II
, Will wears a formal, dark brown blazer with a subtle textured pattern and red cuffs, paired with a light blue collared shirt and a red tie. His trousers are magenta, slightly flared at the bottom, and he wears black shoes. His left hand is gloved in white, while his right hand remains uncovered.
In
Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver
, Will's outfit becomes more elaborate. He wears a black patterned sleeveless tailcoat with gold trim and four large yellow buttons arranged in two rows. The coat is open at the front, revealing a purple undershirt and a wide yellow waistband. Underneath the tailcoat, he wears a white cravat. His magenta pants remain the same in color, and he wears black shoes. His hairstyle and mask remain consistent with his earlier appearance.

Will in Pokémon Adventures
History
Past
Will, along with
Karen
,
Green
,
Silver
,
Sham
, and
Carl
, was one of the children raised by the
Masked Man
to be his servants. Unlike Silver and Green, Will was not kidnapped and went to the Masked Man willingly out of boredom.
Gold, Silver & Crystal
arc
Will debuted with Karen at the
Ilex Forest
, where they have been tasked with guarding the area. When Green arrived to stop the Masked Man's plan to capture
Celebi
, she was attacked by Will and Karen. With the
Lugia and Ho-Oh
they borrowed from the Masked Man, Will and Karen exploited Green's fear of birds to torture her. When Green learned that Will and Karen also defeated and captured Silver, she overcame her fear of birds and sent out
Articuno
,
Zapdos
, and
Moltres
to fight. Green and her allies,
Red
and
Blue
, each used one of the legendary birds to battle Will and Karen. With the assistance of many Pokémon sent from all across
Johto
and
Kanto
, Lugia and Ho-Oh were freed from the Masked Man's control and flew away. Defeated, Will made his escape, only to be punished later by the Masked Man for doing so.
After the Masked Man's defeat, Will and Karen were approached by
Bruno
and
Koga
, who offered to form an alliance since all four of them were once members of various criminal organizations.
HeartGold & SoulSilver
arc
It is revealed that Will, Karen, Bruno, and Koga were all made members of the Johto
Elite Four
by the
Pokémon Association
. The Johto Elite Four went to the
Pokéathlon
as an act of promotion for the Pokémon League. There, they met
Gold
, whom they challenged to the Power Course. Although they easily defeated Gold in the first two events, Gold gained enough points in the third event to win overall.

Trivia
Will's Xatu both know Psychic below the natural learn level. This may be because Will resides in Kanto, where Mr. Psychic lives in
Saffron City
and gives out Psychic TMs.
Will's hair falls over the left side of his face, leading to his left eye being whited out in some depictions. This is rectified in
Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver
, where his overworld sprite clearly shows two eyes, both obscured by white.
Will has several similarities with
Lorelei
: both of them are the first challenger of Kanto
Elite Four
; both of them have used
Slowbro
and
Jynx
in their respective parties; they have similar hairstyles; and both of them have had their hair depicted as either purple or red.
There is data in
Pokémon Crystal
for the gender of every Trainer class in the game, with the exception of
Eusine
. This is used to select which dialogue pool to sample from when randomly choosing the opponent's dialogue. Will is one of two male characters to be identified as female in this array, the other being
Bugsy
.
All of Will's Pokémon (excluding Jynx, which is a female-only species) changed from male in
Gold, Silver
, and
Crystal
to female in HeartGold and SoulSilver.
Similarly to
Saturn
, Bugsy, and
Janine
, Will's Pokémon are the opposite gender despite them having a 50-50 gender ratio.
Will is the only member of the
Indigo Plateau
Elite Four
(in Generation II and HeartGold and SoulSilver) that uses only Pokémon of the type that he specializes in the first match. All others have at least one Pokémon not of their specialty in their first match, and, with the exception of
Lance
, replace the Pokémon not of their specialty with Pokémon of their specialty in rematches.
He shares this trait with
Lucian
of the
Sinnoh
Elite Four in
Pokémon Diamond and Pearl
. Coincidentally, both use Psychic-type Pokémon.
Although Elite Four members usually aren't known to have titles or slogans like Gym Leaders do, a
Themed Collection
in
Pokémon Trading Card Game Pocket
refers to Will as "The Man in the Mysterious Mask." This is consistent with how
the following expansion
added themed collections about
Whitney
and
Morty
, named after their respective Gym Leader titles.
This would make Will one of the few Elite Four members in the series to have a title.
Some Elite Four members known to have used a slogan at some point are
Koga
and
Larry
.
