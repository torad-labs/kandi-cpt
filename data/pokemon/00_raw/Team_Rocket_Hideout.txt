Access
The secret switch concealed behind a poster in the Game Corner
Location preview of Team Rocket Hideout in Generation III
The Rocket Hideout is accessed by pressing a switch behind a poster in the
Rocket Game Corner
in
Celadon City
. A
Team Rocket Grunt
is guarding the poster in question when the
player
first approaches it. After being defeated, the Grunt retreats into the base, leaving the poster unguarded, and enabling the player to approach. Once the player activates a switch behind the poster, the stairs to the hideout will appear. In
Generation VII
, the Grunt will only battle the player once
Cubone
has been kidnapped in
Lavender Town
.
In Generation II, the Rocket Hideout is referred to when the player walks and selects the poster at the back of the Rocket Game Corner in Celadon City. The words, "
Hey! Under this poster... There's nothing!
" will appear, a reference to the original access point.
Description
This is the first area in
Kanto
-based games to feature
spin tiles
, a special type of tile that, when stepped on, will uncontrollably spin the player in the direction of the arrows until they hit a stop tile. These are used on the second and third basement floors to create a complex maze.
Giovanni
's office on B4F can only be reached by taking the elevator, which requires the
Lift Key
to work. In
Generations I
and
III
, the Lift Key is dropped by a
Team Rocket Grunt
on B4F after being defeated, while in
Generation VII
, he instead throws it onto a wall after losing, requiring the player's
partner Pokémon
to climb onto the wall through an air vent and retrieve it. The elevator also grants access to a shortcut to the first floor, where defeating a Grunt causes the gate blocking the exit to open.
In order to access Giovanni's office, the player must first defeat a pair of Team Rocket Grunts

Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!

Trivia
In
Generation I
, it is possible to skip the entire Rocket Hideout. Due to an oversight, the player can use a
Poké Doll
bought at the
Celadon Department Store
to flee from the
Marowak
ghost
at
Pokémon Tower
and progress without the
Silph Scope
. This was corrected in
Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen
.
If the player skips the Rocket Hideout using this method, it is still possible to enter this place at any point later and potentially
break the normal gameplay sequence
. For instance, once the player obtains the
Earth Badge
,
Giovanni
appears to disband the Team Rocket and permanently leave the current game as expected in normal gameplay. However, as long as the player has not obtained the Silph Scope, then Giovanni and the Team Rocket members still permanently remain in the Rocket Hideout as if the Team Rocket was never disbanded.
