Frodomar City
is allegedly the
hometown
of
Ritchie
. It is an
animated series
-exclusive location in
Kanto
, and is only mentioned in the
dub
. It is also possible that this
city
does not exist, and that Ritchie was lying so as not to alter
time
, considering that the only time the place was mentioned was in
Celebi and Joy!
, where Ritchie was transported several years into the past by
Celebi
.
