The
Sea of Wonders
(Japanese:
七不思議の海
Sea of Seven Wonders
) is a region of the
Pokémon world
in the
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series
. It was fully introduced in
Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon
. It contains many islands, including one on which the
Tree of Life
is located. It is first visited in the story when the
Expedition Society
travels to
Fire Island Volcano
. It can be visited at any time on the
Lapras Travel Liner
after completing the main story. It is referred to as
Beyond the sea
if it is visited through
Pelipper Island
prior to completing the main story.
The two easternmost islands are featured in
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness
as the
Hidden Land
and
Blizzard Island
.
