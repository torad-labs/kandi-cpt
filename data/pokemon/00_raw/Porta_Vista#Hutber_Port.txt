Porta Vista
is an
animated series
-exclusive location in
Kanto
. It is located on the
Island of the Giant Pokémon
. It contains two cities,
Acapulco
(Japanese:
アオプルコ
Aopulco
) and
Hutber Port
(Japanese:
ハトバポート
Hatoba Port
), which appeared in
Beauty and the Beach
and
Tentacool & Tentacruel
, respectively.
The fact that the two different towns were referred to as Porta Vista in the dub is a side-effect of
Beauty and the Beach
initially being skipped. At the end of
the prior episode
, the dub showed a wide shot of Acapulco to lead into Hutber Port in
the next episode
, both of which were called Porta Vista so that
Beauty and the Beach
could be skipped without breaking continuity. In the dub of
Beauty and the Beach
, the towns remained combined to avoid any continuity errors between it and the episodes surrounding it.

Acapulco
(Japanese:
アオプルコ
Aopulco
) is the town featured in
Beauty and the Beach
. It was first seen in the very end of
Island of the Giant Pokémon
. It is based on
Acapulco
, complete with beaches, sunny skies, and a huge ocean. It is a very popular tourist destination, and it was also the setting for many different adventures in the animated series.
Brutella
and
Moe
both own restaurants in Acapulco.
Ash
and
his friends
had to help rebuild Moe's dock when they crashed their boat into it. Unfortunately for Moe, Brutella's restaurant was more successful, which threatened to drive his restaurant out of business. With the help of Ash and his friends, Moe was able to bring his business sales back up. In the end, Brutella's restaurant was destroyed by
Team Rocket
's
Gyarados submarine
.
Acapulco also held
a beauty contest
, in which
Misty
,
Gary
and
his cheerleaders
,
Jessie
, and even
James
participated in.
The name "Acapulco" comes from the
Pokémon.com
summary of the episode it was featured in and the two following episodes and makes reference to the real city of
Acapulco
.

Hutber Port
(Japanese:
ハトバポート
Hatoba Port
) is the town featured in
Tentacool & Tentacruel
.
Here, Brutella's cousin,
Nastina
, wanted to build a hotel in the middle of the ocean. However, the
Tentacool
that lived there were being disturbed by the construction and tried to interfere with the project. Nastina hired Team Rocket to get rid of them, but all they ended up doing was forcing one of the Tentacool to evolve into a
Tentacruel
and grow it to several hundred feet tall. It began destroying all of Hutber Port until
Misty
convinced it that not all humans are like Nastina, and that all construction would cease on the hotel.
This is also the location where Misty
caught
her
Horsea
.
The name "Hutber Port" comes from the Pokémon.com summary of the episode. It was also used on the
Topps Trading Card
for the episode.

Trivia
"Aopulco" is a pun in Japanese. "Acapulco" written in Japanese katakana is アカプルコ, which starts with "aka", meaning "red" in Japanese. In "Aopulco", "aka" is replaced "ao", meaning "blue".
