Overview
To enter Indigo Academy, a potential student must complete several interviews with teachers to define their skills and interests with their potential Pokémon partners.
As part of the Indigo Academy initiation process, the Headmistress gives a talk about the
Pokémon world
, and soon after, the teachers give the students their respective
Rotom Phones
and
first partner Pokémon
. The Academy's subjects cover aspects of the Pokémon world, such as
battle
methods, partnership, and
friendship
with the respective Pokémon partner.
Despite being mostly face-to-face, Indigo Academy has a distance learning modality. In this case, the class is broadcast live via a Rotom Phone for students to watch.
As suggested in
Eyes to the Skies Once Again (Part Two)
, Indigo Academy has a student body called the Selection Team, where the students who stand out the most are nominated for a spot in the
Pokémon League
competition. To date, the only known student to have received this recognition was
Ann
.
Pins
Entering Indigo Academy, depending on the academic year they're admitted in, students are given a pin placed on their uniforms, displaying a
Type
symbol. These are the class year symbols currently known.
In
The Pendant That Starts It All (Part One)
,
Liko
was first shown with a
Grass
symbol. After entering into the bus, three other students can be seen with the
Fire
symbol, as of current, their year is unknown.
In
Eyes to the Skies Once Again (Part One)
, after a year later has passed, students who see Liko as their upperclassman can be seen with a
Water
symbol.

Background
At some point in the past,
Liko
's parents,
Lucca
and
Alex
, studied at the academy.
Pokémon Horizons: The Series
In
The Pendant That Starts It All (Part One)
,
Liko
began her classes at Indigo Academy and, after receiving her first Pokémon and meeting her roommate
Ann
, she spent a few days adapting to her new Pokémon partner. Later, the Indigo Academy was the site of the first clash between the
Rising Volt Tacklers
and the
Explorers
.
In
For Sure! 'Cause Sprigatito's with Me!
, Liko revealed to Ann that she would only participate remotely in classes from then on.
A day of classes at Indigo Academy
As Liko started traveling with the Rising Volt Tacklers, the Indigo Academy started to appear in some specific events during the plot of
Pokémon Horizons: The Series
.
In
The Door That Never Opens
, Liko was briefly shown participating in an online class.
In
The Lonely Hatenna
, Liko was seen finishing participating in a class, after which Liko talked a little with Ann about the task they received which involved what each of the students would like to be in the future.
In
The Wild Pair, Friede and Cap!
, Liko was shown participating in another online class, this time the objective of the class was for each student to choose a
Trainer
and their Pokémon and write a dissertation about it.
The Indigo Academy briefly appeared in a flashback in
Farewell, Sprigatito?
, where it was revealed that a group of
Pokémon Breeders
joined the teachers during each student's interviews. These Pokémon Breeders are responsible for raising the
first partner Pokémon
that will go to each of the students. In this episode, Liko met
Marnya
, one of these breeders.
In
Welcome to Naranja Academy!
, Liko transferred to
Naranja Academy
and it was revealed that Ann did the same. In
Shine on, Terastallization! Liko vs. Roy!
, after completing the
Tera Training
, Ann revealed to Liko that she would return to Indigo Academy to improve herself as a Trainer.
In
Where the Adventure Leads
, it was revealed that a year after the events in
Laqua
, Liko returned to Indigo Academy and became Ann's roommate again. She left in
Eyes to the Skies Once Again (Part Two)
to join
Roy
in his and Dot's new quest to investigate Laquium cases worldwide. Sometime afterward and prior to
Welcome to Blueberry Academy!
, Ann left to attend
Blueberry Academy
as an exchange student.

Auditorium:
During the beginning of the school year, students receive a lecture in the auditorium about the
World of Pokémon

Battlefields:
Battlefields are available for students to use to practice their
battling
. In
The Pendant That Starts It All (Part One)
,
Ann
and
Liko
watched as a
Machoke

Oshawott
and
Sprigatito

Dorms:
It is shown that each student at Indigo Academy has a room that they share with another classmate. Liko shared her room with Ann during her stay there. Liko and Ann's room is number 303.
Clocktower:
Indigo Academy has a clocktower, where
Friede
and
Amethio
have their first battle there during Liko's escape from the
Explorers

Leafage

first partner Pokémon
