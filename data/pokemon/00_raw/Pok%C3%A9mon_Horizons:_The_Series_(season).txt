is the first
season
of the
dubbed
version of
Pokémon Horizons: The Series
. It is the twenty-sixth season of the
Pokémon animated series
. The season follows
Liko
and
Roy
as they travel around the
world
with a group of adventurers known as the
Rising Volt Tacklers
.
In
the United States
, episodes were added on
Netflix
beginning on March 7, 2024 and concluding on November 22, 2024. In
the United Kingdom
, it debuted on December 1, 2023 on BBC iPlayer and on December 4, 2023 on CBBC. In
Australia
, it debuted on February 27, 2024 on 9Go!. In
Canada
, it debuted on March 2, 2024 on Cartoon Network. In Asia, it debuted on March 8, 2024 on the Pokémon Asia ENG
YouTube
channel. In sub-Saharan Africa, it debuted on March 25, 2024 on Cartoon Network Africa. In
New Zealand
, it debuted on April 15, 2024 on Netflix.
It is preceded by
Pokémon Ultimate Journeys: The Series
, the last season of the previous animated series in the West,
Pokémon the Series
, and succeeded by
Pokémon Horizons: Season 2—The Search for Laqua
. It comprises the
Liko and Roy's Departure
and
The Sparkling of Terapagos
arcs from the Japanese version.

Blurb
A new adventure is beginning within the wide world of Pokémon! Arriving at Indigo Academy, a girl named Liko receives her first partner Pokémon, Sprigatito. But she soon finds herself pursued by the Explorers, a mysterious group that’s determined to take the pendant necklace she wears. Liko isn’t on her own, though, as Friede, Captain Pikachu and the other Rising Volt Tacklers offer her protection aboard their airship. Meanwhile, a boy named Roy dreams of becoming a Pokémon Trainer, unaware of the secret hiding within the Ancient Poké Ball he carries. As Liko, Roy and the Rising Volt Tacklers set sail for new horizons, what kind of discoveries await?

Trivia
This is the first season:
not to feature
Ash
and
Pikachu
as main protagonists and the
Team Rocket trio
as main antagonists.
to use direct translations of the Japanese titles for most of the English titles of episodes.
where the English
dub
uses different opening themes for different releases of the same episodes depending on the country, with
Becoming Me
for the international release and
We Go (English Ver.)
for the Asian release.
to be promoted with more than two official posters. Each of the four posters corresponds to a batch of episodes released consecutively in March, May, August, and November 2024.
not to feature
James Carter Cathcart
as a cast member in the English dub, after retiring from the animated series at the end of
Pokémon Journeys: The Series
due to him been diagnosed with a throat cancer and undergoing treatment.

the United States
and internationally on February 23, 2024,
