Geography
Carmonte Island is an artificial island housing a city of resorts, as well as many other state-of-the-art facilities such as the
Training Center
, an airport, and a harbor. Ten luxury hotels consecutively line up in a row, leading all the way to symbol of the island—
Jewel Tower
.
Since Pokémon Duel has been discontinued, the last four of these hotels have never become accessible.
