(Japanese:
リコとロイの旅立ち
Liko and Roy's Depature
) is the first arc of
Pokémon Horizons: The Series
that aired on
TV Tokyo
from April 14, 2023 to October 20, 2023. It is followed by
The Sparkling of Terapagos
arc.
The arc focuses on new
Trainers
Liko
and
Roy
, and their
first partners
,
Sprigatito
and
Fuecoco
, as they join a group of adventurers named the
Rising Volt Tacklers
and discover their connection to the Ancient Adventurer
Lucius
and a
mysterious Pokémon
living in Liko's pendant. The arc also introduces the
Explorers
, an opposing faction bent on taking Liko's pendant for themselves.
The arc's opening theme is
Heart-Pounding Diary
performed by
asmi
feat.
Chinozo
, and the ending theme is
RVR ~Rising Volt Tacklers Rap~
by Liko (CV:
Minori Suzuki
) & Roy (CV:
Yuka Terasaki
).

Plot
Liko
, a beginner Trainer from
Paldea
, enrolls at
Indigo Academy
in
Kanto
and receives
Sprigatito
as her
first partner Pokémon
. She encounters
Amethio
and becomes targeted by the mysterious organization called
Explorers
due to her pendant, which briefly reveals a mysterious Pokémon.
Friede
and the
Rising Volt Tacklers
intervene, rescuing Sprigatito from Amethio's captivity, and Liko joins their crew as they head for Paldea.
Upon arriving in Paldea, Liko meets
Roy
, who befriends a stowaway
Fuecoco
traveling with the Rising Volt Tacklers. When the Explorers attack
Roy's hometown
, his
Ancient Poké Ball
opens to reveal a
Black Rayquaza
before it flies away. Roy catches Fuecoco and joins the Rising Volt Tacklers. After this incident,
Gibeon
removes Amethio from the mission and assigns it to
Spinel
instead.
Dot
, who streams as the popular Nidothing, eventually tags along with Liko and Roy. The group discovers a
giant
Arboliva
holding another Ancient Poké Ball, which goes into the ball under Liko's care. They learn about
Lucius
, an ancient adventurer connected to the Ancient Poké Balls.
After Spinel steals Liko's pendant, the group travels to
Levincia
where Dot catches her first Pokémon,
Quaxly
, and helps retrieve the pendant. The Rising Volt Tacklers then journey to
Galar
, where Liko catches a
Hatenna
and they locate Lucius's third Pokémon, a
Galarian
Moltres
, which joins Liko's care. The mysterious Pokémon from the pendant fully emerges and is identified as
Terapagos
when the group meets Liko's grandmother
Diana
at her castle. The arc concludes with Diana tagging along with the Rising Volt Tacklers and revealing that Lucius's
remaining three
Pokémon are
Lapras
,
Kleavor
, and
Entei
.
