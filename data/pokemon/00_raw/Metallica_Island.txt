Metallica Island
(Japanese:
メタリカ島
Metallica Island
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in the
Kanto
region
. It is found between
Cinnabar Island
and the
Seafoam Islands
. It is home to the
Battle Palace
.
The island does contain its own
Pokémon Center
, but most of the island is a large forest where
Spenser
trains. It was featured in
Cutting the Ties that Bind!
and
Ka Boom with a View!
.
