Old Shore Wharf
(Japanese:
カントー波止場
Kanto Wharf
) is an
animated series
-exclusive dock in
Kanto
. It appeared in
Mewtwo Strikes Back
and
its remake
.
Miranda
works here as a pier master.
Old Shore Wharf in
Mewtwo Strikes Back—Evolution
In the
movie
,
Ash
,
Misty
and
Brock
got an invitation from a
Dragonite
. The invitation included a hologram of a
Nurse Joy
, hypnotized by
Mewtwo
, who told them that Mewtwo was waiting for them on
New Island
and they would be able to reach it from Old Shore Wharf.
When they arrived at Old Shore Wharf, they were told that the boats to New Island wouldn't leave, because of the stormy weather conditions, which were caused by Mewtwo.
