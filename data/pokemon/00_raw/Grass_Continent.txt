The
Grass Continent
(Japanese:
草の大陸
Grass Continent
) is a continent of the
Pokémon world
in the
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series
. It was introduced in
Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness
. There are three known settlements on the continent:
Treasure Town
,
Shaymin Village
, and
Capim Town
. The continent was not named until its appearance in
Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon
.
It is the setting of Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness and
Explorers of Sky
. In Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon, it can be visited at any time on the
Lapras Travel Liner
after purchasing the
Grass Pass
.
