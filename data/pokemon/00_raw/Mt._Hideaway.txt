Mount Hideaway
(Japanese:
ミヤーザーキ山
Mount Miyāzāki
) is an
animated series
-exclusive mountain located near
Pallet Town
. The rugged area is home to
unusually large
Onix
. Something in the soil causes the Onix to grow to gigantic proportions.
It is also a
training
area for
Kanto
Elite Four
member
Bruno
. While training for the
Indigo Plateau Conference
,
Ash
and
Brock
decided to find him and seek his advice on training in
To Master the Onixpected!
.
