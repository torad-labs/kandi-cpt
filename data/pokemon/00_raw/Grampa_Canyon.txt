Grampa Canyon
(Japanese:
グランパキャニオン
Grampa Canyon
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in
Kanto
. It is found right outside of
Neon Town
and is located between the
Safari Zone
and
Cinnabar Island
.
When
Ash
and
his friends
were traveling through the canyon in
Attack of the Prehistoric Pokémon
, they learned that some ancient Pokémon
Fossils
were found recently. Along with
Gary
, the kids began digging in the canyon to look for the Fossils. Meanwhile,
Team Rocket
had planned to blow up the entire canyon to collect the Fossils with dynamite, which would have placed several innocent bystanders in mortal danger.
Thanks to Team Rocket, Ash and
Pikachu
ended up in a hidden cavern found in the canyon. There, they found that five extinct Pokémon were living alive and well. They weren't happy about being disturbed, and eventually an
Aerodactyl
lifted Ash out of the cave. He was saved by his
Charmeleon
evolving
into
Charizard
and
battling
it. As a result of the entire incident, paleontological work for finding any Pokémon Fossils was shut down indefinitely.
In addition to the ancient Pokémon, Ash also found an
Egg
in the canyon. It would eventually hatch into a
Togepi
that would imprint
Misty
as its owner.
