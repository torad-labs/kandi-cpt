Silver Town
(Japanese:
シロガネタウン
Shirogane Town
) is an
animated series
-exclusive location in the
Johto
region
. It is situated at the base of
Mt. Silver
and its main attraction is the annual
Silver Conference
.
At least 30 inhabitants are said to be living in the town. However, the population greatly increases during the Silver Conference, when the town receives several
Trainers
who successfully collected eight
Gym
Badges
from the region's
Gym Leaders
. There is an Athlete's Village, which consists of a huge
Pokémon Center
where Trainers participating in the tournament stay at for the duration of the event. There is also a stadium located nearby, where the Silver Conference matches officially take place.
