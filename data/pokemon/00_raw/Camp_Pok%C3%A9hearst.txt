Camp Pokéhearst
is an
animated series
-exclusive location in the
Kanto
region
that appeared in
Go West Young Meowth
. It is located east of
Hollywood
. It was unnamed in the Japanese version.
There is a
baseball
pitch where the campers practice. The campsite has a projector and large sheet, allowing them to show outdoor movies to the campers.
The outdoor movie area, showing
That Darn Meowth!
Meowth
's earliest memory is waking up alone in the rain in a basket here. He was all alone, and was so hungry that he tried to eat a basket of baseballs. He ended up just knocking it over, causing the baseball coach to string him up from a tree as punishment. From this position, that evening he was able to watch the movie
That Darn Meowth!
, which was being shown as a special treat for the campers, direct from Hollywood. He then grabbed onto the next truck heading towards Hollywood.
