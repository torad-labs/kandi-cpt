Location of Withered Wasteland in
Kanto

Withered Wasteland
(Japanese:
パサパサこうやの街
Dry Wasteland Town
) is the first available area in
Pokémon Pokopia
. It is what remains of
Fuchsia City
after a series of catastrophic
weather
anomalies ravaged the
world
and forced
humans
to take refuge in space.
Ditto
is
released
from its
Poké Ball
in a cave on the southern part of the area, inhabited by
Professor Tangrowth
.
Withered Wasteland is connected to
Palette Town
to the west by a bridge, and
Rocky Ridges
to the north-west, the
Sparkling Skylands
to the north, and
Bleak Beach
to the east through
gates
.

Places of interest
Professor Tangrowth's home
Professor Tangrowth
's home is a cave in the southern part of Withered Wasteland.
Ditto
is released from its
Poké Ball
and meets Professor Tangrowth here. The cave is a large open room with
barren ordinary soil
and
dry field grass
on the ground. On the western side of the cave is a wheelbarrow carrying a pile of
lost relics
collected by Professor Tangrowth. Visible are a
Substitute doll
, a
Leppa Berry
, a Poké Ball, a flower pot and several books. Next to the wheelbarrow are a piece of cloth with four photos on it, a
mushroom lamp
, and a
straw bed
.
Wasteland Pokémon Center
The Wasteland
Pokémon Center
is initially a pile of rubble on a wooden platform, having been torn down by extreme
weather
. It is eventually rebuilt by Ditto and a group of other Pokémon, after which a
note
can be found on the floor inside, alongside a counter with a
cash register
, a
3D printer
, a healing machine, and some furniture.
Team R's hideout
Team R
's hideout is a structure discovered by Professor Tangrowth and Ditto while exploring after
breaking down
a wall of
cracked sandstone
. It sits on a platform, appearing as a lighthouse covered in rust and sand. The machine attached to it leads the
Team Initiation Challenge
by playing voice recordings from a Team R
grunt
and giving out
fake Gym Badges
for completed requests.
Magnemite
is sleeping inside, appearing after the building is gone
Mysterious mural
The mysterious mural is a wall made up of tiles with symbols of all
Unown
forms.
Mysterious slates
can be placed on the tile with a symbol matching the one on its back.
Demographics
While there are no longer any
humans
living here, Withered Wasteland is inhabited by a handful of Pokémon before
Ditto
starts making new
habitats
.
Not counting Ditto and
Squirtle
, Withered Wasteland is initially inhabited by five Pokémon:
Professor Tangrowth
Onix
Drilbur
Magikarp
Magnemite
Additional Pokémon appear in the overworld as Ditto completes requests:
Drifloon
