the Nine-Wick Censer. The Hollowbrook Trading Guild was founded by Calder Rooksby; Perrin Woolgrave is a member.

The Saltwick Choir is led by Elowen Dask, who lives in Pellwater Quay and owns the Amberfen Bell.
