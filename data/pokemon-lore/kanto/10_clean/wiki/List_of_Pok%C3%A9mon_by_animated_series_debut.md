Trivia
- Excluding Porygon2 and Porygon-Z (which are unlikely to make major appearances in the animated series as a result of the EP038 incident, having only appeared in a cameo in the opening of Kyurem VS. The Sword of Justice), all Pokémon from Generation VI and earlier have had a major appearance in either an episode or a movie.
- Generation I is the first generation to have all of its Pokémon debut in the main series, as of Alola, Kanto!.
- Generation VII is the first generation to have all of its Pokémon debut in the main series during the generation.
- Mew is the last Generation I Pokémon to debut in the main series.
- Celebi is the last Generation II Pokémon (excluding Porygon2), as well as the first Mythical Pokémon, to debut in the main series.
- Togetic is the last Generation II Pokémon (excluding Porygon2) that is neither Legendary nor Mythical to debut in the main series.
- Latias is the last Generation III Pokémon to debut in the main series.
- Sceptile is the last Generation III Pokémon that is neither Legendary nor Mythical to debut in the main series.
- Giratina is the only Generation IV Pokémon (excluding Porygon-Z) that has not yet physically debuted in the main series.
- Gallade is the last Generation IV Pokémon (excluding Porygon-Z) that is neither Legendary nor Mythical to debut in the main series.
- Klang is the only Generation V Pokémon, as well as the only Pokémon introduced prior to Generation VIII that is neither Legendary nor Mythical (excluding Porygon2 and Porygon-Z), that has yet to debut in the main series.
- Aegislash is the last Generation VI Pokémon that is neither Legendary nor Mythical to debut in the main series.
- Melmetal is the last Generation VII Pokémon to debut in the main series.
- Generation VIII is the first generation to have none of its Pokémon debut in the main series prior to the start of their corresponding series.
- Generation VIII has the most Pokémon that have not yet debuted in the animated series, with 14.
- Generation III has had the most Pokémon make their animated series debut before the start of the generation, with 16. Generation VII has had the fewest (of generations that had any), with only one.
- Generation II and Generation VIII are the only generations to have Pokémon make their debut across all animated media past their debut generation.
- Sandy Cloak and Trash Cloak Burmy have never appeared in the main series or movies. They were shown only once in a Japanese-exclusive segment of Professor Oak's Lecture.
