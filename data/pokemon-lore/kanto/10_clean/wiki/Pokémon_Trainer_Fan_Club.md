Title: Pokémon Trainer Fan Club

Not to be confused with Pokémon Fan Club.

The Pokémon Trainer Fan Club (Japanese: ポケモントレーナーファンクラブ Pokémon Trainer Fan Club) is a club dedicated to famous Pokémon Trainers. It has branches in Kanto and Hoenn. In Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, it is instead known as the Contest Spectacular Trainer Fan Club (Japanese: コンテストライブトレーナーファンクラブ Contest Live Trainer Fan Club).


== In the games ==


=== Kanto ===

In Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen, the club is located in Saffron City, east of the Poké Mart. Initially, the fan club members will be fans of certain NPCs, such as Gym Leaders, Elite Four members, and the player's Rival. After the player enters the Hall of Fame, some members will still be fans of these characters and will move to the rear of the building, but those to the front of the building will become the player's fans. === Hoenn ===

In Pokémon Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald, the club is located in Lilycove City, to the right of the Pokémon Center. Once the player has defeated the Pokémon League, several members of the club announce themselves as fans of the player. In addition to this, and before the completion of the Pokémon League, there will be other members who are fans of some non-player characters. These include Gym Leaders such as Norman, Elite Four members such as Glacia, the Champion Steven, and also the most recent person to have mixed records with the player's game cartridge.
