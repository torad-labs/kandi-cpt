Title: Jessica (TCG GB)

Jessica (Japanese: ナツキ Natsuki) is one of the three Club Members from the Fighting Club, under Club Master Mitch, in Pokémon Trading Card Game.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
Jessica is not found at the Club the first time the player meets her. Instead, she is training at the table in the Fire Club. She will not appear until Michael is defeated at the Grass Club.
Jessica challenges to a 4-card prize match. Upon defeat, Jessica awards the player with two Colosseum booster packs. It is necessary to defeat Jessica to be able to challenge Mitch.

== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==


=== Love to Battle Deck ===
Main article: Love to Battle Deck (TCG)
Love to Battle Deck (Japanese: バトルだいすきデッキ) is played by Jessica. When challenged, Jessica plays a 4 prize card match. Defeating Jessica gets the player a Psychic Battle booster pack.
Jessica is not available to battle until the rescue of Steve from the GR Lightning Fort.
