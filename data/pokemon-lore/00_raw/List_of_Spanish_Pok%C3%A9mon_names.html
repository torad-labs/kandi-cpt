List of Spanish Pokémon names

  • Italiano

This list is a record of the official Spanish spellings for Pokémon names. Names are listed by number in the order dictated by the National Pokédex.

The Spanish names of most Pokémon are the same as their English names, and are not listed below. Although these names are spelled identically to how they are spelled in English, they are pronounced in a way that approximates their English pronunciation; for example, Skitty is pronounced as "Es-kí-ti".[1] Type: Null and the Paradox Pokémon (with the exceptions of Koraidon and Miraidon) are the only Pokémon that have different names in Spanish than they do in English. However, Mr. Mime was named "Señor Mime" in some episodes of the Latin American dub of the animated series.
