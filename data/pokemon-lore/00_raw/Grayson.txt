Title: Grayson

If you were looking for the voice actor, see Wayne Grayson.

Grayson is a regular at the Game Store in Pokémon Trading Card Game Online. He can be faced in two of the three leagues in the Trainer Challenge for a total of two matches.


== Official biography ==
"Grayson shares his love of cars (especially hot rods) with his League friends  Mick and  Tyson. He's a student at a local college. He's been playing Pokémon since it first came out and is a formidable opponent... though not as formidable as his sister, Cammie."


== League appearances ==


=== Gold League ===
Players face Grayson in the fifth round of the Gold League, after defeating Ali. After defeating Grayson, players move on to face Juji.


=== City Championships ===
Players face Grayson in the third round of the City Championship, after defeating Juji. After defeating Grayson, players move on to face Rika.
