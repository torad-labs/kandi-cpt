Title: Charm (Hisui)

Charm (Japanese: オマツ Omatsu) is a bandit and a member of the Miss Fortune sisters, along with Clover and Coin. She is the eldest member of the group. Prior to becoming a bandit, she was a member of the Galaxy Team.
She bears a striking resemblance to both Agatha and Bertha, but the relationship between them, if any, is unknown.


== In the core series games ==

Charm appears as a side antagonist in Legends: Arceus. She is the eldest of the Miss Fortune sisters. She had previous ties to the Galaxy Team, along with Clover and Coin, but is the only member of the Miss Fortune sisters that did not formerly reside in the Diamond Settlement or the Pearl Settlement. Rather, her previous residence before turning to a life of crime was Jubilife Village. Parallel to the rest of the Miss Fortune sisters, Charm bears some resentment to the Diamond and Pearl Clans. She often reflects to the player that her thievery is pure survival instinct, and that to survive in the harsh landscape of Hisui, people need powerful Pokémon by their sides.

== Trivia ==
In the Japanese version, Charm quotes the waka "Hisakata no" by court poet Ki no Tomonori when she bids her first farewell to the player in the Crimson Mirelands.
In both Chinese versions, she instead quotes the poem "Spring Morning" by Chinese poet Meng Haoran.
