Title: Jett

Jett (Japanese: ジェット Jet) is the CEO of Quasartico Inc. in Lumiose City in Pokémon Legends: Z-A. She seemingly desires to deepen the bonds between people and Pokémon through Quasartico's urban redevelopment plan. Vinnie is her secretary at Quasartico.


== In the core series games ==
It is revealed in the postgame that Jett is the grandmother of Urbain/Taunie. Jett's estranged daughter was his/her mother, but because Jett hadn't spoken to her daughter in years she wasn't aware that she even had a grandchild. After discovering their relation, Jett appoints Urbain/Taunie as the new president of Quasartico Inc.


=== Pokémon ===
Jett sends out a Skiddo during the "world of Pokémon" introduction in front of the train station.


==== Gives away ====
Upon completing Side Mission 195: Restarting Magearna, the player receives Magearna from Jett, along with a Magearnite. It will have Jett as its Original Trainer.

== In the TCG ==

Main article: Jett (M-P Promo 75)
Jett was introduced as a Supporter card in the Pokémon Trading Card Game during the English Mega Evolution Series (the Japanese MEGA Series). It was first released as a Japanese M-P Promotional card, with artwork by GIDORA. It allows the player to draw a card for each of their opponent's Mega Evolution Pokémon ex in play.
