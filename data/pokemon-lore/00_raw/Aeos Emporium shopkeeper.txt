Title: Aeos Emporium shopkeeper

This shopkeeper appears in Pokémon UNITE. She works at Aeos Emporium, a store that sells all manner of items to Trainers participating in Unite Battles.


== In the games ==


=== Appearance ===
The Aeos Emporium shopkeeper is a young adult woman with dark complexion, gray eyes, and shoulder-length wavy brown hair.
She wears a white shirt with orange balloon sleeves, red collars, and red cuffs, paired with a short red skirt behind a yellow half-apron with a Poké Ball pattern tied around her waist with a red ribbon. She sports an orange-and-white newsboy cap and red sneakers with gray soles and white linings, paired with white gloves with thick orange cuffs and black knee-length socks.
She has Poké Ball-shaped cufflinks on her cuffs. She also wears an ID card with a lanyard around his neck.


=== History ===
This character welcomes the player into Aeos Emporium, a generalist shop that sells every kind of item that does not fit the Unite Battle Committee and Zirco Trading shops. Her partner Pokémon is Chatot.
She also appears on the menu where the player is purchasing a Trainer fashion item from the Fashion tab of Aeos Emporium. (The Fashion Tab uses a special menu, so this does not occur for purchasing items from the Fashion Ticket Exchange tab.)
She also appears in the menu where the player manages their treasure chests in Treasure Chest Challenge events, behind the table with the treasure chests but in front of the screen displaying the possible rewards, and is posed such that she appears to be highlighting the screen.


== In the manga ==


=== Pokémon UNITE Manga - Come On Over to Aeos Island! ===
This character appeared in the third chapter, Welcome to the Stadium!. She and her Chatot called over a trainer and their father to Aeos Emporium. The two took a look at the items on display, guided by the shopkeeper. Ultimately, the trainer purchased bags upon bags of items, as well as a new hoodie for herself.
