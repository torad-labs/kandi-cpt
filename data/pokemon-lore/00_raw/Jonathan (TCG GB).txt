Title: Jonathan (TCG GB)

Jonathan (Japanese: マサトシ Masatoshi) is one of the three Club Members from the Fire Club, under Club Master Ken, in Pokémon Trading Card Game.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
This person challenges the player to a 4 prize card match.
Upon defeat, Jonathan awards the player with two Colosseum booster packs.

== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==


=== Immortal Flame Deck ===
Main article: Immortal Flame Deck (TCG)
Immortal Flame Deck (Japanese: ふめつのほのおデッキ) is the only deck played by Jonathan, played as a 4 prize card match. Defeating Jonathan gets the player two Legendary Power booster packs.
Jonathan starts the game trapped in a cage with the other Fire Club members. After GR3 is defeated for the second time, he leaves the Fire Club and all members are freed. Jonathan can be battled immediately upon revisiting the club.
