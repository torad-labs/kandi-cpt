Title: Dino (Ranger)

Dino is a character from Pokémon Ranger.  
He lives in Fall City with his wife; Teresa, and several Skitty.
After all Teresa's Skitty have been found he tells Lunick/ Solana that he wishes for a Pokémon of his own and wonders if a Growlithe would be right for him.
