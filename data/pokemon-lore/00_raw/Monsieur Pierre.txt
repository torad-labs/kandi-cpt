Title: Monsieur Pierre

Monsieur Pierre (Japanese: ムッシュ・ピエール Monsieur Pierre) is a recurring character in Pokémon the Series: XY. He is the announcer of Pokémon Showcases.


== History ==

Pierre debuted in Dreaming a Performer's Dream!, where he was seen calling Aria on stage to give an exhibition performance at the Lagoon Town Pokémon Showcase Rookie Class.
He appeared in Serena's dream in The Green, Green Grass Types of Home!. 
In Battling with Elegance and a Big Smile!, Pierre was seen in a Pokévision video recapping Shauna's victory at the Coumarine Showcase.
In Rivals: Today and Tomorrow!, Pierre appeared on television to announce an upcoming Pokémon Showcase in Dendemille Town.
In A Frolicking Find in the Flowers!, Pierre appeared in Serena's guidebook video to announce that an upcoming Pokémon Showcase would be located in Anistar City.
In Party Dancecapades!, Pierre hosted a party for Performers and their Pokémon which included ballroom dancing followed by a Tag Battle. He was Aria's dance partner. At the end of the party, he announced a Pokémon Showcase was slated to be held in Fleurrh City.
In Master Class Choices!, he hosted the Fleurrh City Pokémon Showcase. Near the end of the episode, he announced Gloire City as the location of the Pokémon Showcase Master Class.


== Character ==
Pierre speaks with a French accent and has a tendency of interspersing French phrases with his regular speech, such as "s'il vous plaît".
At the start of a Pokémon Showcase, he announces the theme of the competition. He is also the one to introduce the Pokémon Performers on stage and to explain the proceedings to the audience. Furthermore, he keeps track of the time the Performers are given during the Theme Performance and hands out the Princess Key to the winner.
He has high enthusiasm for his job. His trademark item is his staff, which his Klefki sits on top of.
