Title: Hocus (Ranger)

Hocus (Japanese: マジック Magic) is a talented magician and a member of the Societea in Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs. He uses a giant Crobat when faced in the Sky Fortress, after using his skills to trap the Ukulele Pichu in a box and create a confusing battleground. He is first encountered in Tilt Village, performing magic tricks for a group of children.
His piece of the armor was stolen by Purple Eyes, and he was found by Murph while on board The Union.
