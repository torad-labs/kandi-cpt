Title: Aira (TCG GB)

If you were looking for the character of the day in XYS05, see Aila.

Aira (Japanese: アイラ Aira) is a member of Team Great Rocket from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. He's one of the Trainers at the GR Water Fort.
In all duels against him, the Small Bench (Japanese: せまいベンチ Narrow Bench) rule is active, thus no more than three Benched Pokémon are allowed at any time.
When defeated, he gives out one Team Rocket's Ambition booster pack. Also, when defeated for the first time, he lets the player advance to the next room, allowing the player to reach Kanoko, the Fort Leader of GR Water Fort.
