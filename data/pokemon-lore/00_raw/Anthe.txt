Title: Anthe

Not to be confused with Anthea.

Anthe (Japanese: シャロン Sharon) is a character who appears in Legends: Arceus. She serves as the clothier of Jubilife Village.
She bears a striking resemblance to Darach, but the relationship between them, if any, is unknown.


== In the core series games ==
Anthe runs a store along Floaro Main Street of Jubilife Village that sells various articles of clothing that the player can use to customize their looks. She is open to new ideas for how to expand her inventory to provide to the people of the village. As the player completes story missions, Anthe will ask the player for their assistance in giving her inspiration for new clothing designs and colors. Once her requests are completed by the player, it will unlock more clothing options in her store.
