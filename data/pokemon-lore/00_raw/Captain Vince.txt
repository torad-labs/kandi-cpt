Title: Captain Vince

Captain Vince (Japanese: キャプテン・ビクトル Captain Viktor) is a character in New Pokémon Snap. He is an explorer who traveled the Lental region and recorded the species of Pokémon he found there, including some of the Illumina Pokémon.


== History ==

100 years prior to the events of New Pokémon Snap, Vince first encountered an Illumina Pokémon as a child. He thereafter endeavoured to meet the other Illumina Pokémon, recording his experiences in the Lental Travelogue. He explored many of the islands in Lental, but had never reached Durice Island.
