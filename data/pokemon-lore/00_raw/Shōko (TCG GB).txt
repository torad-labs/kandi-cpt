Title: Shōko (TCG GB)

If you were looking for the character from Pokémon Adventures, see Shoko.
If you were looking for the voice actress also known as Shokotan, see Shōko Nakagawa.

Shōko (Japanese: ショウコ Shōko) is a member of Team Great Rocket from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. She is one of the Trainers at the GR Fire Fort.
She will not duel against the player unless this uses a deck with four Eevee cards.
When defeated, she gives out one of both Team Rocket's Ambition and We Are Team Rocket booster packs. Also, when defeated for the first time, she frees Courtney and opens the door to the next room, allowing the player to reach Hiderō, the Fort Leader of GR Fire Fort.
