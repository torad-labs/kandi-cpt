Title: Philippe

Philippe (Japanese: ジプソ Gypso) is a character from Pokémon Legends: Z-A. He is Corbeau's second-in-command at the Rust Syndicate, as well as the organization's former leader. He specializes in Steel-type Pokémon.
His partner Pokémon is Skarmory.


== In the core series games ==


=== Appearance ===
An imposing man, Philippe is distinguished by his straight posture and wide, rotund body. He has a light complexion, a squarish face, thick eyebrows, and round eyes with large, silver pupils. He styles his black hair in a short mohawk, with gray stripes running across it. He also has mutton chops on the sides of his face, with two tufts of gray hair resembling spikes on each side. 
Philippe wears a custom black three-piece suit over a dark gray dress shirt with a light gray circular pattern that resembles chainmail, as well as black shoes. There are metal strips resembling staples sewn onto various parts of his suit jacket, including the lapels, sleeves, quarters, and on the back. These metal strips are also present on his waistcoat and the buckles of his shoes. Philippe's golden necktie appears to be several blocks of metal stringed together instead of a singular piece of cloth. He keeps a golden pocket square inside his suit's breast pocket, and wears a pin of the Rust Syndicate logo on the left lapel, where he keeps his Key Stone.


=== Personality ===
Philippe is a soft-spoken and polite, yet stern man. Through various conversations with him, it is evident that Philippe puts great emphasis on politeness, upholding one's promises, repaying one's deeds, and following the rules. This demeanor seems to allow Philippe to be more effective in coercing others into doing the Rust Syndicate's bidding, such as when he offered Urbain/Taunie a loan to shoot promotional videos for Hotel Z.
Despite being older than his boss, Philippe is loyal to Corbeau and greatly admires him, formally referring to him as "Mr. Corbeau" (Japanese: カラスバさま Karasuba-sama). Philippe gave away his position as the head of the Rust Syndicate to Corbeau and generally follows his orders to the letter. However, according to one of the Rust Syndicate grunts, Philippe also tries to maintain a boundary with his boss and not blindly do as he says; Philippe looks to his partner Skarmory as a reminder of this.


=== History ===


==== Pokémon Legends: Z-A ====
Prior to the events of Pokémon Legends: Z-A, in his early years, Philippe's physical strength and knack for Pokémon battles made him a force to be reckoned, and allowed him to put the other city ruffians under his command. However, being strong alone wasn't enough to make a living, and Philippe had to rely on Lysandre for financial support. Philippe later caught wind of Corbeau and how he negotiated with Lysandre to invest in various businesses he set up. Philippe and his gang tried to end Corbeau and his ventures, but he and his Venipede were able to outsmart and beat them every time, eventually forcing them to admit defeat, with Philippe relinquishing his position as the Rust Syndicate's boss to him.
The player is contacted by Philippe after reaching Rank D in the Z-A Royale, who confirms that their friend Urbain/Taunie has unpaid loans to the Rust Syndicate, and invites them and Team MZ to the Rust Syndicate Office to discuss the matter. Philippe reveals that he was the one who offered a $100,000 loan to Urbain/Taunie after they helped another Rust Syndicate member, and insists that the loan's terms are written in full despite Lida protesting about the tenfold interest rate.
Philippe later appears in Hotel Richissime as one of the guests invited to Jacinthe's "Le Super-Tournoi de Jacinthe" tournament, and is defeated by Ivor in the first round. He also reveals that Corbeau had asked him for training before the tournament, much to Corbeau's embarrassment.
After the tournament, Philippe appears to block the player and Lida from battling a Rogue Mega Starmie, as Corbeau has sent him to take out the Starmie himself. He challenges the player to another battle, reasoning that he won't be able to do anything to placate the Mega Starmie if his Pokémon happen to be knocked out. Afterward, Philippe relays his and Corbeau's backstory to the player and Lida, before stopping himself and giving Lida a Water Stone to evolve her Staryu as hush money.
During the game's final act, Philippe, Corbeau, and the rest of the Rust Syndicate help the player climb a building to reach Zygarde by forming a human ladder. In the credits, Philippe and the other major characters are seen helping clear the rubble after the incident with Prism Tower. 


=== Pokémon ===
Philippe keeps his Pokémon in Heavy Balls and will Mega Evolve his Skarmory at the first opportunity.

== In the TCG ==

Main article: Philippe (Chaos Rising 79)
Philippe was introduced as a Supporter card in the Pokémon Trading Card Game during the English Mega Evolution Series (the Japanese MEGA Series). It was first released in the Japanese Ninja Spinner expansion before debuting in the English Chaos Rising expansion, with artwork by Souichirou Gunjima. A Full Art ultra rare version with artwork by the same illustrator was released in the same sets. It allows players to attach up to 2 Basic  (Metal) Energy cards from their discard pile to 1 of their Metal-type Pokémon.

Klefki is the only Pokémon Philippe uses that is not capable of Mega Evolving.
