Title: Brittney

For other uses, see Brittany.

Brittney is a regular at the Game Store in Pokémon Trading Card Game Online. She can be faced in two of the three leagues in the Trainer Challenge for a total of two matches.


== Official biography ==
"Brittney likes to act and sing in school plays, and she dabbles in photography. She likes to take pictures of flowers and animals. She is best friends with Ali-unless they are playing Pokémon!"


== League appearances ==


=== Platinum League ===
Players face Brittney in the ninth round of the Platinum League, after defeating Nathan. After defeating Brittney, players move on to face Cammie.


=== City Championship ===
Players face Brittney in the fifth round of the City Championship, after defeating Rika. After defeating Brittney, players move on to face Otis.


== Brittney's decks ==


=== Brittney's XY deck ===
Unlike many opponents, there are only two versions of the deck in the Trainer Challenge, with the first version being used for both Easy and Medium play.
