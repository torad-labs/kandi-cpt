Title: Iscan

Iscan (Japanese: ススキ Susuki) is a warden tasked with watching over a special Basculegion found in the Cobalt Coastlands of Hisui. He is a member of the Diamond Clan.


== In the core series games ==
Iscan lives by the sea, in a secluded area of Aipom Hill. He is timid and easily frightened, and as such, he dislikes Ghost-type Pokémon, with the exception of Basculegion. He and his Basculegion saved Palina—a warden from the Pearl Clan—when he was younger. The two of them soon began a romantic relationship, which they keep a secret from the rest of the clan members, since they are aware of Palina's bad reputation amongst the clans and how Palina did not want Iscan to also get a bad reputation because of her.


=== Pokémon ===


==== In service to ====
As warden, Iscan cares for a special Basculegion that acts as a Ride Pokémon to assist humans.

== Trivia ==
Iscan is the only known member of the Diamond Clan whose home is located outside of the Diamond Settlement.
Iscan is the only warden from the Diamond Clan that cannot be battled.
His dislike of Ghost-type Pokémon is ironic as the Pokémon he is tasked with watching over, Basculegion, is part Ghost-type.
