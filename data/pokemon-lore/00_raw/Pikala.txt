Title: Pikala

Pikala (Japanese: ピカーラ  Pikala) is a recurring character who first appeared in A Plethora of Pikachu!. She is a Pokémon Trainer who lives in Pikachu Valley with all of her Pikachu.


== History ==
Pikala debuted in A Plethora of Pikachu!, where she met Ash, Kiawe, and Mimo when they visited Pikachu Valley. To give her visitors the full experience, she gave them each Pikachu-themed ears and tails to wear as well as giving them their own "Pika-speak" names, Pikash (Japanese: サトピ Satopi), Pikawe (Japanese: カピ Kapi), and Pikamo (Japanese: ホピ Hopi), respectively. Later, when Team Rocket attempted to steal all of the Pikachu, Pikala, with the help of her Shiny Pikachu, nicknamed the Boss, sent the trio blasting off using Catastropika.
She reappeared during a flashback in Beauty is Only Crystal Deep!, where she had her Pikanium Z stolen by a Pheromosa.
In Battle Royal 151!, Pikala entered the Manalo Conference with another one of her Pikachu, named Bolt (Japanese: ボルト Bolt). However, it was defeated by Jessie's Mimikyu during the Battle Royal preliminary round, thus eliminating Pikala from the tournament.


== Character ==
Pikala is an eccentric fan of Pikachu. She even dresses up in a Pikachu outfit and puts "Pika" into all of her sentences, referring to this manner of speech as "Pika-speak". She also has the ability to know which region a Pikachu comes from by touching their fur.

== Trivia ==
The Boss, Curly, and Pikala's other Pikachu wear hairstyles similar to the ones that can be given to the partner Pikachu and Eevee in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!.
Pikala's way of adding "Pika" to her sentences mimics the mannerisms of Frank, another character of the day from Pokémon the Series: XY who is also a big Pikachu fan.
In the Brazilian Portuguese dub, Pikala puts "Chu" into all of her sentences instead of "Pika".
Bolt's haircut and name are the same as those of the character Boruto Uzumaki from the manga and anime series of the same name.
