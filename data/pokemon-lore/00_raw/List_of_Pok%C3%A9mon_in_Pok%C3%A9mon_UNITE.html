The following is a list of Pokémon found in Pokémon UNITE by National Pokédex number.

There are currently 93[note 1] playable licenses across both versions of the game of which:

  • 93 are playable in the international version of the game. There are 88 unique species of Pokémon in the game, four Mega Evolved Pokémon which share Holowear with their base species, and one species with two licenses.
  • 77 are playable in the Chinese version of the game. There 74 unique species of Pokémon in the game, two Mega Evolved Pokémon which share Holowear with their base species, and one species with two licenses.

Defeating wild Pokémon is the primary way of collecting Aeos energy. They will generally attack Trainers' Pokémon that come near or attack them and chase after them a limited distance. As the Unite Battle progresses, some will despawn while other, more powerful Pokémon will spawn. Pokémon marked with an asterisk (*) only appear at the Final Stretch or at the last minute of the match as the evolved form of previous wild Pokémon, while points marked with an asterisk are increased points during that stage. Pokémon in bold are playable in the Catch 'Em in Mer Stadium stage. The following is a list of wild Pokémon that can appear.

Special wild Pokémon

Special wild Pokémon will give the Pokémon that knocked them out buffs. These buffs include damage shields, increased damage against wild Pokémon with low HP, and decreasing the movement speed of enemies hit by the buffed Pokémon. Other wild Pokémon will assist the Pokémon that knocked them out by lowering the opposing team's goal's defenses. This allows goals to be scored faster.
