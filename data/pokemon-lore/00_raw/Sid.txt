Title: Sid

This article is about the movie character. For Secret ID, see Trainer ID number.

Sid (Japanese: ショウタ Shōta) is a major character who appeared in the movie Destiny Deoxys.


== In the movies ==


=== History ===


==== Destiny Deoxys ====
Sid, along with his friends Rafe, Rebecca, Audrey and Kathryn, came to LaRousse City to compete at the Battle Tower. While there, he met Ash and his friends. Once he saw May, he became smitten with her, and throughout the movie he did his best to garner her attention, but with little success. Once at the Battle Tower, Sid and Rafe were assigned a Double Battle against Ash and Tory Lund using their Blastoise and Blaziken against Ash's Pikachu and Torkoal (who was borrowed by Tory at the time). Thanks to Sid's Blastoise, he and Rafe won the match.
Later, Deoxys arrived and unleashed numerous clones to abduct every human and Pokémon in sight, and when one of the clones went after May, Sid sacrificed himself to save her, and was taken prisoner by Deoxys. Thanks to the efforts of Ash and his friends, Sid and the others were saved. When energy needed to be restored to the city, Sid helped by having his Blastoise use Hydro Pump to move the windmills. Even after everything was resolved, May still refused to give him a hug.


==== The Rise of Darkrai ====
Sid briefly reappeared in a montage in The Rise of Darkrai, which chronicled the preceding nine movies.


=== Character ===
Sid comes off as a jolly guy who is willing to act in times of need. He also has a crush on May and tries his best to win her over. Unfortunately for him, May does not reciprocate his feelings, and treats him more as a friend.

== Trivia ==
Sid is similar to Tierno from the animated series. Both have similar builds and outfits, both own a Blastoise, and both have a crush on Ash’s female traveling companion at the time—Tierno towards Serena, and Sid towards May.
