Title: Yōsuke (TCG GB)

Yōsuke (Japanese: ヨウスケ Yōsuke) is a member of Team Great Rocket from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. He is one of the Trainers in the GR Psychic Stronghold.
Yōsuke refuses to duel against the player unless their deck has at least six Gastly and Haunter cards in total—Dark Haunter cards do not count toward this total.
When defeated, he gives out one of both We Are Team Rocket and Legendary Power booster packs.
