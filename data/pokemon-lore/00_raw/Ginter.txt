Title: Ginter

Ginter (Japanese: ギンナン Ginnan) is the head of the Ginkgo Guild. He sells various sets of items to the player.
He bears a striking resemblance to Volkner, but the relationship between the two, if any, is unknown.


== In the core series games ==
Ginter, along with Tuli, manages the Ginkgo Guild cart in Jubilife Village. He sells a variety of rare items, several of which are not found anywhere else in Hisui, such as the mechanical appliances needed to change Rotom's form. When listing his offerings, he will often use descriptions of the items he is selling, rather than the items' actual names. 
As of version 1.1.0, Ginter will have three items for sale at a time. Items like the mechanical appliances can be only purchased once and will never appear in stock again. Every time the player catches 20 Pokémon, Ginter's inventory will refresh, and a new set of items will be available.
