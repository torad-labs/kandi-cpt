Title: John (TCG GB)

John (Japanese: タカシ Takashi) is one of the three Club Members from the Fire Club, under Club Master Ken, in Pokémon Trading Card Game.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
This person challenges the player to a 4 prize card match.
Upon defeat, this person awards the player with two Evolution booster packs.

== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==


=== Flame Festival Deck ===
Main article: Flame Festival Deck (TCG)
Flame Festival Deck (Japanese: ほのおのおまつりデッキ) is the only deck played by John, played as a 4 prize card match. Defeating John gets the player two Psychic Battle booster packs.
John starts the game trapped in a cage with the other Fire Club members. After GR3 is defeated for the second time, he leaves the Fire Club and all members are freed. John still cannot be battled at this point, but will be able to accept challenges after freeing Steve at the GR Lightning Fort.
