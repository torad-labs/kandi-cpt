Title: Nate (Battle Revolution)

Nate (Japanese: ジェイ Jay) is the male default character that can be unlocked at the start of the game as a Rental Pass in Pokémon Battle Revolution, as well as the default name for the Young Boy character style on Custom Passes. He is a Novice by default.


== In the games ==
Nate dons a Summer Cap A, Summer T-shirt A, Summer Wristband A, Summer Jeans A, Summer Shoes A, and a Summer Bag A as default gear.
