Title: Frontier

This article is about the main setting of Pokémon Champions. For a similar search, see Battle Frontier.

Frontier (Japanese: フロンティアシティ Frontier City) is the setting of Pokémon Champions. Kajima is the city's current mayor. All of the battles and events in the city are managed and operated by the Battle Stadium Organization, or BSO for short.


== Places of interest ==


=== Battle Arena ===
The Battle Arena (Japanese: バトルアリーナ Battle Arena) is an arena where Pokémon Trainers take part in battles. Trainers who reach the Champion Tier earn the title of Champion. Caraway has been the owner of the Battle Arena for half a year. It is one of the locations where battles can take place, along with the Gym Court.


=== Cordy's Personal Gym ===
Cordy's Personal Gym (Japanese: コーディのパーソナルジム Cordy's Personal Gym) is a Personal Gym run by Cordy. It includes the front desk managed by Tatora and a Gym Court in the back. At the start of the game, the player is made the manager of the gym. Here, the player can train their Pokémon using VP or Training Tickets, use the Replica Team feature, and complete battle tutorials. The Gym Court is one of the locations where battles can take place, along with the Battle Arena.


=== Roster Ranch ===
Main article: Roster Ranch
Roster Ranch is a ranch where the player can recruit and return Pokémon, as well as collect Pokémon from and send back Pokémon to Pokémon HOME. Kitt is the face of the ranch.
