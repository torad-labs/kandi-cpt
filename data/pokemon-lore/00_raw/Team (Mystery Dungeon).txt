Title: Team (Mystery Dungeon)

Rescue teams (Japanese: 救助隊(きゅうじょたい) rescue teams), exploration teams (Japanese: 探検隊(たんけんたい) exploration teams), and (Japanese: 冒険団(ぼうけんだん) adventure squads) are groups of two to four Pokémon in the Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series. Each team usually has a personal goal they seek to fulfill. They are also found in the Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Ginji's Rescue Team, Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Blazing Exploration Team, and  Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Darkness manga and in the animated specials Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Team Go-Getters Out of the Gate!, Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time & Darkness and Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky - Beyond Time & Darkness.

== Trivia ==

In Japanese official guidebooks for Explorers of Time, Darkness and Sky, all Exploration Team members are assigned a gender. This includes Pokémon that are never referred to as such through in-game dialogue or gameplay.
Male characters revealed this way include Team Flame's Bellsprout, Team Tasty's Wurmple, Team Glee's Togepi, as well as all of Team Raider, Team Razor Wind, Team Seedgey and Team Slacker.
Female characters revealed this way include Team Flame's Ponyta and Camerupt.
The aforementioned guides also reveal that Gallade, Togepi and Machoke are leaders of their respective teams, while Team Razor Wind, Team Seedgey, Team Ebony, Team Poochy and Team Slacker have no particular leader.
