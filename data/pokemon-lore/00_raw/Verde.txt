Title: Verde

Verde (Japanese: ベルデ Verde) is a Cipher Peon who appears in Pokémon Colosseum. He is first encountered at the Mayor's House in Phenac City along with Bluno, Rosso, Miror B., Folly and Trudly. Leaving Wes to Folly and Trudly, Miror B. departs for Pyrite Town. Upon beating Folly and Trudly, Wes exits the Mayor's House to find the three Peons blocking the exits to the city. Verde occupies the spot in front the city's western entrance. Wes must choose one of the three Peons to battle. The other two run away and disappear for quite some time.  If he chooses to fight Verde, he will be known as a Mystery Troop member during the first battle rather than a Cipher Peon. If Wes snags Verde's Shadow Bayleef, but loses all his Pokémon before the end of the battle, he may attempt to fight an alternate Peon, but they will not carry their Shadow Pokémon.
If Wes chooses to fight Verde in Phenac City, he will appear for a rematch in Realgam Tower disguised as a receptionist behind the desk in the tower's casino. After he is beaten by Wes he vanishes and is not seen again, unless Shadow Bayleef wasn't snagged.
Should Wes choose to fight Bluno or Rosso in Phenac then Verde will show up in either Snagem Hideout or Shadow PKMN Lab (chosen at random) after beating the game.
He is dressed uniquely as compared to most other Cipher Peons. His outfit is green, rather than the usual light blue.

== Trivia ==


=== Name origin ===
Verde is the word for green in a number of Romance languages. The choice of his name and the color of his uniform reflects the fact that he is the owner of a Shadow Bayleef.
