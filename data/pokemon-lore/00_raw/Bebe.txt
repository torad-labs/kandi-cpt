Title: Bebe

Bebe (Japanese: ミズキ Mizuki) is a computer technician who lives in Hearthome City in Sinnoh. An expert in computing, she created a Pokémon Storage System, succeeding Lanette's storage system.


== In the core series games ==

Bebe was introduced in Generation IV as an expert computer technician living in Hearthome City in the Sinnoh region. It was she who developed Sinnoh's Pokémon Storage System based on the previous designs of Bill and Lanette. Her house, which is next to the Pokémon Center in Hearthome, is not quite as cluttered as Lanette's. Bebe is good friends with Hayley, who owns a ranch in which Pokémon can be stored, and they still keep in contact with each other.
After the player obtains the National Pokédex, she will give them a level 5 Eevee in Diamond and Pearl. This Eevee came from a good friend in Johto before being passed onto the player. In Pokémon Platinum, the Eevee is at level 20, and available right away, without the need of the National Pokédex.
In Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl, she is notably met earlier, in Jubilife City. After the player defeats the two Team Galactic Grunts, she upgrades their Pokémon Storage System so it can be accessed anywhere, except in Gyms and in the interior of the Pokémon League. She also gives the player Ball Capsules. Like in the original games, she will give the player a level 5 Eevee once the player has obtained the National Pokédex.

===== Diamond & Pearl arc =====
In Double Trouble with Dialga and Palkia V, Bebe appears alongside Hayley after Bebe's house was transported near Hayley's ranch. This was a result of Dialga and Palkia causing a distortion in the space-time continuum while fighting each other.


===== Platinum arc =====
She then later introduces herself to Diamond and Pearl while helping Daisy Oak heal Platinum's Pokémon from the damage caused at the Spear Pillar.

== Trivia ==
Bebe giving the main character an Eevee is a reference to Bill, as he gives the main character an Eevee in Generation II and Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver.
In Pokémon Diamond and Pearl and Pokémon Platinum, Bebe shares the same sprite with the Battle Girl Trainer class.
Her incarnation in the TCG and Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl is based on that of the Battle Girl, but with a different hairstyle, blue shorts, and no gloves.
In the Japanese version, Bebe speaks in Kansai dialect, much like Bill. This may indicate that, like him, she grew up in Johto.
