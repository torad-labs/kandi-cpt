This is a list of Pokémon in the order dictated by the Crown Tundra regional Pokédex.

The Crown Tundra Pokédex is one of three regional Pokédexes featured in Pokémon Sword and Shield, along with the Galar Pokédex and the Isle of Armor Pokédex. It was added to the game in The Crown Tundra expansion and contains 210 Pokémon. The Crown Tundra Pokédex also appears as one of the regional Pokédexes in the mobile version of Pokémon HOME, where it can only be filled with Pokémon that originate from Sword and Shield.

The 210 Pokémon in the Pokédex follow this breakdown:

  • 74 are exclusive to the Crown Tundra (the 5 new and 66 returning Pokémon added in Version 1.3.0, plus the three Swords of Justice, not counting the returning Pokémon added that are not included in the Pokédex).
  • 136 also appear in other areas in Galar.

Only Pokémon in the Galar, Isle of Armor, and Crown Tundra Pokédexes, as well as a select few outside of the aforementioned Pokédexes, can be transferred from Pokémon HOME to Pokémon Sword and Shield.

Although the in-game Pokédex list shows the Pokémon with the numbers listed below, the individual entries for each Pokémon instead display their Galar Pokédex numbers, or no number at all for Pokémon not in the Galar Pokédex.

Trivia

  • The Crown Tundra Pokédex lists Umbreon before Espeon and Glaceon before Leafeon, despite the fact that the reverse is true in all other Pokédex listings these Pokémon appear in (including the Galar Pokédex).
  • Druddigon is the only Pokémon to appear in both the Isle of Armor Pokédex and the Crown Tundra Pokédex but not the main Galar Pokédex.
  • Despite being found as an overworld spawn in the Frigid Sea, Wailmer is not in the Crown Tundra Pokédex. It is the only Pokémon that can be found through random encounters in the Crown Tundra to not appear in this Pokédex.
  • The Crown Tundra Pokédex includes the most Legendary Pokémon of any regional Pokédex in the core series, with a total of 14.
