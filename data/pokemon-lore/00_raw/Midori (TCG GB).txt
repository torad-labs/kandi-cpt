Title: Midori (TCG GB)

Midori (Japanese: ミドリ Midori) is a member of Team Great Rocket from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. She is one of the Trainers at the GR Grass Fort.
When defeated, she gives out one We Are Team Rocket booster pack. Also, when defeated for the first time, she frees Rick, the Club Master of Science Club, from his prison, also allowing the player to advance to the next room, in which Yūta is.
