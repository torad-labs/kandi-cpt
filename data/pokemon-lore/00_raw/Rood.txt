Title: Rood

Rood (Japanese: ロット Rood) is one of the Seven Sages of Team Plasma.


== In the core series games ==
In Pokémon Black and White, Rood is encountered along with Ghetsis, Bronius, and several Team Plasma Grunts at Team Plasma's hideout in Castelia City. He leaves the building shortly after the arrival of the player. He later appears with all the other sages at N's Castle, blocking the player's way to N. However, he is stopped by the arrival of the Unova Gym Leaders as they clear a path for the player. After Ghetsis has been defeated, Rood can be found on Route 18, where he gives the player TM32 (Double Team) and is arrested by Looker.
In Pokémon Black 2 and White 2, Rood is encountered living with Anthea, Concordia and several Grunts in the original Team Plasma's safehouse in Driftveil City. After losing in a battle with the player, he gives the player N's Zorua. Shortly afterwards, he appears on Route 6 after the player has encountered Cobalion. Later, he appears leading the members of the original Team Plasma against Ghetsis's new Team Plasma at the Giant Chasm, occupying their forces to allow the player to reach Ghetsis himself.

=== Pokémon Adventures ===


==== Black & White arc ====
Rood is one of the Seven Sages of Team Plasma. Three years prior to the start of the Black & White arc, he was tasked with finding information on the Mythical Pokémon Victini so that its power may be used to bring victory for Team Plasma. Rood debuted in person in Letting Go, where he and the other sages were seen in Accumula Town after Ghetsis gave a speech convincing people to release their Pokémon.
Although not seen in person, in At Liberty on Liberty Garden, Rood sent three of his subordinate Team Plasma Grunts to Liberty Garden so they could capture Victini. The Grunts were eventually defeated by Black, who used Victini's ability to bring victory to help him defeat the Grunts' Pokémon. 
In Cold Hard Truth, Team Plasma launched their attack on the Pokémon League, surrounding the building with N's Castle. As a form of mockery and display of Team Plasma's power, the Gym Leaders were hoisted on cross-like fixtures and displayed in front of the masses. While Black went ahead to face N, a group of regular Trainers led by Hiker Andy fought the sages in his place. Together, Andy and the others managed to free the Gym Leaders, forcing Rood and the other sages to flee. 


==== Black 2 & White 2 arc ====
Two years later, Rood and the other sages were sought after by the International Police members Blake and Looker, who aimed to arrest them along with the other remaining Team Plasma members. Unlike the other sages, Rood, along with Gorm, decided to side with the members of Team Plasma who believed in N's ideals.
In Frozen World, Rood arrived in Castelia City shortly after it was frozen by the Plasma Frigate. There, he met Whitley, a former Team Plasma Grunt tasked with guarding a memory card containing data on a device that could counteract the Pokémon-controlling Colress Machine. Whitley revealed she lost the memory card, but it was found by Hugh, a classmate of hers. Rood had his subordinates search the unconscious Hugh's body, only to discover it wasn't on his person. Whitley came to conclusion he must have left it behind at the Aspertia City's Trainers' School. Rood decided to leave immediately, but Whitley feared leaving her classmates behind would expose her identity as a member of Team Plasma. Rood had N's Zorua take Whitley's form to fool the classmates long enough for them to find the memory card. Before they could act, Whitley pushed Rood out of the way of an incoming Mandibuzz, leading to her being captured and taken aboard the Plasma Frigate, allowing Rood to escape.
In Abyssal Ruins, Hugh awakened at the Aspertia Trainers' School. Rood requested Hugh reveal the location of the memory card, but the boy refused as Team Plasma stole his younger sister's Purrloin years ago. Rood assured Hugh that Purrloin was safe in the care of someone else and promised to retrieve it once the memory card was dealt with. Before Hugh could respond, a group of Plasma Grunts broke into the room in order to stop them. Hugh reluctantly gave up the key to his room and told Rood to get the memory card while he stayed behind and kept the Grunts busy. 
In Deduction Time, after the memory card was retrieved, Rood and Hugh fled to the white Team Plasma's hideout and activated the anti-Colress Machine device. Afterward, Rood went to the Giant Chasm, where he confronted Colress, telling him that his plans had been foiled. The device successfully freed Kyurem from Colress's control, only for it to absorb Reshiram and transform into White Kyurem.
In Epilogue, after Team Plasma's defeat, Rood was arrested and taken into custody by the International Police. He was visited by Whitley, who returned to her normal life after her friends agreed to keep her status as a former Team Plasma member a secret. Rood informed Whitley that N would be released on bail so he could work to return the Pokémon liberated by Team Plasma back to their original owners.

== Trivia ==
Rood is the only sage that gives a TM that raises the user's stats by a total of one stage. The TMs given by the other sages either raise one stat two stages or two stats one stage.
In Black and White, Rood quotes lines from Chinese classical text:
 "Superior men understand what is right. Inferior men understand what will sell." refers to the quote 君子喻於義，小人喻於利 in the Analects, an ancient philosophical text of sayings attributed to Confucius.
"Constant dripping wears away the stone." is a Chinese idiom with its roots in the book of Shizi, an eclectic Chinese classic.
Rood can be seen as Zinzolin's parallel in that Rood is a sage who decided to stay with the sect that remains loyal to N while Zinzolin joins the other sect that is loyal to Ghetsis.
The Zorua that Rood gives to the player in Black 2 and White 2 has 30 IVs in every stat.
