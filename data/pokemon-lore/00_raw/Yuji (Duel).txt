Title: Yuji (Duel)

Yuji (Japanese: ユージ Yūji) is a character in Pokémon Duel. He is the owner of Ulex.


== Personality ==
Yuji is a big fan of Sharon, often referring to her as "Sharry", despite her disliking it. He is sensitive to comments about his weight.
He lowers his guard when speaking to Sharon, but otherwise exudes confidence in his dueling abilities when he faces other challengers.
