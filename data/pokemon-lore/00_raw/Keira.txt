Title: Keira

Keira (Japanese: ミミィ Mimmy) is a character in Pokémon Diamond, Pearl, and Platinum. She is one of the judges of the Pokémon Super Contests. Her appearance is designed after the Idol Trainer class.


== In the games ==
Keira is a member of the panel of judges for the Pokémon Super Contests. She seems to be friends with Johanna. When entering Hearthome City for the first time, the player comes across Keira's Buneary, which is being chased by her Trainer. After reclaiming her Buneary, Keira thanks the player and asks them to visit her at the Super Contest Hall. There, Keira is seen talking with Johanna. She rewards the player with a Glitter Powder and wonders if they will be the next big Contest star.

== In the manga ==


=== Pokémon Diamond and Pearl Adventure! ===
In the Pokémon Diamond and Pearl Adventure! manga, Keira appeared in Beauty Contest: The Pokémon Super Contest!! along with her fellow judges during a Pokémon Super Contest in which Hareta and Jun were participating. She was not pleased with their performance in the Dance Competition.


=== Pokémon Adventures ===


==== Diamond & Pearl arc ====
Keira makes her first appearance in Boogying with Buneary, where she picks up her Buneary that had run off earlier and punched Pearl in the jaw. She apologizes and makes up for it by giving Pearl a Glitter Powder. She and her Buneary appear again in Perturbed by Pachirisu, judging the Normal Rank Coolness Contest.  She is later seen with her fellow judges at the Lost Tower after having been sent there because of the fight between Dialga and Palkia that caused time and space to distort.

== Trivia ==
In the Japanese version of the Pokémon Adventures manga, Earma's nickname sounds exactly the same as Keira's name, with the only difference being that Earma's name is spelled in hiragana, while Keira's is in katakana.
