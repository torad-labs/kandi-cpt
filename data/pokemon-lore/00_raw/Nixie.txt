Title: Nixie

Nixie (Japanese: ミナモ Minamo) is a character in Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs. She is found at Lapras Beach. She is friends with Lapras and will allow the player to travel out to Coral Sea. 
When the player meets Nixie for the first time, she is having an argument with Nema, who is trying to get Nixie to let her ride on Lapras into Coral Sea to obtain a UFO that crashed there. Eventually, the player is allowed access to the Coral Sea.
She plays a similar sole to Lapras Guy in Fiore.
