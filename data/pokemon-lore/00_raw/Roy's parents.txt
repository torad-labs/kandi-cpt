Title: Roy's parents

Roy's parents are characters of the day in Pokémon Horizons: The Series.


== History ==


==== Pre-series ====
Revealed in a flashback, they live on an island together with their son and his grandfather. They were sailors and had to go on a long voyage, leaving Roy in the care of his grandfather.  


==== Pokémon Horizons: The Series ====
Roy's parents were first mentioned in A New Song for Fuecoco, where Roy explained his reasons for liking singing.
