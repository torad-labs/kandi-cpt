Title: Ali

If you were looking for the Altaria nicknamed Ali, see Lisia → Pokémon.

Ali is a regular at the Game Store in Pokémon Trading Card Game Online. She can be faced in one of the three leagues in the Trainer Challenge for a total of one match.


== Official biography ==
"Ali is best friend with Brittney and usually enjoys having Juji around-unless she's being annoying! Brittney taught her to play Pokémon TCG some time ago, but she's only really gotten into it recently. She's also into fortune-telling, so she's handy with cards-her shuffle is a sight to see!"


== League appearances ==


=== Gold League ===
Players face Ali in the fourth round of the Gold League, after defeating Calvin. After defeating Ali, players move on to face Grayson.

=== Daily Challenge ===
Ali assists players with looking up information regarding "Active Challenges" and "Level Progress" in the Daily Challenge feature of the Pokémon Trading Card Game Online, which may be viewed in the center icon/tab under Profile.
