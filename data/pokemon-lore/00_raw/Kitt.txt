Title: Kitt

Kitt (Japanese: ルッコラ Rucola) is a rancher who will help the player at Roster Ranch in Pokémon Champions.


== Appearance ==
Kitt is a young freckled woman with fair complexion and baby blue eyes. Her hair is blonde with braids on the sides tied with red-and-white ribbons near each tip. 
Her outfit consists of denim shortalls with a ruffle sleeve black blouse underneath. She sports a red cowboy hat and brown cowboy boots that are both trimmed with beige fur, complemented by a pair of white gloves with a line of red hem by the cuffs. A red belt with a gold plaque buckle featuring an image of a Bouffalant hangs by her waist where a grooming brush with red bristles can be seen dangling by the right side of her belt. A tattoo of a stylized Bouffalant can be seen on her upper left arm. 
Kitt is commonly shown wielding a four-tined red pitchfork with a wooden shaft and a red handle grip by her left hand.


== Artwork ==


== Trivia ==
The original artwork of Kitt released on February 27, 2026 erroneously did not include her freckles. An updated version was released on March 9, 2026, alongside an apology on the official @poke_times X (Twitter) account.
