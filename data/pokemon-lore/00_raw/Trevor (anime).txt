Title: Trevor (anime)

Trevor (Japanese: トロバ Trova) is a recurring character in Pokémon the Series. He is a Pokémon Trainer and photographer from Lumiose City and a rival of Ash.


== History ==


=== Pokémon the Series: XY ===

Trevor debuted in Summer of Discovery! along with Shauna and Tierno, and his Charmander. After Ash and his friends saw the complete Kanto first partner Pokémon trio, Trevor came down from the stairs, exhausted from chasing Charmander. Later, he had a battle with Clemont and his Chespin. The battle didn't continue after Chespin was accidentally burned by Charmander.
In Day Three Blockbusters!, Trevor fished a Wailord during the fishing competition which gave his team the win. He was also in a Pokévision video along with his teammates but did not win.
In Foggy Pokémon Orienteering!, Trevor, along with Team Squirtle and their rival team, Team Froakie, put aside their differences to find Bonnie and Pikachu.
In Battling Into the Hall of Fame!, he had a Team Battle with Ash, Serena, and Clemont. Despite putting up a great fight, Trevor was the first one to get defeated and then Shauna and Tierno got defeated afterwards.
In Performing with Fiery Charm!, Trevor watched Serena competing in the Dendemille Town Pokémon Showcase Rookie Class along with Tierno and Shauna.
In A Legendary Photo Op!, Trevor met up with Ash and his friends in Mt. Molteau. He tried getting Charmeleon to calm down but it didn't work. He then battled Ash and lost. He revealed to the group that he sought out to take a picture of the Legendary Moltres, as he was able to get Articuno and Zapdos. He showed them pictures of Pokémon that were different than normal, such as Scatterbug's hair length. Trevor and the group conducted their search on Mt. Molteau. Along the way, he got distracted by a Crustle. He eventually found Moltres at the top of Mt. Molteau when the battle with Team Rocket disturbed it from its slumber. He and the group took cover when Moltres began attacking them. Trevor then witnessed Ash's Fletchinder evolve into Talonflame in an attempt to protect the group. After leaving Mt. Molteau, Trevor revealed that he succeeded in getting Moltres's picture and waved goodbye to Ash and his friends.

In Tag Team Battle Inspiration!, Trevor was seen taking a picture of a wild Lairon.
In Master Class is in Session! and Performing a Pathway to the Future!, Trevor watched the Gloire Showcase Master Class on television along with Tierno.
In A League of His Own!, Trevor was revealed to have fulfilled the requirements to enter the Lumiose Conference. In the opening of the conference, he and Tierno were seen talking to Ash. The next day, he battled Alain in the first round and lost without knocking out any of his Pokémon, leaving Trevor disappointed in himself. After losing, he decided to remain in Lumiose City to watch the rest of the League together with his friends, which he did up until Down to the Fiery Finish!.
In A Towering Takeover!, Trevor joined the fight against Team Flare. Diantha tasked him, Shauna, Tierno and Sawyer with helping the citizens during the crisis. In Coming Apart at the Dreams!, Rocking Kalos Defenses!, and Forming a More Perfect Union!, he watched the crisis unfold on television in a Pokémon Center. He later reappeared in The First Day of the Rest of Your Life! to watch the performance that Serena put on to boost morale.
In Till We Compete Again!, Trevor and the rest of his group were watching the wild Pokémon who had escaped Lumiose City during the Team Flare incident.


=== Pokémon Journeys: The Series ===
In Pride of a Champion!, Trevor appeared watching the Masters Eight Tournament match between Lance and Diantha on TV, alongside Sawyer, Tierno, and Shauna.


== Character ==

Like his games counterpart, his goal as a Trainer is to meet every Pokémon in the Pokédex as stated during his introduction. Throughout his journey, Trevor aims to take pictures of all the Pokémon he encounters. He is mostly interested in ones with differences in their features, however slight, as seen in A Legendary Photo Op!.
Trevor shares a close friendship with Tierno and Shauna as they been friends since they were little, and would met up with each other whenever they could during their journeys to pursue their goals. Trevor also has feelings for Serena as he saw her Pokévision video many times before meeting her. During the Kalos League, Trevor quickly became friends with Sawyer and gets along really well with him.
