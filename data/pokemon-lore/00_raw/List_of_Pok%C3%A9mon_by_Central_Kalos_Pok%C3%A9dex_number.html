This is a list of Pokémon in the order dictated by the Central Kalos Pokédex (Japanese: セントラルカロス図鑑 Central Kalos encyclopedia).

The Central Kalos Pokédex appears in Pokémon X and Y as one of the three regional Pokédex lists of the Kalos region (along with the Coastal Kalos Pokédex and the Mountain Kalos Pokédex). It contains 153 Pokémon, though only 150 are required to be obtained to complete the Pokédex (Diancie, Hoopa, and Volcanion are not required).

The Central Kalos Pokédex also appears as one of the selectable regional Pokédex orders that can be viewed in the Pokédex in Pokémon Bank.

Trivia

  • The three sections of the Kalos Pokédex contain in total 457 species of Pokémon. This is the largest number of species that a regional Pokédex has included without DLC.
  • The three icons of the Coastal, Central, and Mountain Kalos Pokédex are a pentagon with a blue, white, or red stripe, respectively. When looked at together, the icons resemble the flag of France, the country Kalos is based on.
  • The Central Kalos Pokédex is the only regional Pokédex in the series to not contain an Ice-type Pokémon. Additionally, its only Rock-type Pokémon (Diancie) is event-exclusive.
  • The Central Kalos Pokédex is the only regional Pokédex in the series to not include any Legendary Pokémon.
