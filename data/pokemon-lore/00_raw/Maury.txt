Title: Maury

Maury (Japanese: カツミ Katsumi) is a minor character who appeared in The Rise of Darkrai.


== In the movies ==

He, along with Kai and Allegra, challenged Ash and his friends to a battle. He went up against Ash using his Torterra, while Ash used his Pikachu, who made a folly by commanding a Thunderbolt on Maury's Torterra—likely not realizing it is Grass/Ground—and then switched to Iron Tail.
Maury was later invited by Baron Alberto to help fight off Darkrai.
During the ending credits, Maury participated in the Alamos Town Pokémon Contest, where he was subsequently defeated by Kai in the Battle Stage. He is not onstage when Allegra receives her winning Ribbon.
Maury made a cameo appearance in I Choose You!, which is set in a continuity separate from Pokémon the Series. He was seen with his Torterra in Alamos Town.

== In the manga ==


=== Movie adaptations ===
Maury appeared in the manga adaptation of the tenth film, in which he, along with Kai and Allegra, confronted Darkrai after being convinced by Baron Alberto that the Pitch-Black Pokémon was responsible for the strange events taking place in Alamos Town.

== Trivia ==
Maury, Kai, and Allegra each own the final form of one Sinnoh first partner Pokémon.
Though he was not referred to by name in the dub, Pokémon.com has since revealed that his name is Maury.
Maury's character design resembles that of Torterra's color scheme.
Maury was referred to as Maurice on his voice actor's résumé.
