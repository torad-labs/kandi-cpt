Title: Matthew (TCG GB)

Matthew (Japanese: ヤストモ Yasutomo) is one of the three Club Members from the Rock Club, under Club Master Gene, in the Pokémon Trading Card Game.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
Matthew challenges the player to a 4-card prize match. Upon defeat, he awards the player with two Mystery booster packs.

=== Rolling Stone Deck ===
Main article: Rolling Stone Deck (TCG)
Rolling Stone Deck (Japanese: ローリングストーンデッキ) is played by Matthew. When challenged, Matthew plays a 4 prize card match. Upon defeat, he awards the player with one Beginning Pokémon booster pack.
This battle is available from the start of the game.
