Title: Jordan

If you were looking for the Pokémon franchise in Jordan, see Pokémon in the Arab world.

Jordan (Japanese: サスケ Sasuke) is a character in Pokémon Diamond, Pearl, and Platinum. He is one of the judges of the Pokémon Super Contests. His design is based on that of the Rich Boy Trainer class.


== In the games ==
Jordan appears in Pokémon Diamond, Pearl, and Platinum. After the player has received a Poffin Case from the Chairman of the Hearthome City Pokémon Fan Club, Jordan will give them a level 60 Mild Poffin with 40 smoothness when talked to. It is a unique Poffin that raises all of the five condition stats at once, which is impossible for Poffins that were made by the player.

=== Pokémon Adventures ===


==== Diamond & Pearl arc ====
Jordan is a judge for the Pokémon Super Contests, similar to his in-game role. He debuted in Perturbed by Pachirisu, in which he is seen judging a Normal Rank Coolness Contest. In Double Trouble with Dialga and Palkia V, he is seen with his fellow Contest Judges at the Lost Tower after having been sent there because of the fight between Dialga and Palkia that caused time and space to distort.


=== Pokémon Diamond and Pearl Adventure! ===
Jordan appears in Beauty Contest: The Pokémon Super Contest!! along with Dexter and Keira judging a Pokémon Super Contest in which Hareta and Jun were participating.
