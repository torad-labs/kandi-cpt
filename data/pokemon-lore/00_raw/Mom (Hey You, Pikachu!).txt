Title: Mom (Hey You, Pikachu!)

Mom (Japanese: ママ Mom) is the player character's mother in Hey You, Pikachu!.


== Quotes ==

The Bedroom
At the start of the game:
"It's morning. Time to get up!"
"Wake up. Isn't Prof. Oak's test today?"
"Don't forget the PokéHelper that Prof. Oak sent. I wonder what kind of test it is? I hope it goes well. Good luck!"
After the test:
"Welcome home. You sure look happy. Did the test go well? Is that so...you got to talk to PIKACHU? Are you going out again tomorrow? Then you should go to bed early tonight. Good night."
When Pikachu comes to the player's house for the first time:
"Oh, you're so cute! So this is PIKACHU? It came to play on its own! I wish a Pokémon that cute would be my friend. You be nice to it!"
"Have a nice time! Take care!"
Repeated the other days:
"Have a nice time! Take care!"
