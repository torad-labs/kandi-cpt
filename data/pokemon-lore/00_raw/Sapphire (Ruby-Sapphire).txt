Title: Sapphire (Ruby-Sapphire)

Sapphire (Japanese: ルビー Ruby) is a character from Pokémon Ruby-Sapphire who first appeared in Let's Try To Be Idols!!. She is based on May from Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire and is one of Red's rivals.


== History ==
Sapphire is a Pokémon Coordinator from Hoenn who introduced herself to Red when he entered the building with his Pokémon. Red takes a liking to her Torchic, seeing that it was the one that he was about to go on a journey with before going to Hoenn. Sapphire recommends him to partake in a Pokémon Contest in which Red does. When it was Sapphire turn to perform on stage, she manages to get a perfect score and wins the competition, placing Red in second place.


== Character ==
Despite being rivals with Red during the Pokémon Contest both of them competed in, she was shown to have some friendly advice.
