Title: Arley

Arley (Japanese: アックス Axe) is a member of the Societea in Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs. He's a large man who boasts incredible strength. In the Sky Fortress, he battles the player with a Regigigas after putting the player through a swarm of angry Pokémon. He is first met in Rasp Cavern.
His piece of the armor was stolen by Purple Eyes, and he was found by Murph while on board The Union.
In the past, he was a wrestler known as "Arley the Destroyer".
