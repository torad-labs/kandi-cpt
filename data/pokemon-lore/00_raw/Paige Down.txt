Title: Paige Down

Paige Down (Japanese: 青葉キイ Key Aoba) is a character who appears in Learn with Pokémon: Typing Adventure.


== In the games ==
At the start of the game, Paige is unfamiliar with most Pokémon, and is often nervous when approached by one. As she and the player research Pokémon together, she becomes more confident in her ability to be around them. After the player completes their research for M Waters, Professor Quentin Werty allows Paige to begin researching Pokémon by herself. At this point, Paige sets a goal to become a better typist than the player.
