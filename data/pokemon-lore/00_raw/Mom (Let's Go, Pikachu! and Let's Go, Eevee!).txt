Title: Mom (Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!)

Mom (Japanese: おかあさん mother) is the mother of Chase or Elaine in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!.
She serves a similar role to the mother of Red or Leaf in the Generation I games and Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen. At the beginning of Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!, she will inform the player that Professor Oak wants to see them. Once the player has received the Pokédex, she will give them the Town Map. After this, if the player's Pokémon are not fully healed, she will heal them when talked to.
