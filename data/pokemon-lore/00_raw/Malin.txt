Malin (Japanese: マリン Malin) is a minor supporting character who appeared in Kyurem VS. The Sword of Justice.
She is an enthusiastic girl who Ash and his friends met during their travels en route to Roshan City. Malin was a little taken aback by Cilan's enthusiasm, but appreciated the group's business. She sells Darumaka boxed lunches, which can be turned into wind chimes, and "Oran Ice" on the Windy Station platform with her Pokémon Vanillite. The business was marked in the travel guide that Cilan was reading on the train. Malin waved Ash and his friends goodbye as their train departed.
