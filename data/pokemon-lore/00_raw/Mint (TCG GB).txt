Title: Mint (TCG GB)

(Japanese: ミント Mint) is the female player character in Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. Her male counterpart is Mark and her rival is Ronald.


== In the games ==
The introductory text of Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! retells the adventure of the player character from Pokémon Trading Card Game. This is the same, regardless of the current player being Mark or Mint (even though only Mark was available as the player character in the previous game). Therefore, Mint effectively had the same journey as Mark on TCG Island. She defeated the Club Masters and Grand Masters, and collected the Legendary Cards. Several characters returning from the previous game, including her rival Ronald as well as Dr. Mason, also know her from her past adventures.
Like Mark, she is a player of the Pokémon Trading Card Game. She is called on for help by Dr. Mason when Team Great Rocket attempts to steal the Legendary Cards and kidnaps many of the Club Masters.
