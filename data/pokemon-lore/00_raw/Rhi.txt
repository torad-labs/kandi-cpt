Title: Rhi

Rhi (Japanese: リイ Rhi) is a member of the GO Ultra Recon Squad. They debuted on Pokémon GO Fest 2022 Day 2 as a replacement of Professor Willow. They specialize in learning about Pokémon that arrive from Ultra Space.


== Character information ==
After Professor Willow goes missing, Rhi of the GO Ultra Recon Squad helps the player find him. Their arrival coincides with Nihilego's appearance in the world of Pokémon GO. They and the player work together to save Professor Willow and learn more about Nihilego. The player is the first human that Rhi meets on the world of Pokémon GO.

== Trivia ==
Rhi was designed by Yusuke Kozaki.
Although the English versions of the game and website use gender-neutral pronouns when referring to Rhi, the German, French, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese translations refer to Rhi as male.
The Traditional Chinese translation refers to Rhi using the pronoun 他 tā, which can be gender-neutral or for males, rather than 她 tā, which is only for females.
