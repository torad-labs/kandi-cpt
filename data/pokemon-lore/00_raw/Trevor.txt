Title: Trevor

Trevor (Japanese: トロバ Trova) is a friend and rival of the player in Pokémon X and Y.


== In the core series games ==

Trevor appears in Pokémon X and Y as a friend of the player. Trevor delivers the player a Pokédex on behalf of Professor Sycamore, and he aims to be the first among his friends to finish collecting data for his Pokédex. Trevor and his older sister live in Lumiose City, near Jaune Plaza. Ever since their parents left them behind in order to travel around the world and train their Pokémon, it has been Trevor's dream to travel all over Kalos with a Pokédex of his own. He first battles the player on Route 7 alongside Tierno. From then on, Trevor occasionally engages in his own kind of battle with the player, comparing how much each has completed the Pokédex and losing every time. 
When the Abomasnow in Frost Cavern is threatened by Team Flare, Trevor comes and helps the player by battling one Team Flare Grunt's Mightyena to let the player beat Mable and save the Abomasnow.
After the player has entered the Hall of Fame, Trevor can be found in Geosenge Town. There, he will tell the player about various Pokémon that can use Mega Evolution.
Trevor captures many different kinds of Pokémon to complete his Pokédex, but the Pokémon he keeps in his party tend to be members of the Fairy Egg Group or ones revived from Fossils.
Trevor also appears as one of the player's partners in the Battle Maison when choosing Multi Battle.
Trevor is mentioned by an NPC in Pokémon Legends: Z-A, stating that he and Professor Sycamore are currently in another region together for research.

== In animation ==


=== Pokémon the Series ===
Main article: Trevor (anime)
Trevor has a recurring role in Pokémon the Series: XY. He is a Pokémon Trainer and photographer from Lumiose City. He debuted in Summer of Discovery! along with his friends Shauna and Tierno.


=== GOTCHA! ===

== In the manga ==


=== Pokémon Adventures ===
Main article: Trevor (Adventures)
In the Pokémon Adventures manga, Trevor is studying to become a scholar. Additionally, he is childhood friends with X, Y, Tierno, and Shauna, and serves as the narrator of the X & Y arc.


== In the TCG ==

Main article: Trevor (XY Promo 33)
Trevor was introduced as a Supporter card in the Pokémon Trading Card Game during the English XY Series (the Japanese XY Era). First released as an XY-P Promotional card in the Pokémon Card Gym Promotional Card Pack 3 from October to December 2014, the Trevor card debuted in English as an XY Black Star Promo included in the Battle Arena Decks: Xerneas vs. Yveltal, with both prints featuring an illustration by Ken Sugimori. It was later reprinted with the same artwork in English in the XY Trainer Kit: Bisharp & Wigglytuff, and it saw two more releases as Japanese XY-P Promotional cards: First as a part of the Event Organizer Trainer's Pack in July 2016, and then in the CoroCoro Ichiban! Pokémon Card Game XY BREAK Victory Guide on July 28, 2016. This card allows the player to search their deck for any Pokémon and put it into their hand.


== Trivia ==
Trevor, along with Shauna, battles the player less than any other rival, only battling them twice throughout the entire game.
