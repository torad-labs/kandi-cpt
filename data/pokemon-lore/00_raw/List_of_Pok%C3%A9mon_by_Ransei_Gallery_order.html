List of Pokémon by Ransei Gallery order

  • Deutsch
  • Español
  • Français
  • Italiano
  • 中文
See also: Gallery (Conquest)
Shortcut
Rdex

This is a list of Pokémon in the order dictated by Pokémon Conquest's Gallery, which acts like a Pokédex for the Ransei region, meaning that the Pokémon listed are all native to Ransei as well. Like in the New Pokédex of Gold, Silver, and Crystal, the Pokémon are not numbered in-game, but are given here for reference. The Gallery starts with Eevee, the Hero/Heroine's first partner Pokémon, and its Evolutions, and ends with hidden, Legendary Pokémon, such as Zekrom.

Pokémon are recorded as seen after a battle in which it participated, after recruiting someone with it or after Evolution. However, evolved Pokémon are not registered as owned until the player wins a battle with it.
