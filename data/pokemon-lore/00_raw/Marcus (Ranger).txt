Title: Marcus (Ranger)

Marcus (Japanese: モリー Molly) is an Operator at the Ranger Union in Pokémon Ranger: Shadows of Almia.
Marcus is a Top Operator working at the Ranger Union alongside Rhythmi and Linda. He is responsible for upgrading the Top Rangers' CapStick when updates are available and providing assistance to them.
