Title: Josée

Josée (Japanese: コタネ Kotane) is a character from Pokémon Legends: Z-A. She is a member of the Fist of Justice. She specializes in Fighting-type Pokémon.


== In the core series games ==
Josée appears in Pokémon Legends: Z-A. She is a member of the Fist of Justice training under Ivor, she can be found at the Justice Dojo and offers the service of being able to help turn a Pokémon's moves into a Plus Move, in exchange for a Seed of Mastery.
