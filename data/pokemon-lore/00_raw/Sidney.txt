Title: Sidney

Sidney (Japanese: カゲツ Kagetsu) is a Dark-type Trainer and member of the Hoenn Elite Four.


== In the core series games ==

Sidney is the first Trainer of the Hoenn Elite Four at the Pokémon League. He is an upbeat person who enjoys what he does, even if he loses.
In Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, the player can also briefly encounter Sidney in the Battle Maison lobby, having failed in his attempt to get Steven to return to the Pokémon League.

==== History ====


===== Ruby & Sapphire arc =====
Sidney and the other Elite Four members were called by Steven to help protect the Hoenn region from the battle between Groudon and Kyogre. Sidney was initially stationed at Desert Ruins as part of the plan to unseal Regirock, Regice, and Registeel. When the giants are unsealed, Sidney is tasked with controlling Regirock alongside Drake. The Elite Four, Steven, and Wallace all control the three legendary giants in three pairs of two and use their power to keep the power released from Groudon and Kyogre's battle from spreading throughout Hoenn. They manage to keep the power contained for several weeks, but the strain is too much for Steven and he eventually dies, causing the three Pokémon to lash out and flee. Sidney later mourns Steven's death before his revival at the hands of Ruby's Celebi.


===== Omega Ruby & Alpha Sapphire arc =====
Sidney, along with various people from around Hoenn, is called to Sootopolis City to assist in stopping Grand Meteor Delta, a giant meteoroid threatening to destroy the planet. When the plan to attack Grand Meteor Delta starts, Sidney and the others have their Pokémon attack the meteoroid at the same time. This, combined with the might of Mega Rayquaza, successfully shatters Grand Meteor Delta into pieces.

== Trivia ==
Sidney is the only member of the Hoenn Elite Four who does not share a specialty type with that of an Elite Four member of Generation I. However, Karen of the Generation II Elite Four is also a specialist in the Dark type.
In Ruby and Sapphire, Sidney is the only Elite Four member to not use two of the same Pokémon or use two Pokémon from the same evolutionary line.
In Ruby, Sapphire, and Pokémon Emerald, Sidney owns a Shiftry that knows Extrasensory at level 48, which Shiftry does not learn until level 49 as a Nuzleaf.
All of Sidney's Pokémon in the first battle give out at least one Attack EV. In addition, all of them have their base Attack as their highest stat.
His Cacturne is the only Pokémon he uses that does not give out at least two Attack EVs. In addition, its base Special Attack is also its highest stat (along with its Attack), unlike his other Pokémon.
Sidney is the only Hoenn Elite Four member to use a Mega Evolution introduced in X and Y rather than Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire.
