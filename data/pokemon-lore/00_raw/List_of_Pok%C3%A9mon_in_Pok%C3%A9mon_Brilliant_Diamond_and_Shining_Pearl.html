List of Pokémon in Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl

This is a list of Pokémon available in Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl. The first 493 Pokémon up to Generation IV appear in the games, including all of the forms for these Pokémon up to Pokémon Platinum; Arceus' Fairy-type form is the only form from games following Pokémon Platinum to be included in these games. 151 Pokémon are featured in the Sinnoh Pokédex.

Pokémon forms are listed on separate sections below. A Pokémon is considered "normally available" if it can be obtained without the use of events or transferring over from another game.
