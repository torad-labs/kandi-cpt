Title: Alternate World Dawn

Alternate World Dawn (Japanese: 平行世界のヒカリ Alternate World Hikari) is a recurring character who first appeared in The Gates of Warp!. She is Dawn's counterpart from the Alternate World.


== History ==


=== Pre-series ===
As a Pokémon Trainer, Dawn participated in the Sinnoh Pokémon League and was able to place second in the League Conference.


=== Pokémon Journeys: The Series ===
Dawn first appeared in The Gates of Warp!, coming through a portal when she spotted the regular Dawn's Piplup and taking it. When regular Dawn and Goh later met her at her home, she apologized for taking Piplup, having done so out of longing after her own Piplup had turned into an Egg.
In Showdown at the Gates of Warp!, Dawn met Ash, Chloe, and Alternate World Ash, and they headed to the Spear Pillar together to get Dialga and Palkia's help in stopping the Pokémon devolutions. At the Spear Pillar, they discovered a portal that led them to a dimension where Alternate World Team Rocket had forced Dialga and Palkia to battle each other, purposefully causing devolutions in the alternate Sinnoh. Dawn joined in battling against Team Rocket, who were eventually defeated when Dialga's power went out of control and caused everyone to turn younger. Ash, Dawn, Goh, Chloe, and their alternate counterparts prayed for Arceus to stop Dialga and Palkia's fight, which it did. After being transported back to alternate Sinnoh, everyone and their Pokémon returned to their proper ages and states. As Ash and his friends returned to their own world, both Dawns bid farewell to each other.


== Character ==
Alternate World Dawn's speech patterns are more meek and polite in comparison to her counterpart, similar to Alternate World Ash.
