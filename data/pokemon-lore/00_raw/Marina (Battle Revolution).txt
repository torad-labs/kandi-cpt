Title: Marina (Battle Revolution)

Marina (Japanese: マリン Marine) is the third Colosseum Leader in Pokémon Battle Revolution. She is the head of Waterfall Colosseum. Similar to the other Colosseum Leaders, she cosplays as a Pokémon, Kyogre. Her appearance is based on the Young Girl character style. She specializes in Water-type Pokémon.
Beating Marina for the first time on Level 50 All awards her Kyogre Girl outfit.
