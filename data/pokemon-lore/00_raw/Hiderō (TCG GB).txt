Title: Hiderō (TCG GB)

Hiderō (Japanese: ヒデロウ Hiderō) is a member of Team Great Rocket and the Fort Leader of GR Fire Fort from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!.
In all duels against him, the Flame Armor (Japanese: ほのおのよろい Flame Armor) rule is active, thus  Pokémon's Weakness to  is omitted.
When defeated, he gives out one of both Team Rocket's Ambition and We Are Team Rocket booster packs and the GR Fire Coin, also known as Magmar Coin, which grants the player the ability to enter GR Fighting Fort if the player also has the GR Water Coin.
