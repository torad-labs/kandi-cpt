Title: Leader (JN014)

The leader (Japanese: 団長 leader) is a recurring character in Pokémon Journeys: The Series.


== History ==
The leader of the ruins excavation team first appeared in Raid Battle in The Ruins! exploring the newly discovered Colossus Ruins at the Desert Resort. He joined Ash, Goh, Kira, and Shane Seeker in a Raid Battle against a giant Golurk, using his Litwick and Lampent. Eventually, the group defeated Golurk, and Goh subsequently caught it.
He reappeared in a flashback in Restore and Renew! when Ash and Goh mistook the curator for him.
In Trial on a Golden Scale!, he showed Ash and Goh the Volcarona mural found at the Colossus Ruins.
He was later seen in a fantasy in Out of Their Elements! and in another flashback in Battling in the Freezing Raid!.

== Trivia ==
The leader bears a resemblance to the curator, who appeared in Restore and Renew!, and the showman, who appeared in Out of Their Elements!. In the case of the curator, they also share the same Japanese, Arabic, Brazilian, Italian, Polish, and European Spanish voice actors.
