Title: Kruger

Kruger (Japanese: ルトガー Rutger) is a Colosseum Leader in Pokémon Battle Revolution and the third Colosseum Master. He is the head of Courtyard Colosseum and owns the castle it is part of.
Kruger's exclusive Trainer classes are the Master Senior (Japanese: マスターシニア Master Senior) in Masters Battle, and Survival King (Japanese: サバイバルキング Survival King) in the Survival Battle.
The Survival Battle is unlocked for Courtyard Colosseum once clearing the Pokétopia Championship. Featuring one hundred battles, Kruger appears as the opponent every ten, hence his unique privilege of appearing in a non-Final round. Every time he appears, the number of Pokémon used by each Trainer has increases by one, up to a maximum of four Pokémon.
By clearing Neon Colosseum, the player obtains the rose corsage on his suit alongside unlocking Rosie as a Custom Pass Trainer.


== Pokémon ==


=== Courtyard Colosseum ===


==== Lv. 30 Open ====

After defeating Kruger for the first time, he can be rebattled in the Courtyard Colosseum before beating the Stargazer Colosseum. The 6 Pokémon in his team are predominantly Dark and Ghost types. He always summons a Dark-type Pokémon first and always summons a Ghost-type Pokémon last.


=== Stargazer Colosseum ===


==== Masters Battle Set 3 ====
Kruger always leads with Ambipom and Drapion.


==== Masters Battle Set 7 ====
Kruger always sends out Azelf first.
