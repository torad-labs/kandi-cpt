Title: Yuki Koide

Yuki Koide (Japanese: 小出優希 Koide Yūki) is a main character in A Pocketful of Adventures. 


== History ==


=== Season One ===


== Character ==
Koide is quite clumsy, which earned him comparison to a Magikarp by Madoka. Despite this, he tries to do what he can for ADventure and his co-workers. With Madoka's help, he managed to demonstrate a firmer stance and impose himself before Erika, even though after that, he still maintained some of his characteristics.
