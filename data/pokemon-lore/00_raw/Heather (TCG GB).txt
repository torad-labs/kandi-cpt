Title: Heather (TCG GB)

Heather (Japanese: ミサオ Misao) is one of the three Club Members from the Grass Club, under Club Master Nikki, in Pokémon Trading Card Game


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
The player is required to battle with Heather, in a 4 prize card match, in order to challenge Club Master Nikki. Upon defeat, Heather awards the player with two Colosseum booster packs.

== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==


=== Steady Increase Deck ===
Main article: Steady Increase Deck (TCG)
Steady Increase Deck (Japanese: ドンドンぞうしょくデッキ) is the deck played by Heather in a 4 prize card match. Defeating Heather gets the player an Island of Fossil booster pack.
Heather is available to battle at some point later in the game after the player can access GR Island. Before the Grass Club is freed from GR2, the player can speak to Heather in the communications room and obtain a free Island of Fossil booster pack.
