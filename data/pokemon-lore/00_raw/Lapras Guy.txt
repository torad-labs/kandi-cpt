Title: Lapras Guy

Lapras Guy is a character from Pokémon Ranger.  
He can be encountered at both; Fall City Harbour and in Summerland. When spoken to, he will allow Lunick/ Solana to travel between these two areas using his Lapras.
In Oblivia, Nixie has a similar role.
