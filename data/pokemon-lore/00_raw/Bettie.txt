Title: Bettie

This article is about the Pokémon Masters EX character. For the Pokémon the Series: Black & White character, see Betty.

Bettie (Japanese: ユイ Yui) is the female player character in Pokémon Masters EX. Her male counterpart is Scottie.
Along with her partner Pikachu, Bettie explores the land of Pasio to compete in the Pokémon Masters League. Bettie also forms sync pairs with other Pokémon during her adventures, including Torchic, Solgaleo, Regirock, Cobalion, Mesprit, Registeel, Alcremie, Raikou, and Buzzwole as well as various Pokémon hatched from Eggs.


== In the games ==


=== PML arc ===
Bettie arrives on the artifical island of Pasio, looking to compete in the Pokémon Masters League, she gets gifted a Poryphone from Trista and forms a team with Brock and Misty. The trio travel across the reigon to find new teammates such as Rosa, and to collect the five PML Badges required to participate.
On her travels she meets Professor Bellis, a researcher specializing in sync pairs such as her. She learns about the villainous Team Break and how they're causing trouble across the island, Bettie helps put a stop to their plans whenever she can. She also forms a rivalry with Paulo, a student with a strong sense of justice and high expectations for himself, and Lear, an arrogant prince who is motivated by becoming the future heir to the throne, being raised strictly to view Pokémon as mere tools and failing to form a true bond with them.
Bettie learns about sygna suits when Brock wears one to help in the upcoming tournament. During one of the breaks, she ends up helping Rising Star Stella in finding her lost Torchic, and is gifted her second Torchic to raise. She wins in battles against various trainers to rise up the tournament bracket, including Paulo and Lear.
Her team ends up winning the PML after a hard fought battle againts Red, Blue, and Leaf, making them the first Champion team of the PML. When Lear announces a second PML at the awards ceremony, she is surprised at first, but is excited to participate again.


=== Villain Arc ===
After her victory in the PML, Bettie gets involved in stopping various villainous teams, such as Team Rocket, Team Galactic, and the Aether Foundation. She also meets Paulo's childhood friend in Tina, who immediately get along well together.
While this is happening, Paulo is going through a series of self confidence issues. This reaches a breaking point where Paulo ends up taking over Team Break with Lear's Hoopa, bringing matters into his own hands and trying to disband other teams with force.
Bettie confronts Paulo about his extreme methods before being defeated and dismissed, as Paulo plans a large-scale attack on Team Rocket. This ends up backfiring with Giovanni appealing to Hoopa and forming a Sync Pair with it. Things go from bad to worse as Giovanni uses Hoopa's newfound power to wreak havoc across the island.
With the aid of Professor Oak, Bettie, Paulo and Tina make amends and set out to stop Team Rocket. The other villainous team leaders are able to help create openings for the trio to reach Giovanni at the stadium. They enact a plan to save Hoopa by using a machine invented by Colress that reverts Hoopa to its Confined state. This succeeds and Giovanni retreats for the time being.


=== Arceus arc ===
After saving the reigon from Team Rocket's plan, Bettie decides to aid in research of the mysterious stones suddenly appearing across the region. Learning of their unique properties and power, Bettie is invited to demonstrate the potential applications to the public. During the demonstration, a bright light envelops the stadium and tells everyone to "Show me thy bond."


=== Other ===
Sometime during Bettie's journey, she helps various people recruit Legendary Pokemon such as Silver with Ho-Oh, Zinnia with Rayquaza, and Professor Sycamore with Xerneas. She also recruits a Solgaleo for her own team after working with Hau and Roxie
She becomes friends with various sync pairs across the island and has numerous conversations and training sessions with them, whether it be planned or coincidental. She is often seen helping Lear, Rachel, and Sawyer with island maintaining duties, and is an active user of facilities like the Trainer Lodge and Battle Villa.
Professor Bellis offers her opportunities to pair up with new Pokémon, such as an Egg containing one of the Kanto first partner Pokémon. She can also choose to pair up with one of many different Alcremie variants.

== Pokémon hatched from Eggs ==
Main article: Egg Pokémon (Masters)
Bettie can also form sync pairs with a number of Pokémon hatched from Eggs. She is first given an Egg upon meeting Professor Bellis, as she recruits Bettie to help her research the sync pair bond formed between Trainers and Pokémon hatched from Eggs. She is allowed to choose between a Bulbasaur, Charmander, and Squirtle Egg. These sync pairs are listed in the Dex, and their details are shown below. Their Dex entries are from Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire, with the exception of Bulbasaur whose Dex entry is from Pokémon Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald.
After hatching her first Egg, Bettie can receive more Eggs from Professor Bellis and form sync pairs with the Pokémon that hatch from them. The list of all other Pokémon that can be hatched from Eggs can be found here.

== Trivia ==
 Flannery  & Torkoal,  Flint  & Infernape, and Bettie & Pikachu were the first sync pairs to get their sync grids expanded.
 Lorelei  & Lapras and Bettie & Torchic were the last sync pairs to get their initial sync grid expansion on Pokémon Masters Day.
Bettie & Pikachu are the first sync pair able to unlock an EX role and superawakening to have a base potential lower than 5★.
According to Rika, if spoken to in the Pokemon Center, Bettie is about the same age as Juliana and Florian.
