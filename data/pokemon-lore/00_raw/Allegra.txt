Title: Allegra

Allegra (Japanese: マキ Maki) is a minor character who appeared in The Rise of Darkrai.


== In the movies ==

Allegra was introduced with Kai and Maury when they wanted to battle Brock, Dawn, and Ash respectively. Allegra battled with her Infernape against Brock and his Croagunk. It is unknown who was the victor. Later she shows up with her friends again in front of the Space-Time Towers wanting to battle again. She was going to battle Dawn and her Buizel until they were interrupted by a bright light over the two towers. Soon Darkrai showed up and the battle was postponed.
Allegra and her friends soon discovered that they couldn't leave Alamos Town because of a mysterious fog. Anyone who tried to run through the fog would end up back where they started. Even her Honchkrow's Defog didn't work on it. She soon began to search for Darkrai, along with many other Trainers, after Baron Alberto said that it was the cause of all the problems. Later on, it was discovered that Dialga and Palkia were the real problem and she used her Infernape, along with the help of Kai's Empoleon and Maury's Torterra, to try to stop them.
After everything returned to normal, Allegra participated in the Alamos Contest where she faced off against Dawn during the Battle Stage. Using her Infernape against Dawn's Piplup, she was able to win the battle, taking home the Ribbon, according to the ending credits.

== In the manga ==


=== Movie adaptations ===
Allegra appeared in the manga adaptation of the tenth film, in which she, along with Kai and Maury, confronted Darkrai after being convinced by Baron Alberto that the Pitch-Black Pokémon was responsible for the strange events taking place in Alamos Town.

== Trivia ==
Allegra, Kai, and Maury own the final form of each Sinnoh first partner Pokémon.
Out of all of these three, Allegra is the only one who has been shown to own any Pokémon other than their Sinnoh first partner Pokémon.
Allegra's dub name was revealed via a Mailbag question on Pokémon.com.
During her Infernape's battle against Brock's Croagunk, she seems to be moving in sync with it while they were battling close up.
