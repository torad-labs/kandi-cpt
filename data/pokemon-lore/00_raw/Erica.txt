Title: Erica

If you were looking for the Kanto Gym Leader, see Erika. For the English voice actress, see Erica Schroeder.

Erica (Japanese: エリコ Eriko) is a recurring character in Pokémon the Series: Ruby and Sapphire. She is a Pokémon Coordinator.


== History ==
Erica debuted in Mean With Envy. She and her boyfriend Joshua were training together for the Pacifidlog Town Contest. Erica hoped that she and Joshua would be the first couple to become winners in the Contest.
Much to Erica's chagrin, May began training with Joshua. They were only doing so as friends, but Erica refused to believe May's story. She was convinced that May was going to steal Joshua away from her.
During the Contest in Pacifidlog Jam, Joshua faced off against May in the Contest Battles. Erica hoped that Joshua would defeat her, but she was stunned when May ended up defeating him. Now more than ever, Erica wanted to defeat May. However, when May defeated Erica, the Coordinator had a change of heart. She told May that she was jealous of her, and that she shouldn't have been.
She and Joshua both had cameos in Deceit and Assist, where they were watching May on TV during the Hoenn Grand Festival.


== Character ==
Erica was immediately jealous of May after spotting her talking with her boyfriend Joshua. However, despite May and Joshua's reassurances of otherwise, Erica remained jealous and set on capturing Joshua's attention. During the Contest, Erica hoped that she and Joshua would be the ones to win the event and share the Ribbon. However, her dreams were shattered when May defeated Joshua and advanced to the final round. Erica later had a change of heart after Joshua revealed his feelings on the jealousy, and she gained a respect of May.
