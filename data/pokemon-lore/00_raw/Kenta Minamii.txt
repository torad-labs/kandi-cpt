Title: Kenta Minamii

(Japanese: 南井健太 Kenta Minamii) is the main character of Aim to Be a Card Master!!, a manga series taking place in the real world. Kenta is a player of the Pokémon Trading Card Game. After being bored of playing with his friends, he goes, at the advice of his friend Natsuki Shibata, to the Pokémon Center in Tokyo where he meets a young boy named Shou Okabe who defeats him easily. He decides to train until he's good enough to become a champion of the game.
