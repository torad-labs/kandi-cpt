Title: Becca

Becca (Japanese: ベッキー Becky) is the female player character of Pokémon Champions.


== Appearance ==
Becca is a young woman with fair skin, brown hair past the shoulders, and brown eyes. She wears a pair of browline glasses with black rims. Her outfit consists of loose white crop top with a logo in black, yellow-and-green sleeveless undershirt, black compression pants with a turquoise lining on both sides, white belt to hold Poké Balls, and a pair of white shoes with black linings and yellow soles. She also wears a turquoise-and-black fingerless glove on her right hand and an Omni Ring on her left wrist.


== In the games ==


== Names ==
Becca (Japanese: ベッキー Becky) is the name given to this character in pre-release media for Pokémon Champions.
The internal name in the non-English European languages differs from the name used in the corresponding languages of pre-release media, where the latter all use her English name Becca.
