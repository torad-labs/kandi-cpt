Title: Teresa (Ranger)

Teresa is a character from Pokémon Ranger.  
She lives in Fall City with her husband; Dino, and several Skitty. She is first encountered by Lunick/ Solana when her beloved Pokémon go missing around the city. In the process of finding them she meets and adopts a 6th Skitty.
