Title: Senta (TCG GB)

Senta (Japanese: センタ Senta) is a member of Team Great Rocket from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. He's one of the Trainers at the GR Water Fort.
He will not duel against the player unless they use a deck with 4 Magikarp cards.
When defeated, he gives out one Psychic Battle booster pack. Also, when defeated for the first time, he frees the imprisoned Grand Master Jack and puts a bridge over the water to make it possible to go to the next room, allowing the player to reach Aira.
