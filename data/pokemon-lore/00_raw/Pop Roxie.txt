Title: Pop Roxie

Pop Roxie (Japanese: ホミカパパ Homika's Papa) is a character appearing in Pokémon Black 2 and White 2 and the Pokémon Adventures manga. He is the father of the Virbank Gym Leader Roxie.


== In the core series games ==
Pop Roxie is a sailor who runs a ferry between Virbank City and Castelia City. He is first seen arguing with his daughter in front of Virbank's Pokémon Center over his movie career, insisting that since Roxie is both a Gym Leader and a musician, he can be both a sailor and a movie star.
While he is auditioning at Pokéstar Studios, the gate to the studios cannot be entered until the player obtains the Toxic Badge. Upon entering Pokéstar Studios, the player watches Pop Roxie's first movie, Midlife Man of Justice vs. Brycen-Man of the Brycen-Man Series. As Riolu-Man, he ends up acting scared and being defeated. He later claims that one day, he will act once more. He then reopens his ferrying service.

=== Pokémon Masters EX ===
Pop Roxie is mentioned in the Academy Founding Chapter event Explosive Art, Ninja's Craft by Roxie, who details to Mela about his dream of becoming a movie star.


== In the manga ==


=== Pokémon Adventures ===
Pop Roxie debuted in Choir Tournament. Roxie had her father take the students of the Aspertia City Trainers' School to Castelia City as a reward for winning the Autumn Choir Tournament. Roxie later revealed that her father once wanted to be a movie star and planned on giving up his job as a ship captain to do so. When Pop Roxie saw Blake and Whitley's performance at Pokéstar Studios, he realized that they were better than him and gave up.
