Title: Kendall (TCGO)

Kendall is a regular at the Game Store in Pokémon Trading Card Game Online. She can be faced in all three of the leagues in the Trainer Challenge for a total of four matches.


== Official biography ==
"Kendall isn't just a skilled TCG player-she's also a successful Mathlete who loves statistics, manga, and electronic music. When she goes to tournaments, she can often be found playing Pokémon games on her Nintendo DS system between rounds."


== League appearances ==


=== Gold League ===
Players face Kendall in the seventh round of the Gold League, after defeating Juji. After defeating Kendall, players move on to face Tyson.


=== Platinum League ===
Players face Kendall in round six of the Platinum League, after defeating Calvin. After defeating Kendall, players move on to face Penelope.
Later in the League, players face Kendall again in the eleventh round, after defeating Cammie. After defeating Kendall a second time, players move on to face Mick, also for the second time.


=== City Championship ===
Players face Kendall in the eleventh round of the City Championship, after defeating Rika. After defeating Kendall, players move on to face Daniel in the final match of Trainer's Challenge.
