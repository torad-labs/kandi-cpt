Title: Dragonite Ranger

Dragonite Ranger is a character from Pokémon Ranger.  
He can be found on the upper level of all four Ranger Bases in Fiore. When Lunick/ Solana has reached a high enough Ranger Rank, they are allowed to bond with Dragonite via capture and then are able to use the Pokémon to quickly travel between the four Ranger Bases.
