Title: Digging Duo

The Digging Duo (Japanese: 穴掘り兄弟 Digging Brothers) is a pair of non-player characters found at Bridge Field, across from the entrance to the Pokémon Nursery. They are two brothers who share an interest in digging for buried treasure, and give the player any items they find. They both have the appearance of Hikers.


== In the core series games ==
The Digging Duo is a pair of Hiker brothers who specialize in finding items in succession, and offer to begin digging for treasure if the player pays either of them 500 W. The game is automatically saved before each digging session. Upon discovering an item, the player has a chance to stop the digging or to continue, which repeats with each new item dug up until the Hiker eventually tires out. Occasionally after finishing a streak, when the player speaks to the brother who just finished digging, he may continue digging for additional items without charging additional Watts.
The brother on the left—closest to the Pokémon Nursery—focuses on skill, being able to find rarer items, but tires out faster. The brother on the right focuses on stamina, finding less rare items, but also has a higher chance to hold a longer digging streak.

== In the TCG ==

Main article: Digging Duo (Crown Zenith 126)
Digging Duo was introduced as a Supporter card in the Pokémon Trading Card Game during the English Sword & Shield Series (the Japanese Sword & Shield Era). It was first released in the Japanese VSTAR Universe expansion and the English Crown Zenith expansion, with artwork by Yuu Nishida. This card allows the player to choose a card from a certain number of cards from the bottom of their deck, depending on a coin flip: they look at the bottom 8 cards of their deck if they flip heads, or the bottom 3 cards if they flip tails. The remaining cards are shuffled back into the deck.
