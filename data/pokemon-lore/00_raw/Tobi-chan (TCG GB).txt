Title: Tobi-chan (TCG GB)

Tobi-chan (Japanese: とびちゃん) is one of the Ghost Masters located in the Sealed Fort, located on GR Island in Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! Tobi-chan can be found in the fifth of the nine statues in the main room. Players who defeat Tobi-chan will obtain a Present Pack, which can consist of any card that can be found in a booster pack in the game.
