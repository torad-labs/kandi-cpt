This is a list of Pokémon in the order dictated by the Hisui regional Pokédex, meaning that the Hisui first partner Pokémon will appear first, followed by other Pokémon that live in the Hisui region.

The Hisui Pokédex appears in Pokémon Legends: Arceus, containing 242 Pokémon, though only 238 are required to be obtained to complete the Pokédex (Phione, Manaphy, Shaymin, and Darkrai are not required). The Hisui Pokédex also appears as one of the regional Pokédexes in the mobile version of Pokémon HOME, where it can only be filled with Pokémon that originate from Legends: Arceus.

In addition to the order for the entire Hisui region, the Hisui Pokédex splits Pokémon into five sub-listings for each of the region's areas: the Obsidian Fieldlands (containing 85 Pokémon), the Crimson Mirelands (containing 75 Pokémon), the Cobalt Coastlands (containing 96 Pokémon), the Coronet Highlands (containing 90 Pokémon), and the Alabaster Icelands (containing 91 Pokémon). The same Pokémon can appear in multiple sub-listings. In-game, the Pokémon in each area's listing retain their general Hisui Pokédex numbers, similar to the Alola Pokédex. 26 Pokémon are present in the overall Hisui Pokédex but do not appear in any of the five sub-listings.

Trivia

  • All Pokémon introduced in Generation IV are included in the Hisui Pokédex.
    • However, a number of Pokémon from the Sinnoh Pokédex that were introduced before Generation IV are missing, those being the evolutionary lines of Goldeen, Hoothoot, Girafarig, Wooper, Wingull, Azurill, Meditite, Feebas, and from the expanded Pokédex in Pokémon Platinum, Swablu, Houndour, Tropius, and Absol.
      • This makes the Hisui Pokédex the first regional Pokédex to exclude Wingull, Pelipper, and Absol since their debut.
    • Heatran, Regigigas, Cresselia, Phione, Darkrai, Shaymin, and Arceus had not appeared in any regional Pokédex until Pokémon Legends: Arceus; they had been the only Pokémon with that distinction since Platinum.
    • Some Pokémon present in the Hisui Pokédex are not present in the Sinnoh Pokédex. This includes the evolutionary lines of Vulpix, Paras, Growlithe, Voltorb, Cyndaquil, Stantler, Qwilfish, Teddiursa, and Spheal, as well as some Pokémon that were introduced after Generation IV (the evolutionary lines of Oshawott, Petilil, Basculin, Zorua, Rufflet, Goomy, Bergmite, and Rowlet, as well as Sylveon and the forces of nature).
      • Of these, only the Paras, Spheal, and Vulpix evolutionary lines did not receive a new Evolution, regional form, or group member.
      • Some other Pokémon in the Hisui Pokédex are not present in the original Sinnoh Pokédex but appear in its extended Platinum version, namely the evolutionary lines of Magnemite, Rhyhorn, Lickitung, Tangela, Scyther, Electabuzz, Magmar, Eevee, Porygon, Togepi, Yanma, Gligar, Swinub, Ralts, Nosepass, Duskull, Snorunt, Rotom, and Giratina.
  • All Generation V, VI, and VII Pokémon in the Hisui Pokédex have some connection to a Hisuian form or Pokémon introduced in either Pokémon Legends: Arceus or Generation IV.
    • The only Generation VII Pokémon in the Hisui Pokédex are Rowlet and its Evolutions.
  • The only Generation VIII Pokémon in the Hisui Pokédex are those newly introduced in Pokémon Legends: Arceus.
  • The Burmy and Geodude evolutionary lines are the only Pokémon that appear in all five Hisui Pokédex sub-listings.
  • The Hisui first partner Pokémon, the Pokémon of myth, all Mythical Pokémon, Unown, Spiritomb, and the Porygon, Cranidos, and Shieldon lines are the only Pokémon in the Hisui Pokédex to not appear in any of its five sub-listings.
