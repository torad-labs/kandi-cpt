Title: Henry

If you were looking for the character from Pokémon Adventures, see Henry Sword.

Henry (Japanese: シニア Senior) is a non-player character in Pokémon Ranger: Shadows of Almia.


== In the games ==
He is first met in Haruba Village, with a Gigaremo unit which he has mistaken for a coffee machine, his mistake resulted in Hippopotas creating sandstorms in the village, which leads to high sand hills trapping people in, and that the main source of water dries up. In the quest to capture the Gigaremo Units, he mistakes the Claydol as a new unit of Gigaremo that could move at their own will.
In the Ranger Net mission, Palkia in Haruba Desert!?, he follows Sven and the player through the spatially torn Almia to find Palkia. During this, he gains a new life dream of traveling with his son, Hank.
It is also revealed that Henry has protected Haruba Village his entire life.
