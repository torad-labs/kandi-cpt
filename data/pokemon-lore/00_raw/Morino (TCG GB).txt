Title: Morino (TCG GB)

Morino (Japanese: モリノ Morino) is a member of Team Great Rocket and the Fort Leader of GR Grass Fort from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!.
In all duels against him, the Green Power (Japanese: みどりのちから Green Power) rule is active, thus no  Pokémon can be inflicted with any Special Conditions.
When defeated, he gives out two We Are Team Rocket booster packs and the GR Grass Coin, also known as Golbat Coin, which grants the player to enter GR Lightning Fort.
