Title: Logan

Logan is a regular at the Game Store in Pokémon Trading Card Game Online. He can be faced in one of the three leagues in the Trainer Challenge for a total of one match.


== Official biography ==
"Logan used to collect Pokémon cards when he was younger, and recently he got into playing the game. He's always confident of victory, even when things aren't going his way. When he's not playing cards, he's training to become a park ranger."


== League appearances ==


=== Platinum League ===
Players face Logan in the second round of the Platinum League, after defeating Zach. After defeating Logan, players move on to face Juji.
This is currently Logan's only appearance in the League.
