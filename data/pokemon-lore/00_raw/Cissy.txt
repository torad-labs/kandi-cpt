Title: Cissy

Cissy (Japanese: アツミ Atsumi) is the Gym Leader of the Mikan Island's Gym, known officially as the Mikan Gym. She hands out the Coral-Eye Badge to Trainers who defeat her.


== History ==
Cissy debuted when Ash challenged her for the Coral-Eye Badge in Fit To Be Tide.
Ash discovered that Cissy, like the rest of the Orange Crew, conducts a different kind of Gym battle than what he was used to in the Indigo League. The first part of the match is an accuracy test where both Trainers have their Pokémon use Water Gun to accurately hit targets. The second test is a surfing race around some buoys near the coast. While Ash and Cissy had a draw in the first challenge, Ash prevailed in the second challenge after having Lapras use its Ice Beam, thus winning the Badge.
She reappeared in flashbacks in A Way Off Day Off, Hello, Pummelo!, and Viva Las Lapras.


== Character ==
According to Tracey, Cissy is known as one of the toughest Trainers in the Orange Crew. She specializes in Water-type Pokémon, and one of the rules of her Gym is that her opponents are required to use Water-type Pokémon as well.
She lives with her little brother, Senta, who is also a Pokémon Trainer and serves as a Gym assistant. The pair have a good relationship, though she is prepared to pull Senta into line if he is misbehaving.
Cissy is confident in her abilities and certain of her Pokémon's power. She proudly showed off her Seadra's Water Gun control during the first challenge. While she has a strong competitive spirit, Cissy will also commend a challenger's abilities and clever tactics.
