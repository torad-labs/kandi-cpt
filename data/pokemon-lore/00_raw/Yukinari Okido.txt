Title: Yukinari Okido

Yukinari Okido (Japanese: 大木戸ユキナリ Ōkido Yukinari) is a supporting character in A Pocketful of Adventures. He is Midori Kudo's grandfather and a Kendo instructor. He is based on Professor Oak.
