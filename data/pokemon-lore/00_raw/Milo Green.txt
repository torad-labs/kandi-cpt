Title: Milo Green

Milo Green is a character in the game Detective Pikachu.


== In the games ==


=== Detective Pikachu ===
Milo is an aspiring Pokémon Ranger who lives on Cappucci Island. He is good friends with John Waals, who is teaching him all about Pokémon. When he was young, he nearly drowned in the lake on Cappucci Island, but he was saved by a wild Milotic. While he was unsure what Pokémon saved him, he kept one of its scales, and he currently wears it on his headband.
Milo first appears in Chapter 4, after Tim Goodman runs into a wild Ducklett that's injured. He stops Tim from doing anything to Ducklett and uses a Potion to heal it. Upon learning that Tim has come to the island to talk to Dr. Waals, Milo offers to guide Tim to Dr. Waals's house. Upon seeing Brad McMaster trying to arrest Dr. Waals for injuring Pokémon at the lake, Milo vehemently defends Dr. Waals, as he doesn't believe Dr. Waals did anything wrong. Tim decides to prove Dr. Waals's innocence, and Milo supports him in his investigation however he can.
