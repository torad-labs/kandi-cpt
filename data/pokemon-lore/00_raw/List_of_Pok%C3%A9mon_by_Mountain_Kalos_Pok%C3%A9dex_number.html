List of Pokémon by Mountain Kalos Pokédex number
Shortcut
MoKdex

This is a list of Pokémon in the order dictated by the Mountain Kalos Pokédex (Japanese: マウンテンカロス図鑑 Mountain Kalos encyclopedia).

The Mountain Kalos Pokédex appears in Pokémon X and Y as one of the three regional Pokédex lists of the Kalos region (along with the Central Kalos Pokédex and the Coastal Kalos Pokédex). It contains 151 Pokémon, though only 150 are required to be obtained to complete the Pokédex (Mewtwo is not required; however, it is required for National Pokédex completion).

The Mountain Kalos Pokédex also appears as one of the selectable regional Pokédex orders that can be viewed in the Pokédex in Pokémon Bank.

Trivia

  • The three sections of the Kalos Pokédex contain in total 457 species of Pokémon. This is the largest number of species that a regional Pokédex has included without DLC.
  • The three icons of the Coastal, Central, and Mountain Kalos Pokédex are a pentagon with a blue, white, or red stripe, respectively. When looked at together, the icons resemble the flag of France, the country Kalos is based on.
  • It is possible to catch Pokémon registered in the Mountain Kalos Pokédex without owning the Pokédex first.
  • The Mountain Kalos Pokédex is the only regional Pokédex in the series to not contain any first partner Pokémon (if Pikachu and Eevee are counted).
