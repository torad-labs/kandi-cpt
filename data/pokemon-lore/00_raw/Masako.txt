Title: Masako

Masako (Japanese: まさこ Masako) is a supporting character of A Pocketful of Adventures. She is portrayed by Eriko Sato.


== History ==


=== Pre-series ===
Before having her current cafe, Masako had a restaurant in the district of Ginza. She revealed that during that time, she was a very shy and naive person, but that changed when she needed to "change her skin".


=== Season One ===
In PPA01, Masako saw Madoka Akagi alone while studying Pokémon's type advantages. When asking Madoka about this, she made a correlation about the type advantages and the people each of them interacts with.


== Character ==
Masako is a young but very wise woman. Talking about her experiences and advice, she helped Madoka understand the best way to interact with people.
