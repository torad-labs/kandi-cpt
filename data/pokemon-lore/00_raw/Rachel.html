Rachel (Japanese: チェッタ Chetta) is a character introduced in Pokémon Masters EX. She forms a sync pair with Umbreon. The sync pair can be obtained through the limited sync pair scout, and was first available on July 30, 2021. The pair's potential can be upgraded to 6★ EX from September 19, 2024, which unlocks Rachel's EX style.

Rachel also forms a sync pair with Roaming Form Gimmighoul. This pair can be obtained through a variety sync pair scout beginning on September 17, 2024. This pair can be raised to 6★ EX since it first released, though this pair lacks an EX style.

In the games

This section is incomplete.
Please feel free to edit this section to add missing information and complete it.
Reason: Role in Pokémon Masters EX

Rachel, along with Sawyer, is one of Lear's subordinates. She is utterly dedicated to Lear and adores him, sometimes spacing out when thinking about him. Despite Lear's arrogant tendencies, Rachel respects Lear for his single-minded dedication, while Lear comes to respect and value Rachel and Sawyer more as time goes.

In her sync pair story, Rachel reveals that she used to be an orphan girl before being taken in as one of Lear's maids. She apparently had a very thick country accent, which she worked hard to change to sound more professional. Sawyer thought her accent was a sign of her being disrespectful, but Lear claimed that he liked her voice.

During her time as a maid, her Umbreon was still an Eevee. When Eevee evolved at night, she was initially disappointed as she apparently wanted a different evolved form of Eevee, but after Lear said that he liked Umbreon, Rachel learned to be happy with Eevee's Evolution.

Trivia

  • Along with Sawyer & Honchkrow, Rachel & Umbreon were the first non-5★ sync pair to be featured in a limited-time sync pair scout.
    • Along with Sawyer & Honchkrow, they were also the first fair-exclusive Dark-type sync pair.
  • In her sync pair story, Rachel reveals that she's owned Umbreon ever since it was an Eevee.
  • Rachel's overall design appears to be based on the popular depiction of a jester, an entertainer in a king's court. Her overly long sleeves and checkered tights fit the clown-like aesthetic of a court fool, along with her large twintails resembling the silhouette of a foolscap.
