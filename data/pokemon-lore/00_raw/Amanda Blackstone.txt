Title: Amanda Blackstone

Amanda Blackstone is a supporting character in the game Detective Pikachu.


== In the games ==


=== Detective Pikachu ===
Amanda is an assistant at the Baker Detective Agency. Her job duties include compiling reports of things her boss Mike Baker has investigated, communicating with clients, and handling appointments. She is generally kind, though she has little patience with Detective Pikachu and is always getting on his case about things like eating sweets that have been left out for clients. 
She chose to have the Baker Detective Agency's car be a convertible because she insisted it's better to have a sporty vehicle when tailing or chasing down suspects.
Amanda's Pokémon partner is Fletchling. Fletchling enjoys helping Amanda with small tasks, such as fetching the newspaper and delivering letters and packages. It also likes having visitors at the agency. Wherever Amanda goes, Fletchling is always with her.
