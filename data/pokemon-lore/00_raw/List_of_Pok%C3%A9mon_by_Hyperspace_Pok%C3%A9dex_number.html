This is a list of Pokémon in the order dictated by the Hyperspace Lumiose regional Pokédex.

The Hyperspace Pokédex is one of two regional Pokédexes featured in Pokémon Legends: Z-A, along with the Lumiose Pokédex (with which it shares an additional section called the Mega Evolution Pokédex). It was added to the game in the Mega Dimension DLC and contains 132 Pokémon, though only 131 are required to be obtained to complete the Pokédex (Zeraora is not required). The Hyperspace Pokédex also appears as one of the regional Pokédexes in the mobile version of Pokémon HOME, where it can only be filled with Pokémon that originate from Legends: Z-A; this version of the Pokédex has only 131 Pokémon, as Zeraora is excluded.

Trivia

  • The Hyperspace Pokédex only includes Pokémon found in Hyperspace Lumiose that weren't present in the base game's Lumiose Pokédex. As a result of this:
    • This is the first DLC Pokédex to not share any Pokémon with the Pokédex from the base game.
    • The Hyperspace Pokédex is the only regional Pokédex in Generation IX to not feature Tynamo, Eelektrik, and Eelektross.
  • The Hyperspace Pokédex includes the most Mythical Pokémon out of every regional Pokédex featured in a core series game, with a total of 11.
  • At 132 species of Pokémon, the Hyperspace Pokédex is the smallest numbered regional Pokédex in the core series by number of species.
  • Despite its name, some Pokémon in the Hyperspace Pokédex are not found in Hyperspace, namely Volcanion, Marshadow, and Magearna.
