List of Pokémon that do not appear in any regional Browser

  • Italiano

This is a list of Pokémon that do not appear in any regional Browser, listed in their National Pokédex order. 65 of the 493 Pokémon in existence at the time of the release of Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs, the latest entry in the Pokémon Ranger series, are in this list. Pokémon introduced after Generation IV are excluded from this list, as all of them appear in no regional Browsers.

Trivia

  • Despite Manaphy not appearing in the Browser of any Pokémon Ranger game, the series was the only way to legitimately obtain Manaphy in the core series outside of events until the release of Pokémon Legends: Arceus. This is because in the Ranger games, only Manaphy's Egg can be obtained, which can only be hatched in a core series game.
  • Unown is the only Pokémon introduced in Generation II to not be in any Regional Browser.
