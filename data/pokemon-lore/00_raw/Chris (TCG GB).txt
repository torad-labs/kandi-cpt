Title: Chris (TCG GB)

Chris (Japanese: タツロー Tatsurō) is one of the three Club Members from the Fighting Club, under Club Master Mitch, in Pokémon Trading Card Game.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
Chris is not found at the Club the first time the player meets him. Instead, he is at the Rock Club, located north of where the player enters the side room. He will only appear once Michael is defeated at the Grass Club.
Chris challenges to a 4-card prize match. Upon defeat, he awards the player with two Evolution booster packs. It is necessary to defeat Chris to be able to challenge Mitch.

== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==


=== Skilled Warrior Deck ===
Main article: Skilled Warrior Deck (TCG)
Skilled Warrior Deck (Japanese: むしゃしゅぎょうデッキ) is played by Chris in this game. When challenged, Andrew plays a 4 prize card match. Upon defeat, he awards the player with one Legendary Power booster pack and one Beginning Pokémon booster pack.
Chris is originally not able to be battled. After the player first defeats GR3 at the Water Club, Chris becomes available to battle and will always play this deck.
