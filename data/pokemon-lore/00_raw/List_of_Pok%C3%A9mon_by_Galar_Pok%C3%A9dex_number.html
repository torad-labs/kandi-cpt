Gdex

This is a list of Pokémon in the order dictated by the Galar regional Pokédex, meaning that the Galar first partner Pokémon will appear first, followed by other Pokémon native to the Galar region.

The Galar Pokédex appears in Pokémon Sword and Shield and contains 400 Pokémon. It is one of three regional Pokédexes featured in the game, with the other two, the Isle of Armor Pokédex and the Crown Tundra Pokédex, being added in the Pokémon Sword and Shield Expansion Pass DLC. The Galar Pokédex also appears as one of the regional Pokédexes in the mobile version of Pokémon HOME, where it can only be filled with Pokémon that originate from Sword and Shield.

Only Pokémon in the Galar, Isle of Armor, and Crown Tundra Pokédexes, as well as a select few outside of the aforementioned Pokédexes, can be transferred from Pokémon HOME to Pokémon Sword and Shield.

In Pokémon Sword and Shield, the Galar Pokédex is titled simply "Pokédex" (in constrast to the Isle of Armor Pokédex and Crown Tundra Pokédex using their full titles). However, the "Galar Pokédex" name is used in Pokémon HOME.

Trivia

  • The three Galar Pokédexes combined contain the most species of Pokémon of all regions, with a total of 584.
  • Of the 400 Pokémon in the main Galar Pokédex, 176 exclusively appear in the main landmass of Galar. 89 also appear in the Isle of Armor, 123 also appear in the Crown Tundra, and 12 appear in both DLC areas.
  • The seven Pokémon evolutionary lines that appear in all three Galar Pokédexes are Magikarp, Barboach, Karrablast, Shelmet, Skwovet, Pincurchin, and Morpeko.
  • Some forms that can be registered in the Galar Pokédex were not present in the game until the release of The Isle of Armor expansion, namely Pikachu's World Cap form and the Gigantamax forms of Rillaboom, Cinderace, and Inteleon.
  • The West Sea forms of Shellos and Gastrodon are the only Pokémon able to be registered in a Pokédex in Pokémon Sword and Shield that cannot be obtained without transferring them from Pokémon HOME.
  • Krabby and Kingler are the only Pokémon sharing the same numbers (#098 and #099) in both the Galar Pokédex and the National Pokédex.
