Title: Ansha

Ansha (Japanese: アンシャ Ansha) is a friend character from the Pokémon Legends: Z-A's Mega Dimension DLC. She is a talented chef who creates donuts that dreams of finding the Legendary Pokémon Rayquaza in Hyperspace Lumiose and gift it to her "dear mama" Diantha. During the course of the game, Ansha becomes a full-fledged member of Team MZ and assists the group by powering up Hoopa with her donuts in order create portals to the hyperspace.


== In the core series games ==


=== Appearance ===
Ansha is a short, young girl with fair complexion. Her hair is mostly covered in her fluffy white hat; the hair on the back of her head is styled to resemble a mochi donut. She wears a knee-length black overcoat with a fluffy pink collar over a white sweater, and black shoes with fluffy pink legwarmers, and she carries a small white backpack with golden ornaments. When cooking donuts, Ansha dons her cooking attire, consisting of a bluish-gray apron and head scarf with a donut pattern; Naveen and Lida later sewed the Team MZ logo onto her apron.


=== Personality ===

Ansha is a young girl who shows surprising maturity and eloquence by the way she speaks and is also polite with the adults around her, addressing them properly. She enjoys making donuts and has been getting better at it over the years: this ability  and her caring and kind personality has led the Mythical Pokémon Hoopa to gather an interest in her and two ended up becoming friends. She cares a lot for her mother Diantha, so much so that she wants to get the Legendary Pokémon Rayquaza to give her as a present. Despite this, she seems to be worried that she might not live up to the expectations of being a Champion's daughter, should she pursue being a Pokémon Trainer herself. Moreover, she seems saddened by the fact she does not get to spend much time with her mother, given how busy she is with her acting career.


=== History ===


==== Background ====
At some point in the past, Diantha personally taught her how to make donuts. 
About 6 months prior to the events of Mega Dimension, Ansha stumbled upon Hoopa, who was  heavily weakened after losing a battle with a mighty Pokèmon it played a prank on. She fed it a fresh-baked donut, causing Hoopa to regain its strength and since then, it followed her around, helping her visit Hyperspace Lumiose through the use of its Hyperspace Holes.
A few months prior to the events of Mega Dimension, Ansha witnessed a Rayquaza flying over her in Hyperspace Lumiose., inspiring her to catch it for her mother.
At some point prior to her first meeting with Team MZ, Ansha is booked to stay at Hotel Richissime with Lebanne assigned to be her babysitter and bodyguard. Jacinthe grew fond of her, to the point that she kept Ansha company during meals. 


==== Pokémon Legends: Z-A ====


===== Mega Dimension =====
Ansha is a talented donut chef who, along with her partner Hoopa, seeks the legendary Pokémon Rayquaza to give to her "dear mama", Diantha. Along the way, Ansha seeks the help of Team MZ to make a donut that will give Hoopa a surge of power, and ends up guiding them to Hyperspace Lumiose. 
Ansha first meets Paxton/Harmony in the beginning of Side Mission 120: Donuts of Unworldly Deliciousness!, while sitting on on the roof of Hotel Z. In this initial conversation she requests Team MZ's help with the baking of donuts. Taunie/Urbain assigns her the front desk of the hotel as well as the kitchen as a workspace.

== Trivia ==
Ansha's Hoopa's love for donuts appears to reference the movie Hoopa and the Clash of Ages, which features a Hoopa that loves to eat donuts.
The beginning of Ansha's theme contains elements of her mother's battle theme.
Ansha's portion of Taunie/Urbain's Crossiant Curry is smaller and with a lighter colour on the curry.
Ansha is currently the only friend character from the core series to not appear in any sort of battle at all.
She is also the only member of Team MZ who is not a Trainer and cannot be battled as well.
