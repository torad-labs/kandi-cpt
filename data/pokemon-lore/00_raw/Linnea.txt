Title: Linnea

If you were looking for the Leafeon User, see Eevee users → Leafeon User Linnea.

Linnea (Japanese: アサミ Asami) is a Furisode Girl from the Laverre Gym. In Pokémon the Series, she is a recurring character who first appeared in A Fashionable Battle!.


== In animation ==


=== Pokémon the Series ===


==== Pokémon the Series: XY ====
Linnea is a Furisode Girl loyal to Laverre City Gym Leader Valerie. When Sawyer came to Laverre City to ask for a Gym battle, Linnea told him that Valerie wasn't available at the moment due to her collection being displayed in a fashion show. During the fashion show, Linnea picked Sawyer from the audience to battle against Valerie in an official Gym battle that was part of the show.
Linnea reappeared in Fairy-Type Trickery!, where she was following Ash and Valerie's Gym battle.
She reappeared in a flashback in Till We Compete Again!.

== Trivia ==
Linnea shares her name in Japanese, French, Spanish, Italian, Korean, and Chinese with Asami.
Linnea is characterized in the games as being focused on learning from Valerie to become a fashion designer, and Asami is a fashion designer in Lumiose City five years after the events of X and Y. However, due to their drastically different character designs, the relationship between the two characters is unknown, and may be coincidental or a simple reference.
