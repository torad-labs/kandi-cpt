Title: Amanda (TCG GB)

Amanda (Japanese: メグミ Megumi) is one of the three Club Members from the Water Club, under Club Master Amy, in Pokémon Trading Card Game.  She is Sara's twin sister.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
The player duels with Amanda in a 3 prize card match in order to later have the right to challenge Club Master Amy.
Upon defeat, Amanda awards the player with two Mystery booster packs.

== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==


=== Beach Deck ===
Main article: Beach Deck (TCG)
Beach Deck (Japanese: なみうちぎわのデッキ) is the only deck played by Amanda, played as a 4 prize card match. Defeating Amanda gets the player an Island of Fossil booster pack and a Psychic Battle booster pack.
This battle becomes available after freeing Steve at the GR Lightning Fort.
