Title: Erik (TCG GB)

Erik redirects here. For the character introduced in the Generation I core series games, see Erik and Sara.

Erik (Japanese: マサシ Masashi) is one of the three Club Members from the Science Club, under Club Master Rick, in Pokémon Trading Card Game.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
Erik can be dueled in an optional 4 prize card match. Upon defeat, Erik awards the player with two Evolution booster packs.

== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==


=== Gathering Nidoran Deck ===
Main article: Gathering Nidoran Deck (TCG)
Gathering Nidoran Deck (Japanese: あつまれニドランデッキ) is the only deck played by Erik, played as a 4 prize card match. Defeating Erik gets the player an Island of Fossil booster pack.
Erik is not available to battle at the beginning of the game. He remains this way when GR2 is defeated. When the GR Coin is completed, Erik will acquire the cards needed to build his deck and will be available to battle at that point. If GR2 is the final GR member defeated, Erik will be available to battle immediately upon return to the club.
This deck is available for the player to build automatically in Autodeck Machine 1.
