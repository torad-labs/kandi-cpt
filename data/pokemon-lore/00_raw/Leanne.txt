Title: Leanne

Leanne (Japanese: レイラ Leila) is a character in Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs.
She is an archaeologist who is studying the connection between the ruins and fairy tales of the Oblivia region. The player first meets her at the Wireless Tower with her husband, Rand. After the player has obtained the symbol for Raikou, Leanne investigates the symbol and figures out its meaning. She is later kidnapped by Purple Eyes and held hostage by Steelheads in the Oblivia Ruins. She eventually discovers the secret of the Rainbow Grail and the Rainbow Dais and finds out how to stop the Sky Fortress from destroying the entire region of Oblivia with a tsunami.
