Title: Miyajima (TCG GB)

Miyajima (Japanese: ミヤジマ Miyajima) is a member of Team Great Rocket from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. He's one of the Trainers at the GR Water Fort.
He will not duel against the player unless this uses a deck with only  Energy cards.
When defeated, he gives out one Island of Fossil booster pack. Also, when defeated for the first time, he opens the door to the next room, allowing the player to reach Senta.
