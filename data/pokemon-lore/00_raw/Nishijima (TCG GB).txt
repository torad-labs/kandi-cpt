Title: Nishijima (TCG GB)

Nishijima (Japanese: ニシジマ Nishijima) is a member of Team Great Rocket from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. He's one of the Trainers at the Colorless Altar.
He refuses to duel against the player unless this has four cards of the type he has chosen at that time (he can request either Pidgey, Spearow, or Double Colorless Energy cards).
When defeated, he gives out two Legendary Power booster packs.
