Trivia

  • A Pokémon's food preferences correlate with the Egg Groups of its evolutionary family:
    • Springy Mushrooms are exclusively preferred by all available Pokémon families in the Bug, Field, Grass, Human-Like, and Amorphous Egg Groups.
      • Although its description states it's beloved by the more monstrous Pokémon and dragon Pokémon, Pokémon in the Monster and Dragon Egg Groups do not necessarily prefer Springy Mushrooms.
    • Dazzling Honey is exclusively preferred by all available Pokémon families in the Monster, Bug, Flying, and Fairy Egg Groups.
      • The preference for it among Pokémon in the Bug and Fairy Egg Groups is alluded to in the item's description.
    • Hearty Grains are exclusively preferred by all available Pokémon families in the Monster, Water 1, Bug, Flying, Field, Human-Like, Water 3, and Water 2 Egg Groups.
      • The preference for it among Pokémon in the Field Egg Group is alluded to in the item's description.
    • Plump Beans are exclusively preferred by all available Pokémon families in the Monster, Water 1, Bug, Flying, Field, Human-Like, Water 3, Amorphous, Water 2, and Dragon Egg Groups.
      • The preference for it among Pokémon in the Water 2 and Flying Egg Groups is alluded to in the item's description.
    • Crunchy Salt is exclusively preferred by all available Pokémon families in the Grass and Mineral Egg Groups.
      • The preference for it among these Pokémon is alluded to in the item's description.
