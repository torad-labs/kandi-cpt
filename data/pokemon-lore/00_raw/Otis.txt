Title: Otis

Otis is a regular at the Game Store in the Pokémon Trading Card Game Online. He can be faced in the game's tutorials and City Championship in the Trainer Challenge.


== Official biography ==
"Otis is a latecomer to the Pokémon TCG, but after joining up with Daniel and Rika, he's become a force to be reckoned with. He's a man of few words, but he regularly attends football games and his backpack is usually loaded with books on popular science and the statistical analysis of sports."


== League appearances ==


=== City Championship ===
Players face Otis in the sixth round of the City Championship, after defeating Brittney. After defeating Otis, players move on to face Cammie.
