Title: Harrington

Harrington (Japanese: イヌガヤ Inugaya) is a character in Pokémon Scarlet and Violet. He is the former director of Naranja Academy/Uva Academy, now currently serving as Ortega's piano instructor.


== In the core series games ==
Harrington is first encountered outside the entrance of Ruchbah Squad's Base, where he attempts to stop the player from infiltrating the base.

After the player defeats Ortega, Harrington returns and reveals that he previously served as the director of the academy. Having recognized the bullying problem as a failure of his administration, Director Harrington had given the big boss of Team Star a lenient punishment and then resigned along with all other teaching staff at the time.
