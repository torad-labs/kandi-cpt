Title: Yūki (TCG GB)

Yūki (Japanese: ユウキ Yūki) is a member of Team Great Rocket from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. She is one of the Trainers at the GR Fire Fort. She refuses to battle the player unless they have only  Energy in the active deck.
When defeated, she gives out one We Are Team Rocket booster pack. Also, when defeated for the first time, she opens the door to the next room, allowing the player to reach Shōko.
