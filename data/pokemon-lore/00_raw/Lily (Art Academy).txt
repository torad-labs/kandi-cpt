Title: Lily (Art Academy)

Lily (Japanese: リリー Lily) is a friendly non-player character in Pokémon Art Academy. She is a student at the academy of the same name.
She appears if the player chooses to play as male. Her male counterpart is Lee.


== Overview ==
Lily is a student at the Pokémon Art Academy. Her ambition is to be a master Pokémon artist and famous illustrator of Pokémon cards. Professor Andy is her favorite Pokémon card illustrator. She was already a student at the academy before the player applied to join and meets them at the start of the game.
She attends the same lessons as the player and tries to draw the same image. Her drawings are typically rushed and have many mistakes. She is usually embarrassed when Professor Andy points out flaws in her drawings, but she quickly cheers up and is motivated to keep drawing and improve. Professor Andy encourages her to keep drawing as well.
