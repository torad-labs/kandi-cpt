This is a list of Pokémon evolutionary lines including Evolutions from different generations. For example, Pichu (a Generation II Pokémon) evolves into Pikachu (a Generation I Pokémon). A few of these Pokémon, as a result of new Evolutions, become a part of evolutionary lines with branched Evolutions.

Starting with Generation VII, several old Pokémon lines were modified by introducing regional forms of older Pokémon. While regional forms of the same line are always related via breeding, lines entirely consisting of regional forms of the exact same species are not considered to be in the scope of this page.

Entries in each section are listed in ascending order based on the lowest National Pokédex number among the members of the evolutionary line.

Common stage and new species

These evolutionary lines have received new Pokémon species introduced at some point after the debut of the earliest members that either evolve into or evolve from preexisting stages. In other words, these lines have at least one instance where a Pokémon can evolve into a stage introduced in a different generation and the two species in question also debut in different generations.

New species only

These lines consist of evolutionary lines with regional forms that do not share a common unevolved stage with other forms of the species involved and do not have stages introduced in different generations on each line. However, certain Pokémon species will only evolve from a specific regional form and, therefore, have no counterpart. As such, these cases do not have cross-generational Evolutions in the sense where a specific Pokémon can evolve into a stage introduced in a different generation, but rather, these lines have member species introduced in different generations.

Trivia

  • Pokémon which evolve into a Pokémon of a previous generation, instead of from it, are always baby Pokémon. The only baby Pokémon introduced in the same generation as their evolved form are Togepi, Riolu, and Toxel.
  • Generation IV introduced the most evolutionary relatives to Pokémon from previous generations, with 29, and Generation VI introduced the fewest (of generations that introduced any), with only one, Sylveon.
    • Generation V and Generation VII did not introduce any evolutionary relatives to Pokémon from previous generations (though the latter did introduce Alolan forms that evolve from previously introduced Pokémon).
  • Some cross-generational evolutionary lines have unique distinctions:
    • Hitmonlee and Hitmonchan are the only Pokémon to have become evolutionary relatives after originally not being so.
    • The Scyther line is the only branched Evolution line to have each member species introduced in a different generation.
    • The Chansey, Mr. Mime, Electabuzz, Magmar, and Porygon lines are the only three-stage evolutionary lines where every member was introduced in a different generation.
    • Roselia is the only Pokémon to have both a pre-Evolution and an Evolution (Budew and Roserade, respectively) introduced in the same generation outside its debut generation.
  • As of Generation IX, no Pokémon introduced in Generation IV, VI, or VII have received new Evolutions or pre-Evolutions in later generations.
