Title: Clover

Clover (Japanese: オタケ Otake) is a bandit who is a member of the Miss Fortune sisters, along with Charm and Coin. In terms of age, she is the middle member of the three. Prior to becoming a bandit, she was a member of the Diamond Clan and the Galaxy Team's Survey Corps.
She bears a striking resemblance to Candice, but the relationship between them, if any, is unknown.

== Trivia ==
Clover wears a Ginkgo Guild uniform except for the guild’s boots and symbol apron, and she also uses bags that the guild’s members attach to their backpacks.
Volo mentions that the Miss Fortunes have plundered the Ginkgo Guild on multiple occations.
Ironically, while she's a former member of the Diamond Clan, Clover's apparent modern-day doppelgänger, Candice, is the Gym Leader of Snowpoint City, which seemingly is located on the same spot as the Pearl Settlement.
