Title: Ishii (TCG GB)

If you were looking for the seventeenth Ga-Olé set, see Grand Rush set 2 (Ga-Olé).

Ishii (Japanese: イシイ Ishii), also known as the Enigmatic Mask GR2 (Japanese: GR2号 GR Number 2), is a member of Team Great Rocket and the Fort Leader of Colorless Altar from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!.
Like a couple other Team Great Rocket members, he has disguised himself behind a mask with a  pattern across it so little can be said about his real appearance. After the player has first met Nikki at Mr. Ishihara's House, he appears at either the Grass Club (if the player is female) or Science Club (if the player is male), where he may be challenged in a 6-prize duel.
His deck focuses on  and  Pokémon. After defeating him, the player is awarded with one of both We Are Team Rocket and Legendary Power booster packs and one piece of the Great Rocket Coin.
The player meets him again at the Colorless Altar, where he is the Fort Leader. His deck, Eye of the Storm Deck, focuses on  Pokémon, mainly on the ones that are of the Flying-type in the main games. He refuses to duel against the player unless they have four cards of the type he has chosen at that time (he can request either Rattata, Meowth, or any Dark Pokémon cards).
When defeated there, he gives out two Island of Fossil booster packs and, if the player has defeated both Nishijima and Samejima, the GR Colorless Coin, also known as Snorlax Coin, which grants the player to enter Team GR Castle if they also have the GR Psychic Coin.
