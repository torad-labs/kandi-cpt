Title: Renna (TCG GB)

Renna (Japanese: レンナ Renna) is a member of Team Great Rocket from Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!. She is one of the Trainers at the GR Lightning Fort.
She accepts duels only if the player has 4 Pikachu cards in the active deck. Flying and Surfing Pikachu don't count.
When defeated, she gives out one We Are Team Rocket booster pack. Also, when defeated for the first time, she opens the door to the next room, allowing the player to reach Ichikawa.
