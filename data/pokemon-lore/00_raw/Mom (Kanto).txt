Title: Mom (Kanto)

If you were looking for the character named Mom in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!, see Mom (Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!).

Mom (Japanese: おかあさん mother) is the mother of Red or Leaf depending on the game.


== In the core series games ==
In the Generation I games and their remakes, Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen, at the beginning of the games, she will inform the player that Professor Oak wants to see them. After that,  if the player's Pokémon are not fully healed, she will heal them when talked to. 
This same mother also appears in the Generation II games and Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver, telling the player how worried she is about Red, her son, but also how proud she is of him.
She does not appear in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!, where her role is served by Chase or Elaine's mother instead.

== In animation ==


=== Pokémon the Series ===
In Pokémon the Series, Delia, Ash's mother, serves as her counterpart. She was introduced in the very first episode and is shown to travel to most of the Pokémon League competitions that her son takes part in. She also has her own Pokémon, a Mr. Mime nicknamed Mimey, which cleans the house for her.


=== Pokémon Origins ===
In Pokémon Origins, Mom appeared in File 1: Red, telling him that the device Professor Oak was working on is ready and that the Professor wanted to see him in his lab. This caused Red to fall down the stairs with excitement. She asked him if he was okay before he rushed out the door. She made no other appearances.


== In the manga ==


=== Pokémon Adventures ===


==== FireRed & LeafGreen arc ====
She debuts in this arc as Green's mother and has a husband. They had been searching for Green ever since she had been kidnapped for the Masked Man, and had finally tracked her down. Before she and her husband could properly reunite with Green, they are sucked into a black hole by Deoxys. She and her husband are eventually rescued by Lorelei and are finally able meet their daughter.


==== Emerald arc ====
She appears alongside her husband in Epilogue coming to the Battle Frontier.
