Title: Dundee

Dundee is a Pokémon Trainer who battled Ash in the beginning of Celebi: The Voice of the Forest. He was not given an official name in the Japanese version.


== History ==
Dundee battled Ash's Pikachu with his Croconaw. Both Pokémon collided head to head, after which Dundee ordered Croconaw to launch a barrage of Water Guns. Pikachu evaded most of the Water Guns and the two Pokémon ran to each other. Dundee ordered Croconaw to get in close with a Slash, which Pikachu evaded again.
After a short period of hit-and-run tactics, with Croconaw trying to Bite Pikachu, Croconaw was headbutted away by Pikachu and collided with its Trainer. Pikachu launched a powerful Thunder Shock on the Croconaw which hit not only the Pokémon but Dundee as well.
It was not Ash's intention to shock Dundee too and he quickly ran up to him to check if he was okay. After this, Brock's Crobat showed up to take Ash to his friends as they didn't want to miss the boat, leaving Dundee behind.


== Character ==
During the battle with Ash's Pikachu, for unknown reasons, Dundee would usually get hit by any attack that is supposed to be aimed at Croconaw.

== Trivia ==
Dundee may have received his dub name from Crocodile Dundee for the fact that he trains a Croconaw, which is based on a crocodile. He was also given an apparent Australian accent, much like his proposed namesake.
