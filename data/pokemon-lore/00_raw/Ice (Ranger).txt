Title: Ice (Ranger)

Ice (Japanese: アイス Ice) is the leader of the Sinis Trio.


== In the games ==
Ice is first briefly mentioned when several Team Dim Sun administrators are speaking about which one of the trio are they going with.
Ice is later seen in the depths of Almia castle, where he says he is also looking for the Blue Gem, and he offers the player sarcastically some blue soda if he helps him get the Blue Gem, since that's also blue. He afterwards challenges the player with a Froslass. When the player captures the Froslass, he leaves.
He is later seen very briefly mounting the Altru helicopter in the Oil Field Hideout.
Afterwards, he is seen at the Ranger Union trying to get Isaac back to Altru, but is quickly interrupted by Lavana, which uses Melody as ransom to get him back. After that, he complains how boring was that, and challenges the player again with a Garchomp.
In Altru Tower, he is seen taking care of his barrier and used a Gallade to chase off the player. Then, after being defeated, he summons his Gallade along with Lavana's Magmortar and Heath's Rhyperior. After he fails, he leaves the tower, never to be seen again. He is only mentioned once when Isaac speaks about him creating the stairs' electric trap.

=== Pokémon Adventures ===


==== Pokémon Ranger: The Comic – Shadows of Almia ====
Ice, along with his teammates, Heath and Lavana, first appear watching from afar as several Pokémon Rangers arrive at the Altru Tower. They show annoyance that the Rangers arrived sooner than expected, but confidently point out that Team Dim Sun has been revived under a new leader. 
When Kellyn worries about the Luminous Crystal losing its power, Ice and Lavana reveal themselves and explain it was their doing after they removed the Tears of Princes. They introduce themselves as the members of Team Dim Sun's Sinis Trio and that they were the ones that injured the guardian of the Luminous Crystal, Darkrai. Ice uses his Froslass to freeze the two Rangers in place and they prepare to control all the Pokémon in the area with a Gigaremo and Miniremo powered by the Shadow Crystal. However, with his upgraded Capture Styler, Kellyn is able to free himself from their trap. Ice and Lavana are quickly defeated by a Huntail Kellyn captured and the tears they stole are retrieved by Kellyn and Kate.
Ice and his teammates are last seen lamenting over their defeat but aim to find a new, better leader in the future.
