Title: Kali

Kali (Japanese: キリカ Kirika) is a Furisode Girl from the Laverre Gym. In Pokémon the Series, she is a recurring character who first appeared in A Fashionable Battle!.


== In animation ==


=== Pokémon the Series ===


==== Pokémon the Series: XY ====
Kali is a Furisode Girl loyal to Laverre City Gym Leader Valerie. She was first shown ordering other Furisode Girls around in preparation for a fashion show showcasing Valerie's fashion collection. She later appeared as one of the announcers of the fashion show and refereed a Gym battle between Valerie and Sawyer that was part of the fashion show.
Kali reappeared in Fairy-Type Trickery!, acting as the referee of Ash and Valerie's Gym battle.
She reappeared in a flashback in Till We Compete Again!.


==== Voice actors ====


== In the core series games ==
Kali appears in Pokémon X and Y as one of the Furisode Girls in the Laverre Gym.
