Title: Brandon (TCG GB)

Brandon (Japanese: トシヒコ Toshihiko) is one of the three Club Members from the Lightning Club, under Club Master Isaac, in Pokémon Trading Card Game.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
The player is required to defeat Brandon in a 4 prize card match in order to battle Isaac.
Upon defeat, Brandon awards the player with two Colosseum booster packs.

== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==


=== 10,000 Volts Deck ===
Main article: 10,000 Volts Deck (TCG)
10,000 Volts Deck (Japanese: 10まんボルトデッキ) is the only deck played by Brandon, played as a 4 prize card match. Defeating Brandon will get the player an Island of Fossil booster pack and a Legendary Power booster pack.
Brandon cannot be battled at the start of the game. After GR4 leaves the Lightning Club, Brandon will return to the main hall, but he will not battle until the GR Coin is completed.
