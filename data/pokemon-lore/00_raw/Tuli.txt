Title: Tuli

Tuli (Japanese: ツイリ Tsuiri) is a character in Pokémon Legends: Arceus. She is one of the Ginkgo Guild merchants and sells various items to the player.


== In the core series games ==
Tuli, along with Ginter, runs the Ginkgo Guild Cart in Jubilife Village. She sells a variety of Berries, as well as plants that can be used as crafting materials. These items typically cannot be bought from other sellers, such as the general store.
