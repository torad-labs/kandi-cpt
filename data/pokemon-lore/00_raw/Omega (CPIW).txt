Title: Omega (CPIW)

Omega (Japanese: オメガ Omega) is one of the two main characters and older brother of Alpha in the Pokémon Omega Ruby Crimson Passion and Pokémon Alpha Sapphire Indigo Wisdom manga.


== Pokémon ==


== Trivia ==
Despite Omega being after Alpha in the Greek alphabet, Omega is the older of the two brothers.
