The majority of Pokémon that evolve do so at or above a certain level upon leveling up. This is a list of those Pokémon ordered by the lowest level at which they may evolve, from the lowest (level 7) to the highest (level 64).

Trivia

  • As of Generation IX (counting regional forms separately), the mean (average) Evolution level is 27.6. The median level is 28. The mode level, with 35 Pokémon, is 30.
    • The mean Evolution level for Pokémon with a two-stage evolutionary line is 29.7. The median level is 30 and the mode level is 20, with 23 Pokémon. The minimum and maximum are 10 (Kricketot) and 59 (Larvesta), respectively.
    • The mean Evolution level for the first stage of Pokémon with a three-stage evolutionary line is 21.1. The median level is 18 and the mode is 16 with 21 Pokémon. The minimum and maximum are 7 (Caterpie, Weedle, and Wurmple) and 52 (Pawniard), respectively.
    • The mean Evolution level for the second stage of Pokémon with a three-stage evolutionary line is 32.3. The median level is 35.5 and the mode level is 36 with 20 Pokémon. The minimum and maximum are 10 (Metapod, Kakuna, Silcoon, and Cascoon) and 64 (Zweilous), respectively.
  • On average, Pokémon introduced in Generation II evolve at the lowest level, 25.8. Pokémon introduced in Generation V evolve at the highest level, 35.6.
  • Generation VII introduced the fewest Pokémon that can evolve by level-up, with 26. Generation V introduced the most Pokémon that can evolve by level-up, with 59.
  • Generation IV introduced the smallest range of Pokémon Evolution levels, which is 38. Generation VIII introduced the largest range, which is 50.
  • At least one Pokémon can evolve starting at each level from 14 to 45.
