Title: Tammy (M13)

Tammy (Japanese: トモー Tomo) is a supporting character who appeared in Zoroark: Master of Illusions. She owns a flower shop in Crown City that she operates with her Tangrowth. She is also good friends with Joe.


== History ==
Tammy and Joe, a close friend of hers, were first seen fleeing from the intense flames left behind by an Entei by taking refuge in her flower shop. All the while, they were unaware that the Entei was actually an illusion created by a Zoroark.
Ash and his friends first met her after her Tangrowth put Zorua to sleep. She was excited to see Karl, Joe's grandson, after being away from Crown City for a while, and invited everyone back to her shop for tea. There, she explained the legend of the city's guardians, as well as the story of the disaster that befell Crown City twenty years prior. Tammy, like Joe, refused to evacuate like the other townspeople because they believed that the city's guardians would not seek to destroy Crown City, like the news had been claiming.
Her suspicions proved to be correct, as she and Joe later witnessed the real city's guardians battling against Zoroark. She joined Ash, his friends, Karl, and Rowena at Joe's workshop. They soon discovered Grings Kodai's scheme to steal the Time Ripple's power. Tammy became fearful of the city's destruction occurring again, and urged Ash and his friends to do something.
Tammy and Joe later joined Ash and his friends after they foiled Kodai's plot. She was overjoyed to see the city's plants intact, and watched as Celebi made several plants grow before it time travelled away. She was among those who confronted Kodai and witnessed his arrest by Officer Jenny. Afterwards, she joined Ash, his friends, and Joe at the seaport to see Karl, Rowena, Zorua, and Zoroark set sail for the Unova region.
