Title: Rod (TCG GB)

If you were looking for the fishing item, see Fishing.

Rod (Japanese: リュウドー Ryūdō) is the fourth Grand Master. He plays using the Legendary Dragonite Deck (Japanese: でんせつのドラゴンデッキ Legendary Dragon Deck). In Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!, he uses the だいドラゴンデッキ Great Dragon Deck.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
Rod is the last of the four Grand Masters that is available to battle after all eight Master Medals have been obtained.


=== Legendary Dragonite Deck ===
Main article: Legendary Dragonite Deck (TCG)


== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==
Since the start of the game, Rod was kidnapped by Team Great Rocket and held imprisoned in the GR Psychic Stronghold. When freed by the main player after defeating Mami, he will give away a Dragonite.
Before the player can duel with Kanzaki, Biruritchi's bodyguard, the player is required to fight him in the Pokémon Dome to obtain one of the Legendary cards: Dragonite.
