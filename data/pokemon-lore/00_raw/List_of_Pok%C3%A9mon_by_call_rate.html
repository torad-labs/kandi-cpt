List of Pokémon by call rate

  • Italiano
  • 中文
It has been suggested that this article be moved to Call rate.
Please discuss whether or not to move it on its talk page.

This is a list of Pokémon by their call rate. The call rate is the default probability, in percentage, of a wild Pokémon calling an ally for an SOS Battle at the end of each turn. While the call rate is a fixed value, the probability may change depending on the conditions of the battle.

Pokémon with the highest call rate of 15 are most likely to successfully call for help during SOS Battles, while Pokémon with a call rate of 3 are least likely. Pokémon with a call rate of 0 will never call for help; notably, this includes all Legendary Pokémon, all Ultra Beasts, all Pokémon encountered via Island Scan, and all Pokémon encountered in Ultra Space Wilds.

This list only includes Pokémon that can legitimately encountered in the wild in games with SOS Battles.
