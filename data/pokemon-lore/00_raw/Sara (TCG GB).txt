Title: Sara (TCG GB)

Sara (Japanese: マリ Mari) is one of the three Club Members from the Water Club, under Club Master Amy, in Pokémon Trading Card Game. She is the twin sister of fellow Club Member, Amanda.


== Pokémon Trading Card Game ==
The player duels with Sara in a required 2 prize card battle in order to eventually reach Club Master Amy.
Upon victory, the player is rewarded with two Colosseum booster packs.

== Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! ==


=== Splashing About Deck ===
Main article: Splashing About Deck (TCG)
Splashing About Deck (Japanese: みずあそびデッキ) is the only deck played by Sara, played in a 4 prize card battle. Defeating Sara will get the player two Legendary Power booster packs.
This battle becomes available after GR3 is defeated at the Water Club.

== Trivia ==
Sara challenges the player in a two prize card duel. It is the only time that this happens in both Pokémon Trading Card Game and Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!.
