Title: Sam (TCG GB)

Sam (Japanese: ミカサ Mikasa) is one of Dr. Mason's technicians in the video games Pokémon Trading Card Game and Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!.


== In the games ==
At the beginning of Pokémon Trading Card Game, Dr. Mason introduces him to the player Mark. The player may wish to ask Sam various questions about the rules of the game, and then he must battle Sam in a tutorial match narrated by Dr. Mason, where all actions of the player must follow a script. Mark uses Practice Deck and Sam uses Sam's Practice Deck. 
Afterwards, Sam remains available to answer the same questions, or to repeat the same tutorial match, or for a non-scripted "normal duel" with the decks from the tutorial match. In both the tutorial match and the normal duel, the decks are not shuffled, so the cards are dealt the same way. When defeated in the normal duel, Sam rewards the player a Colosseum booster pack containing only Energy cards.
At the beginning of Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR!, the player Mark/Mint can choose to duel him as an optional tutorial match. The player uses a different version of the Practice Deck and Sam uses a different version of Sam's Practice Deck.
