Title: Magnus

If you were looking for the character of the day from The Bonds of Evolution!, see Magnus (XY028).

Magnus (Japanese: コブシ Kobushi) is the founder of the Pokéathlon Dome in Johto, located near to the National Park.


== In the core series games ==
Magnus will first meet the player when they visit the Pokéathlon Dome for the first time. He can later be seen in the stands, observing the competition, but is inaccessible and cannot be talked to. Upon entering the Friendship Room, he will congratulate the player. After talking to him and obtaining the National Pokédex, the Supreme Cup is unlocked.
