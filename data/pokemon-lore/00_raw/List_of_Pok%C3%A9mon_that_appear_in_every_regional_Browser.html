List of Pokémon that appear in every regional Browser

  • Italiano

This is a list of Pokémon that appear in every regional Browser in the Pokémon Ranger series, listed in their National Pokédex order. As of Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs, this includes the Fiore Regional Browser, the Almia Regional Browser, and the Oblivia Regional Browser. 69 of the 386 Pokémon in existence at the time of the release of Fiore's Browser are in this list.

Trivia

  • There are no Generation IV Pokémon in this list as they had not been released at the time of Fiore's Browser. However, every Generation IV Pokémon found in Almia's Browser is also featured in Oblivia's Browser, with the three exceptions of Spiritomb, Cresselia, and Darkrai.
    • Similarly, because no Pokémon Ranger games were made after Generation IV, all Pokémon from subsequent generations are absent from this list.
