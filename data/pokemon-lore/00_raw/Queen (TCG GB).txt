Title: Queen (TCG GB)

Queen (Japanese: クィーン) is the fifth and final Dungeon Master in the Card Dungeon, located within the Game Center in Pokémon Trading Card Game 2: The Invasion of Team GR! When battled, Queen will ask the player to wager either 50 or 100 Coins on the outcome of the battle; refusing will cause the player to immediately leave the dungeon. Players who defeat Queen receive double the amount wagered. Win or lose, the player will leave the dungeon after the game is completed.


== Powerful Pokémon Deck ==
Powerful Pokémon Deck (Japanese: きょうりょくポケモンデッキ) is the deck played by Queen. The deck features Pokémon of varying types as well as energy matching each type. It is based on a variation of the Haymaker deck archetype.
