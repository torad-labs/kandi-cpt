List of Pokémon by Photodex order

Ldex

This is a list of Pokémon in the order dictated by Lental's Pokémon Photodex, which acts like a Pokédex for the Lental region, meaning that the Pokémon listed are all native to Lental as well. The Gallery starts with Vivillon, and roughly follows the order in which it is possible to encounter Pokémon in New Pokémon Snap in order of course unlock, starting with the Florio Nature Park and following its native Pokémon all the way to research level 3. Pokémon introduced in the 2.0.0 update are placed after the originals, also in the approximate order they are encountered in each course.

There are 234 numbered Pokémon in the Photodex. A Pokémon is recorded in the Photodex after a photo of the Pokémon is submitted to Professor Mirror for the first time.
