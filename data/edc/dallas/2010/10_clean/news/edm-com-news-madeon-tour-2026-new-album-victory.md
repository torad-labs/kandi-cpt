# Madeon Announces 2026 Headline Tour Ahead of New Album, 'Victory' - EDM

Just a week after releasing ‘Fire Away‘ alongside Slayyyter, Madeon has announced a 31-date headlining tour of North America in support of his upcoming album, *Victory*.

The French producer and singer-songwriter, whose real name is Hugo Leclercq, has never been content to just release music. Due out June 26th, *Victory* is his first full-length since the Grammy-nominated *Good Faith* dropped back in 2019, but the album and the tour are almost beside the point. What Leclercq is really selling is a mythology.

Weeks before today’s announcement, a cryptic billboard appeared in LA pointing to a website for something called *Victory*, presented as an actual band: Leclercq on vocals alongside three fictional bandmates named Spikey, Juno and Red. The site receives weekly lore updates and its backstory deepens in real time for fans willing to dig in. It’s a continuation of a practice he has refined across his illustrious career, planting ciphers, coded messages and puzzle trails across songs, videos and websites for his most devoted listeners to unravel together.

Madeon previewed the ‘Victory Live Tour’ at a sold-out Red Rocks Amphitheatre show last October, teasing live production that builds on the stunning stagecraft of his celebrated ‘Good Faith Forever’ run. The visual team from that tour is also returning, which is better news for fans than they probably realize.

We sat down with Leclercq and his team at the time for an in-depth interview about the story and ambitious mechanics behind the illusory show. They developed *The CODEX*, which they called a “secret bible of techniques” used to execute their sophisticated approach to interpolating darkness, vanishing points and other visual elements into a cohesive experience.

“It takes a while to figure out how to communicate what’s in your heart,” Leclercq said. “Everyone has a different language, and when you figure out what your language is, that’s when the world opens up.”

Pre-sale tickets to the ‘Victory Live Tour’ will be available May 13th at 10am local. You can sign up here and check out the full list of shows below.
