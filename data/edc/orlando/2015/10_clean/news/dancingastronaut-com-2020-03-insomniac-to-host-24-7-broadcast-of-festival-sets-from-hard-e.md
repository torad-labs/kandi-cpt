# Insomniac to host 24/7 broadcast of festival sets from HARD, EDC, Okeechobee, and more with 'Insomniac Rewind' [Stream]

While 2020’s festival circuit remains on hold for the foreseeable future due to the COVID-19 pandemic, Pasquale Rotella and Insomniac Events are providing a sustainable source of home entertainment for the season of social distancing. Running Thursday, March 19 at 2:00 p.m. EST through March 31, the acclaimed event production giant will be endlessly broadcasting live sets from dusk until dawn, pulling from the Insomniac archives between 2015 and 2020.

The round-the-clock “Insomniac Rewind” stream will host a bottomless sequence of DJ performances from Insomniac Events’ staple festivals from around the globe including EDC Las Vegas, EDC Orlando, EDC Mexico, Hard Summer and Okeechobee.
