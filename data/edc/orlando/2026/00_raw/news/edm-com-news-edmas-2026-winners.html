Martin Garrix, Dom Dolla, ILLENIUM and More Win 2026 EDMAs: See the Full List of Winners

The 2026 Electronic Dance Music Awards ceremony takes place Friday at the Clevelander.

Jason Heffler • March 23, 2026
FacebookTweetPin

The winners of the Electronic Dance Music Awards have been revealed ahead of the 2026 ceremony in Miami.

Driven by its fan-based voting system, the EDMA ceremony annually takes place at the height of Miami Music Week and dishes out unique trophies that resemble DJ decks, including a functional jog-wheel. Organizers have announced the 2026 winners ahead of this year’s event, which heads to its new South Beach home at the famed Clevelander on Friday, March 27th.

This year’s winners include superstar DJs like Martin Garrix, John Summit, ILLENIUM, Hardwell, Alison Wonderland, Dom Dolla, Armin van Buuren, Sub Focus, Mau P and Subtronics, among many others.

Chris Lake, Skrillex and ANITA B QUEEN are taking home the EDMA for Dance Song of the Year, winning for ‘LA NOCHE.’ Meanwhile, Seven Lions nabbed the EDMA for Favorite Album, winning for his stunning third solo album, Asleep in the Garden of Infernal Stars.

Elsewhere, EDM.com is presenting the EDMA for Breakout Artist of the Year, won by the fast-rising dubstep producer LEVEL UP. Max Styler, a member of the EDM.com Class of 2025, won the EDMA for Producer of the Year and AVELLO, a member of this year’s Class, nabbed the EDMAs for Best New Artist and Breakout Remixer of the Year.

Check out the full list of 2026 winners below and find out more via the EDMAs’ official website.
