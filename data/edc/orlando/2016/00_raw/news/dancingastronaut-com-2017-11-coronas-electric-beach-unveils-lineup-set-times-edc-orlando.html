Corona Electric Beach unveils lineup & set times for EDC Orlando

Dancing Astronaut November 4, 2017
2 min read
Share

Like a lime in lager, Corona’s Electric Beach stage will prove a sweet addition to Electric Daisy Carnival Orlando’s seventh edition, set to unfold on November 10 and 11.
The place where “beats meet the beach,” Corona’s Electric Beach stage will feature trap and bass tastemaker, Party Favor as Friday’s headlining artist, hosting the head honcho of the Fool’s Gold record label, A-Trak as Saturday’s top bill. With set times newly announced for Electric Beach, EDC ticket holders can expect to beat the Orlando heat with the likes of Bonnie X Clyde, Doc Brown, and various other performers.


Corona’s Electric Beach stage will feature an exclusive performance from Sharps on Friday, the recently announced winner of Dancing Astronaut, Corona Electric Beach, and Insomniac “Discovery Project’s” ‘Once in a Lifetime’ DJ contest. The contest required entrants to submit a 3-8-minute original track and a 25-35-minute mix for judging. The unanimous victor of the aptly titled contest, Sharps will take the decks from 3:30-5:00 PM on Friday.

Sharps’ Electric Beach debut is reflective of the brand’s commitment to create a high caliber festival experience, a derivative of its engagement with artists commensurately dedicated to their craft.

“Corona works hard to provide meaningful experiences for all of our consumers,” John Alvarado, Vice President of Marketing for Corona states of the initiative. “Working with artists that are passionate and dedicated to their craft is part of the essence of Corona Electric Beach.”
Corona’s Electric Beach has accordingly become a festival mainstay, featured at a number of dance music events, including Chicago’s Spring Awakening Music Festival, and HARD Summer Music Festival.
