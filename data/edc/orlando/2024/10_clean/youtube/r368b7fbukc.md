# GRYFFIN LIVE @ EDC LAS VEGAS 2024

Scraped 2026-05-30 from https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R368B7FBUkc.

---

My first time ever playing the EDC mainstage, and to be completely honest, I was really nervous.

As I was prepping my set, I knew I could either play it safe or try something new. This was one of the biggest stages I’ve ever had the chance to play, so at first I didn't know which route to take. But after months of working on a potential new direction, combining the new sounds of PULSE alongside styles of music that are truly inspiring me right now, I realized how much fun I was having and I knew I had to go for it.

Though I went in uncertain, I truly felt the love during the performance, and it reminded me how fortunate I am to have all of your support. Thank you for a night I’ll never forget 🫶
