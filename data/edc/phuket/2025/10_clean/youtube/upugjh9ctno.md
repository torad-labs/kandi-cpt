The most complete overview  Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Thailand 2025 - Phuket

LOCATION:
Boat Avenue Lakefront, Phuket
With over 27 years of history, Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) has expanded its international festival footprint to eight countries across four continents around the world. A Thai promoter has brought the EDC experience to Thailand in 2025.

STAGES
Designed by Insomniac’s globally renowned production team, kineticFIELD will incorporate top-notch lighting, fireworks and lasers to create a mind-blowing audiovisual experience, allowing Headliners to experience world-class music in An immersive space. Bring the energy to the dance floor!
Besides the main stage, the event will also feature three more stages including circuitGROUNDS, stereoBLOOM and boomBOX artCAR. Each area contained within EDC represents a distinct union of technology and nature, where elements mix to ignite the senses and inspire the imagination, presenting diverse music genres like house, hardstyle, bass, techno and trance music to bring fresh excitement and anticipation to the fans.

EXPERIENCE
EDC is known as an ultimate dreamlike carnival. Thus, in addition to significant stage performances, this year’s carnival experience has been fully upgraded. Beautiful art installations, circus performances, and unique EDC KANDI bracelet DIY and beauty bar experiences will take every attendee into a dreamlike, immersive wonderland, providing a comprehensive cultural tourism experience.
