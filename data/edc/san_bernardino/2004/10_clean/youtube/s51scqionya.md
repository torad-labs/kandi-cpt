On September 1, 2004, we lost a brother, when Deputy Ron Ives was killed in the line of duty when his police motorcycle was struck.  
Ron served the department for eight years. In 2002, Ron rode cross-country from Long Beach to Ground Zero in New York City.  Ron also coordinated the Rancho Cucamonga Station’s Annual Bicycle Rodeo, now named “The Ron Ives Bicycle Rodeo” in his memory.  
Every day, men and women in law enforcement risk their lives to keep their communities safe.  Ron made the ultimate sacrifice. We honor his bravery and his legacy will live on in our hearts forever.  


E.O.W. September 1, 2004
