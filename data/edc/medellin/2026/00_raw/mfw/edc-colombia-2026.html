The Scene

EDC Colombia is the Colombian edition of Electric Daisy Carnival, the globally renowned electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac Events. This 2-day event brings EDC’s signature mix of world-class DJs, massive stage production, immersive art installations, and carnival-style attractions to the Unidad Deportiva Atanasio Girardot sports complex. Featuring artists across genres like house, techno, trance, and bass music, EDC Colombia aims to deliver the vibrant, high-energy atmosphere of the iconic festival while highlighting Medellín’s growing reputation as a hub for electronic music culture in Latin America. - Copyright Music Festival Wizard
