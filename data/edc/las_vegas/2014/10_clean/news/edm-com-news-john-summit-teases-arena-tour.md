# John Summit Signals Arena Tour Is Coming for 'CTRL ESCAPE' - EDM

Tier 2 (reference) corpus material.

John Summit took to social media this week to hint that an arena tour is on the horizon.

The Chicago native shared a string of posts on X and an Instagram story pointing toward what fans have been waiting on since the rollout of his sophomore album, *CTRL ESCAPE*, began earlier this spring. On his Instagram story, he shared a clip of the orchestral ‘Where You Are’ intro from his last run with a promise that he plans to “go all out” on the next one.

He also shared a rendering of a packed arena with an elaborate set design that appeared to lean into the album’s corporate-escape concept, featuring what appear to be office windows running along the sides of the stage. One fan replied to the X post, writing, “I see the vision with the offices below,” to which Summit responded with a smirk emoji.

As for the orchestra reference, the concept first took shape during his three-night Kia Forum run in November 2024. Summit closed out his *Comfort in Chaos* tour by reimagining ‘Where You Are’ and ‘Shiver’ with a live string section backing vocalist HAYLA. Those Kia Forum dates were part of a broader stretch in which he has also sold out Madison Square Garden, The O2 Arena in London, and Folsom Field.

*CTRL ESCAPE* arrived on April 15th, 2026, the same Tax Day the former accountant once dreaded back in his cubicle days. The rollout included a surprise open-to-close set at Red Rocks Amphitheatre one week prior, followed by a last-minute pop-up at Coachella’s Do LaB stage two days later.

The tease arrives during a packed period in his calendar. Summit is preparing for a nine-week Monday residency at [UNVRS] in Ibiza beginning in June, the return of his Experts Only Festival on Randall’s Island in September, and slots at Lollapalooza, Tomorrowland, and EDC Las Vegas across the summer.
