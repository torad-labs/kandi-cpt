*New music highlights from Dancing Astronaut’s Artists to Watch in 2025*

**Ugo Banchi – Dance With Ibiza (Linksa Remix)**

Back in May, Linska was sidestage at EDC Las Vegas when she heard a familiar track begin to mix in during Tiësto’s set. And that track was none other than her then-unreleased reimagining of Ugo Ibanchi’s “Dance With Ibiza,” with it subsequently finding its way into tracklists from Gorgon City — who technically debuted it just a few days earlier — Dom Dolla, Adam Beyer, and more. Fast forward to the first New Music Friday of October and Linska has green lit her update of the 2023 release on Solomun’s Diynamic Records, with her making it available as a free download on her SoundCloud. – Ross Goldenberg

**Prospa – Love Songs (feat. Kosmo Kint)**

Named a *Dancing Astronaut* Artist to Watch in 2025, Prospa have finally dropped their long-awaited ID, “Love Songs” featuring Kosmo Kint. The release marks another milestone in the duo’s rapid ascent, following a string of successful 2025 singles including “You Don’t Own Me,” “This Rhythm,” and “Don’t Stop.” Widely regarded as one of the UK’s most exciting electronic acts, Prospa continue to build on their reputation for crafting instantly recognizable records that resonate on a global scale. *– Alex Lambeau*

**LUSU – Bang The Box**

LUSU return to Eli Brown’s Arcane label with their new single “Bang The Box.” Eli Brown and Arcane were early supporters of LUSU, having been the home of 2024’s “Ready For The Bass” single and 2025’s *Siren* EP previously. LUSU — comprised of DJ/producers Wiwek and Mike Cervello — recently released an *ID Mix* on their YouTube, previewing even more of what’s to come for the duo specializing in a futuristic, high-energy brand of techno. *– Zach Salafia*

**Gabss – Giddy Up**

Gabss returns this week with his new delivery “Giddy Up,” out now as the maiden release on AYYBO’s freshly launched GDB Records. A longtime fan favorite, Gabss brings his signature high-energy style to the label’s early catalog, making this release a standout moment for both artists. With Gabss’ infectious groove and AYYBO’s bold curatorial direction, “Giddy Up” sets the tone for the kind of momentum GDB Records is ready to build.* – Danielle Carty*

**KETTAMA – Archangel [Album]**

KETTAMA‘s debut album *Archangel* has marked a massive leap for the Galway producer. The album showcases his evolution from raw club disruptor to fully realized artist. Across *Archangel’s* 15 tracks, he blends hard-house intensity with trance-laced euphoria, reflecting a decade of honing his craft. Collaborations with Interplanetary Criminal, DJ HEARTSTRING, and Fred again.. expand his range while staying true to his G-Town roots. The debut is more than just a collection of dancefloor weapons, with *Archangel* proving KETTAMA’s ability to craft a narrative body of work that balances emotion and club catharsis.* – Oliver Broek*

**Charlotte de Witte – The Heads That Know**

Belgian powerhouse and techno queen Charlotte de Witte unveils her latest album preview, “The Heads That Know” featuring Comma Dee. The track is an acid-laced, high-energy techno production, blending her signature dark style with Comma Dee’s rhymes. This single arrives as the final preview of her self-titled debut album, *Charlotte de Witte*, set for release on November 7. Already tested on dancefloors worldwide, “The Heads That Know” has become a fan-favorite in her sets and a tribute to the underground spirit that defines her sound. *– Liana Stern*

**Baset – Drift**

Cairo-born DJ and producer Baset makes his debut on ARTBAT’s UPPERGROUND with “Drift,” marking a major step in his rise as one of melodic techno’s most exciting new names. The single follows the runaway success of his breakout collaboration “Sierra” with Argy on Cercle Records. Baset’s arrival on UPPERGROUND signals both a personal milestone and a statement of intent for one of melodic techno’s emerging talents. *– Alex Lambeau*

**Chris Lake, Skrillex & ANITA B QUEEN – LA NOCHE**

The generational Chris Lake release run continues. All he’s done to follow up his debut album, *Chemistry*, is release his “Galvanize” remix on top of collaborations with Sammy Virji, Disclosure, Chris Lorenzo (as Anti Up), and now Skrillex. In what is one of the year’s biggest collaborations to date, Chris Lake and Skrillex team up with ANITA B QUEEN on “LA NOCHE.” Fans will recognize the new single as the opening track from Lake’s recent sets — a highlight from his Brooklyn Storehouse and Black Book on the Water shows. *–Zach Salafia*
