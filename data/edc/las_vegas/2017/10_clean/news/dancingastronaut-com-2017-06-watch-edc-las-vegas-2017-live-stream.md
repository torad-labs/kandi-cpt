*The time has come.*

Tonight, June 16, Electric Daisy Carnival touches down on the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. This year, the country’s largest summer electronic music festival attracts a predictably impressive roster of talents from various points of the dance spectrum, as well as ravers from around the world.

For those unable to make the journey to Vegas for the weekend, EDC has partnered with Red Bull TV to present a comprehensive live stream of some of the lineup’s most enticing talents. Presented by Hannah Rad, the broadcast appeals a broad array of tastes through its diverse selection of over 80 performances. Regardless of whether one prefers REZZ over RÜFÜS, or Nicole Moudaber b2b Chris Liebing over Alison Wonderland b2b Diplo b2b Jauz, Red Bull TV’s live stream provides a schedule which will satisfy thoroughly.
