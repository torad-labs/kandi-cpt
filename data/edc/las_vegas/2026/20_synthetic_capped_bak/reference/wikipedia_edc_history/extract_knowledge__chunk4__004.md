Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music festivals held globally. Between 2016 and 2019, the flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, was hosted at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. In 2016, it took place from June 17–19, featuring a performance by DJ Marshmello and DJ Tiësto. The 2017 event saw over 135,000 attendees on its opening night; however, it recorded a twofold increase in medical calls compared to the previous year, including one fatality attributed to heat exposure and the ingestion of MDMA and TFMPP. In 2018, the event was rescheduled to May 18–20 to mitigate heat, introducing on-site camping and earlier opening times. Total attendance for the 2018 event was estimated at 411,400. The 2019 event occurred May 17–19, where high winds led to partial stage closures and the subsequent accidental departure of attendees due to confusing safety instructions. Local police reported 65 arrests during the 2019 festival.

International iterations of the festival occurred across several regions:

*   **Mexico:** Held at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City. Events took place February 27–28, 2016; February 25–26, 2017; February 24–25, 2018; and February 23–24, 2019.
*   **Orlando:** Hosted at Tinker Field. Events occurred November 4–5, 2016; November 10–11, 2017; and November 9–10, 2018. In 2019, the festival duration was extended from two days to three.
*   **United Kingdom:** Held in Milton Keynes on July 9, 2016. The 2017 event was cancelled.
*   **Asia:** EDC India debuted at the Buddh International Circuit in New Delhi on November 12–13, 2016. EDC China debuted at the Shanghai International Music Park on April 29–30, 2018. EDC Japan was held at Zozo Marine Stadium and Makuhari Beach Park in Chiba on May 12–13, 2018 (affected by heavy rain) and May 11–12, 2019. EDC Korea took place at Seoul Land in Gwacheon on August 31 and September 1, 2019.
*   **New York:** Held at Citi Field in Queens from May 14–15, 2016.
