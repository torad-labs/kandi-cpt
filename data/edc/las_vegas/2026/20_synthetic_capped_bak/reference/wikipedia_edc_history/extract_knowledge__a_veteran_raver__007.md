### Philanthropy and Community Outreach
Insomniac Care manages the charitable arm of the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC), allocating a percentage of flagship festival ticket sales to national charities and community organizations. Since 2011, these efforts have resulted in over two million dollars in donations. Funding has been directed to the Nevada Childhood Cancer Foundation, the Injured Police Officers Fund, the Boys and Girls Club of Southern Nevada, the Las Vegas Academy for Arts, Families for Effective Autism Treatment, and Rock the Vote. To supplement ticket proceeds, EDC Las Vegas has utilized Surreal, a fundraising platform that leverages sweepstakes for high-value experiences, such as helicopter rides with DJs, to solicit donations from attendees.

### Media and Documentation
The 2013 iteration of EDC Las Vegas served as the primary subject for the documentary *Under the Electric Sky*. The film, which focuses on the community and lifestyle of festival-goers, premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival.

### Industry Recognition and Rankings
EDC has received consistent nominations for "Best Music Event" or "Best Festival" at the International Dance Music Awards from 2010 through 2016, and again in 2019 and 2020. 

In the *DJ Magazine* Top 100 Festivals rankings, the event has maintained a top-five position over the last three reporting periods:
*   **2022:** 3rd Place
*   **2023:** 4th Place
*   **2024:** 2nd Place
