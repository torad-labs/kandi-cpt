Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a music festival produced by Insomniac Events. The entity was established after Pasquale Rotella, CEO of Insomniac Events, secured the rights to the "Electric Daisy Carnival" name from Philip Blaine in 1995. Following the creation of the Organic '96 event, Insomniac debuted EDC in 1997 at the Shrine Expo Hall in Los Angeles, California. In 1999, the event was hosted at Lake Dolores Waterpark in Newberry Springs, California.

Between 2000 and 2009, the festival relocated frequently and expanded geographically. The 2000 event at the International Agri-Center in Tulare, California, drew 24,000 attendees but resulted in a contract cancellation due to noise complaints. The 2001 event at Hansen Dam in Southern California introduced a multi-stage format featuring the Merry Go Round, Fun House, Clown Alley, Confusin' & Amuzin' Mirror Maze, Bassrush Arena, and Cosmic Healing Temple. This year also marked the first EDC Texas, held at Thunderhill Raceway Park in Austin.

In 2002, the California event took place at Queen Mary Events Park in Long Beach. A planned Texas event at the Travis County Exposition Center was cancelled shortly before its date, though a make-up show with an altered lineup was held one week later. From 2003 to 2006, the festival was hosted at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino, California, before moving to the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum in Exposition Park for 2007 and 2008.

Expansion continued in 2008 with the introduction of EDC Colorado on June 14 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora. In 2009, the California event utilized the southern half of Exposition Park and the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum. The 2009 Colorado event occurred on June 13 and featured headliners including Paul Van Dyk and Infected Mushroom. Additionally, the first EDC Puerto Rico was held on August 14, 2009, at the Arena Fairgrounds in San Juan.
