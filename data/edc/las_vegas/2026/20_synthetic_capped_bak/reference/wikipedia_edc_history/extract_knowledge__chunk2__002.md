The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music festivals. In 2010, the Los Angeles event recorded an attendance of approximately 185,000 people, with ticket prices ranging from $60 to over $100. The event faced criticism from promoters and local authorities after minors under the age of 16 entered the venue. A crowd stampede resulted in over 100 hospitalizations, and a 15-year-old attendee, Sasha Rodriguez, died following the ingestion of MDMA.

In response to these incidents, the city of Los Angeles suspended all remaining 2010 events and future scheduling pending the implementation of new safety provisions. These mandates required the hiring of on-site medical doctors and raised the minimum attendee age to 18. Other 2010 regional events included EDC Colorado on June 12 at the Arapahoe County Fairground in Aurora, EDC Dallas on June 19 at Fair Park (11,000 attendees), and EDC Puerto Rico on August 28 at the Estadio Sixto Escobar in San Juan.

In 2011, the flagship festival relocated from Los Angeles to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in Nevada, taking place from June 24–26 with a reported attendance of 230,000. Additional 2011 events included EDC Orlando (May 27–28 at Tinker Field and the Florida Citrus Bowl grounds), which saw 12,000 attendees on Friday and 20,000 on Saturday. EDC Colorado returned to the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds on June 11.

The 2011 EDC Dallas event occurred on June 18 at Fair Park with approximately 25,000 attendees. Extreme temperatures exceeding 110 degrees Fahrenheit caused dozens of hospitalizations and at least one fatality, resulting in the cessation of the Dallas series. EDC Puerto Rico was held on August 27 at the Estadio Sixto Escobar. Michael Calfan performed at the 2016 Las Vegas iteration of the festival.
