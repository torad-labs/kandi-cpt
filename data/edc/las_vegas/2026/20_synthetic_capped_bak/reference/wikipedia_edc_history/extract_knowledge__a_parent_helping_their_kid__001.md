Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is an electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac Events. The rights to the name were granted in 1995 by Philip Blaine to Pasquale Rotella, the CEO of Insomniac Events. Following the 1996 "Organic '96" event, Insomniac debuted the inaugural Electric Daisy Carnival in 1997 at the Shrine Expo Hall in Los Angeles, California. In 1999, the event was hosted at Lake Dolores Waterpark in Newberry Springs, California.

From 2000 to 2009, the festival transitioned through various venues. The 2000 event at the International Agri-Center in Tulare, California, drew 24,000 attendees but was terminated by the venue due to noise complaints. In 2001, the festival moved to Hansen Dam in Southern California and introduced a multi-stage format featuring the Merry Go Round, Fun House, Clown Alley, Confusin' & Amuzin' Mirror Maze, Bassrush Arena, and Cosmic Healing Temple. That same year, EDC expanded to Austin, Texas, at the Thunderhill Raceway Park.

Between 2002 and 2007, venues included the Queen Mary Events Park in Long Beach (2002), the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino (2003–2006), and the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum (2007). In 2008, the California event took place on June 28 at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, while a Colorado edition occurred on June 14 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora.

In 2009, the California festival expanded to a two-day format at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum and the southern half of Exposition Park, recording approximately 55,000 attendees on Friday and 99,000 on Saturday. Additional 2009 events included a June 13 show at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora, headlined by Paul Van Dyk and Infected Mushroom, and the first EDC Puerto Rico on August 14 at the Arena Fairgrounds in San Juan.
