# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac Events. The events are characterized by large-scale production, themed stages, and a diverse lineup of international DJs and electronic music producers.

## Event History

### EDC Mexico
The 2020 edition of EDC Mexico took place from February 28 to March 2 at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City.

### EDC Las Vegas
The 2020 festival was originally scheduled for May 15–17 but was postponed to October 2–4 due to the COVID-19 pandemic. A virtual iteration of the festival was streamed via Twitch and YouTube during the original May dates; however, the October physical event was cancelled in August 2020.

In 2021, the event was initially scheduled for May 21–23. Following a reopening plan passed by Clark County on April 20, which required a 60% vaccination rate among residents before lifting restrictions on large-scale gatherings, Insomniac CEO Pasquale Rotella announced the event's postponement. The festival was subsequently held from October 22–24, 2021.

### EDC Orlando
The 2024 edition of EDC Orlando featured a lineup comprising both established and emerging artists within the electronic dance music genre.

### EDC Thailand
EDC Thailand debuted from January 17–19, 2025, at Boat Avenue Lakefront in Phuket.

## Logistics and Production

### Site Management
For recent iterations, Insomniac has implemented changes to site layouts to optimize crowd flow and increase the size of dance floors. In Las Vegas, the festival utilized expanded areas of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway grounds following the removal of various RV hookups that had previously rendered certain sections of the venue unusable.

### Stage Design
The festival utilizes various themed environments, including the kineticBloom mainstage.

## Safety and Incidents
The inaugural EDC Thailand event in 2025 was marked by safety concerns, including the deaths of two foreign tourists in suspected drug-related incidents. Additionally, three individuals were arrested for the possession or use of illegal substances during the event.
