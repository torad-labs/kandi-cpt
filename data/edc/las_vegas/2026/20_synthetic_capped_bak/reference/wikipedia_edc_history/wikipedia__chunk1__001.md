**Electric Daisy Carnival** (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music festivals produced by Insomniac Events. Founded by Pasquale Rotella and Philip Blaine, the event has evolved from a single-day gathering into one of the world's largest dance music festivals.

### Overview
The origins of the festival date back to 1995, when Philip Blaine granted Pasquale Rotella the rights to use the name "Electric Daisy Carnival." Prior to the official debut of the festival, Rotella and Blaine produced the Organic '96 event. The inaugural Electric Daisy Carnival debuted in 1997 at the Shrine Expo Hall in Los Angeles, California.

### Timeline and Venues
The festival's location shifted frequently during its first decade:
* **1999:** The event was held at Lake Dolores Waterpark in Newberry Springs, California.
* **2000:** The festival moved to the International Agri-Center in Tulare, California.
* **2002:** The event was hosted at the Queen Mary Events Park in Long Beach, California.
* **2003–2006:** The NOS Events Center in San Bernardino, California, served as the primary venue.
* **2007–2009:** The event returned to Los Angeles, utilizing the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum and the southern half of Exposition Park.

### Stages and Production
In 2001, at the Hansen Dam venue in Southern California, the festival expanded its production to include multiple themed stages. These included:
* merryGoRound
* funHouse
* clownAlley
* mirrorMaze (Confusin' & Amuzin')
* bassrushArena
* cosmicHealingTemple

### Regional Expansion
Starting in 2001, Insomniac expanded the EDC brand beyond California:
* **Texas:** The first EDC Texas occurred in 2001 at Thunderhill Raceway Park in Austin. A 2002 edition planned for the Travis County Exposition Center was cancelled by the venue shortly before the date, resulting in a rescheduled make-up show with a revised lineup.
* **Colorado:** Events were held at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora in 2008 and 2009. The 2009 edition featured headliners including Paul Van Dyk and Infected Mushroom.
* **Puerto Rico:** The first EDC Puerto Rico was held on August 14, 2009, at the Arena Fairgrounds in San Juan.

### Logistics and Attendance
The festival experienced significant growth in attendance between 2000 and 2009. The 2000 event in Tulare attracted 24,000 attendees. By 2009, the Los Angeles event had expanded to a two-day format, recording approximately 55,000 attendees on Friday and 99,000 attendees on Saturday.

### Safety and Community Relations
The festival has faced challenges regarding local ordinances. Following the 2000 event at the International Agri-Center, the venue cancelled its contract with Insomniac Events due to numerous noise complaints from the surrounding community.
