**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is an electronic dance music (EDM) festival organized by the promoter and distributor Insomniac. The event features various subgenres of electronic music, including house, techno, drum and bass, trance, and dubstep. While the brand operates internationally—with current events in Orlando, Mexico City, Medellín, Suzhou, Phuket, and Seoul—the flagship event is EDC Las Vegas.
