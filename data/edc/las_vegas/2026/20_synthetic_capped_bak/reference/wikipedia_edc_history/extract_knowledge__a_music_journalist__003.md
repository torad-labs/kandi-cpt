**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Historical Operations: 2012–2015**

**Las Vegas Iterations**
Between 2012 and 2015, the flagship EDC event was hosted at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. The 2012 festival (June 8–10) saw a 30% attendance increase to 320,000 total participants. On the second night, winds reaching 30 MPH forced a 1:00 a.m. shutdown, displacing 90,000 attendees to the bleachers. DJs Markus Schulz and Steve Aoki performed brief, impromptu sets on the "Insomniac Wide Awake" mobile art car before officials closed the stage due to safety concerns. To compensate for the disruption, Insomniac permitted Saturday pass-holders to return on Sunday. 

The 2014 event (June 20–22) sold all 345,000 tickets by June 18, averaging 134,000 attendees per day. This iteration featured the largest structural stage in North America. The 2015 event (June 19–21) utilized a blinking owl as its central stage theme.

**Regional and International Expansion**
Insomniac expanded the EDC brand across several domestic and international markets:
*   **United States:** EDC New York was held at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey (May 18–20, 2012; May 23–24, 2015), drawing 45,000 daily attendees in 2012. EDC Orlando was hosted at Tinker Field (November 9–10, 2012; November 6–7, 2015). A Chicago event occurred in 2013.
*   **International:** EDC Mexico debuted March 15–16, 2014, with a second event at Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez (February 28–March 1, 2015). The UK events took place at the National Bowl in Milton Keynes (2013, 2014, and July 17, 2015). Other locations included San Juan, Puerto Rico (Estadio Sixto Escobar), and a debut in São Paulo, Brazil (Autódromo José Carlos Pace, December 4–5, 2015).

**Incidents and Media**
The festivals were marked by specific casualties and media coverage. In 2012, a 31-year-old male from Florida died after being struck by a truck upon exiting the Las Vegas Motor Speedway on June 11. In 2015, a UC Irvine graduate died during the second night of the Las Vegas event; the death was attributed to MDMA-induced dehydration and hyperthermia. In 2014, the festival was the subject of the documentary *Under the Electric Sky*, which premiered at the Sundance Film Festival.
