# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival** (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac. The events are recognized globally for their scale and production, frequently appearing in industry rankings and award nominations.

## Philanthropy
Insomniac Care, the charitable arm of the event's producer, allocates a percentage of ticket sales from each flagship festival to national charities and community organizations. Since 2011, these initiatives have raised over $2 million. To facilitate fundraising, EDC Las Vegas has partnered with Surreal, a fundraising platform that offers festival attendees opportunities to enter sweepstakes for experiences such as helicopter rides and interactions with performing artists.

Beneficiary organizations have included:
*   Boys and Girls Club of Southern Nevada
*   Families for Effective Autism Treatment
*   Injured Police Officers Fund
*   Nevada Childhood Cancer Foundation
*   Rock the Vote
*   The Las Vegas Academy for Arts

## Media
The 2013 edition of EDC Las Vegas served as the subject of the documentary film *Under the Electric Sky*. The film, which examines the culture and community of the festival's attendees, premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival.

## Recognition and Awards

### International Dance Music Awards
The festival has been a recurring nominee for "Best Music Event" or "Best Festival" at the International Dance Music Awards. Nominations were received annually from 2010 through 2016, and again in 2019 and 2020.

### DJ Magazine
*DJ Magazine* has consistently ranked Electric Daisy Carnival among the top festivals globally:
*   **2022:** 3rd place
*   **2023:** 4th place
*   **2024:** 2nd place
