**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Global Events: 2016–2019**

**2016 Events**
From June 17–19, EDC Las Vegas took place at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, featuring a performance by DJ Marshmello and DJ Tiësto. Other international iterations included EDC Mexico at Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez (February 27–28), EDC New York at Citi Field (May 14–15), EDC UK in Milton Keynes (July 9), EDC Orlando at Tinker Field (November 4–5), and EDC India at the Buddh International Circuit (November 12–13).

**2017 Events**
EDC Las Vegas returned to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in June, recording over 135,000 attendees on its opening night. This event saw a twofold increase in medical calls related to heat; one fatality occurred involving a 34-year-old man attributed to heat exposure and the consumption of MDMA and TFMPP. Additional 2017 events included EDC Mexico (February 25–26) and EDC Orlando (November 10–11). The scheduled EDC UK event in Milton Keynes was cancelled.

**2018 Events**
To mitigate high temperatures, EDC Las Vegas shifted its dates to May 18–20 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. The organizers introduced on-site camping, RV parking, and earlier opening times to manage traffic, with total attendance estimated at 411,400. Other events included EDC China's debut at the Shanghai International Music Park (April 29–30), EDC Japan at Zozo Marine Stadium and Makuhari Beach Park (May 12–13), where heavy rain caused equipment failure, and EDC Orlando (November 9–10).

**2019 Events**
EDC Las Vegas occurred May 17–19. On the second day, high winds led to stage closures. Miscommunication regarding safety instructions caused some attendees to leave the venue; they were subsequently barred from returning due to a strict no-reentry policy. Las Vegas police reported 65 arrests during the event. Other 2019 festivals included EDC Mexico (February 23–24), EDC Japan (May 11–12), and EDC Korea at Seoul Land (August 31–September 1). EDC Orlando expanded its duration from two days to three days at Tinker Field.
