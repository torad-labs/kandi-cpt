# Electric Daisy Carnival (2016–2019)

The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac. Between 2016 and 2019, the brand expanded its international presence and modified its flagship Las Vegas event to address logistical and safety concerns.

## Overview
During this period, the flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, remained centered at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Insomniac also maintained a global circuit of festivals, including recurring events in Mexico and Orlando, and expansions into the United Kingdom, India, Japan, China, and South Korea.

## Stages and Lineup
Performances across the festivals featured prominent EDM artists. A notable event occurred during EDC Las Vegas 2016, where DJ Marshmello participated in a stage gimmick involving the removal of his helmet to reveal DJ Tiësto.

## Event Logistics

### EDC Las Vegas
To mitigate the effects of extreme heat, Insomniac moved the scheduling of EDC Las Vegas from June to May beginning in 2018. In the same year, the festival introduced on-site camping, including RV parking and rentable campers, and implemented earlier opening times to alleviate traffic congestion. In 2018, total attendance for the three-day event was estimated at 411,400.

### International and Regional Editions
*   **EDC Mexico:** Held annually at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City (2016–2019).
*   **EDC Orlando:** Held at Tinker Field in Orlando, Florida. The event transitioned from a two-day to a three-day format in 2019.
*   **EDC New York:** Held at Citi Field in Queens, New York, in May 2016.
*   **EDC UK:** Held in Milton Keynes, England, in 2016; the 2017 event was cancelled.
*   **EDC India:** Held at the Buddh International Circuit in New Delhi in November 2016.
*   **EDC Japan:** Held in Chiba at Zozo Marine Stadium and Makuhari Beach Park (2018–2019). The 2018 event experienced equipment failure due to heavy rainfall.
*   **EDC China:** Debuted at the Shanghai International Music Park in April 2018.
*   **EDC Korea:** Held at Seoul Land in Gwacheon, South Korea, in August/September 2019.

## Safety and Incidents

### Health and Medical
During the 2017 Las Vegas event, there was a significant increase in heat-related medical emergencies, with medical calls on the opening night doubling compared to the previous year. One fatality was reported involving a 34-year-old male; the cause was attributed to the ingestion of MDMA and TFMPP, exacerbated by heat exposure.

### Weather and Security
At EDC Las Vegas 2019, high winds necessitated the closure of several stages. Due to the phrasing of audio and visual instructions advising attendees to "take cover in your car," some patrons mistakenly believed the entire festival had been cancelled and exited the venue. Because of the event's strict no-reentry policy, these individuals were denied admission upon realizing the closures were localized. 

Law enforcement reported 65 arrests during the 2019 Las Vegas festival, including 51 felony or gross misdemeanor charges and two driving under the influence (DUI) charges.
