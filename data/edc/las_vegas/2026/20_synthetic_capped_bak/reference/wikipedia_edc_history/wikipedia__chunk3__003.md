# Electric Daisy Carnival (2012–2015)

The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music festivals produced by Insomniac Events. Between 2012 and 2015, the event expanded its global footprint, establishing flagship festivals in Las Vegas and satellite events across North America, Europe, and South America.

## Overview
The flagship EDC Las Vegas event is held annually at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. From 2012 to 2014, the festival experienced significant growth in attendance. In 2012, attendance increased by 30% to 320,000 total attendees. By 2014, the event reached a capacity of approximately 134,000 attendees per day, with all 345,000 available tickets sold by June 18.

Beyond Las Vegas, Insomniac expanded the brand to several international and domestic locations:
* **North America:** Orlando (Tinker Field), New York (MetLife Stadium), Chicago, and Mexico City (Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez).
* **Caribbean:** San Juan, Puerto Rico (Estadio Sixto Escobar).
* **Europe:** London and Milton Keynes, UK (National Bowl).
* **South America:** São Paulo, Brazil (Autódromo José Carlos Pace).

## Stages and Production
In 2014, Insomniac Events set a record for the largest structural stage in North America. The 2015 Las Vegas production featured a blinking owl as the primary background theme for the main stage.

The festival utilizes mobile performance platforms, such as the wideAwake art car. During the 2012 event, this mobile stage was used for impromptu performances by DJs Markus Schulz and Steve Aoki following the closure of the primary stages.

## Logistics and Operations
The 2012 Las Vegas event faced significant operational disruptions on its second night. High winds reaching 30 MPH necessitated the early closure of the festival at 1:00 a.m., with 90,000 attendees directed to the speedway's bleachers for safety. To compensate for the cancellation, Insomniac permitted Saturday pass holders to return on Sunday.

The New York iteration of the festival is held at the MetLife Stadium within the Meadowlands Sports Complex in East Rutherford, New Jersey. Despite the venue's location, the event is branded as "Electric Daisy Carnival New York" due to its proximity to New York City. In 2012, this event drew 45,000 attendees per day.

## Safety and Incidents
The festival has been associated with several fatalities:
* **2012:** On June 11, a 31-year-old male from Florida was struck and killed by a truck while exiting the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Reports indicated the individual had been consuming alcohol and smoking.
* **2015:** A graduate of the University of California, Irvine, died on the second night of the Las Vegas festival. The death was ruled to be related to MDMA use, specifically citing dehydration and hyperthermia (increased body temperature).

## Media
The festival was the subject of the documentary film *Under the Electric Sky*, which premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival.
