# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival** (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac Events. Since its inception in 1991, the event has expanded from a regional California gathering into a global brand with editions held across North America, Europe, Asia, and South America.

## Overview
The festival debuted in 1991 in Chino, California, with an initial attendance of approximately 3,000 to 3,500. After several years of sporadic organization in the 1990s, the event transitioned into a large-scale production in the 2000s. The flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, has seen significant growth in attendance, peaking at an estimated 525,000 attendees in 2023 and 2024.

## Event History and Expansion
### Early Years (1991–2006)
In its early iterations, EDC was held at various California venues, including El Mirage Lake, the Shrine Auditorium and Expo Hall in Los Angeles, and Lake Dolores Waterpark. By 2000, the event moved to the International Agri-Center in Tulare, attracting 24,000 attendees. From 2003 to 2006, the festival established a multi-year residency at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino.

### Regional and Global Growth (2007–2013)
Beginning in 2007, Insomniac expanded the brand to other cities. Early regional editions included EDC Los Angeles (at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum), EDC Colorado, and EDC Dallas. International expansion began in 2009 with EDC Puerto Rico. In 2011, the festival launched EDC Las Vegas at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, which would become the primary flagship event. Other expansions during this period included EDC New York (MetLife Stadium), EDC Orlando (Tinker Field), EDC Chicago, and EDC London.

### International Diversification (2014–Present)
From 2014 onward, the festival expanded into non-English speaking markets. Major additions included EDC Mexico (Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez), EDC Brasil, and EDC India. The brand further penetrated the Asian market with events in China, Japan, and South Korea. In 2022, the festival expanded into Europe via EDC Portugal. In 2023, Insomniac introduced EDSea, a cruise-based festival held on the *Norwegian Joy*.
