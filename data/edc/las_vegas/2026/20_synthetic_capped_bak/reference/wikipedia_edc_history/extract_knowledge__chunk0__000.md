Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is an electronic dance music (EDM) festival organized by the promoter and distributor Insomniac. The flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, is held annually in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. As of 2024, it is the largest EDM festival in North America, recording a total attendance of 525,000 over three days. The next scheduled occurrence of EDC Las Vegas is set for May 15–17, 2026.

The festival features a range of electronic music genres, including house, techno, drum and bass, dubstep, and trance. The venue infrastructure consists of eight stages; the largest, Kinetic Field, has a capacity of 70,000 people. The site incorporates three-dimensional superstructures, LED-lit flora and fauna, interactive art installations, and a staff of over 5,000 personnel. Additionally, the event includes 18 carnival rides and four Ferris wheels.

Since its origin in 1991 in Chino, California, the festival has expanded its scale and offerings. EDC Las Vegas transitioned to a two-day format in 2009 and a three-day format in 2011. In 2018, the festival introduced on-site camping. In 2019, ticket purchasing was expanded to mobile applications. 

Beyond Las Vegas, the EDC brand operates in multiple international and domestic locations. Current annual events are held in Orlando (Florida), Mexico City (Mexico), Medellín (Colombia), Suzhou (China), Phuket (Thailand), and Seoul (South Korea). Former event locations include various U.S. cities, as well as sites in Puerto Rico, England, Brazil, India, Portugal, and Japan.
