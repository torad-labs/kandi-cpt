# Electric Daisy Carnival (2010–2011)

The **Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a series of electronic dance music festivals produced by Insomniac. Between 2010 and 2011, the event expanded into multiple regional iterations across the United States and Puerto Rico, while undergoing a significant relocation of its flagship event.

## 2010 Events

### EDC Los Angeles
The 2010 flagship event in Los Angeles drew an attendance of approximately 185,000 people. Ticket pricing began at $60, with some tiers exceeding $100. 

The event faced significant criticism from promoters and local authorities following security breaches that allowed attendees under the age of 16 to enter. A crowd stampede resulted in the hospitalization of more than 100 individuals. Additionally, a 15-year-old attendee, Sasha Rodriguez, died following the ingestion of MDMA.

In response to these incidents, the city of Los Angeles suspended all remaining scheduled events for 2010 and future dates. The suspension remained in effect pending the implementation of new safety and security provisions, which included the mandatory hiring of on-site medical doctors and an increase in the minimum entry age to 18.

### Regional Events
*   **EDC Colorado:** Held on June 12 at the Arapahoe County Fairground in Aurora.
*   **EDC Dallas:** Held on June 19 at Fair Park in Dallas, with an attendance of 11,000.
*   **EDC Puerto Rico:** Held on August 28 at the Estadio Sixto Escobar in San Juan.

## 2011 Events

### EDC Las Vegas
Following the controversies surrounding the Los Angeles events, Insomniac moved the flagship festival to Las Vegas, Nevada. The event took place from June 24–26 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, with a reported attendance of 230,000 over the three-day period.

### Regional Events
*   **EDC Orlando:** Held May 27–28 at Tinker Field and the grounds adjacent to the Florida Citrus Bowl. Attendance was approximately 12,000 on Friday and 20,000 on Saturday.
*   **EDC Colorado:** Held on June 11 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora.
*   **EDC Dallas:** Held on June 18 at Fair Park with an estimated 25,000 attendees. Extreme weather, with temperatures exceeding 110 degrees Fahrenheit, resulted in dozens of hospitalizations and at least one fatality. This event marked the final iteration of EDC held in Dallas.
*   **EDC Puerto Rico:** Held on August 27 at the Estadio Sixto Escobar in San Juan.

## Logistics and Safety
Safety protocols were significantly altered following the 2010 Los Angeles event. The city of Los Angeles mandated a strict 18+ age requirement and the presence of professional medical staff on-site to mitigate health risks associated with crowd density and substance use.
