# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival** (EDC) is an electronic dance music (EDM) festival organized by the promoter and distributor Insomniac. The flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, is held annually in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway and is the largest EDM festival in North America as of 2024.

## History
The origins of the event date back to 1986, when Stephen Hauptfuhr began organizing underground parties. Hauptfuhr hosted the first Electric Daisy Carnival in 1991 in Chino, California, featuring an outdoor carnival atmosphere that attracted between 3,000 and 3,500 attendees. Subsequent editions were organized in 1992 and 1995. Insomniac assumed organization of the event in 1997.

EDC Las Vegas expanded its duration to two days in 2009 and to three days in 2011. In 2017, the festival received the Festival of the Year award at the Electronic Music Awards.

## Event Experience
The festival features a diverse range of electronic music genres, including house, techno, drum and bass, and dubstep. Beyond musical performances, the event is characterized by three-dimensional superstructures, LED-lit flora and fauna, and glow-in-the-dark environments. Attendees have access to interactive art installations, free-roaming carnival performers, and various carnival rides.

## Stages and Lineup
The festival utilizes eight distinct stages, each dedicated to different genres of electronic music. The largest of these, kineticField, has a capacity of 70,000 attendees. The lineup consists of international electronic music producers and disc jockeys.

## Logistics and Operations
### Attendance and Capacity
In 2024, EDC Las Vegas recorded a total attendance of 525,000 people over its three-day duration. The event is supported by a staff of more than 5,000 employees.

### Infrastructure
The festival site includes 18 carnival rides and four Ferris wheels. In 2018, the festival expanded its offerings to include an on-site camping experience. Ticketing processes were modernized in 2019 with the introduction of mobile phone applications.

## Global Presence
While the flagship event takes place in Las Vegas, Nevada, Insomniac has expanded the EDC brand globally. Current annual events are held in Orlando (Florida), Mexico City (Mexico), Medellín (Colombia), Suzhou (China), Phuket (Thailand), and Seoul (South Korea). 

Previous editions have been hosted in various other locations, including Los Angeles, San Bernardino, Denver, New York City, Chicago, Austin, and Dallas within the United States, as well as in San Juan (Puerto Rico), Milton Keynes (England), São Paulo (Brazil), New Delhi (India), Portimão (Portugal), and Chiba (Japan).
