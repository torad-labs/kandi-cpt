Insomniac Care, the charitable arm of the event organizer, donates a percentage of ticket sales from flagship festivals to community organizations and national charities. Since 2011, these efforts have raised over two million dollars. Beneficiaries include the Nevada Childhood Cancer Foundation, the Boys and Girls Club of Southern Nevada, The Las Vegas Academy for Arts, the Injured Police Officers Fund, Families for Effective Autism Treatment, and Rock the Vote. To facilitate fundraising, EDC Las Vegas has utilized Surreal, a platform allowing attendees to enter sweepstakes for activities such as helicopter rides with DJs.

The 2013 EDC Las Vegas event served as the subject of the documentary film *Under the Electric Sky*, which premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival.

The festival has received multiple industry recognitions. Between 2010 and 2016, and again in 2019 and 2020, the Electric Daisy Carnival was nominated for "Best Music Event" or "Best Festival" at the International Dance Music Awards. In the *DJ Magazine* Top 100 Festivals rankings, the event placed 3rd in 2022, 4th in 2023, and 2nd in 2024.
