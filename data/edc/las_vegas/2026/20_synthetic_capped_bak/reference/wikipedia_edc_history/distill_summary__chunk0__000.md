EDC Las Vegas, organized by Insomniac, is North America's largest EDM festival, featuring house, techno, drum and bass, and dubstep. The next event is scheduled for May 15–17, 2026, at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway.

The festival utilizes eight stages: kineticFIELD (capacity 70,000), cosmicMEADOW, circuitGROUNDS, neonGARDEN, bassPOD, wasteLAND, quantumVALLEY, and stereoBLOOM (plus bionicJUNGLE). Attendees experience 3D superstructures, LED flora/fauna, interactive art, free-roaming performers, 18 carnival rides, and four Ferris wheels.

Key policies and logistics include on-site camping (introduced 2018) and mobile app ticket purchasing (introduced 2019). The 2024 event saw 525,000 attendees over three days. Staffing exceeds 5,000 personnel. Historically, the event evolved from underground parties (1986) and an initial 1991 Chino, California debut to a three-day format by 2011. EDC was named Festival of the Year at the 2017 Electronic Music Awards. Other active global editions occur in Orlando, China, and Mexico.
