Because of the venue's size and the physical demands of the environment, attendees are advised to treat the experience as a "marathon" rather than a sprint to avoid premature exhaustion.

The festival grounds, primarily located at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, consist of rough concrete and unpaved sections. Attendees are cautioned against wearing sandals, flip-flops, or new, unbroken-in shoes. Recommended attire includes comfortable, broken-in sneakers. To manage the physical toll of the event, a common itinerary involves arriving in Las Vegas on Thursday and departing on Tuesday.
