# Electric Daisy Carnival (Attendance Guidelines)

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a large-scale electronic dance music festival held annually in Las Vegas, Nevada. Due to the extreme desert environment and the scale of the event, experienced attendees and community forums frequently emphasize specific logistical and health-related precautions to ensure attendee safety and longevity over the multi-day event.

## Overview
Attendees are advised to manage their physical and mental stamina to avoid premature exhaustion. A common failure point cited by veterans is "over-exertion" on the first night, which can lead to the inability to attend subsequent days of the festival. To mitigate this, it is recommended that attendees limit their itinerary to a few high-priority performances per night rather than attempting to attend a high volume of sets.

## Health and Safety

### Nutrition and Hydration
Maintaining electrolyte balance is critical in the desert climate. Reliance on pure water alone can lead to hyponatremia or "bonking" (sudden fatigue). The consumption of electrolyte-enhanced beverages is recommended. Additionally, consistent caloric intake is emphasized; skipping breakfast or pre-festival meals can hinder the body's recovery process between event nights.

### Auditory Protection
Due to the high decibel levels of the sound systems, the use of earplugs is recommended to prevent temporary or permanent tinnitus.

### Substance Safety
The use of testing kits is encouraged to ensure substance safety. Organizations such as **DanceSafe** provide reagent kits and fentanyl test strips, which are legal in most U.S. states, to help attendees identify contaminants.

## Logistics

### Attire
Clothing choices are dictated by the extreme temperature fluctuations of the Nevada desert. 
* **Temperature Management:** Wearing all-black clothing in temperatures reaching 95°F is discouraged. Breathable, layered clothing is recommended.
* **Footwear:** The use of new sneakers, sandals, or high heels is discouraged due to the high volume of walking and the risk of blisters.

### Transportation and Timing
Traffic congestion is a significant factor for those utilizing shuttle services. Leaving for the venue at peak times (e.g., 5:30 AM) may result in extended wait times, sometimes exceeding two hours.

### Communication and Meeting Points
Due to unreliable cellular network coverage caused by high crowd density, attendees are advised to establish a concrete meeting plan that does not rely on mobile phones. The "Daisy" (the central festival icon) is generally discouraged as a meeting point due to its size and the volume of crowds, making it inefficient for locating specific individuals.

## Stages and Lineup
The festival features various thematic stages, including the **kineticFIELD**, **circuitGrounds**, and **cosmosCove**. Strategic planning regarding which sets to attend is suggested to avoid burnout and ensure a higher quality experience of the performances.
