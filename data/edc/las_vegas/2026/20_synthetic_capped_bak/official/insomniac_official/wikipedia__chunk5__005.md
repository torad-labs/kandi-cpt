## Safety
### Medical Escalation
Insomniac defines specific clinical criteria for when an impaired individual should be escorted to medical services rather than provided with basic shade and water. These escalation triggers include:
* **Physical Symptoms:** Vomiting, seizures, pale or bluish skin discoloration, abnormal heart rate or respiratory patterns (tachypnea or bradypnea), and abnormal body temperature (hyperthermia or hypothermia).
* **Neurological/Psychological Symptoms:** Delusions, hallucinations, paranoia, confusion, stupor (conscious but unresponsive), or coma/inability to be roused.
* **Ocular Symptoms:** Pupils that fail to react to changes in light.
