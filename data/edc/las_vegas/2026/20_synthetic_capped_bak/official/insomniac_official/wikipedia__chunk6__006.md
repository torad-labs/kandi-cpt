# Electric Daisy Carnival Las Vegas (Event Operations)

## Overview
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is a large-scale electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac. The event adheres to the "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect) ethos, encouraging attendees to utilize the buddy system, maintain hydration, and wear appropriate footwear.

## Schedule and Stages
The event takes place over three consecutive days, with operations concluding at 5:30 AM each following morning. 

*   **Friday, May 15:** The opening ceremony commences at the cosmicMeadow stage at 5:00 PM, with full grounds access granted at 7:00 PM.
*   **Saturday, May 16 and Sunday, May 17:** Full grounds open at 7:00 PM.

## Ticketing and Admission
Admission is restricted to holders of 3-day passes, with the exception of VIP guests purchasing single-day bottle service. Ticket purchases are limited to a maximum of four per person and are processed exclusively via credit or debit cards.

### Age Requirements
Attendees must be at least 18 years of age to enter the festival. A minimum age of 21 is required for access to alcohol and the VIP Enhanced Experience.

### Identification Policy
Valid identification is mandatory for entry and the purchase of alcohol. Accepted forms of identification include:
*   Current government-issued IDs.
*   Expired IDs accompanied by official DMV renewal documentation.
*   Foreign government passports.
*   Foreign government driver's licenses accompanied by a photocopy of a passport.
Consular IDs are not accepted.

## Logistics and Policies
### Site Access
The festival maintains a strict no re-entry policy. Attendees may enter the venue up until one hour before the event's conclusion, provided they possess a valid, unscanned ticket.

### Parking
Overnight sleeping in the parking lot is prohibited. Vehicles remaining on-site overnight are subject to towing at the owner's expense.

### Photography and Media
The use of professional cameras is prohibited. Photo passes are not required for handheld video devices under six inches or standard cameras featuring fixed, non-detachable lenses. Insomniac does not issue media or photo credentials to independent photographers.

## Information Services
Event details, including the festival map, lineup updates, and set times, are disseminated through the Insomniac mobile application, the official EDC Las Vegas website, social media channels, and email updates. Additionally, printed festival guides are distributed to attendees upon entry.
