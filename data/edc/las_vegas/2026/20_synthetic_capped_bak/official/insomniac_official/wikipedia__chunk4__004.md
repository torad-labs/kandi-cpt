# Electric Daisy Carnival (Event Details)

## Overview
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a large-scale electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac. The event features multiple thematic stages, diverse musical genres, and integrated camping facilities.

## Logistics

### Camp EDC
Camp EDC provides overnight accommodations for attendees. Access is granted via four-night camping passes covering Thursday through Sunday; single-night options are unavailable. 

**Facilities and Services**
The campground is staffed 24 hours a day by security, medical personnel, and Ground Control. Amenities include free showers, portable toilets, drinking water, and a General Store. ADA camping is available across all tiers. RV camping is permitted for those providing their own vehicles, with power hookups, water refills, and pump-out services available for an additional fee.

**Regulations**
* **Vehicles:** Cars are prohibited within the campground proper; gear transport assistance is provided from the parking area. Additional parking passes for separate arrivals are available for $95 (plus taxes and fees). Sleeping in cars or oversized vans is prohibited due to heat-related health risks.
* **Cooking:** Small portable propane stoves and grills are permitted, with a limit of 4 lbs of propane per group. Butane devices are prohibited.
* **Prohibited Items:** Steel knives and other utensils classified as weapons are banned from the campground.

## Safety

### Ground Control
Ground Control is the designated Insomniac safety team. Personnel are identifiable by purple shirts, fanny packs, and light sabers. They serve as a general resource for attendee assistance and hydration.

### Medical Services
Medical care is provided free of charge at designated tents throughout the festival grounds. Insomniac Health & Safety officers patrol the venue to provide immediate assistance for conditions ranging from minor ailments (nausea, blisters, lightheadedness) to critical emergencies. The organization maintains a policy that attendees will not face disciplinary action for seeking medical assistance.
