**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026: Operations and Policies**

**Schedule and Access**
The event takes place from Friday, May 15, through Sunday, May 17. On May 15, the opening ceremony begins at the Cosmic Meadow stage at 5:00 p.m. Full grounds open at 7:00 p.m. on all three days. Each night concludes at 5:30 a.m. the following morning. Entry is permitted until one hour before the event ends, provided the attendee possesses a valid, unscanned ticket. Re-entry is prohibited.

**Ticketing and Age Requirements**
Tickets are sold exclusively as 3-day passes, with a maximum limit of four tickets per person. Single-day access is available only for VIP attendees purchasing bottle service. Payments are accepted via credit and debit cards only. All attendees must be at least 18 years old. Access to alcohol and the VIP Enhanced Experience requires a minimum age of 21.

**Identification Standards**
Valid identification is required for entry and alcohol purchases. Expired IDs are only accepted if accompanied by DMV renewal documentation. Accepted forms of identification include foreign government passports and foreign government driver's licenses (the latter must be accompanied by a passport photocopy). Consular IDs are not accepted.

**Equipment and Parking**
Professional cameras are prohibited. Standard cameras with fixed non-detachable lenses and handheld video devices under six inches do not require photo passes. Insomniac does not issue credentials to independent photographers. Regarding parking, overnight sleeping in vehicles is forbidden; cars left overnight are subject to towing at the owner's expense.

**Information Resources**
The Insomniac app, the official EDC Las Vegas website, social media, and email updates provide the festival map, lineup updates, and set times. A printed festival guide is also distributed upon entry.
