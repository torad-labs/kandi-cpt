# Electric Daisy Carnival (Las Vegas)

Held annually at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, the event is characterized by its adherence to the "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect) ethos and its integration of large-scale art installations, carnival rides, and pyrotechnics.
