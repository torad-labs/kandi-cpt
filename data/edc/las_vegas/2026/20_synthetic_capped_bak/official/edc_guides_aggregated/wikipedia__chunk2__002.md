## Logistics and Safety

### Footwear and Attire
Due to the diverse terrain of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway—which includes asphalt, grass, gravel, and stairs—attendees are advised to wear broken-in, closed-toe sneakers. It is generally recommended to avoid heels, flip-flops, or unseasoned footwear. Attendees frequently walk between 10 and 20 miles per night; consequently, the use of gel insoles, extra socks, and blister pads is common among experienced attendees.

### Hydration and Health
To prevent dehydration and hyponatremia, attendees are encouraged to consume approximately 16 to 27 ounces of fluid per hour. The use of electrolytes (such as electrolyte tablets or Electrolit sticks) is recommended over pure water. Free water refill stations, identified by a water droplet symbol on the festival map, are available throughout the venue. Attendees typically utilize reusable containers, such as CamelBaks or Insomniac-branded bottles.

### Hearing Protection
Given the high decibel levels and the duration of music exposure (exceeding 30 hours over three nights), the use of high-fidelity earplugs is considered essential to prevent tinnitus. Recommended brands include Etymotic, Loop, EARasers, or custom-molded plugs.
