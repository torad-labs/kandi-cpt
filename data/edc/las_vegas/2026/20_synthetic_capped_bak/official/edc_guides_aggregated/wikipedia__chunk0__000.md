# Electric Daisy Carnival Las Vegas

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas** is the largest electronic dance music (EDM) festival in North America. Produced by Insomniac, the event originated in Los Angeles in 1997 before relocating to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in 2011. The festival is characterized by its dusk-to-dawn operating hours, large-scale art installations, and a cultural adherence to the ethos of "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect). Attendees are officially referred to by the organization as "Headliners."

## Overview
The festival takes place over three nights in mid-May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, located approximately 15 miles north of the Las Vegas Strip. Daily attendance is estimated at 140,000, with total weekend attendance exceeding 525,000. The 2026 edition marks the 30th anniversary of the event, scheduled for May 15–17.

The event typically begins with an opening ceremony at the cosmicMEADOW stage on Friday at 5:00 PM, with general gates opening at 7:00 PM. Performances continue until 5:30 AM the following morning. Due to its desert location, the venue experiences high diurnal temperature variation, with daytime highs often exceeding 90°F and nighttime lows around 60°F, accompanied by low humidity and unpredictable wind.

## Stages
The festival features multiple themed stages, each designed with distinct architectural and visual identities.

### kineticFIELD
The kineticFIELD serves as the festival's primary main stage and is recognized as the largest stage in North America, measuring 480 feet in width and 100 feet in height. The stage is redesigned annually; past themes include the "Mother Gaia" concept (2017), "Crystal Village" (2015), the "Celestial" figure of kineticAWAKENING (2023), and the "KineticCIRCLE" (2024), which featured a cathedral design with stained-glass depictions of previous years' themes.

The stage hosts the festival's premier headliners and is the site of the "Sunrise Sets," traditionally performed by artists such as Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren.
