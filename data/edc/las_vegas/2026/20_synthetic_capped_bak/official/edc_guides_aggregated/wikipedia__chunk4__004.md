**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac. While the brand operates multiple global editions, including EDC Orlando, EDC Mexico City, EDC Thailand, and the cruise-based EDSea, the Las Vegas event serves as the flagship production.

### Overview
The Las Vegas edition is held annually in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. The festival operates with a consistent schedule, opening at 5:00 PM on Friday and 7:00 PM on Saturday and Sunday, with all performances concluding at 5:30 AM each night. The event is restricted to attendees aged 18 and older, with VIP and alcohol-serving areas restricted to those 21 and older.

The festival is a cashless event and maintains a strict no-re-entry policy. Attendees are supported by "Ground Control" staff, identifiable by purple shirts, and have access to complimentary water stations marked with a droplet symbol. The event is characterized by the "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect) culture, including the tradition of trading "kandi" (beaded bracelets). Recurring visual elements include the owl mascot, the "Daisy" moving installation, and nightly fireworks and drone shows typically occurring around 1:00 AM.

### Stages and Programming
The festival features nine primary stages:
*   kineticField
*   cosmicMeadow
*   circuitGrounds
*   neonGarden
*   basspod
*   wasteland
*   quantumValley
*   stereoBloom
*   bionicJungle (introduced in 2022)

Additional programming is hosted at the Forest House, various Art Cars, and Downtown EDC venues. While the roster of stages remains largely consistent, their physical layout within the Speedway is subject to annual reorganization. For example, in 2024, quantumValley and neonGarden were relocated closer to kineticField, and basspod was moved to the right of Electric Avenue.

Each edition features a unique lineup and theme song; Tiësto's "My City" served as the official theme for 2024. A recurring tradition of the event is the "Sunrise Set," typically performed by Above & Beyond or Armin van Buuren.

### Logistics and Accommodations
Insomniac provides two primary on-site and partner lodging options:

**Camp EDC**
An on-site campground accessible exclusively via four-night camping passes (Thursday through Sunday). The facility includes:
*   **Amenities:** A 24-hour General Store, complimentary showers, portable toilets, and drinking water.
*   **Lodging:** RV camping with utility hookups and rental Shiftpod tents.
*   **Programming:** Dedicated pool parties and the Mesa gathering area.
*   **Access:** Campers enter and exit the festival grounds via the dedicated Camp shuttle entrance.

**Hotel EDC**
A corporate partnership with Virgin Hotels (formerly Resorts World) involving a property takeover. The partnership integrates the hotel experience with the festival through themed decor, pop-up DJ sets, and pool parties.
