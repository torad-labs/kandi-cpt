## Festival Overview

EDC Las Vegas 2026 takes place Friday May 15 through Sunday May 17, 2026
at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. It's the largest electronic dance music
festival in North America, produced by Insomniac Events.

Full grounds open 7:00pm to 5:30am on all three nights. The opening
ceremony is held at Cosmic Meadow on Friday May 15, beginning at 5:00pm.

The festival features nine main stages plus art cars and smaller stages,
hundreds of artists across the weekend, free water refill stations
presented by Electrolit, the Ground Control safety team in purple shirts,
and an on-site Camp EDC campground accessible via 4-night camping passes.

The minimum age to attend is 18. The VIP Enhanced Experience and Bottle
Service areas are 21-plus. EDC is a cashless event — credit, debit,
Apple Pay, Samsung Pay, and Android Pay are accepted. Cash can be
exchanged onsite for a prepaid card.

Ins-and-outs are not allowed. Once you enter the festival each night,
you cannot leave and re-enter. The nearest airport is Harry Reid
International (LAS).

The festival is held in the Nevada desert. Heat exhaustion and
dehydration are real risks. Dress for comfort, wear comfortable
sneakers (not sandals), and use the free water refill stations marked
by water-droplet symbols on the festival map.

## The Nine Main Stages

### Kinetic Field

The legendary Kinetic Field is EDC's iconic main stage. It has known
many forms: a cathedral for sonic worship, a crystal village inhabited
by owl guardians, a temple of sacred sounds, the open arms of mother
Gaia, the epicenter of universal love, and the embodiment of movement
and energy. It can hold around 70,000 attendees and hosts the
biggest-name headliners of the festival.

### Cosmic Meadow

Cosmic Meadow is where Headliners converge to kick off the festival
with EDC's epic opening ceremony Friday at 5pm. It's an open-grounds
stage featuring big-room and mainstage acts alongside experimental
programming.

### Circuit Grounds

Another renowned stage that has evolved over time. Circuit Grounds
features LED walls and plumes of fire that ignite the atmosphere,
producing positively charged energy felt far beyond ordinary perception.

### Neon Garden

The dark and deep techno stage. Factory 93 unlocks the gates of Neon
Garden, where subliminal minimalism and immersive live art
cross-pollinate with cutting-edge technology and exceptionally curated
deep house and techno.

### Basspod

Headbangers make their annual pilgrimage to Basspod, the proving ground
of the Bassrush massive. Here, lovers of dubstep, bass house, drum and
bass, and trap are united. Grab the rail with both hands, and prepare
to go hard.

### Wasteland

Throughout its history at EDC, Wasteland has been the refuge of ravers
looking to experience the blistering tempos and relentless energy of
hard dance, hardcore, and all forms of hardstyle. Welcome to the
Basscon universe.

### Quantum Valley

Follow Rainbow Road to a higher plane of consciousness. The
international Trance Fam gathers at Quantum Valley, where Dreamstate
presents the entire spectrum of the trance genre, from flowing,
ethereal melodies to complex psychedelic strains.

### Stereo Bloom

Take in the various shades of the dance music genre, from house to
tech house to bass music and beyond. Curated by the ever-growing
family at Insomniac Music Group, Stereo Bloom offers up sounds from
known artists and up-and-comers alike.

### Bionic Jungle

The newest edition to EDC. Bionic Jungle takes you on a journey deep
into the wild with a carefully selected roster of house artists.
Small but mighty stage that lets you unleash your inner animal.

### Art Cars

From glowing buggies to massive party ships, mobile sound systems roam
the Speedway broadcasting all forms of dance music. Go exploring, and
you might catch a special DJ set. Notable art cars at EDC include the
BeatBox Art Car, Picnic Playtime Art Car, and Metaphoenix.

## Camp EDC

Camp EDC offers 4-night camping passes that cover Thursday, Friday,
Saturday, and Sunday nights. Single-night camping is not available.

Camp EDC has free showers, porta-potties, drinking water, and a
General Store that's open the entire time the campground is. The
campground is fully staffed by Ground Control, medical, and security
personnel, 24 hours a day.

Cooking at camp: small portable propane stoves and grills are allowed.
Tanks up to 4 lbs per group are permitted. Butane devices are not
allowed. Steel knives and other utensils deemed weapons are not
allowed into the campground.

Sleeping in cars or oversize vans is not permitted at Camp EDC due to
serious heat health risk.

Cars are not permitted to drive into the campground proper. Assistance
is provided for moving gear from the parking area to your campsite.

ADA camping is available in all tiers of camping.

RV camping is available with power hookup, RV water refills, and
pump-outs for an extra charge. Camp EDC does not offer preset or
rental RVs — you bring your own.

Additional parking passes for group members arriving separately are
available for purchase at $95 plus taxes and fees.

## Ground Control and Medical

Ground Control is the Insomniac safety team dedicated to keeping
Headliners happy, healthy, and hydrated. You can spot Ground Control
team members by their signature purple shirts, fanny packs, and light
sabers as they make their way through the festival. Approach any of
them if you need assistance or just want a friend.

Medical staff are stationed in tents throughout the festival grounds
and provide care free of charge, no questions asked. Insomniac Health
& Safety officers roam the venue with watchful, caring eyes. Flag any
of them down if you need help. You can also visit a first aid tent for
anything from lightheadedness, nausea, or blisters to more serious
medical needs.

The festival explicitly states: "You will not get in trouble for
seeking medical help."

### When to escalate to medical

Insomniac's official escalation criteria for taking a friend to medical
(rather than just shade and water): vomiting, seizures, pale skin
color or bluish tinge, abnormal heart rate or breathing (too fast or
slow), abnormal body temperature (too hot or cold), delusions or
hallucinations or paranoia, pupils that don't react to changes in
light, confusion or stupor (unresponsive but conscious), coma, or
inability to be roused.

### Caring for impaired friends

Drunk people should not be responsible for other drunk people. Binge
drinking is the most dangerous kind there is — the faster you drink,
the higher your blood alcohol concentration becomes. Strong coffee,
a cold shower, sleeping, and walking it off do not help someone
suffering from alcohol poisoning. Always try to keep intoxicated
people awake and in an upright sitting position, not lying down.

### Use the Buddy System

It's easy to get separated at festivals. Know where your friends are,
set up meeting points, and stick together. Two or three brains are
better than one. If you think a new or old friend needs help, ask
Insomniac staff to assist. Always leave the show with everyone in
your crew.

## Water and Hydration

Free water refill stations are provided throughout the venue,
presented by Electrolit. Free Electrolit hydration sticks are
distributed alongside the water at the stations. Look for the water
droplet symbol on the festival map to find the nearest station.

You cannot bring sealed water into the festival. You can bring an
empty CamelBak, an empty reusable water bottle (including the
Insomniac-branded ones sold at merch booths), or any empty beverage
container. Fill up inside the festival for free.

The water is filtered through state-of-the-art systems including UV
sterilization. It may not be ice cold — warm water is easier to digest.

## Amenities and Services

### Food and Beverage

Food and beverage stands are located throughout the event. Roaming
vendors carry water, snacks, and other beverages. Vegan and vegetarian
options are available. Credit, debit, Apple Pay, and Android Pay are
accepted. EDC is a cashless event.

### Alcohol

Bars located throughout the festival serve beer and liquor. You must
be 21 or older to consume alcohol (ID required). Service hours may
be limited.

### Merchandise and General Store

The Insomniac Shop has multiple merch booths on-site. The brand-new
EDC apparel collection plus artist collections for the event include
T-shirts, jackets, backpacks, sweatshirts, water bottles, and
accessories. Booth locations are shown on the festival map.

The Insomniac General Shop is stocked with items you may have
forgotten at home — flip-flops, eye drops, ring pops, and similar
festival essentials.

### Lockers and Charging

Lockers allow unlimited in-and-out access throughout both days, and
each one features a universal cell phone charger. Battery charging
stations are also placed around the festival — check the map for
locations.

### Info Booths and Lost & Found

Info booths are stationed throughout the venue — check your festival
map for specific locations. Lost & Found services are available at
the venue during and between show days.

## Hours and Policies

Friday May 15: opening ceremony at Cosmic Meadow begins 5:00pm; full
grounds open 7:00pm. Saturday May 16 and Sunday May 17: full grounds
open 7:00pm. All three nights run until 5:30am the following morning.

Re-entry: ins-and-outs are not allowed. You may enter until one hour
before the end of the event with a valid unscanned ticket.

Tickets: 3-day passes only (Bottle Service available single-day for
VIP). Maximum 4 tickets per person. Cards only — credit and debit.
You must be 18 or older to enter; 21 or older for VIP Enhanced
Experience and alcohol.

Camera policy: photo passes not required for standard cameras with
fixed non-detachable lenses or handheld video devices under 6 inches.
Professional cameras are not permitted. Insomniac does not provide
media or photo credentials to independent photographers.

Cars in the parking lot: no overnight sleeping permitted. Cars left
overnight will be towed at the owner's expense.

ID: must present identification to enter and/or buy alcohol. Expired
IDs accepted only with DMV renewal documentation. Foreign government
driver's licenses accepted with photocopy of passport. Foreign
government passport accepted on its own. Consular IDs not accepted.
