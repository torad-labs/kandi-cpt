## What EDC Las Vegas IS

Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is the largest electronic dance music festival
in North America. It started in 1997 in Los Angeles and moved to the Las
Vegas Motor Speedway in 2011. Insomniac promotes it. EDC Las Vegas 2026 is
the 30th anniversary edition — May 15-17, 2026, three nights at the
Speedway, which is 15 miles north of the Las Vegas Strip. Daily attendance
runs ~140,000, total weekend headcount ~525,000+.

The festival operates from dusk to dawn — gates open 7pm Friday/Saturday/
Sunday (with opening ceremony at Cosmic Meadow Friday at 5pm); music runs
until 5:30am the following morning. Las Vegas in mid-May is hot (90°F+ days,
60°F nights), dry (15-20% humidity), and the desert wind picks up
unpredictably.

PLUR — Peace, Love, Unity, Respect — is the unofficial code of conduct,
and Insomniac calls attendees "Headliners." Trading kandi (beaded
bracelets) is the cultural friendship signal.

## The Stages — Founder Pasquale Rotella's own breakdown

**Kinetic Field** is the iconic main stage. It carries a different theme
every year (Crystal Village 2015, kineticAWAKENING 2023 with figure
"Celestial", a cathedral with stained-glass windows showing prior-year
themes for the 2024 KineticCIRCLE, mother-Gaia-themed 2017). It spans 480
feet wide, stands 100 feet tall, and is the largest stage in North
America. It hosts the biggest-name headliners (Martin Garrix, Tiësto,
Zedd, Hardwell, Kaskade, John Summit, The Chainsmokers, Charlotte de
Witte, Steve Aoki, etc.). Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren do Sunrise
Sets here. Past artists include Fisher, Subtronics, Cloonee, Porter
Robinson DJ Set, Sofi Tukker, GRiZ B2B Wooli, DJ Snake, Excision, David
Guetta, Boris Brejcha, Mau P, Alesso, Illenium, Kx5.

going to feel like it hugs the crowd a bit. We have all genres of dance
music on this stage, but a lot of our live performances are usually here,

## What veterans always tell first-timers

**Comfortable shoes are non-negotiable.** Closed-toe sneakers. Break them
in BEFORE the festival. Don't wear heels, flip-flops, or new shoes you've
never walked in. Bring blister pads, gel insoles, extra socks. Expect to
walk 10-20 miles per night. The Speedway has asphalt, grass, gravel,
stairs.

**Hydrate with electrolytes.** Aim for 16 oz per hour, cap at 27 oz per
hour to avoid hyponatremia. Pure water without electrolytes doesn't fix
dehydration — bring electrolyte tablets or use the free Electrolit sticks
at the water refill stations. Free water stations are marked with a water
droplet symbol on the festival map. Bring an empty CamelBak or Insomniac
water bottle.

**Earplugs are mandatory.** High-fidelity ones (Etymotic, Loop, EARasers,
custom-molded). EDC is loud for 30+ hours of music exposure over three
nights. Tinnitus from a single weekend is real.

**Cell service is unreliable to non-existent.** Plan accordingly. Set a
fixed meeting spot at a specific time — the lockers area, info booth,
merchant store. Don't use the Daisy (it moves throughout the night).
Screenshot the schedule and map ahead of time.

**Plan transport early.** Shuttle pass is the cleanest option (pickup at
designated Strip hotels). Premier shuttle reserves specific time slots.
Lyft and Uber surge hard. Helicopter rides exist for $$$. Carpooling
works but plan the meeting point. Maverick Helicopters runs ~15-minute
transfers from the Strip to the Speedway.

**Arrive early on night 1.** Get to the speedway before 9pm, ideally for
opening ceremony at 5pm Friday. Avoid traffic. Get photos in daylight.
Scope out the grounds before they're dark + chaotic.

**Leave before 4:30am or get stuck.** Post-festival shuttle and rideshare
lines are brutal after 4:30am. If you commit to staying for sunrise
sets, expect a 1-2 hour wait to get home.

**No re-entry.** Once you leave for the night, you can't come back unless
you have a Camp EDC wristband. Plan accordingly.

**Use the buddy system.** Two or three brains are better than one.
Always leave with the crew you came with.

**Take breaks.** EDC is a marathon, not a sprint. Sit down, ride the
Ferris wheel, eat real food during the day before, sleep when you can.

## Festival safety (paraphrased from multiple sources + Insomniac official)

If a friend looks sick or in distress, take them to the nearest medical
tent or flag down a Ground Control team member (purple shirts, fanny
packs, light sabers). Medical care is free, no questions asked. "You
will not get in trouble for seeking medical help" — explicit Insomniac
policy.

Signs your friend needs medical: vomiting, seizures, pale skin or
bluish lips/fingertips, abnormal heart rate or breathing, abnormal body
temperature, severe headache, delusions/hallucinations/paranoia, pupils
not reacting to light, confusion or stupor, unresponsiveness.

Keep impaired friends UPRIGHT/SITTING, never lying flat. Coffee, cold
showers, and "walking it off" don't help with alcohol poisoning — only
medical care does.

Drug policy is officially zero-tolerance, but the harm reduction reality
is: if you've taken something, drink electrolyte water, take breaks,
seek medical IMMEDIATELY if any of the escalation signs appear, don't
let drunk people take care of drunk people, don't accept anything from
strangers.

## EDC culture and lore

The festival mascot is an owl (visible on Kinetic Field's design across
many years). The Daisy is the symbolic flower of EDC and appears as a
moving art installation. Drone shows happen each night around 1am,
followed by fireworks visible from the bleachers (which are widely
regarded as the best vantage point). PLUR (Peace Love Unity Respect) is
the ethos. Kandi trading happens through the "PLUR handshake" — peace
sign → heart-shape → handshake → exchange bracelet.

Downtown EDC is the mini-city of side-quests inside the festival —
art installations, the YeeDC! line-dancing saloon, Queen Bar drag, the
Chapel where ravers can renew vows or get pretend-married, the Memory
Lane installation showing photos and videos from past EDCs, the carnival
rides, and the Ferris wheel.

Camp EDC is the on-site campground accessible only via 4-night camping
passes (Thu/Fri/Sat/Sun). It has its own pool parties, the Mesa
gathering area, Shiftpod tents for rent, RV camping with hookups, free
showers, porta-potties, drinking water, and a 24-hour General Store.
Campers can come and go from the festival via the Camp shuttle entrance.

Hotel EDC is the partnership with Virgin Hotels (formerly Resorts World)
that transforms the property into a takeover with pop-up DJ sets, pool
parties, and themed decor.

Insomniac runs other EDC editions: EDC Orlando (Florida), EDC Mexico
City, EDC Thailand, EDSea (cruise ship). Las Vegas is the flagship.

## What changes year to year

- Stage themes and designs
- Specific lineup
- Stage layout/positioning within the Speedway (2024 moved Quantum
  Valley + Neon Garden closer to Kinetic Field; 2024 moved Basspod to
  the right of Electric Avenue)
- New stages occasionally added (Bionic Jungle added 2022; Ubuntu and
  Stereo Bloom added at different points)
- Theme songs (Tiësto's "My City" was the official 2024 theme song)

## What stays the same

- May at Las Vegas Motor Speedway
- 7pm gates Sat/Sun, 5pm opening Friday
- 5:30am close each night
- 9 main stages: Kinetic Field, Cosmic Meadow, Circuit Grounds, Neon
  Garden, Basspod, Wasteland, Quantum Valley, Stereo Bloom, Bionic
  Jungle (plus Forest House, Art Cars, Downtown EDC venues)
- Ground Control purple shirts
- Free water stations marked with droplet symbol
- No re-entry, cashless event, 18+ minimum, 21+ for VIP/alcohol
- Owl mascot, Daisy moving installation
- Drone show + fireworks ~1am nightly
- Above & Beyond / Armin van Buuren Sunrise Set tradition
- PLUR / kandi trading culture
