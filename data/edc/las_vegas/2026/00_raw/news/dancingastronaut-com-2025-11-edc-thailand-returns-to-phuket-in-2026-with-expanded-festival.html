EDC Thailand Returns to Phuket in 2026 With Expanded Festival Experience including Hotel EDC debut

Liana Stern November 26, 2025
1 min read
Share

Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is returning to Thailand, bringing its world-famous production and dynamic energy back to Phuket for a second edition. From January 16–18, 2026, Rhythm Park will transform into a sprawling, multi-sensory playground. This year’s headliners include Zedd, Tiësto, Armin van Buuren, Fisher, GRiZ, and a special Axwell back-to-back Sebastian Ingrosso performance, among many others.

Following a landmark debut in 2025, the 2026 installment arrives with an expanded footprint, new activations, and the highly anticipated launch of Hotel EDC Thailand – the first official EDC hotel package outside of Las Vegas. Hosted at Angsana Laguna Phuket, the resort takeover will feature themed rooms, exclusive merch, pool parties, and wellness sessions. The celebration continues with EDC Week Thailand, bringing dance-focused events across the island ahead of the main festival weekend.

With its signature blend of world-class talent, immersive stage designs, art installations, and a global community of attendees, EDC Thailand is set to produce another unforgettable music experience this January. Tickets are available here.
