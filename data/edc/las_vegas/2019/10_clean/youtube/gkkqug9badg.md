For one weekend every summer, something unique and beautiful awakens in the heart of the Nevada desert: a tribe of beautiful, like-minded souls drawn to a musical utopia filled with carnival rides, performance artists, and unusual art cars powered by light and fire. In this place, we celebrate life, love, and individuality with a global community. This is Electric Daisy Carnival.

Join us Under the Electric Sky at Las Vegas Motor Speedway, as we explore eight unique arenas of sound curated by more than 200 of the world’s best DJs. Looking to enhance your experience? Located right next to the festival grounds, Camp EDC is a four-day community experience, complete with after-parties, transformative activities, a full-size Oasis Pool, and much more.
