Swedish House Mafia Adds Singapore F1 Grand Prix to Their 2019 Calendar

So when is the official world tour going to be announced?

Katie Stone • March 6, 2019 (Updated: August 6, 2025)
FacebookTweetPin

Iconic EDM trio Swedish House Mafia are keeping themselves busy this year by adding yet another stop on their international calendar. The latest addition is a performance at the 2019 Singapore Grand Prix. They are set to kick off before the Friday practice sessions on September 20th at Mariana Bay Street Circuit. The Swedish supergroup last performed at 2013 edition of the racing event during their One Last Tour. 

On the lineup for the weekend with SHM will be Muse, Red Hot Chili Peppers, Toots and The Maytals, Cardi B, Fatboy Slim, Hans Zimmer, and many more. 

At the time of writing, Swedish House Mafia has confirmed 2019 performances in Stockholm, Mexico City, Helsinki, Seoul, Daresbury, Ultra Europe, and many other cities. This addition at the Grand Prix to this years calendar has fans speculating if an official world tour will be announced. As of now, no confirmations have been issued.  

The Singapore Grand Prix is the 15th race in the F1 Calendar. Racing and electronic music have gone hand in hand over the years. Previously Armin van Buuren & Hardwell performed at the 2018 Mexican Grand Prix, Axwell / Ingrosso took the stage at the 2016 Bahrain Grand Prix and 2018 Azerbaijan Grand Prix after party. 

Get tickets for the 2019 Singapore Grand Prix here taking place September 20th-22nd.
