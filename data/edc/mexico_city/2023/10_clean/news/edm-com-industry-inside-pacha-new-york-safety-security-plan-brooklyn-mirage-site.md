# Inside Pacha's Ambitious Plan to Fix the Safety Failures That Plagued the Brooklyn Mirage - EDM

When Pacha New York opens in Bushwick this summer, the club will arrive with a promise its predecessor couldn’t keep: that the neighborhood around it will be as safe as the dancefloor within.

Kabir Mulchandani, Chairman of The Pacha Group, recently appeared before Brooklyn’s Community Board 1 to present a detailed operational framework addressing safety inside the new venue, on surrounding streets and across the transportation corridors that tens of thousands of concertgoers will traverse.

The property’s checkered past provided an unavoidable backdrop to Mulchandani’s presentation. Avant Gardner, the Mirage’s beleaguered parent company, had faced an unrelenting drumbeat of calls for increased security measures since launching the East Williamsburg EDM venue in 2017. Dubai-based hospitality and real estate powerhouse FIVE Holdings will operate the complex in the wake of Avant Gardner’s explosive bankruptcy case.

In the summer of 2023 alone, two men, Karl Clemente and John Castic, both 27, disappeared after leaving the Mirage on separate nights and were later found dead in Newtown Creek. A third attendee, ophthalmologist Michael Bautista, was allegedly kidnapped after getting into a car outside the venue, prompting a public outcry over the absence of exterior lighting, the presence of unlicensed drivers posing as rideshare vehicles, and what concertgoers described in a formal letter to their city councilmember as “extreme negligence.”

Mulchandani’s appearance before the community board appeared designed to address those concerns directly, and to signal that the new venture intends to operate differently. According to his presentation, a copy of which was obtained by *EDM.com*, the framework covers security staffing, surveillance, NYPD coordination, transportation, street lighting and emergency cellular infrastructure.

At the core of Pacha’s security plan is a staffing deployment of one security guard for every 75 guests, a ratio that appears in the NYPD’s official “Best Practices for Nightlife Establishments” bulletin. Formal weekly coordination meetings with the department’s 90th Precinct will also be held throughout the operating season to review event schedules and adjust operational planning as conditions on the ground evolve.

The club’s safety perimeter will extend beyond its walls. According to Mulchandani’s briefing, the property will be monitored by roughly 200 video surveillance cameras positioned both inside the venue and throughout its exterior grounds.

Security personnel will maintain a visible presence within 300 feet of the entrance to assist guests as they leave, and patrol vehicles will be deployed after concerts to support the dispersal of crowds through the surrounding streets. Pacha is commissioning an independent traffic management firm to provide on-site guards at every show, managing vehicle flow and conducting a post-event sweep of the area after the conclusion of each night’s programming.

Transportation has historically been a major flashpoint at the complex on Stewart Avenue, where guests had to navigate poorly lit industrial blocks on foot to reach the nearest subway stations. To address that pain point, Pacha has committed to operating shuttle buses to key transit hubs throughout all hours of operation.

Those buses will serve Morgan Avenue Station, approximately a 15-minute walk away; Jefferson Street Station, roughly 13 minutes on foot; and Union Square in Manhattan. In addition, Mulchandani said temporary lighting will be deployed along designated pedestrian routes to transit stops and rideshare pickup areas during and shortly after events, brightening the passageways that ravers use to get home. Pacha will also deploy staff to clean the surrounding streets and clear them of litter.

Perhaps the most technically ambitious element of the safety plan is the installation of a dedicated Verizon 4G and 5G macro cell site at the venue. The infrastructure is intended to serve several simultaneous functions: strengthening call reliability and data access for guests and staff so emergency calls to first responders can get through reliably, enabling smooth Uber and Lyft bookings for post-event transportation, and providing what the deck describes as permanent wireless capacity that will bolster connectivity across the surrounding neighborhood.

Mulchandani characterized the plan as a “global benchmark for fan safety” in a statement shared with *EDM.com*.

“We’ve heard the community clearly, and we’re rebuilding this venue from the ground up to operate the right way: by the book with full DOB compliance and with transparency and accountability at every level,” he said. “Overall capacity has been reduced, with real-time AI tracking and open access offered to the community board and NYPD to verify at any time. We’re eliminating overselling and completely reengineering the experience: from advanced entry and screening that can move 3,000 guests per hour, to coordinated exits with shuttles, traffic management, enhanced lighting and a dedicated 5G cell tower to ensure safe dispersal and connectivity.

“Independent third-party acoustic analysis confirms that engineered sound containment ensures sound leaving the venue remains well within city limits, reinforced by continuous real-time monitoring. We’re also implementing full sanitation and street management after every event, in addition to expanded security, patrol and on-site medical capabilities. This is not just a reset. It’s a global benchmark for fan safety, while being respectful of the local community.”

It’s important to note that community boards in New York City hold no formal veto power over liquor licenses or venue permits, but their recommendations carry weight with the State Liquor Authority and with local elected officials. Whether the safety framework presented by Mulchandani satisfies the board’s concerns will become clearer in the weeks ahead as the venue’s opening approaches.

The grand opening of Pacha New York is scheduled for June 20th and 21st with headline performances by Michael Bibi and Black Coffee, respectively. Programming is confirmed to run through October 2026.

As disgruntled ticket-holders continued to voice concerns over unrefunded Mirage passes, The Pacha Group announced a plan to offer credit in the form of coupons, allowing ticket-holders to apply the full dollar amount toward drinks, food or merchandise. The company has so far supported over 30,000 patrons and returned more than $3.1 million in value, a representative confirmed to *EDM.com*.

Tickets to Pacha New York are available via the club’s official website.
