Electric Daisy Carnival is undeniably one of the biggest festival series in the world. From its humble beginnings in 1997, the flagship festival for Insomniac has expanded, recently wrapping up its fifth year in New York City, and also gone abroad. Ever since leaving Los Angeles for Las Vegas, however, that show has become the superlative EDC experience.

2016 will be no different and the festival took a novel approach to unveiling its lineup. Using the Night Owl Radio show, the festival used a series of mixes — playing six-second clips of artists’ tracks — to reveal the lineup at each of this year’s eight stages. The lineup for each stage, as culled from the broadcast, are available below.
