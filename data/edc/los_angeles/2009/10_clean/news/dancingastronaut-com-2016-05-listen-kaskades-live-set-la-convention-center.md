On May 7, Kaskade made his triumphant return to Los Angeles to set another milestone in dance music history. The DJ/producer became the first electronic artist to headline a solo performance at the Los Angeles Convention Center, following up to his last city performance in 2012 at the Staples Center.

For his sold-out show, Kaskade stunned an estimated 20,000 attendees with a monumental set that lasted almost two-and-a-half hours. Take a listen as Kaskade mixes and makes magic for the city of Los Angeles, beginning with essentials like “4AM,” and powering through until “We Don’t Stop” closes the night.
