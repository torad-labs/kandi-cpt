Title: Grand Oak

Grand Oak (Japanese: ダイオーキド Dai Okido) is a Pokémon researcher and the developer of Pokémon HOME. His goal is to make a Pokédex that includes every Pokémon in the world.
His name, appearance, and theme music all resemble those of Professor Oak, but the exact relationship between the two characters is unknown.


== In the games ==
Grand Oak greets the player when they first start either version of Pokémon HOME, and asks for the player's assistance in completing a comprehensive National Pokédex. In the mobile version, he also gives the player a Hidden Ability Kanto first partner Pokémon to bring into a compatible game.
In the mobile version, since the addition of Shiny Pokémon given as rewards for completing the regional Pokédex for a certain game, Grand Oak appears again to congratulate the player upon completion of a regional Pokédex.
