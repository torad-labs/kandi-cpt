This is a list of Pokémon in the order dictated by the Isle of Armor regional Pokédex.
The Isle of Armor Pokédex is one of three regional Pokédexes featured in Pokémon Sword and Shield, along with the Galar Pokédex and the Crown Tundra Pokédex. It was added to the game in The Isle of Armor expansion and contains 211 Pokémon, though only 210 are required to be obtained to complete the Pokédex (Zarude is not required). The Isle of Armor Pokédex also appears as one of the regional Pokédexes in the mobile version of Pokémon HOME, where it can only be filled with Pokémon that originate from Sword and Shield; this version of the Pokédex has only 210 Pokémon, as Zarude is excluded.
The 211 Pokémon in the Pokédex follow this breakdown:
- 109 are exclusive to the Isle of Armor (the 3 new and 102 returning Pokémon added in Version 1.2.0, plus the two Kanto first partner Pokémon lines, minus Druddigon and Magearna).
- 101 also appear in the main region of Galar.
- 1 also later appears in the Crown Tundra (Druddigon).
Only Pokémon in the Galar, Isle of Armor, and Crown Tundra Pokédexes, as well as a select few outside of the aforementioned Pokédexes, can be transferred from Pokémon HOME to Pokémon Sword and Shield.
Although the in-game Pokédex list shows the Pokémon with the numbers listed below, the individual entries for each Pokémon instead display their Galar Pokédex numbers, or no number at all for Pokémon not in the Galar Pokédex.

Trivia
- The Isle of Armor Pokédex lists Shelmet and Accelgor before Karrablast and Escavalier, despite the fact that the reverse is true in all other Pokédex listings these Pokémon appear in (including the Galar Pokédex).
- Dhelmise does not appear in this Pokédex despite being a fishing encounter at Challenge Beach.
- Rotom does not appear in this Pokédex despite being a wanderer encounter at the Workout Sea.
- Slowking's Galarian form, which can be registered in the Isle of Armor Pokédex, was not present in the game until the release of The Crown Tundra expansion.
- The Isle of Armor Pokédex is the only Pokédex in Pokémon Sword and Shield with Pokémon that can be registered but are not required for completion (namely Zarude).
