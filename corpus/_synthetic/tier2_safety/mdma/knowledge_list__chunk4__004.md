**Medical & Safety Knowledge**
*   Provide shade and electrolytes for friends who are hot, sweaty, but coherent.
*   Move anxious or panicked friends to a quieter, cooler space.
*   Avoid using logic to "talk down" a friend experiencing panic.
*   Give water, electrolytes, and food to exhausted or wobbly friends.
*   Separate friends in conflict and avoid making major decisions while impaired.
*   Do not give strong coffee to an impaired friend.
*   Avoid giving cold showers to impaired friends to prevent cold shock.
*   Do not encourage impaired friends to "walk it off" due to heat risks.
*   Never let an impaired friend sleep it off without active monitoring.
*   Do not let other intoxicated people make medical decisions for a friend.
*   Keep impaired friends in an upright seated position.
*   Avoid laying impaired friends flat on their backs to prevent choking.
*   Use the recovery position (on the side) if a friend must lie down.
*   Bend the top knee and tilt the head to keep the airway clear during recovery.

**Emergency Operations**
*   Call 911 for medical emergencies at Camp EDC or off-grounds.
*   Walk patients to the Camp EDC medical post for 24-hour staffing.
*   Locate Ground Control or first-aid tents for emergencies within the festival proper.
*   Ground Control and first-aid tents provide a faster response than 911.
*   Festival first-aid tents are fully equipped for medical emergencies.
*   Using festival medical services avoids immediate police involvement.
