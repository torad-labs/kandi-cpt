Friend: My kid is heading to EDC 2026 and I'm trying to be the "cool parent" and actually give them a safety briefing. If they decide to take MDMA, how long does it actually last?

Vet: It's a bit of a ride. Usually, it kicks in within 20-60 minutes, though if they ate a huge meal right before, it could take up to 2 hours. The "come up" can be pretty sharp and kind of unnerving for a few minutes before they hit the peak.

Friend: And once they're "there," how long are we talking?

Vet: The peak usually lasts about 90-120 minutes, and the primary effects wrap up in 3-5 hours. Just a heads-up: there's a residual period for another 2-6 hours after that where they'll probably find it really hard to sleep.

Friend: What about the dose? I want to make sure they aren't overdoing it.

Vet: A standard dose is 80-125mg. Most pressed pills fall in that 80-120mg range. There's a "sweet spot" with this stuff—once you hit it, taking more doesn't actually make the high better or longer; it just ramps up the negative side effects.

Friend: I've heard people "redose" to keep the party going. Is that safe?

Vet: It can be, but only if done right. Taking one-third to one-half of the original dose about 90 minutes in can extend the peak by a few hours. Anything more than that just makes the hangover brutal. Also, tell them not to do this too often; if they need more and more to feel it, they're building a tolerance and need to space out their rolls by 3-6 months to keep the "magic" alive.

Friend: I'm mostly worried about the heat. It's Vegas in May, right?

Vet: Exactly. That's the biggest danger. MDMA raises core body temp, and when you combine that with the Nevada heat, dancing for 6 hours at kineticFIELD, and mixing in other stimulants like coke or amps, you're looking at a potential heat-stroke disaster.

Friend: What should I tell them about water? Just drink a ton of it?

Vet: Actually, no. Drinking *too much* water can be just as deadly as not drinking enough because it dilutes the sodium in your blood. They should aim for 1-3 cups an hour, or up to 4 if they're going hard at circuitGROUNDS.

Friend: So how do they stay hydrated without overdoing the water?

Vet: Electrolytes are key. They shouldn't just drink plain water. Tell them to grab those Electrolit sticks at the free water stations around the grounds or eat some salty snacks. It keeps the balance right.

Friend: Are there any "red flag" symptoms they should look out for?

Vet: Definitely. If they get a severe, splitting headache while they're rolling, that can be a sign of dangerously high blood pressure. Tell them: do NOT tough that out. They need to head to the medical tent immediately.

Friend: Got it. I'll make sure they know about the medical tents and the electrolytes. Thanks for the specifics!
