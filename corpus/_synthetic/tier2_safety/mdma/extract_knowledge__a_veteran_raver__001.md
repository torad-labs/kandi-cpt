### MDMA Pharmacokinetics and Duration
The onset of MDMA typically occurs within 20 to 60 minutes, though ingestion on a full stomach may delay effects for up to two hours. The initial transition to the peak is often characterized by a rapid "launch." The peak effects generally persist for 90 to 120 minutes, with total primary effects lasting between three and five hours. Following the primary phase, a residual period characterized by sleep difficulty and cognitive shifts lasts an additional two to six hours.

### Dosage and Administration
A standard oral dose ranges from 80 to 125 mg, with most pressed pills containing 80 to 120 mg. Beyond a specific "sweet spot" threshold, higher doses do not increase desired effects or duration but instead increase the prevalence of side effects. 

To extend the peak by several hours, a single redose of one-third to one-half of the original dose may be administered approximately 90 minutes after the initial dose. Doses exceeding this amount typically increase the severity of the subsequent hangover.

### Tolerance and Recovery
Frequent administration leads to pharmacological tolerance. To allow for serotonin replenishment, a minimum interval of several weeks between uses is required, though a spacing of three to six months is recommended to prevent the permanent loss of efficacy (the "magic") and to mitigate post-use depression and hangovers.

### Critical Safety Risks in Festival Environments
**Hyperthermia:** MDMA increases core body temperature. The risk of overheating is compounded by high ambient temperatures (e.g., Las Vegas in May), high dosages, heavy physical exertion, and the concurrent use of other stimulants such as cocaine or amphetamines.

**Hypertension:** The onset of a severe, splitting headache during stimulant use may indicate dangerously high blood pressure and requires immediate medical intervention.

**Hydration Management:** Improper fluid intake can lead to two distinct crises: hypernatremia (dehydration) or hyponatremia (water poisoning/sodium dilution). Recommended intake is 6 to 18 oz (1–3 cups) of water per hour, increasing to 24 oz (4 cups) during heavy exertion. Water must be supplemented with electrolytes via salty snacks or electrolyte powders, such as the Electrolit products provided at EDC water stations, to maintain sodium balance.
