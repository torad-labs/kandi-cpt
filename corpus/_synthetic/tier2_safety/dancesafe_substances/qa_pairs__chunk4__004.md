Q: Why does the festival distribute Electrolit hydration sticks at water stations?
A: They are provided to prevent hyponatremia (water poisoning), which can occur when chugging water without electrolytes.

Q: What is the maximum amount of water recommended for consumption per hour?
A: Less than 800 mL (27 oz) per hour.

Q: What is the recommended hourly water intake when mixed with electrolytes?
A: 480 mL (16 oz) per hour.

Q: What immediate steps should be taken if a friend is suspected of overheating, being over-stimulated, or having too much of anything?
A: Get them to shade, get them seated, and provide water and electrolytes.

Q: What are the escalation signs to watch for in an over-stimulated or overheating individual?
A: Vomiting, seizures, bluish skin, abnormal heart rate or breathing, abnormal temperature, delusions/hallucinations, and unresponsiveness.

Q: What should be done if an escalation sign appears in a festival attendee?
A: Go to the nearest first-aid tent or flag down Ground Control.

Q: Will a festival attendee get in trouble for seeking medical help?
A: No.
