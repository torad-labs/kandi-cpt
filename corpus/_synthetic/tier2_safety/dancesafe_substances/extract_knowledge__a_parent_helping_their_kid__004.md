### Hydration and Medical Safety at Electric Daisy Carnival

**Hydration Guidelines**
Proper hydration at Electric Daisy Carnival requires a balance of water and electrolytes to prevent hyponatremia, a condition also known as "water poisoning" that occurs when water is consumed without sufficient salt. To mitigate this risk, the festival provides Electrolit hydration sticks at designated water stations. 

Fluid intake should be regulated to avoid exceeding 800 mL (27 oz) per hour. The recommended intake is 480 mL (16 oz) per hour, combined with electrolytes.

**Emergency Response and Monitoring**
Individuals showing signs of over-stimulation or overheating should be moved to a shaded area and seated. Treatment includes the administration of water and electrolytes. 

Observers should monitor the individual for escalation signs, which include:
*   Vomiting or seizures
*   Bluish skin discoloration
*   Abnormal heart rate, breathing, or body temperature
*   Delusions, hallucinations, or unresponsiveness

**Medical Assistance**
If any escalation signs are present, the individual must be taken to the nearest first-aid tent or an attendant from Ground Control must be flagged down. The festival maintains a policy that individuals will not face disciplinary action or legal trouble for seeking medical assistance.
