Q: Which class of drugs does ketamine belong to?
A: Ketamine belongs to a class of drugs called "dissociative anesthetics."

Q: What are the typical durations of the effects of lower-dose ketamine and the time it takes to return to baseline?
A: The effects last about 45 to 60 minutes, and most people return completely to baseline within 1.5 to 2 hours.

Q: What is a "k-hole" and what dose of ketamine is usually enough to enter this state?
A: A k-hole is a full dissociative state often compared to a near-death experience; 100 mg is usually enough to enter it.

Q: How long does it take for the effects of snorted ketamine "bumps" (30 to 60 mg) to begin?
A: The effect comes on within about 5 to 15 minutes.

Q: Which substances are identified as risky depressants to combine with ketamine?
A: Alcohol, benzos, and GHB.

Q: What is ketamine cystitis and what are its symptoms?
A: Ketamine cystitis is a condition where the bladder lining is damaged, causing pelvic pain, frequent urination, and incontinence.

Q: Why is it dangerous to enter a k-hole in a public setting like a festival?
A: Because it is dangerous and very difficult to move, as ketamine disrupts communication between the brain and body and reduces motor control and pain signaling.

Q: Can a user test if ketamine is one isomer or another (such as r-ketamine or s-ketamine) without advanced lab equipment?
A: No, there is no way to test this without the most advanced lab equipment available.

Q: How long does an LSD trip typically last?
A: An LSD trip lasts 8 to 12 hours.

Q: What is "chapter switching" in the context of an LSD experience?
A: It is when the experience is broken into chunks with different themes or emotions that can change abruptly due to a thought, an event, or a change in environment.
