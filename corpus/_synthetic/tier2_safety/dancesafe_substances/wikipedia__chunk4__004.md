### Health and Safety

**Hydration and Electrolyte Management**
To prevent hyponatremia (water intoxication), attendees are advised against the excessive consumption of water without accompanying electrolytes. The event provides Electrolit hydration sticks at designated water stations to mitigate this risk. Recommended fluid intake is limited to less than 800 mL (27 oz) per hour, with a suggested target of 480 mL (16 oz) per hour mixed with electrolytes.

**Medical Intervention**
Individuals exhibiting signs of over-stimulation or overheating should be moved to a shaded area, seated, and administered water and electrolytes. Immediate medical intervention via the nearest first-aid tent or "Ground Control" personnel is required if escalation signs appear, which include:
*   Vomiting or seizures
*   Cyanosis (bluish skin)
*   Abnormal heart rate, breathing, or body temperature
*   Delusions, hallucinations, or unresponsiveness

The event maintains a policy ensuring that individuals seeking medical assistance will not face disciplinary action or legal repercussions.
