Friend: I’m starting to prep for EDC 2026! I know I need to stay hydrated, but is just chugging water enough?

Vet: Actually, no. Just drinking water can actually be risky—it can lead to hyponatremia, which is basically water poisoning. You need electrolytes to balance it out.

Friend: Oh wow, I didn't know that. How do I make sure I'm getting enough electrolytes while I'm running between kineticFIELD and circuitGROUNDS?

Vet: Insomniac has you covered. They distribute Electrolit hydration sticks at the water stations specifically for this reason. Just grab those and mix them in.

Friend: Got it. How much water should I actually be drinking per hour so I don't overdo it?

Vet: Keep it under 800 mL (about 27 oz) per hour. Ideally, aim for 480 mL (16 oz) per hour mixed with those electrolytes.

Friend: That's a good rule of thumb. What if I notice one of my friends is starting to struggle? Like, if they seem over-stimulated or just way too hot?

Vet: First things first: get them out of the sun and into the shade. Get them seated and start getting water and electrolytes into their system immediately.

Friend: What should I be looking for to know if it's actually a medical emergency?

Vet: Watch for escalation signs. If they start vomiting, have a seizure, get bluish skin, or have an abnormal heart rate or temperature, it's serious. Also watch for delusions, hallucinations, or if they become unresponsive.

Friend: If I see that happening, I'm worried about getting in trouble if we've been... partying. Should I still call for help?

Vet: Absolutely. If you see any of those signs, head to the nearest first-aid tent or flag down Ground Control immediately. You will not get in trouble for seeking medical help. Your safety is the priority.
