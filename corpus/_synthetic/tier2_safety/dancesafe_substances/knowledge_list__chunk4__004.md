# Chunk: 4

* Drink less than 800 mL of water per hour.
* Aim for 480 mL of water per hour mixed with electrolytes.
* Chugging water without electrolytes can cause hyponatremia.
* Electrolit hydration sticks are distributed at water stations.
* Move over-stimulated or overheating friends into the shade.
* Ensure overheating friends are seated.
* Provide water and electrolytes to friends showing signs of distress.
* Monitor for vomiting or seizures as escalation signs.
* Watch for bluish skin or abnormal heart rates.
* Be alert for abnormal breathing or temperature.
* Identify delusions, hallucinations, or unresponsiveness as critical signs.
* Go to the nearest first-aid tent if escalation signs appear.
* Flag down Ground Control for immediate medical assistance.
* Attendees will not get in trouble for seeking medical help.
