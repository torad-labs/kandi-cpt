**Cocaine Risks:** Higher cardiovascular injury risk than other stimulants; can cause strokes, aneurysms, heart attacks, increased heart rate, blood pressure, and body temperature. Combining with exertion/stimulants in desert heat risks overheating, arrhythmia, and hypertension. Short-acting nature leads to unpleasant comedowns and compulsive redosing.

**Harm Reduction:** Use saline spray for nasal damage; avoid sleep deprivation (>2 days) to prevent psychosis; use disposable straws/Post-Its to avoid Hepatitis C.

**Drug Checking:** Helps identify novel substances/adulterants. 
*   **Reagent Testing:** Color-change chemicals (e.g., DanceSafe's nine reagents). Marquis turns black for MDMA/MDA; Simon's turns blue for MDMA. Reagents provide "red flags, not green lights" and cannot confirm purity or exact composition; only labs can. Note: PMMA/PMA may not react with Marquis, potentially masking dangerous fillers. 
*   **Fentanyl:** No reagent can test for fentanyl.
