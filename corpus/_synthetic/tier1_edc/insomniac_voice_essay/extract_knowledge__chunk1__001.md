Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is held in the Nevada desert. Transportation to the event is available via official EDC Las Vegas shuttles from various locations, private coaches, party buses, or personal vehicles. 

Attendee health and safety are managed through several onsite resources. Free water stations are provided for those utilizing empty CamelBaks or Insomniac-branded water bottles. Medical assistance is available at designated medical tents and through Ground Control, a trained safety crew.

Logistical information, including the festival map, set times, and the locations of ATMs, food vendors, charging stations, and lockers, is accessible via the Insomniac app. Due to the mobile nature of the "Daisy" installation, attendees are advised to establish fixed meet-up locations and times.

Additional event programming is organized under "EDC Week," which includes various after-parties within Las Vegas.
