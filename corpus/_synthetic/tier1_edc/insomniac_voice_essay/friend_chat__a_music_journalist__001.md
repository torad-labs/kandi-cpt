Friend: I'm finally pulling the trigger on EDC 2026, but I’m totally overwhelmed. How are we even getting to the Speedway?

Vet: Honestly, don't drive yourself if you can avoid it. Take the official Insomniac shuttles from the hotels or grab a party bus with a group. If you absolutely have to drive, download "Find My Car Smarter" immediately—trust me, finding your car in that lot after a 12-hour set is a nightmare.

Friend: Fair point. I’m already planning my outfits, but I want to go all out with the platforms and the fur. Is that a bad move?

Vet: Look, the costumes are legendary, but your feet will scream by midnight. Go for the "gorgeous but comfy" vibe. If you insist on the platforms, jam some gel insoles in there or you'll be sitting out the headliners at kineticFIELD because you can't stand up.

Friend: Good call. What about water? I know the desert is brutal.

Vet: It's a death trap if you aren't careful. Bring an empty CamelBak or an official Insomniac bottle. There are free water stations everywhere, so just keep topping off. Dehydration is the fastest way to end your weekend early.

Friend: I've heard about "Ground Control." Are they actually helpful or just for show?

Vet: They're lifesavers. If you see someone looking rough or if you're feeling overwhelmed, find Ground Control or a medical tent. They’re trained to help no matter what, no questions asked. Just keep an eye on your crew.

Friend: Speaking of the crew, we usually just say "meet me at the Daisy." Does that work?

Vet: Absolutely not. The Daisy moves all over the place; it's a moving target. Pick a fixed landmark on the map or just Snapchat your location. If you try to meet at the Daisy, you'll spend three hours wandering in circles.

Friend: Got it. I'll just rely on the map. Is there a better way to track everything than a PDF?

Vet: Download the Insomniac app. It’s the only way to handle set times and find the ATMs, lockers, and charging stations without getting lost. You'll need it to navigate between circuitGROUNDS and the other stages.

Friend: I want to see every single set. Do I even sleep?

Vet: If you want to survive until Sunday, you have to pace yourself. Sleep when you can, eat actual food, and load up on Vitamin C. Also, don't forget sunscreen—the Nevada sun doesn't care about your rave aesthetic.

Friend: And once the festival closes for the night, is that it?

Vet: Not even close. The city is wide open. Check out the official EDC Week schedule for the best after-parties. The festival is the main event, but the Vegas strip life is where the real wind-down happens.
