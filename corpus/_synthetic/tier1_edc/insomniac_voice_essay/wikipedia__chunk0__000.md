# Electric Daisy Carnival Las Vegas

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas** is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival produced by Insomniac Events. Held annually in Las Vegas, Nevada, the event is characterized by high-production stage design, immersive art installations, and a culture emphasizing inclusivity and expressive attire.

### Overview
The festival serves as a central hub for the global dance music community, featuring a wide array of electronic genres. Attendees frequently employ "festival fashion," which often includes glitter, costumes, and themed outfits. The event is noted for its communal atmosphere and the adherence to the tenets of PLUR (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect).

### Stages and Lineup
The festival features multiple performance areas, including various Insomniac-branded stages such as kineticCATHEDRAL and other thematic installations. The lineup typically consists of a diverse range of international DJs and electronic music producers across subgenres including house, techno, dubstep, and trance.

### Logistics
Due to the scale of the event and the environment of the Mojave Desert, specific logistical preparations are recommended for attendees:

*   **Accommodations:** Because of high demand during the festival period, securing hotel reservations in advance is advised to ensure guaranteed lodging.
*   **Transportation:** Attendees are encouraged to research available transportation options to and from the venue to navigate the Las Vegas metropolitan area efficiently.
*   **Packing:** Recommended items for the Nevada climate include sunscreen, hats, sunglasses, and sealed lip balm to prevent dehydration and sun exposure.

### Safety and Policies
Insomniac Events enforces specific festival policies regarding prohibited and permitted items. Attendees are advised to review official guidelines prior to entry to ensure compliance with security screenings. The festival environment emphasizes peer-to-peer support and community care among attendees.
