# Electric Daisy Carnival Las Vegas (Attendee Guidelines)

## Overview
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is a large-scale electronic dance music festival held annually in the Nevada desert. Due to the environmental conditions and the scale of the event, organizers provide specific guidelines regarding health, logistics, and safety to ensure attendee well-being.

## Logistics
### Transportation
Attendees utilize several modes of transport to reach the venue, including official EDC Las Vegas shuttles operating from various designated locations, private coaches, carpools, and party buses. For those utilizing personal vehicles, the use of mobile applications, such as "Find My Car Smarter," is recommended to locate vehicles in large parking lots.

### Navigation and Amenities
The festival grounds feature a variety of amenities, including ATMs, food vendors, charging stations, and lockers. Insomniac provides a dedicated mobile application containing the festival map and performance set times to assist attendees in navigating the venue.

### Coordination
Due to the mobile nature of certain landmarks, such as the daisy, attendees are advised to establish fixed meet-up locations and times. Digital communication tools and location-sharing services are frequently used to coordinate parties within the venue.

## Health and Wellness
### Environmental Protection
Given the arid climate of the Nevada desert, hydration is critical. Insomniac provides free water stations throughout the venue; attendees are encouraged to bring empty hydration packs (e.g., CamelBaks) or Insomniac-branded water bottles. Additional self-care measures include the application of sunscreen, regular sleep, nutritional maintenance, and the use of vitamin supplements.

### Attire
While costumes and elaborate dress are common, the emphasis is placed on footwear that balances aesthetics with comfort to prevent physical strain during extended periods of activity. The use of gel insoles is a common recommendation for foot support.

## Safety
### Medical Assistance
Medical tents are stationed throughout the event for attendees in distress. Ground Control, a specialized safety crew, is deployed to provide immediate assistance and support to attendees regardless of the circumstance.

## Related Activities
The festival is often integrated into a broader itinerary known as EDC Week, which includes various official events and after-parties located throughout Las Vegas.
