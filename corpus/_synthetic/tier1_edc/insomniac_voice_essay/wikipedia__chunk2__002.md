# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival and event production. Characterized by its large-scale art installations, pyrotechnics, and diverse range of electronic subgenres, the event serves as a global gathering for the electronic music community.

### Overview
EDC is designed as an immersive experience, blending musical performances with thematic environmental design. The event is noted for its emphasis on "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect), a cultural ethos adopted by the rave community that encourages inclusivity and mutual support among attendees. This social framework is often manifested in the formation of spontaneous communal bonds between strangers during the event's duration.

### Stages
The festival utilizes multiple themed performance areas, typically utilizing camelCase nomenclature for its primary installations. These include:

*   **kineticFIELD**: The primary main stage, featuring large-scale LED screens and synchronized pyrotechnics.
*   **circuitGROUNDS**: A secondary large-scale stage focusing on high-energy performances.
*   **laserHedge**: A dedicated area specializing in immersive light shows and laser choreography.
*   **electricForest**: A wooded, atmospheric environment featuring ambient lighting and smaller-scale performances.

### Lineup
The event features a curated selection of international DJs, electronic music producers, and live acts. The lineup typically spans a broad spectrum of genres, including big-room house, techno, trance, dubstep, and drum and bass. Performances are scheduled across multiple stages simultaneously, requiring attendees to navigate the venue to access specific artists.

### Logistics
Due to the scale of the production, EDC employs extensive logistical planning. This includes coordinated transportation shuttles, designated camping areas for multi-day attendees, and a comprehensive ticketing system. The event's physical layout is designed to accommodate tens of thousands of visitors, integrating wide thoroughfares with concentrated "experience zones."

### Safety and Welfare
To manage the risks associated with large-scale crowds and high-sensory environments, EDC implements various safety protocols. The festival provides dedicated medical tents, hydration stations, and "ground control" teams tasked with assisting overwhelmed or distressed attendees. These measures are intended to mitigate the psychological and physical impact of the event's intensity, ensuring a secure environment for first-time and returning visitors.
