Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is a large-scale electronic dance music festival held in Nevada. Due to the regional climate, attendees are advised to bring protective gear against heat and sun, including sunscreen, hats, sunglasses, and sealed lip balm.

Festival attendance requires adherence to specific site policies regarding prohibited and permitted items. Organizers recommend that participants research these policies and secure hotel accommodations in advance to ensure lodging during the event.

Transportation planning is a critical component of attendance, as the venue is located a significant distance from residential areas. Coordination of transit options is necessary for entry and exit from the festival grounds.
