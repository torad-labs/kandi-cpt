**Transportation and Logistics**
Transit to Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is available via official Insomniac shuttles from various designated locations, private coach, carpool, or party buses. For those utilizing personal vehicles, GPS-based parking assistance applications are recommended for vehicle retrieval.

**Health and Safety Protocols**
Due to the Nevada desert climate, hydration is critical. Attendees may bring empty CamelBaks or Insomniac-branded water bottles for use at free onsite water stations. For medical emergencies or distress, assistance is provided by medical tents and Ground Control safety crews. Physical wellness is managed through regular sleep, nutrition, sunscreen application, and vitamin C supplementation.

**Onsite Navigation and Coordination**
The festival layout is accessible via the Insomniac app and physical maps, which identify the locations of ATMs, food vendors, charging stations, and lockers. Because the "Daisy" installation changes position throughout the event, it is an unreliable landmark for group coordination. Fixed meeting points and digital location sharing (e.g., Snapchat) are the established methods for party reunification.

**Attire and External Events**
Footwear requirements prioritize comfort and support, with the use of gel insoles recommended to mitigate foot fatigue. Beyond the primary festival grounds, the "EDC Week" itinerary provides a schedule of official after-parties and related events within Las Vegas.
