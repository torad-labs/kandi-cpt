Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 features several distinct performance areas, each categorized by musical genre and production style.

**Cosmic Meadow** is an open-grounds field stage hosting a variety of dance music, live performances, hip-hop, and rock. This stage serves as the location for the Opening Ceremony on Friday at 5:00 p.m. Previous performers at this venue include Snoop Dogg, The Prodigy, and Alison Wonderland.

**Circuit Grounds** is the second-largest stage, characterized by a wraparound steel frame, LED walls, and fire plumes. Scheduled 2026 performers include Tiësto on Saturday and The Outlaw. Past headliners include Martin Garrix, Eric Prydz, and John Summit.

**Neon Garden** (also known as Factory 93) focuses on deep techno and house music. The 2026 schedule includes Adriatique, Eli Brown, and Joseph CapriLati on Friday; Luciano, Silvie Loto, and Peggy Gou (B2B with KI/KI) on Saturday; and Adiel, DJ Gigola, Frankie Bones, Indira Paganotto, and Klangkuenstler on Sunday. Other featured artists include DJ Tennis B2B Chloé Caillet and a B2B set featuring Josh Baker, KETTAMA, and Prospa.

**Basspod**, hosted by Bassrush, is dedicated to bass music, including dubstep, drum and bass, and trap. The 2026 lineup features Culture Shock and Deathpact on Friday; Hybrid Minds, AVELLO B2B Dennett, Eptic B2B Space Laces, and Doctor P B2B Flux Pavilion B2B FuntCase on Saturday; and Virtual Riot, Peekaboo, Hamdi, and INFEKT B2B Samplifire on Sunday. Note that Subtronics is scheduled for Saturday at Kinetic Field rather than Basspod.

**Wasteland**, hosted by Basscon, utilizes a post-apocalyptic theme for hard dance, hardstyle, and hardcore music. Scheduled 2026 artists include GRAVEDGR, Rebekah, DYEN, KUKO, and DØMINA on Friday; Lady Faith B2B LNY TNZ, Da Tweekaz, Audiofreq B2B Code Black B2B Toneshifterz, Rob Gee B2B Lenny Dee, Lil Texas, and Mish on Saturday; and Sub Zero Project, Warface, MADGRRL B2B VESSEL, DJ Isaac, and The Purge on Sunday.
