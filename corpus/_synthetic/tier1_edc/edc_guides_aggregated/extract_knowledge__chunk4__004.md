Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is the flagship event of a global series produced by Insomniac, which includes editions in Orlando, Mexico City, Thailand, and the cruise-based EDSea. The festival is held annually in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Standard operating hours include a 5:00 PM opening on Friday and 7:00 PM openings on Saturday and Sunday, with all nights concluding at 5:30 AM.

The festival features nine primary stages: Kinetic Field, Cosmic Meadow, Circuit Grounds, Neon Garden, Basspod, Wasteland, Quantum Valley, Stereo Bloom, and Bionic Jungle. Additional installations include the Forest House, Art Cars, and Downtown EDC venues. While stage themes, layouts, and lineups vary annually—such as the 2024 repositioning of Basspod, Quantum Valley, and Neon Garden—the core infrastructure remains consistent. Recurring nightly elements include a drone and fireworks display at approximately 1:00 AM and the tradition of the Above & Beyond or Armin van Buuren sunrise sets.

On-site logistics are managed through a cashless system. Ground Control staff are identified by purple shirts, and free drinking water stations are marked with a droplet symbol. The event is restricted to attendees aged 18 and over, with a 21+ requirement for VIP areas and alcohol consumption. No re-entry is permitted.

Accommodation options include Camp EDC and Hotel EDC. Camp EDC is an on-site campground requiring a four-night pass (Thursday through Sunday) and features the Mesa gathering area, Shiftpod rentals, RV hookups, and a 24-hour General Store. Access to the festival is provided via the Camp shuttle entrance. Hotel EDC is a partnership with Virgin Hotels that incorporates themed decor, pop-up DJ sets, and pool parties.
