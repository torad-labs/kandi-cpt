Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 features several distinct performance areas, including Quantum Valley, Stereo Bloom, and Bionic Jungle.

**Quantum Valley**, operated by Dreamstate, specializes in trance music ranging from ethereal to psychedelic styles. The 2026 schedule includes Paul van Dyk, Gareth Emery, Cosmic Gate, Ilan Bluestone, Darren Porter, Cold Blue, and Sarah de Warren on Friday; Andrew Rayel, Astrix, Paul Oakenfold, SUPERSTRINGS, T78, and Thomas Schumacher on Saturday; and Tinlicker (DJ Set), Eli & Fur, Mathame, Massano, Cassian, KREAM, and Rebūke on Sunday.

**Stereo Bloom** is curated by the Insomniac Music Group and focuses on house, tech-house, and bass music. The Friday lineup consists of Wax Motif, Luke Dean, Luuk van Dijk, Max Dean, Max Dean B2B Luke Dean, Obskür, Omar+, SLAMM, and Toman. Saturday performers include Sammy Virji, CID, Noizu, Discip, HNTR, and OMNOM. Sunday artists include Chris Lorenzo B2B Bullet Tooth, Skream, Klo, Lu.Re, Morgan Seatree, Murphy's Law, Sidney Charles B2B Bushbaby, Silva Bumpa, and Slugg. Hamdi is also listed in the Stereo Bloom lineup information, though primarily associated with Basspod.

**Bionic Jungle**, introduced in 2022, is a house-focused stage characterized by a forest aesthetic.

Logistical requirements for attendees include the use of broken-in, closed-toe sneakers to accommodate 10–20 miles of walking per night across asphalt, grass, and gravel surfaces. To prevent dehydration and hyponatremia, the recommended fluid intake is 16 to 27 ounces per hour, supplemented with electrolytes. Free water refill stations are identified on festival maps by a water droplet symbol.

Due to high decibel levels over approximately 30 hours of exposure, the use of high-fidelity earplugs is advised. Because cellular service is unreliable, attendees are encouraged to screenshot maps and schedules and establish fixed meeting points at the info booth, merchant store, or lockers area.
