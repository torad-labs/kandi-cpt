Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is the largest electronic dance music festival in North America, produced by Insomniac. Founded in 1997 in Los Angeles, the event relocated to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in 2011. The 30th anniversary edition is scheduled for May 15–17, 2026, at the Speedway, located 15 miles north of the Las Vegas Strip. The festival maintains a daily attendance of approximately 140,000 people, with a total weekend headcount exceeding 525,000.

The event operates from dusk until 5:30 a.m. the following morning. Gates open at 7:00 p.m. on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, though the opening ceremony occurs at the Cosmic Meadow at 5:00 p.m. on Friday. Environmental conditions in mid-May typically include temperatures exceeding 90°F during the day and dropping to 60°F at night, with humidity levels between 15% and 20%.

Festival culture is centered on the "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect) code of conduct. Attendees, referred to by Insomniac as "Headliners," frequently engage in the trading of "kandi," which are handmade beaded bracelets used as social friendship signals.

The Kinetic Field serves as the festival's primary stage and is the largest stage in North America, measuring 480 feet wide and 100 feet tall. The stage features annual thematic redesigns, such as the 2015 Crystal Village, the 2017 Mother Gaia theme, the 2023 kineticAWAKENING (featuring the "Celestial" figure), and the 2024 KineticCIRCLE (a cathedral design incorporating stained-glass windows of previous themes). The Kinetic Field hosts major headliners—including Martin Garrix, Tiësto, Zedd, Hardwell, Kaskade, John Summit, The Chainsmokers, Charlotte de Witte, and Steve Aoki—and is the site of "Sunrise Sets" performed by Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren.
