# Electric Daisy Carnival Las Vegas

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas** is the largest electronic dance music (EDM) festival in North America. Produced by Insomniac, the event originated in Los Angeles in 1997 before relocating to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in 2011. The festival is characterized by its large-scale production, dusk-to-dawn scheduling, and a cultural emphasis on the ethos of "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect). Attendees are referred to by the organization as "Headliners."

## Overview
The festival typically takes place over three consecutive nights in mid-May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, located approximately 15 miles north of the Las Vegas Strip. Daily attendance is estimated at 140,000 people, with total weekend attendance exceeding 525,000. The 2026 edition marks the 30th anniversary of the event, scheduled for May 15–17.

Operations generally begin at 7:00 PM and conclude at 5:30 AM the following morning. The event is subject to the arid climate of the Mojave Desert, characterized by high daytime temperatures (often exceeding 90°F), significant nocturnal cooling (approximately 60°F), and low humidity.

## Stages
The festival features multiple themed stages, each utilizing distinct audiovisual production.

### kineticField
The kineticField serves as the festival's primary main stage and is recognized as the largest stage in North America, measuring 480 feet in width and 100 feet in height. The stage undergoes a complete thematic redesign annually; notable past themes include "Mother Gaia" (2017), "Crystal Village" (2015), "kineticAWAKENING" (2023), and "KineticCIRCLE" (2024). 

The stage typically hosts the festival's highest-profile artists, including Martin Garrix, Tiësto, Zedd, Hardwell, Kaskade, John Summit, The Chainsmokers, Charlotte de Witte, and Steve Aoki. It is also the traditional site for "Sunrise Sets" performed by artists such as Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren. Other notable performers include Fisher, Subtronics, Cloonee, Porter Robinson, Sofi Tukker, GRiZ, Wooli, DJ Snake, Excision, David Guetta, Boris Brejcha, Mau P, Alesso, Illenium, and Kx5.

## Culture and Logistics
EDC Las Vegas is noted for its specific social customs, most notably the trading of "kandi" (handmade beaded bracelets), which serves as a gesture of friendship among attendees.

The festival's logistics are managed to accommodate a massive transient population in a desert environment, requiring significant infrastructure for transport and hydration due to the extreme weather conditions and the remote location of the Speedway.
