# Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac Events. While various editions are held globally—including EDC Orlando, EDC Mexico City, EDC Thailand, and the cruise-based EDSea—the event held annually in Las Vegas serves as the organization's flagship production.

## Event Logistics
The flagship festival takes place every May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. The event typically follows a standardized schedule, opening at 5:00 PM on Friday and 7:00 PM on Saturday and Sunday, with operations concluding at 5:30 AM each night. 

The festival is a cashless event with a minimum age requirement of 18 for general admission and 21 for VIP and alcohol access. Re-entry to the venue is prohibited. On-site amenities include free water stations identified by a droplet symbol and staff members known as "Ground Control," identifiable by their purple shirts.

### Accommodations
Insomniac provides two primary lodging options for attendees:
*   **Camp EDC:** An on-site campground accessible exclusively via four-night camping passes. Facilities include the Mesa gathering area, RV camping with hookups, Shiftpod tent rentals, free showers, and a 24-hour general store. Campers utilize a dedicated shuttle entrance for festival access.
*   **Hotel EDC:** A corporate partnership with Virgin Hotels (formerly Resorts World) that involves a property takeover featuring themed decor, pop-up DJ sets, and pool parties.

## Production and Stages
The festival is characterized by a rotating selection of stage designs, layouts, and official theme songs (e.g., Tiësto's "My City" for the 2024 edition). While the specific positioning of stages within the Speedway may shift annually—such as the 2024 relocation of Basspod, Quantum Valley, and Neon Garden—the event consistently features nine primary stages:

*   kineticField
*   cosmicMeadow
*   circuitGrounds
*   neonGarden
*   basspod
*   wasteland
*   quantumValley
*   stereoBloom
*   bionicJungle

Additional installations include the Forest House, various Art Cars, and venues located within the Downtown EDC area. Recurring visual elements include the festival's owl mascot, the "Daisy" moving installation, and nightly drone and fireworks displays occurring approximately at 1:00 AM.

## Lineup and Culture
While the specific artist roster changes annually, the festival maintains several traditions, most notably the "Sunrise Set" performed by Above & Beyond or Armin van Buuren. 

The event is a hub for the "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect) subculture, which manifests in the practice of "kandi" trading (the exchange of handmade beaded bracelets) among attendees.
