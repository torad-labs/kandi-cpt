### Logistics and Transportation
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 is held at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Transportation options include shuttle passes with pickups at designated Strip hotels, with "Premier" shuttles offering reserved time slots. Private transportation includes rideshare services (Uber and Lyft), carpooling, and helicopter transfers via Maverick Helicopters, which operate 15-minute flights from the Strip to the venue.

For the opening night on Friday, the opening ceremony begins at 5:00 PM; arrivals before 9:00 PM are recommended to avoid traffic. To avoid extended queues for shuttles and rideshares, departure before 4:30 AM is advised. General admission attendees are prohibited from re-entering the venue once they have exited; re-entry is permitted only for those possessing a Camp EDC wristband.

### Medical and Safety Protocols
Medical assistance is provided free of charge at designated medical tents. Ground Control staff, identified by purple shirts, fanny packs, and light sabers, are stationed throughout the grounds to assist attendees. Insomniac policy explicitly states that individuals will not face penalties for seeking medical help.

Critical indicators for medical intervention include seizures, vomiting, pale skin, bluish extremities, abnormal heart rate or temperature, delusions, or unresponsiveness. Protocol for impaired individuals requires they be kept in an upright or sitting position; lying flat is prohibited.

### Cultural Elements and Installations
The festival is guided by the "PLUR" ethos (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect). A central social practice is "Kandi trading," conducted via a specific four-step handshake involving a peace sign, a heart shape, a handshake, and the exchange of beaded bracelets. The festival's primary symbols are the owl, featured in Kinetic Field designs, and the Daisy.

Nightly programming includes drone shows at approximately 1:00 AM followed by fireworks, with the bleachers serving as the primary vantage point. "Downtown EDC" serves as a centralized hub for art and entertainment, featuring the YeeDC! line-dancing saloon, the Queen Bar drag show, a wedding chapel, the Memory Lane historical installation, and various carnival rides, including a Ferris wheel.
