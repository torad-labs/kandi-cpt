### Logistics and Transportation
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 is held at the Las Vegas Speedway. Transportation options include shuttle passes with pickups at designated Strip hotels, with Premier shuttles offering reserved time slots. Private transport options include rideshare services (Uber and Lyft), carpooling, and aerial transfers via Maverick Helicopters, which provide approximately 15-minute trips from the Strip to the venue.

### Schedule and Access
The festival opens Friday at 5:00 PM with an opening ceremony. Attendees are advised to arrive by 9:00 PM on the first night to avoid traffic. General admission attendees are subject to a no re-entry policy; only those with a Camp EDC wristband may re-enter the grounds after exiting. To avoid significant wait times for shuttles and rideshares, departure is recommended before 4:30 AM.

### Health and Safety
Medical assistance is provided free of charge at medical tents and by "Ground Control" staff, identified by purple shirts and light sabers. Insomniac policy states that individuals will not face disciplinary action for seeking medical help. Critical symptoms requiring immediate intervention include seizures, vomiting, bluish skin/extremities, abnormal heart rates, hallucinations, or unresponsiveness. Medical protocols dictate that impaired individuals must remain upright or sitting; they should not be laid flat.

### Festival Layout and Culture
The event is governed by the "PLUR" ethos (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect), which is practiced through "Kandi trading" via a specific four-step handshake (peace sign, heart-shape, handshake, bracelet exchange). 

The venue features several distinct areas:
*   **Kinetic Field:** The primary stage, featuring the owl mascot.
*   **Downtown EDC:** A hub of installations including the YeeDC! line-dancing saloon, Queen Bar, a wedding chapel, and the Memory Lane historical exhibit.
*   **Amenities:** The grounds include carnival rides and a Ferris wheel.
*   **Daily Events:** Drone shows occur nightly at approximately 1:00 AM, followed by fireworks, with the bleachers serving as the primary vantage point.
