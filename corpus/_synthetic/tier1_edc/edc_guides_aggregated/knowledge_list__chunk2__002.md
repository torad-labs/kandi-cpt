**Operations & Safety**
* Wear broken-in, closed-toe sneakers.
* Avoid heels, flip-flops, or new shoes.
* Pack blister pads and gel insoles.
* Use high-fidelity earplugs to prevent tinnitus.
* Drink 16 to 27 ounces of water per hour.
* Use electrolyte tablets or free Electrolit sticks.
* Bring an empty CamelBak or Insomniac water bottle.
* Locate free water stations via the water droplet symbol on the map.

**Wayfinding & Tips**
* Expect to walk 10-20 miles per night.
* The Speedway terrain consists of asphalt, grass, gravel, and stairs.
* Cell service is often unreliable or non-existent.
* Set a fixed meeting spot like the lockers or info booth.
* Do not use the Daisy as a meeting point because it moves.
* Screenshot the festival map and schedule in advance.
