# Electric Daisy Carnival Stage and Attendee Guidelines

## Overview
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) features a variety of curated stages designed to categorize electronic dance music (EDM) genres through specific thematic environments. These stages range from large-scale productions focusing on trance and house to smaller, immersive installations.

## Stages

### quantumValley
Managed by Dreamstate, quantumValley is dedicated to the trance genre, spanning a spectrum from ethereal to psychedelic styles. Historically, the stage has hosted artists such as Paul Oakenfold, Paul van Dyk, Above & Beyond, Gareth Emery, Ferry Corsten, Andrew Rayel, and Cosmic Gate. 

The 2026 schedule includes:
* **Friday:** Paul van Dyk, Cosmic Gate, Gareth Emery, Ilan Bluestone, Darren Porter, Cold Blue, and Sarah de Warren.
* **Saturday:** Paul Oakenfold, Astrix, Andrew Rayel, SUPERSTRINGS, T78, and Thomas Schumacher.
* **Sunday:** Tinlicker (DJ Set), Eli & Fur, Mathame, Massano, Cassian, KREAM, and Rebūke.

### stereoBLOOM
Curated by Insomniac Music Group, stereoBLOOM focuses on a diverse array of house, tech-house, and bass music. Past performers include James Hype, Noizu, Matroda, San Pacho, Westend, and Mele.

The 2026 schedule includes:
* **Friday:** Wax Motif, Luke Dean, Luuk van Dijk, Max Dean (including a B2B set with Luke Dean), Obskür, Omar+, SLAMM, and Toman.
* **Saturday:** Sammy Virji, CID, Noizu, Discip, HNTR, and OMNOM.
* **Sunday:** Chris Lorenzo B2B Bullet Tooth, Skream, Klo, Lu.Re, Morgan Seatree, Murphy's Law, Sidney Charles B2B Bushbaby, Silva Bumpa, and Slugg. (Note: Hamdi is also listed in stereoBLOOM documentation despite appearing at the Basspod stage).

### bionicJungle
Introduced in 2022, bionicJungle is characterized by a jungle and forest aesthetic. The stage primarily hosts house music artists and is positioned as a smaller-scale venue. MALUGI is cited as a primary anchor artist for the 2025 season.

## Logistics

### Communication and Navigation
Due to high crowd density, cellular service is frequently unreliable or unavailable. Attendees are advised to establish fixed meeting points at static locations, such as the lockers area, information booth, or merchant store, rather than mobile landmarks like the Daisy. It is recommended that attendees save offline screenshots of the event map and performance schedules.

## Safety

### Footwear and Physical Health
Given the terrain of the venue—which consists of asphalt, grass, gravel, and stairs—and an estimated walking distance of 10 to 20 miles per night, closed-toe sneakers are recommended. The use of heels or flip-flops is discouraged to prevent injury.

### Hydration
To prevent dehydration and hyponatremia, attendees are advised to consume between 16 and 27 ounces of fluid per hour. While free water stations (marked by a water droplet symbol on the map) provide water, the use of electrolyte tablets or Electrolit sticks is recommended. Reusable containers, such as CamelBaks or Insomniac-branded bottles, are standard for fluid transport.

### Auditory Protection
Due to the volume levels and the duration of exposure (exceeding 30 hours over three days), the use of high-fidelity earplugs—such as those produced by Etymotic, Loop, or EARasers—is advised to prevent tinnitus and permanent hearing loss.
