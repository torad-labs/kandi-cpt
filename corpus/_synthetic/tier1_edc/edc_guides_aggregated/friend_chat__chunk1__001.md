Friend: I'm finally hitting EDC 2026! I'm a bit overwhelmed by the map though. Where should I even start on Friday?

Vet: You definitely have to hit cosmicMeadow at 5pm on Friday for the Opening Ceremony. It's the open-grounds field stage, and it's got a really cool vibe—Rotella says it feels like it "hugs the crowd." They do a mix of everything there, from live acts to hip-hop and rock.

Friend: Nice. I'm really into the heavy stuff, though. Where do the headbangers hang out?

Vet: That's basspod, hosted by Bassrush. If you want that dubstep and trap energy, that's the spot. For 2026, you've got ATLiens, Culture Shock, and Deathpact on Friday, and a massive Doctor P B2B Flux Pavilion B2B FuntCase set on Saturday.

Friend: Wait, is Subtronics playing basspod?

Vet: Actually, no! He's playing kineticFIELD on Saturday this time around. If you want more bass on Sunday, though, you can catch Virtual Riot, Peekaboo, and Hamdi over at basspod.

Friend: Got it. What about the "dark" side of the festival? I've heard there's a techno spot.

Vet: That's neonGarden (also known as Factory 93). It's deep house and techno. Friday is stacked with Adriatique, Eli Brown, and Joseph Capriati. Sunday is also huge with Indira Paganotto and Klangkuenstler.

Friend: I saw Peggy Gou is playing—which stage is she on?

Vet: She's actually doing a double dip at neonGarden! She has a solo set on Friday and then a B2B with KI/KI on Saturday.

Friend: What's the deal with circuitGrounds? I've seen photos of those insane LED walls.

Vet: Oh, circuitGrounds is a visual masterpiece. It's the second-largest stage with a massive wraparound steel frame and fire plumes. You can't miss Tiësto and The Outlaw there in 2026; they're both slated for Saturday.

Friend: I've also heard people talking about a "post-apocalyptic" area. Is that real?

Vet: Totally. That's wasteland, hosted by Basscon. It's all about hardstyle and hardcore. If you want that high-BPM energy, check out Lady Faith B2B LNY TNZ or Da Tweekaz on Saturday, and Sub Zero Project on Sunday.

Friend: That sounds intense. Any other must-sees for the hard dance fans?

Vet: For sure. At wasteland, make sure you catch GRAVEDGR, Rebekah, and DØMINA on Friday. Then on Sunday, you've got DJ Isaac and The Purge to wrap things up.

Friend: This is helpful. One last thing—if I want a "variety" vibe like the Opening Ceremony, who else usually plays cosmicMeadow?

Vet: It's super diverse. In the past, they've had everyone from Snoop Dogg and The Prodigy to San Holo and Alison Wonderland. It's the best place for those unique live performances.
