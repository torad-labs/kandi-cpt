Friend: I’m finally doing it! I got my tickets for EDC 2026, but I’m low-key terrified of messing something up. Since you’re a pro, what’s the biggest mistake people make with their group?

Vet: Honestly? Not picking a meeting spot. Last time I was there, my crew got totally separated while we were all staring at El Pulpo. Between the fire and the neon, we just lost each other. Pick one specific landmark before you walk in—it takes five seconds but saves your entire night.

Friend: Good call. I’ve already started a spreadsheet for my itinerary. I'm trying to map out every single set so I don't miss anyone. Is that the way to go?

Vet: Actually, don't do that. I used to have every artist and set time memorized down to a science, and I realized on the final night that I barely had any fun because I was too stressed about the "plan." Forget the strict itinerary. Let the Electric Sky guide you and just wander.

Friend: That's a relief, I was stressing. What about the "Vegas" part of the trip? Should I go all out with the pool parties and gambling before the festival starts?

Vet: Go for it! Hit the pools, gamble, get all the glitter you can find. Just a warning: don't go *too* hard. You've got three consecutive nights of the biggest festival in the US; if you burn out on day one, you're missing out. Pace yourself.

Friend: Noted. I'm trying to figure out my outfit now. I found these amazing platforms that match my look perfectly, should I go for it?

Vet: Absolutely not. If they're heels, leave them at home. They look cute for ten minutes, but your feet will be screaming by midnight. Wear your everyday, reliable shoes. And if you do buy new sneakers, break them in *before* you get to the Speedway.

Friend: I'll stick to my comfy kicks then. Anything I should remember about the logistics of getting in and out of the venue?

Vet: The parking lots are a nightmare. Whatever you do, remember where you parked. Drop a GPS pin on your phone or make sure you exit through the same entrance you came in. Trust me, you do not want to be wandering the lot in a daze at 4 AM.

Friend: Smart. I'm packing a small bag now—just the basics. Anything essential I might be forgetting?

Vet: Bring a small carrier for your ID and essentials, but the biggest thing is a portable charger. I once forgot to charge my phone and it's a total nightmare trying to find people or check a map with a dead battery.

Friend: I'll make sure my power bank is full. Last question—since I'm just wandering and not following a strict plan, how do I actually make sure I'm taking care of myself?

Vet: Just stay mindful. Hydrate, take breaks, and don't spend the whole night staring at your phone screen. Be smart about your needs, and you'll have a way better experience at kineticFIELD or circuitGROUNDS than if you're just rushing around.
