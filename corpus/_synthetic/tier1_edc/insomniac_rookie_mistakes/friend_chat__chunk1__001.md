Friend: I’m finally doing it! I'm hitting EDC 2026. Since you've been a million times, what's the biggest mistake rookies make?

Vet: Honestly? Forgetting that you're in a literal desert. You’ll be dancing at kineticFIELD and sprinting to circuitGROUNDS for hours. If you don't hammer the H2O and actually eat a real sandwich, you're going to end up dizzy and passed out on the grass. Trust me, I've been that guy.

Friend: Noted. Plenty of water and snacks. What about the "vibe"? Do I need to be documenting everything for my followers?

Vet: Look, post a few stories, but for the love of god, stay off Instagram. This happens once a year. If you spend the whole set at neonGARDEN staring at your screen, you're missing the magic. Just bang your head and be in the moment; the app will still be there on Monday.

Friend: Fair point. I'm a bit nervous about the crowd size, though. What happens if I get separated from my group?

Vet: That’s where the "leave no raver behind" rule comes in. We're all family under the Electric Sky, but you stick with your crew. If someone goes missing, don't just assume they're fine—go find them. You'll probably make ten new friends while searching for them anyway.

Friend: That's a relief. Besides the main stages, is there actually other stuff to do?

Vet: Dude, you'd be a sucker not to explore. You have to get out on the Speedway. There are art cars roaming everywhere and the Insomniac character parade is a total must-see.

Friend: Are there any specific landmarks I should look for?

Vet: Definitely hunt for El Pulpo—it's that massive fire-breathing installation that usually hangs out in the downtown area. It's iconic.

Friend: I've heard people do weird stuff there, like getting married or something?

Vet: Totally! The possibilities are endless. You can join a random conga line, write on a stranger's arm, or even mail a postcard to a friend who couldn't make it. Just get out there and experience everything the venue has to offer.

Friend: I'm starting to get hyped. Any last tips for the schedule?

Vet: Just keep your energy up for all three days. Between the art and the music at worldsBEYOND, your body is working overtime. Hydrate, eat, and explore!
