Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is a three-day music festival held in a desert environment. Due to the combination of high temperatures and physical exertion, maintaining hydration and caloric intake is necessary to prevent dizziness and dehydration.

The event takes place on the Speedway, a large-scale venue featuring various installations and attractions. Notable landmarks include "El Pulpo," a fire-breathing art installation located in the Downtown EDC area, as well as various roaming art cars and a parade featuring Insomniac characters.

Attendees are encouraged to navigate the venue as a group to ensure the safety of all party members and to explore the available interactive elements, such as postcards and art installations.
