EDC Las Vegas, also referred to as EDC LV, is an event held in the US. The festival takes place over a single weekend each year, lasting for three days in a desert environment.

Attendees are encouraged to explore the entirety of the Speedway to experience all that EDC has to offer, including the parade and various art cars. Notable installations such as El Pulpo are featured within Downtown EDC.
