Friend: I’m finally hitting EDC 2026, but I’m low-key panicking. Since you’ve done this a million times, tell me the truth: do I need a rigid itinerary to make sure I don't miss the headliners?

Vet: Honestly? That’s the first mistake I made. I used to have every set time memorized down to a science, but it actually killed the vibe. My advice for 2026: let the Electric Sky guide you. If you’re vibing at kineticFIELD and a random set starts peaking, just stay. Don't let a spreadsheet ruin your fun.

Friend: Fair enough. Speaking of the layout, I’ve heard it’s a nightmare to navigate. What happens if I lose my squad in the crowd?

Vet: It happens faster than you think. One minute you're staring at El Pulpo—the giant fire-breathing octopus—and the next, your friends are gone. Before you even enter the gates, pick one specific meeting place. Trust me, it takes five seconds to decide but saves your entire night.

Friend: Noted. I’m already planning my outfits, and I’ve got these killer platforms that match my look. Are those a go?

Vet: Absolute rookie move. Please, for the love of everything, do not wear heels. They look cute for the first hour, but your feet will be screaming by the time you hit CIRCUITGROUNDS. Wear your most reliable, broken-in sneakers. Your soles will thank you.

Friend: Yikes, okay, sneakers it is. What about gear? Just my phone and some cash?

Vet: You need more than that. Bring a small carrier for the essentials—definitely your ID—and for the love of god, charge your phone before you leave the hotel. There is nothing more stressful than a dead battery when you're trying to coordinate a pickup.

Friend: Speaking of pickups, I’m driving in with a few people. Is the parking situation as bad as they say?

Vet: It can be total madness. Do yourself a favor: drop a digital pin exactly where you park. Also, make sure you exit through the same entrance you came in. If you try to wing it, you’ll be wandering the lot for an hour.

Friend: I’m planning on hitting a few pool parties and gambling before the festival starts. Should I just go full-throttle from Friday to Sunday?

Vet: Go for it—hit the pools, wear all the glitter—but don't go *too* hard too fast. It’s three consecutive nights of high energy. If you burn out on Friday at neonGARDENS, you’re going to miss the magic of the Sunday finale. Pace yourself.

Friend: That makes sense. Any last-minute survival tips before I head out?

Vet: Just remember to actually take care of yourself. Stay hydrated, take breaks, and don't spend the whole weekend staring at your phone screen. Be present in the moment, and you'll have an epic experience.
