**Event Overview: Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas**

Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival held at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. The event is characterized by a community-centric culture based on the principles of Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect (PLUR). Attendees, referred to as "Headliners," frequently engage in the exchange of "kandi"—handmade beaded bracelets and jewelry—as a gesture of friendship and connection.

**Accommodations and Site Logistics**
On-site camping is available, including "Moonglow" camping. Options such as "shift pods" are provided for groups (e.g., four-person capacities), though reporting indicates inconsistent availability of bedding despite promotional claims. The campsite features artificial grass, which some attendees noted causes static electric shocks. Amenities include communal showers and portable toilets.

The festival utilizes a General Admission (GA) system, with a "GA Plus" tier that offers expedited entry through specific ID check queues and access to dedicated restroom facilities, though efficacy in reducing wait times is disputed.

**Programming and Attractions**
The festival schedule typically begins with a "Day Zero" (Thursday), featuring pre-parties at locations such as "The Mesa." The main event follows, with performances running from approximately 7:00 p.m. until 10:00 a.m. the following morning.

*   **Stages and Artists:** The event features multiple stages, including the "Kinetic Forest." Notable performers include Steve Aoki and Koven.
*   **Interactive Installations:** Attractions include a wedding chapel (legally registered in the state of Nevada), free fairground rides (such as a drop tower), "Life Force IV" hydration therapy, and creative workshops for making kandi and dioramas.
*   **Technology:** The official event app includes a collectible token system that tracks attendee visits to various stages.

**Commerce and Services**
Food and beverage options include vendors such as "Sin City Pizza" and "Ben & Jerry’s." Pricing for basic items is high, with reports of pizza slices costing approximately $10–$11 and bottled water priced at $6.

**Operational Observations**
Reports from attendees highlight several logistical challenges:
*   **Signage:** Navigation is noted as difficult due to poor signposting and an ineffective map.
*   **Infrastructure:** Issues include insufficient water refill stations for the desert climate and unstable cellular signal due to high crowd density (estimated at 200,000 people).
*   **Security:** Entry involves standard security searches and pat-downs. Regulations prohibit spirits with an alcohol content above 16%.
