EDC Las Vegas takes place at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Attendees are referred to as "Headliners."

**Stages & Artists**
*   **kineticFIELD**: Featured David Guetta, Steve Aoki, and Razi.
*   **bionicJUNGLE**: Hosted Koven.
*   Other stages include cosmicMEADOW, circuitGROUNDS, neonGARDEN, bassPOD, wasteLAND, quantumVALLEY, and stereoBLOOM.

**Timeline & Experience**
*   **Day 0 (Thursday)**: Arrival and camping setup. Includes the "pre-party" at the Mesa and pool parties.
*   **Friday**: Opening ceremony.
*   **Sunday**: Final day.

**Policies & Logistics**
*   **Camping**: Moonglow camping offers "shift pods" (four-person tents).
*   **Ticketing**: General Admission (GA) and GA+ are available; GA+ provides expedited ID checks and dedicated toilets.
*   **Security/Prohibited Items**: Strict searches are conducted. Spirits must be below 16% alcohol content to be brought into the arena.
*   **Amenities**: Free fairground rides; water refill points; "Life Force IV" hydration therapy; "Candy" (Kandi) trading culture; "SpongeBob Zone" cereal vendor.
*   **General Info**: The festival features a wedding chapel for legal ceremonies. Signage is noted as poor; artificial grass can cause static electric shocks.
