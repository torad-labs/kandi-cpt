**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas**

Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival held at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in Nevada. The event is characterized by a culture centered on "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect). Attendees, referred to as "Headliners," frequently engage in "kandi" culture, which involves the creation and trading of colorful beaded bracelets and jewelry.

**Event Logistics and Accommodations**
The festival includes "Day Zero," a preliminary day for those utilizing on-site camping. One available camping option is "Moonglow," which features "Shift Pod" tents. These pods are situated on artificial turf, which some attendees reported caused static electric shocks. On-site amenities include showers and a "Village" area containing general stores and food vendors.

**Tickets and Access**
The festival offers various admission tiers, including General Admission (GA) and General Admission Plus (GA+). GA+ provides expedited entry through dedicated ID check queues and access to specific GA+ restroom facilities, though attendee reports indicate these may still experience significant wait times.

**Arena Features and Attractions**
The festival arena consists of multiple music stages, such as the Kinetic Forest. Non-musical attractions include:
*   **Fairground Rides:** Various rides, including a drop tower and a Ferris wheel, are provided free of charge to attendees.
*   **Interactive Experiences:** The "Mesa" area hosts "Day Zero" pre-parties and art installations, such as the "Project Wonder" immersive video game experience and "diorama" crafting stations.
*   **EDC Downtown:** A dedicated area featuring a fully registered wedding chapel where legal marriages are performed.
*   **Services:** On-site health services include "Life Force IV," offering hydration and vitamin injections for recovery.

**Operational Observations**
*   **Pricing:** Food and beverage costs are high; for example, a single slice of pizza was reported at $11 and bottled water at $6.
*   **Infrastructure:** Attendees noted challenges regarding signage and navigation, as well as limited cellular service and a perceived insufficiency of water refill stations.
*   **Scheduling:** Musical performances typically run from approximately 7:00 PM until 10:00 AM the following morning.
