**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas: 2025 Event Analysis**

**Camp Logistics and Infrastructure**
Camp check-in for the 2025 event commenced at approximately 7:00 a.m., preceding the official 9:00 a.m. scheduled start. The accommodation infrastructure utilized "pods," which remained consistent with previous years' designs. These units were equipped with air conditioning; attendees noted that the application of reflective tarps over the pods significantly reduced internal temperatures. On-site amenities included shuttles for gear transport and "The Mesa," a wellness and recreation area featuring a pool and yoga sessions.

**Programming and Performance**
The event featured a diverse electronic music lineup across multiple days. Notable performances included:
*   **Day 1:** Sets by Armin van Buuren, Sarah Landry, and DJ Snake.
*   **Day 2:** Performances by Alesso, Afrojack, Eleante, and Sub Zero Project.
*   **Additional Acts:** Dimension and a "Back to Back" (B2B) set featuring 33 Below and NBA.

**Operational Observations**
Weather conditions during the event varied from unusually cold nights to high wind advisories on Day 2, with winds expected to subside by 9:00 p.m. Attendees reported that the 2025 layout changes successfully mitigated "bottlenecking" and "crowd crush" issues that had been noted during the 2023 iteration of the festival.

**Cultural Context**
The event maintained a strong adherence to "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect) culture, characterized by the trading of "kandi" (beaded jewelry) and trinkets among attendees. The 2025 edition was cited as a significant improvement in both musical curation and crowd management compared to the 2023 event and the early Los Angeles iterations (2008–2010).
