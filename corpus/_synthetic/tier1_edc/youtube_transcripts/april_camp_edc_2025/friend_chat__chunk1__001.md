Friend: I’m thinking about hitting up EDC 2026! I’ve heard the camping is a whole thing. What’s the deal with the pods?

Vet: Honestly, they're the same as previous years. We had four people in one and it worked, though it's not exactly "luxury" spacing. Pro tip: bring a reflective tarp to put over the top of your pod. It lowers the temperature significantly—absolute game changer.

Friend: Good call. What about the check-in process? I’m worried about waiting in the sun for hours.

Vet: It can be a bit chaotic, but actually pretty smooth. For the last trip, they said check-in started at 9:00 a.m., but they actually started letting people in shortly after 7:00 a.m. If you get there early, you can breeze through.

Friend: Once I'm in, what should I do first? Any secrets to beating the crowds?

Vet: Head straight to the mesa on Thursday right after you check in. Hardly anyone is there the first day, so you can basically guarantee yourself access to the pool. It’s a total vibe for trading trinkets and candy before the madness starts.

Friend: I love the PLUR side of things. Is the camp community actually friendly, or is it just for the cameras?

Vet: I swear, camp EDC is where you go to restore your faith in humanity. People are incredibly sweet—they'll feed you, help you out, or even help you glue your eyelashes on if you're struggling. There's even a "pancake mom" family that does breakfast; they're legends.

Friend: Nice. I’m starting to look at the lineup. Who were the standouts last time?

Vet: The music was on another level. I was surprisingly blown away by DJ Snake and Sarah Landry. But the real highlight for me was the last 20 minutes of Alesso’s set—it totally brought me back to the golden era.

Friend: Which stages should I prioritize? I want the high-energy stuff.

Vet: You definitely want to hit kineticFIELD for the big names and then swing by the bass-heavy stages for Sub Zero Project and Dimension. If you want that heavy, immersive energy, those are your go-to spots.

Friend: I've heard the crowds can be a nightmare. Is it actually safe now?

Vet: It used to feel a bit like a "crowd crush" in some years, but Insomniac really fixed the layout recently. The flow is way better now, which is why it's felt like a 10/10 experience again. It doesn't feel as bottlenecked as it did a few years back.

Friend: Any final advice for a first-timer?

Vet: Prioritize your wellness! I used to try and do every single yoga class and activity at the mesa just to get my money's worth, but now I realize sleep and hydration come first. Do the pool parties, get your sleep, and the rest of the time just enjoy the electric sky!
