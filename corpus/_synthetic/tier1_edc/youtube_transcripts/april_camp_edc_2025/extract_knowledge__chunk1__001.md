### EDC Camp Operations and Accommodations
Camp EDC utilizes a pod-based housing system featuring air conditioning. To further regulate interior temperatures, the use of reflective tarps over the pods is a common practice among attendees. Check-in for camp began shortly after 7:00 a.m., preceding the officially scheduled 9:00 a.m. start time. Shuttles are provided for transporting gear, though some attendees opt to transport items manually.

### The Mesa and Wellness Facilities
The Mesa serves as a hub for wellness and social activities. Facilities include a swimming pool and scheduled yoga sessions. On the first day (Thursday), pool access is generally more accessible due to lower attendance. The culture within the camp is characterized by "PLUR" (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect), manifested through the trading of trinkets and candy.

### Musical Performances and Programming
The event features a variety of electronic dance music (EDM) artists across multiple days. Notable performances include:
*   **Day One:** Armand Hammer, Sarah Landry, and DJ Snake.
*   **Day Two:** Alesso, Afrojack, Eleante, and Sub Zero Project.
*   **Additional Acts:** Dimension and 33 Below.

### Logistics and Infrastructure
Recent iterations of the festival have implemented layout changes to address historical issues regarding overcrowding, bottlenecking, and "crowd crush," specifically comparing the current experience to the 2023 event. Weather conditions during the event included a period of high wind advisories and temperature fluctuations between warm days and chilly nights.
