The festival operates over multiple days, typically spanning a long weekend in April. *   **Performance Timing:** Sets are scheduled in blocks throughout the day and night, with headlining performances occurring during the late-night hours.

The event incorporates "Kinetic" art installations and interactive exhibits throughout the venue. Essential infrastructure includes hydration stations, medical tents, and food vendor courts. To maintain safety and flow, the festival utilizes designated walkways and crowd-control barriers.
