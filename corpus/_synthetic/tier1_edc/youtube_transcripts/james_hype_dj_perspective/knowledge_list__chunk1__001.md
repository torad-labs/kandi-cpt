### Artist & Performance

* James Hype played on the kineticFields stage.
* James Hype performed immediately before Martin Garrix.

### Stage & Production
* The main stage is called kineticFields.
* Artists can choose special effects from a menu.
* Artists are required to pay for their own special effects.
* Special effect costs increase for sets performed after 11 p.m.
* Large flame units cost $4,600.
* Medium flame units cost $2,800.
* Large pyro hits cost $1,850.
* Extra large pyro hits cost $2,600.
* Total special effects costs for James Hype's set were approximately $12,000.
* Stage programming can be done via a console screen before moving to the actual rig.
* Lighting Directors (LDs) can program the show in two locations simultaneously.

### Operations & Policies
* Wristbands are provided upon entry to the festival.
* Official policies restrict filming in certain backstage and programming areas.
* Artists use "coms" (communication headsets) to coordinate with the pyro and lighting teams.
* Special effect queue points must be precisely timed to the tracklist.
* Artists can be transported to the festival via helicopter.

### Safety & Wayfinding
* The festival crowd utilizes totem poles for visibility and wayfinding.
* Attendees often carry flags to mark their location in the crowd.
* The "sky deck" is a specific location within the venue.

### General Knowledge
* EDC is located in Las Vegas.
* The festival is considered one of the biggest dance music events of the year.
* Crowd sizes for main stage sets can reach approximately 100,000 people.
* The event theme is "United by Music."
