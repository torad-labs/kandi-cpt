### Event Overview: Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a major global electronic dance music festival held annually in Las Vegas. A central feature of the event is the **Kinetic Field**, the festival's main stage.

### Performance Profile: James Hype
Artist James Hype performed a 60-minute set on the Kinetic Field main stage on Sunday, positioned as the opening act immediately preceding Martin Garrix. 

**Set Composition and Technical Execution:**
*   **Track Selection:** The set balanced established hits with unreleased material, including the track "Sweat Couture."
*   **Genre Diversification:** The performance incorporated Drum and Bass edits and a remix of the 1990s track "Let Me Show You" by Kamisra.
*   **Experimental Elements:** The set featured a "freestyle" transition utilizing audio clips of DJ Demox, though the artist noted technical difficulties with the stage monitors during the performance.
*   **Testing:** The set was road-tested at a prior engagement in El Paso, Texas, to gauge crowd reaction to new edits.
