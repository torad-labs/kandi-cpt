Friend: Twelve thousand dollars just for the "wow factor"! Do they just wing it on stage, or is it planned?

Friend: Does everything always go perfectly with that much money on the line?

Vet: Not always. Even at that level, things happen. James mentioned his monitors were terrible and he couldn't hear the music well, and one of his freestyle transitions didn't land as well as he hoped. But the crowd usually doesn't notice—they just see the sea of people and totem poles and feel the energy.

Friend: That's a good lesson for my kid. So, for 2026, if we want the full experience, we just need to look for the kineticFIELD schedule?
