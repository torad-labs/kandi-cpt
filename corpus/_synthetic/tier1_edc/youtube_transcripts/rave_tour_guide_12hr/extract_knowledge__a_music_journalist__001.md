**Venue and Stage Architecture**
The festival layout incorporates several specialized stages and installations:
*   **Ubuntu Stage:** Characterized by immersive smaller rooms within the larger design.
*   **Wasteland:** A designated thematic area within the festival grounds.
*   **Metropolis Stage:** A city-themed installation featuring LED-lit skyscrapers and a centrally located mechanical owl with horizontal head movement.
*   **Kinetic Field:** The primary stage area, which houses the "Department of Power" VIP experience. This installation utilizes Tesla coils synchronized to the track "Wavy" by Eli Brown.
*   **Cosmic Meadow:** A high-capacity area hosting performances, including a B2B (back-to-back) set by Kaskade and Alice In Wonderland.
*   **Bionic Jungle:** A designated photography location within the venue.
