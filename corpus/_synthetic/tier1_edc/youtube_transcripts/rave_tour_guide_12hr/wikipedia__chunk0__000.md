# Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas** is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival produced by Insomniac Events. Held annually at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, the event is characterized by its multi-genre musical lineups, large-scale art installations, and immersive pyrotechnics.

### Overview
EDC Las Vegas serves as the flagship event for the Insomniac brand. The festival operates as an overnight experience, with performances typically running from sunset until sunrise. The event is designed as a "city" of music, utilizing a vast open-air layout to accommodate hundreds of thousands of attendees across multiple themed environments.

### Stages
The festival features a variety of stages, each curated to host specific sub-genres of electronic music. These include:

*   **kineticFIELD:** The primary main stage, known for high-production visual effects and headlining acts.
*   **circuitGrounds:** A stage focused on bass music, dubstep, and high-energy performances.
*   **neonGARDEN:** A space dedicated to house, techno, and underground electronic sounds.
*   **quantumValley:** A stage frequently used for trance and progressive house.

### Lineup and Programming
The musical programming spans a broad spectrum of EDM, including house, techno, trance, dubstep, and trap. The lineup typically consists of globally recognized DJs and producers, alongside emerging artists. Performances are synchronized with large-scale LED displays, lasers, and synchronized pyrotechnics to enhance the sensory experience.

### Logistics
Due to the scale of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, the festival implements specific logistical frameworks to manage crowd flow:

*   **Navigation:** Attendees typically utilize digital maps and physical landmarks to navigate between the various stages.
*   **Hydration:** Water stations are distributed throughout the venue to provide free refills to attendees.
*   **Transport:** The festival coordinates shuttle services and ride-share hubs to facilitate movement between the venue and Las Vegas hotels.

### Safety and Welfare
Insomniac incorporates several safety measures to mitigate the risks associated with high-density crowds and overnight events:

*   **Ground Control:** A dedicated team of staff members tasked with providing directions, emotional support, and basic assistance to attendees.
*   **Medical Services:** On-site medical tents and emergency personnel are stationed throughout the grounds to treat dehydration, exhaustion, or other medical emergencies.
*   **Harm Reduction:** The event emphasizes the importance of hydration and "pacing" to ensure attendee safety over the duration of the 12-hour performance cycles.
