Friend: I’m trying to plan my 2026 trip, but I only have one day to spend at EDC. Is that even enough time?

Vet: Honestly, 12 hours is a whirlwind, but you can totally do it. I did a one-day run recently and it was wild. You just have to be strategic about where you go.

Friend: I keep hearing about these "experiences" outside the main music areas. What’s the deal with the Salvage City Supper Club?

Vet: It’s a total vibe. It’s like this "temple of sin" with a queen of the lost and lustful hosting. I had the tri-tip and sea bass there—definitely do it during the day if you can, because the venue looks amazing in the sunlight before you head into the madness.

Friend: That sounds intense. What about the actual festival grounds? Any "must-see" stages besides the big ones?

Vet: You have to check out the Ubuntu stage; the design is cool and there are these little rooms you can explore. Also, hit up the Wasteland and the Metropolis stage. Metropolis is basically a digital city with skyscrapers and a massive owl in the center that moves its head.

Friend: I've heard people talk about "Downtown EDC." Is that a real thing or just a nickname?

Vet: It's a real area! You have to visit the Candy Casino. You can play blackjack and bet your trinkets or candies. I actually won three extra candy singles because the dealer busted during my hand. It’s a bit of a wait, but totally worth it.

Friend: Nice. I really want to see the big production. Who should I look for at kineticFIELD?

Vet: You can't miss Martin Garrick at kineticFIELD. Also, if you're in the VIP area there, look for the Department of Power. I saw Tesla coils firing in sync with Eli Brown’s track "Wavy"—it was insane.

Friend: What about the other stages? Any other recommendations?

Vet: Definitely swing by Stereo Bloom and then head over to Cosmic Meadow. I caught Cascade B2B Alice in Wonderland there. Also, make sure you get your photos at Bionic Jungle before the night ends.

Friend: I don't want to miss the finale. How does the night usually wrap up?

Vet: You’ve got to end it at amELENS for the fireworks show. It’s the ultimate way to close out the night under the electric sky. 

Friend: Sounds like a plan. I'm guessing I'll be exhausted by the end?

Vet: Oh, absolutely. By the time I hit the rideshare parking lot at 5:22 AM, I was completely spent. Just make sure you grab some electrolytes throughout the day for a pick-me-up, or you'll crash way too early!
