### Electric Daisy Carnival 2026: Saturday Schedule

The Saturday programming of Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 features a diverse array of electronic dance music (EDM) genres, including house, techno, trance, and dubstep. Notable headlining performances and high-profile artists scheduled for this day include Tiësto, Hardwell, Kaskade, Steve Aoki, and The Prodigy.

Specialized performance formats are utilized for specific artists. BUNT. and VTSS are scheduled for "In The Round" sets, a stage configuration that places the performer in the center of the audience. "Sunrise Sets," which occur during the transition from night to morning, are scheduled for Above & Beyond and BOLO.

The lineup includes several collaborative "Back-to-Back" (B2B) sets, where two or more artists perform consecutively. Notable B2B pairings include Audiofreq with Code Black and Toneshifterz; Doctor P with Flux Pavilion and FuntCase; and Peggy Gou with KI/KI. Other collaborative performances include Eptic B2B Space Laces and Josh Baker B2B KETTAMA B2B Prospa.

Additional artists performing on Saturday include John Summit, Subtronics, Sub Focus, Maddix, Noizu, and Paul Oakenfold. The roster further comprises a wide range of supporting acts such as Hannah Laing, Boys Noize, Delta Heavy, and Hybrid Minds.
