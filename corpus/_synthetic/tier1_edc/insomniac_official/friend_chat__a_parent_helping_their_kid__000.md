Friend: My kid is dying to go to EDC Las Vegas in 2026, but I have no idea how this works. When is it actually happening?

Vet: It's running Friday, May 15th through Sunday, May 17th, 2026. It's held at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Just a heads up: the grounds are open from 7:00pm to 5:30am each night, but the opening ceremony at Cosmic Meadow starts early on Friday at 5:00pm.

Friend: Okay, and what about the age limit? Is it all ages?

Vet: Nope, the minimum age to get in is 18. If they're looking at the VIP Enhanced Experience or Bottle Service, those areas are strictly 21-plus.

Friend: Got it. I'm worried about the logistics. Can they just pop in and out of the venue if they forget something or need a break?

Vet: Definitely not. Ins-and-outs are not allowed. Once they enter for the night, they're in until the end. Also, tell them to leave the cash at home—it's a completely cashless event. They'll need a credit card, debit card, or something like Apple Pay.

Friend: That's helpful. I've heard the desert heat is brutal. What should I tell them to pack?

Vet: It is brutal. Tell them to wear actual sneakers—no sandals—and to stay hydrated. There are free water refill stations presented by Electrolit all over the place; just look for the water-droplet symbols on the map. If they get lost or need help, they should look for the "Ground Control" team in the purple shirts.

Friend: There are so many stages listed. Which ones are the "must-sees"?

Vet: If they want the big headliners and that iconic "main stage" feel, they have to hit kineticFIELD—it can hold 70,000 people. For the opening ceremony and some experimental stuff, they'll be at Cosmic Meadow.

Friend: My kid is really into the heavier stuff, like dubstep and hardstyle. Where do they go for that?

Vet: For the heavy bass, dubstep, and trap, they need to make a pilgrimage to Basspod. If they want the really fast, relentless energy of hardstyle and hardcore, they should head straight to Wasteland.

Friend: What about the more "underground" sounds? They mentioned something about techno.

Vet: That would be Neon Garden. It's curated by Factory 93 and focuses on deep house, techno, and minimalism. If they want something with massive LED walls and fire plumes, Circuit Grounds is the spot for that high-energy vibe.

Friend: One last thing—where are they staying? Is there a hotel option?

Vet: Most people stay at hotels near Harry Reid International airport, but if they want the full experience, they can get a 4-night camping pass for Camp EDC, which is right on-site.
