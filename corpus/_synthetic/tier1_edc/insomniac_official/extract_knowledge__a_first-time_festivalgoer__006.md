### Electric Daisy Carnival 2026: Logistics and Regulations

**Schedule and Access**
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 takes place from Friday, May 15, through Sunday, May 17. On the opening day, the ceremony at the Cosmic Meadow stage begins at 5:00 p.m. For all three days, the full festival grounds open at 7:00 p.m. and operate until 5:30 a.m. the following morning. Entry is permitted until one hour before the event concludes, provided the attendee possesses a valid, unscanned ticket. Re-entry is prohibited.

**Ticketing and Age Requirements**
The event is sold exclusively as 3-day passes, with the exception of single-day Bottle Service for VIP guests. Purchase is limited to four tickets per person and is restricted to credit or debit card transactions. Attendees must be at least 18 years old for general admission. A minimum age of 21 is required for alcohol consumption and access to the VIP Enhanced Experience.

**Identification and Entry Policies**
Valid identification is mandatory for entry and alcohol purchases. Accepted documents include current government IDs, foreign government passports, or foreign government driver's licenses accompanied by a passport photocopy. Expired IDs are only accepted if accompanied by DMV renewal documentation; consular IDs are not permitted.

**Equipment and Vehicle Regulations**
Standard cameras with fixed, non-detachable lenses and handheld video devices under six inches are permitted without photo passes. Professional cameras are prohibited, and Insomniac does not issue media credentials to independent photographers. Regarding parking, overnight sleeping in vehicles is forbidden, and cars left overnight are subject to towing at the owner's expense.

**Information Resources**
Event details, including the festival map, lineup updates, and set times, are available via the Insomniac app, the official EDC Las Vegas website, social media, and email updates. A printed festival guide is distributed to attendees upon entry.
