# Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Event Logistics

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a large-scale electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac. The event employs a standardized set of health, safety, and logistical protocols to manage attendee wellbeing and venue operations.

## Safety and Medical
Insomniac implements specific escalation criteria to determine when an attendee requires professional medical intervention rather than basic first aid (such as shade and hydration).

### Medical Escalation Criteria
Attendees are advised to seek immediate medical assistance if a peer exhibits any of the following symptoms:
* **Physical Distress:** Vomiting, seizures, pale or bluish skin discoloration, or abnormal heart rate and respiration (tachycardia or bradycardia).
* **Thermoregulation:** Abnormal body temperature, including hyperthermia or hypothermia.
* **Neurological/Psychological Impairment:** Delusions, hallucinations, paranoia, confusion, or stupor (conscious but unresponsive).
* **Critical Condition:** Non-reactive pupils, coma, or inability to be roused.

### Impairment Management
Guidelines for managing intoxicated peers emphasize that individuals under the influence of alcohol should not be responsible for the care of other impaired persons. To mitigate the risks of alcohol poisoning, the organization advises keeping intoxicated individuals awake and in an upright sitting position. The guidelines specify that coffee, cold showers, sleep, and physical exertion are ineffective treatments for alcohol poisoning.

### The Buddy System
To prevent separation and ensure safety, Insomniac promotes a "buddy system," encouraging attendees to establish designated meeting points and remain in small groups. Attendees are instructed to contact Insomniac staff for assistance when a peer requires help and to ensure all members of a group depart the venue together.

## Logistics and Amenities

### Hydration
Hydration services are provided via free water refill stations sponsored by Electrolit. These stations, identified by a water droplet symbol on the event map, offer filtered water treated with UV sterilization and distribute free Electrolit hydration sticks.

Venue policy prohibits the entry of sealed water containers. However, attendees may bring empty reusable containers, such as CamelBaks or Insomniac-branded water bottles, to be filled on-site.

### Food and Beverage
EDC operates as a cashless event, accepting credit, debit, Apple Pay, and Android Pay. Food and beverage options include stationary stands and roaming vendors, with dedicated vegan and vegetarian selections. Alcohol is available at designated bars for attendees aged 21 and older with valid identification.

### Retail and Supplies
Merchandise is available via the Insomniac Shop, which sells event-specific apparel and artist collections. Additionally, the Insomniac General Shop provides essential items, such as eye drops and footwear.

### Venue Services
* **Storage and Power:** Lockers are available for multi-day use and include universal cell phone chargers. Supplemental battery charging stations are distributed throughout the venue.
* **Information:** Info booths and Lost & Found services are positioned across the venue to assist attendees during and between show days.
