### Programming and Stage Allocations

**Neon Garden**
The Neon Garden stage features a rotation of house and techno artists. Friday's lineup includes Adriatique, Peggy Gou, Eli Brown, and Joseph Capriati. Saturday's schedule features Peggy Gou performing back-to-back (B2B) with KI/KI, DJ Gigola performing B2B with MCR-T, mink, Luciano, and Silvie Loto. Sunday's roster consists of Frankie Bones, DJ Gigola (solo), Adiel, Indira Paganotto, and Klangkuenstler.

**Quantum Valley**
Dedicated to the trance genre, Quantum Valley hosts Cosmic Gate, Gareth Emery, and Paul van Dyk on Friday. Saturday's performances include Andrew Rayel, Astrix, and Paul Oakenfold. Above & Beyond is scheduled for a Quantum Valley set in addition to a Kinetic Sunrise appearance. Tinlicker is slated for a DJ set on Sunday.

**Basspod**
The Basspod stage is dedicated to bass music. The lineup includes Subtronics, GRiZ B2B Wooli, Sub Focus, Peekaboo, Virtual Riot, Hybrid Minds, and Culture Shock. Featured collaborative sets include Eptic B2B Space Laces, a throwback set by Adventure Club, and a B2B performance by Doctor P, Flux Pavilion, and FuntCase.

**Wasteland**
The Wasteland stage focuses on hardstyle. Friday's performers include GRAVEDGR and Rebekah. Saturday's schedule features Lady Faith B2B LNY TNZ, Da Tweekaz, Rob Gee B2B Lenny Dee, and a B2B set by Audiofreq, Code Black, and Toneshifterz. Sunday's lineup consists of Sub Zero Project and Warface.

### Camp EDC Logistics

Camp EDC provides four-night camping passes covering Thursday through Sunday; single-night options are unavailable. The facility includes complimentary showers, portable toilets, and drinking water, alongside a General Store. The grounds are monitored 24 hours a day by security, medical personnel, and Ground Control.

**Regulations and Facilities**
Cooking is restricted to small portable propane stoves and grills with fuel tanks limited to 4 lbs per group; butane devices are prohibited. Steel knives and designated weapons are banned from the campground. Due to heat-related health risks, sleeping in cars or oversized vans is prohibited. Vehicles are barred from the campground proper, though gear transport assistance is provided from the parking area.

**Accommodations and Access**
ADA camping is available across all tiers. RV camping requires guests to provide their own vehicles; power hookups, water refills, and pump-outs are available for an additional fee. Separate parking passes for group members are priced at $95 plus taxes and fees.

### Safety and Medical Services

Ground Control, the Insomniac safety team, is identified by purple shirts, fanny packs, and light sabers. They serve as the primary point of contact for general assistance.

Medical care is provided free of charge at designated tents throughout the festival grounds. Insomniac Health & Safety officers patrol the venue to assist with conditions ranging from nausea and blisters to critical medical emergencies. The organization maintains a policy that seeking medical assistance will not result in disciplinary or legal trouble.
