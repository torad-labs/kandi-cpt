Friend: I'm finally pulling the trigger on EDC 2026! I'm looking at the Saturday lineup and it's absolutely massive. Who are the absolute must-sees?

Vet: Oh man, you're in for it. Saturday is stacked. If you want the big energy, you've gotta hit the main stage for Tiësto, Hardwell, and Kaskade. But for something different, keep an eye on BUNT. and VTSS—they're both doing "In The Round" sets, which are always a vibe.

Friend: I'm really into that melodic, ethereal sound. Anyone on Saturday fitting that bill?

Vet: Definitely. You cannot miss Above & Beyond doing a Sunrise Set. It’s legendary. Also, check out BOLO for another Sunrise Set—starting the morning like that at kineticFIELD is the only way to do it.

Friend: I've been getting into harder techno and bass lately. Who should I track down for the heavier stuff?

Vet: For the heavy hitters, head over to bassLAGOON for Subtronics, Getter, and the madness of Eptic B2B Space Laces. If you want that raw techno/hardstyle energy, look for Maddix and the chaos of Doctor P B2B Flux Pavilion B2B FuntCase.

Friend: I saw some interesting B2B pairings on the list. Any that stand out as "can't miss"?

Vet: For sure. The Peggy Gou B2B KI/KI set is going to be a huge highlight for the house heads. Also, keep an eye out for the Josh Baker B2B KETTAMA B2B Prospa trio—that's going to be a journey.

Friend: What about the legends? I see some big names from the old school.

Vet: You got Paul Oakenfold and The Prodigy both playing Saturday. Those are total bucket-list sets. If you want a mix of classic and modern, Steve Aoki and John Summit are also on the bill.

Friend: I'm seeing a lot of names I don't recognize, like Snow Strippers or VTSS. Are they worth the trek across the grounds?

Vet: Absolutely. Snow Strippers and VTSS are bringing that cutting-edge, underground energy. If you're into that "industrial" feel, you should also hunt down Boys Noize or the Rob Gee B2B Lenny Dee set.

Friend: Is there anyone I should look for if I just want to dance to some groovy house?

Vet: Hit up Noizu, Wax Motif, or Sammy Virji. They'll keep the energy high without being full-on "mainstage" EDM.

Friend: This is a lot to process. Any final tips for navigating Saturday?

Vet: Just remember that with a lineup this big, you can't see everyone. Pick your "musts"—like that Above & Beyond sunrise—and then just wander. Between the kineticFIELD and the smaller stages, you'll find some gems like Hannah Laing or CID regardless.
