Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026 is scheduled for May 15, 16, and 17. The event features multiple performance areas, including Kinetic Field, Circuit Grounds, and Cosmic Meadow.

**Kinetic Field Schedule**
Performances on Friday, May 15, include The Chainsmokers, Charlotte de Witte, Porter Robinson (DJ Set), and Sofi Tukker. Saturday, May 16, features Hardwell, John Summit, Subtronics, Kaskade, and Steve Aoki. Sunday, May 17, features Martin Garrix, Zedd, Fisher, Cloonee, and GRiZ B2B Wooli. Kinetic Field also hosts specific sunrise sets: Above & Beyond on May 16 and Armin van Buuren on May 17.

**Cosmic Meadow Schedule**
Underworld is scheduled for Friday, May 15. The Prodigy performs on Saturday, May 16. Sunday, May 17, features Alison Wonderland, Seven Lions, and a "Wholesome Riddim Set" by San Holo.

**Circuit Grounds Schedule**
Tiësto is scheduled to perform at Circuit Grounds on Saturday, May 16.

**Sunday, May 17 Lineup**
The Sunday roster includes a diverse array of electronic artists. Notable performers include 999999999, A.M.C with MC Phantom, ÆON:MODE (Sunrise Set), AHEE B2B Liquid Stranger, Alison Wonderland, ANNA, Armin van Buuren (Sunrise Set), Black Tiger Sex Machine, Boogie T B2B Distinct Motive, Cassian, Chris Lorenzo B2B Bullet Tooth, Cloonee, Cristoph, Dabin, DJ Isaac, Eli & Fur, GRiZ B2B Wooli, Hamdi, Indira Paganotto, Innellea, KREAM, Layton Giordani, Martin Garrix, Massano, Peekaboo, Rebūke, Rohaan (DnB Set), San Holo (Wholesome Riddim Set), Seven Lions, Skream, Solomun, Sub Zero Project, Tiga, Tinlicker (DJ Set), Vintage Culture, Virtual Riot, Whethan, William Black, and Zedd.
