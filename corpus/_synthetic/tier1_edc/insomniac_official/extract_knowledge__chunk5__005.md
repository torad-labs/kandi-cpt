### Medical Intervention and Safety
Insomniac establishes specific escalation criteria for transporting attendees to medical services. Immediate medical attention is required if an individual exhibits vomiting, seizures, pale or bluish skin discoloration, abnormal heart rate or respiration, or abnormal body temperature. Neurological and psychological indicators requiring intervention include delusions, hallucinations, paranoia, non-reactive pupils, confusion, stupor, coma, or an inability to be roused.

For alcohol-related impairment, guidelines prohibit intoxicated individuals from acting as caregivers. Management of alcohol poisoning excludes the use of coffee, cold showers, sleep, or physical activity. Affected individuals should be kept awake and maintained in an upright sitting position. The "Buddy System" is the recommended protocol for group cohesion and safety; attendees are advised to establish meeting points and utilize Insomniac staff for assistance with impaired peers.

### Hydration and Water Access
Filtered water, processed via UV sterilization, is provided free of charge at stations marked with a water droplet symbol on the festival map. These stations, presented by Electrolit, also distribute free hydration sticks. 

The venue prohibits the entry of sealed water. Permitted containers include empty CamelBaks, empty reusable water bottles (including Insomniac-branded versions), and other empty beverage containers.

### Venue Amenities and Services
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) operates as a cashless event, accepting credit, debit, Apple Pay, and Android Pay. Food and beverage services are provided via stationary stands and roaming vendors, with vegan and vegetarian options available. Alcohol service is restricted to individuals 21 years of age or older with valid identification.

Retail services are divided between the Insomniac Shop, which sells event apparel and artist collections, and the Insomniac General Shop, which stocks essentials such as flip-flops and eye drops.

Logistical support includes:
*   **Lockers:** Provide unlimited access over both event days and include universal cell phone chargers.
*   **Charging Stations:** Additional battery charging locations are distributed across the venue.
*   **Information and Recovery:** Info booths and Lost & Found services are available throughout the event duration and between show days.
