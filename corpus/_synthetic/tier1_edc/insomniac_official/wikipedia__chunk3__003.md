# Event Overview

The event is a multi-day electronic dance music festival featuring a diverse array of international artists across multiple stages. The festival schedule includes performances from Friday, May 15, through Sunday, May 17, with a particular emphasis on high-profile headliners and specialized set formats, including sunrise performances.

## Stages and Headliners

### kineticField
The kineticField stage serves as the primary venue for the festival's major headliners. 
* **Friday, May 15:** Featured performances by The Chainsmokers, Charlotte de Witte, Porter Robinson (DJ set), and Sofi Tukker.
* **Saturday, May 16:** Featured performances by Hardwell, John Summit, Subtronics, Kaskade, and Steve Aoki.
* **Sunday, May 17:** Featured performances by Martin Garrix, Zedd, Fisher, Cloonee, and a back-to-back (B2B) set by GRiZ and Wooli.

### cosmicMeadow
The cosmicMeadow stage hosts a mix of live acts and specialized electronic sets.
* **Friday, May 15:** Featured Underworld.
* **Saturday, May 16:** Featured The Prodigy.
* **Sunday, May 17:** Featured Alison Wonderland, Seven Lions, and a "Wholesome Riddim Set" by San Holo.

### circuitGrounds
* **Saturday, May 16:** Featured a performance by Tiësto.

## Sunday Lineup

The Sunday program features an extensive roster of artists across various subgenres of electronic music, including techno, house, and drum and bass.
