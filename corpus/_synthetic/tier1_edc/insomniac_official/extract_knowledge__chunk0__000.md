**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas 2026** is an electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac Events. The event takes place from Friday, May 15, through Sunday, May 17, 2026, at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in the Nevada desert. The festival grounds are open nightly from 7:00 p.m. to 5:30 a.m. The opening ceremony occurs Friday, May 15, at 5:00 p.m. at the Cosmic Meadow stage.

The festival operates as a cashless event, accepting credit, debit, and mobile payment systems (Apple Pay, Samsung Pay, and Android Pay); cash must be exchanged for prepaid cards on-site. Attendance is restricted to individuals aged 18 and older, while the Bottle Service and VIP Enhanced Experience areas require a minimum age of 21. The event prohibits re-entry once a guest has exited the grounds. Logistics include on-site lodging via Camp EDC (requiring 4-night passes) and proximity to Harry Reid International Airport (LAS).

The festival layout features nine main stages, supplemented by art cars and smaller performance areas. Key stages include:
*   **Kinetic Field:** The primary main stage with a capacity of approximately 70,000 attendees, hosting the festival's headlining artists.
*   **Cosmic Meadow:** An open-grounds stage featuring big-room acts and experimental programming.
*   **Circuit Grounds:** A stage characterized by LED walls and pyrotechnics.
*   **Neon Garden:** A stage curated by Factory 93, focusing on deep house, techno, and minimalism.
*   **Basspod:** A venue dedicated to bass-heavy genres, including dubstep, bass house, drum and bass, and trap.
*   **Wasteland:** A venue dedicated to the Basscon universe, focusing on hard dance, hardcore, and hardstyle.
*   **Quantum Valley:** One of the nine primary stages.

Health and safety infrastructure includes the Ground Control safety team, identifiable by purple shirts, and free water refill stations presented by Electrolit, marked by water-droplet symbols on the festival map.
