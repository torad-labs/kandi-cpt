# Event Program: Saturday, May 16

## Overview
The Saturday programming for the event features a diverse array of electronic dance music (EDM) performances, ranging from mainstage commercial acts to underground techno and hardstyle. The schedule includes several specialized performance formats, including "In The Round" sets and "Sunrise Sets," designed to alter the traditional stage-audience dynamic.

## Performance Stages and Formats
The event utilizes specific production formats for select artists:
*   **In The Round:** This immersive stage configuration is utilized by BUNT. and VTSS.
*   **Sunrise Sets:** Specialized early-morning performances are delivered by Above & Beyond and BOLO.

## Lineup
The Saturday roster consists of solo performances, collaborative B2B (back-to-back) sets, and multi-artist collaborations.
