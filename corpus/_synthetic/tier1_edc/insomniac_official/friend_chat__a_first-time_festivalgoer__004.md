Friend: I'm so hyped for EDC 2026, but honestly, I'm a little overwhelmed. Where do I even start with the music?

Vet: Don't stress! It's all about the stages. If you want that deep house and techno vibe, head to Neon Garden. Friday is huge there—you've got Adriatique, Eli Brown, and Joseph Capriati. Peggy Gou is playing solo Friday, then she's back Saturday for a B2B with KI/KI.

Friend: I've heard people talking about "trance" stages. Where do I go for that?

Vet: That's quantumValley. Friday is stacked with Cosmic Gate, Gareth Emery, and Paul van Dyk. Saturday has Andrew Rayel, Astrix, and Paul Oakenfold. Also, Above & Beyond is doing a quantumValley set on top of their Kinetic Sunrise show, and Tinlicker closes things out with a DJ set on Sunday.

Friend: What about the heavy stuff? I want some bass and maybe some of that hardstyle I've seen on TikTok.

Vet: For bass, you're hitting basspod. You can't miss Subtronics, Virtual Riot, or the Doctor P B2B Flux Pavilion B2B FuntCase madness. For the hardstyle, go to wasteland. Friday has GRAVEDGR and Rebekah, Saturday is wild with Lady Faith B2B LNY TNZ and Da Tweekaz, and Sunday has Sub Zero Project and Warface.

Friend: Okay, let's talk logistics. I'm thinking about camping. How does Camp EDC work?

Vet: It's a vibe, but remember: you have to buy the 4-night pass (Thursday–Sunday). They don't do single nights. You'll have free showers, water, and a General Store. Just a heads-up: you can't sleep in your car or a van because of the heat risk, and you can't drive your car into the actual camp area, though they help you move your gear.

Friend: Can I bring a stove to make some breakfast?

Vet: Yeah, small portable propane stoves and grills are fine, as long as the tanks are under 4 lbs per group. Just leave the butane devices and steel knives at home—they'll treat those as weapons.

Friend: What if I'm bringing an RV? Do they rent them?

Vet: Nope, you have to bring your own. They provide power hookups, water refills, and pump-outs for an extra fee. Also, if your friends are driving separately, you can grab extra parking passes for $95 plus taxes.

Friend: I'm a little nervous about the crowds and getting sick or overwhelmed. Who do I look for?

Vet: Look for the "Ground Control" crew. They wear purple shirts and carry light sabers—they're basically the festival's friendly guardians. If you need something more serious, there are medical tents everywhere.

Friend: Wait, if I go to medical, am I going to get in trouble?

Vet: Absolutely not. Insomniac is super clear about this: you will not get in trouble for seeking medical help. Whether it's just a blister or something serious, the medical staff provides care free of charge, no questions asked. Just flag down anyone in a Health & Safety vest!
