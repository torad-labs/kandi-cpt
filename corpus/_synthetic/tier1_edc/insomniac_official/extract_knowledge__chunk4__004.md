### Electric Daisy Carnival 2026 Performance Schedule

**Neon Garden**
The Neon Garden stage features a rotating lineup of electronic artists. Friday's performances include Adriatique, Peggy Gou, Eli Brown, and Joseph Capriati. Saturday's roster consists of Peggy Gou and KI/KI (B2B), DJ Gigola and MCR-T (B2B), mink, Luciano, and Silvie Loto. Sunday's performers are Frankie Bones, DJ Gigola (solo), Adiel, Indira Paganotto, and Klangkuenstler.

**Quantum Valley**
Dedicated to trance music, Quantum Valley hosts Cosmic Gate, Gareth Emery, and Paul van Dyk on Friday. Saturday's lineup includes Andrew Rayel, Astrix, and Paul Oakenfold. Above & Beyond performs a set at this stage in addition to their Kinetic Sunrise appearance. Tinlicker performs a DJ set on Sunday.

**Basspod**
Basspod focuses on bass music and features Subtronics, GRiZ B2B Wooli, Sub Focus, Peekaboo, Virtual Riot, Hybrid Minds, and Culture Shock. The stage also hosts an Adventure Club throwback set, Eptic B2B Space Laces, and a B2B set featuring Doctor P, Flux Pavilion, and FuntCase.

**Wasteland**
Wasteland is dedicated to hardstyle. Friday's performers are GRAVEDGR and Rebekah. Saturday's schedule includes Lady Faith B2B LNY TNZ, Da Tweekaz, Audiofreq B2B Code Black B2B Toneshifterz, and Rob Gee B2B Lenny Dee. Sunday's performers are Sub Zero Project and Warface.

### Camp EDC Operations

**Accommodations and Facilities**
Camp EDC provides 4-night camping passes covering Thursday through Sunday; single-night options are unavailable. Facilities include complimentary showers, portable toilets, drinking water, and a General Store. The site is staffed 24 hours a day by security, medical personnel, and Ground Control. ADA camping is available across all tiers. RV camping is permitted for those providing their own vehicles, with power hookups, water refills, and pump-outs available for an additional fee.

**Regulations and Safety**
Sleeping in cars or oversized vans is prohibited due to heat-related health risks. Vehicles are barred from the campground interior, though assistance is provided for transporting equipment from parking areas to campsites. Additional parking passes for separate group arrivals are priced at $95 plus taxes and fees.

**Prohibited and Permitted Items**
Small portable propane stoves and grills are permitted, with a limit of 4 lbs of propane per group. Butane devices, steel knives, and other items classified as weapons are prohibited.

### Health and Safety Services

**Ground Control and Medical Staff**
Ground Control is the Insomniac safety team, identified by purple shirts, fanny packs, and light sabers. Medical services are provided free of charge at designated tents throughout the festival grounds. Insomniac Health & Safety officers patrol the venue to provide assistance for symptoms ranging from nausea and blisters to critical medical emergencies. The organization maintains a policy that individuals will not face disciplinary action for seeking medical assistance.
