Q: What are Insomniac's official escalation criteria for taking a friend to medical?
A: The criteria are vomiting, seizures, pale skin color or bluish tinge, abnormal heart rate or breathing (too fast or slow), abnormal body temperature (too hot or cold), delusions or hallucinations or paranoia, pupils that don't react to changes in light, confusion or stupor (unresponsive but conscious), coma, or inability to be roused.

Q: How should intoxicated people be positioned when being cared for?
A: They should be kept awake and in an upright sitting position, not lying down.

Q: Which methods are identified as not helping someone suffering from alcohol poisoning?
A: Strong coffee, a cold shower, sleeping, and walking it off do not help.
