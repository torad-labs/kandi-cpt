Friend: I’m finally doing EDC 2026! I'm a bit nervous though—what are the actual hours? When do we even get in?

Vet: You're gonna love it. Friday, May 15th, kicks off with the opening ceremony at Cosmic Meadow at 5:00pm, but the rest of the grounds don't open until 7:00pm. Saturday and Sunday it's just a straight 7:00pm open. Everything runs until 5:30am the next morning.

Friend: Got it. If I forget something in the car or need a break, can I just pop out and come back in?

Vet: Absolutely not. No ins-and-outs allowed. Once you're in, you're in. The only exception is if you have a valid unscanned ticket; you can enter up until one hour before the party ends.

Friend: Yikes, okay. I'm looking at tickets now—can I just grab a one-day pass for Saturday?

Vet: Nope, it's 3-day passes only. The only way to get a single-day entry is if you're paying for VIP Bottle Service. Also, don't try to hoard them—there's a 4-ticket limit per person, and they only take cards, no cash.

Friend: I'm 20, so I'm good for entry, right? What about the fancy VIP areas?

Vet: You're good for the general grounds since you're 18+, but for the VIP Enhanced Experience or buying alcohol, you have to be 21.

Friend: I want to take some cool photos. Do I need to apply for a media pass or something for my camera?

Vet: Only if you're bringing professional gear, but honestly, professional cameras aren't permitted anyway. If you have a standard camera with a fixed lens or a handheld video device under 6 inches, you're golden—no pass needed. Just don't expect them to give you credentials if you're an independent photographer.

Friend: I'm planning on driving. Can I just crash in my car in the parking lot to save money on a hotel?

Vet: Bad idea. No overnight sleeping in the lot. If they catch you, they'll tow your car at your own expense.

Friend: Noted. I'm traveling with a friend from overseas—will their ID be a problem at the gate?

Vet: It depends. A foreign passport works on its own, or a foreign driver's license if they have a photocopy of their passport. Just a heads up: Consular IDs are not accepted. Also, if any ID is expired, you need DMV renewal papers or you aren't getting in.

Friend: This is a lot to remember. Is there a way to keep track of the lineup and where the stages are?

Vet: Just download the Insomniac app. It’s got the map, set times, and lineup updates. If you hate apps, you can check the website, social media, or just grab the printed guide they give you at the entrance. You can also sign up for email updates on the EDC Las Vegas site.

Friend: Thanks for the tips! Anything else I should know before I go?

Vet: Just the basics: use the buddy system, stay hydrated, and for the love of god, wear comfortable shoes. Most importantly, be kind to everyone. It's all about PLUR!
