Friend: I'm finally going to EDC 2026! I'm hyped but honestly a bit overwhelmed. Where should I go if I want that classic, ethereal trance vibe?

Vet: You gotta head to Quantum Valley. That's where Dreamstate takes over. It’s basically the hub for the Trance Fam, and they cover the whole spectrum—everything from those flowing, pretty melodies to the heavier psychedelic stuff.

Friend: Nice. I've seen some mentions of "Stereo Bloom" and "Bionic Jungle" too. What's the deal with those?

Vet: Stereo Bloom is curated by the Insomniac crew; it's a bit of a melting pot with house, tech house, and bass music. It's a great spot to catch a mix of huge names and new artists. Bionic Jungle is actually the newest addition to the festival. It's a smaller stage, but it's specifically focused on house music. Very "wild" vibe.

Friend: I'll definitely add those to the list. What about the Art Cars? Are they actually worth hunting for?

Vet: Absolutely. They roam the Speedway and broadcast all kinds of dance music. If you're exploring, keep an eye out for the BeatBox Art Car, Picnic Playtime, or the Metaphoenix. Sometimes you can even stumble upon a surprise DJ set on one of them.

Friend: That sounds sick. I'm looking at the Friday, May 15th lineup now... it's massive. Who are the absolute must-sees for that day?

Vet: Friday is stacked. If you want the big names, you've got Porter Robinson doing a DJ set, Fisher, and Peggy Gou. For the techno side, Charlotte de Witte and I Hate Models are going to be insane.

Friend: What about the trance and hard dance stuff on Friday? I know you love that.

Vet: Oh, Friday is perfect for that. You've got Paul van Dyk, Cosmic Gate, and Gareth Emery. If you want the faster stuff, look for Hixxy and Darren Porter.

Friend: I saw some interesting B2B sets on the list too. Any recommendations?

Vet: Definitely check out Walker & Royce B2B VNSSA or maybe salute B2B Chloé Caillet. Also, keep an eye out for Max Dean B2B Luke Dean—that should be a vibe.

Friend: I noticed a "Throwback Set" on the list. Who's doing that?

Vet: Adventure Club! They're doing a throwback set on Friday, so if you've been following them for a while, you can't miss that.

Friend: Last question—any underground picks for Friday that I might have missed?

Vet: For sure. Check out Eli Brown for some heavy techno, or if you want something a bit more experimental, look for Adriatique or Robert Hood. You'll have a blast!
