Friend: Yo! I'm finally locking in my plans for EDC 2026. I've been away for a few years—what's the deal with the new stage setups?

Vet: It’s wild this time around. You’ve gotta hit Quantum Valley if you want that proper Trance experience. Dreamstate is taking over the whole spot, doing everything from the ethereal stuff to the heavy psychedelic strains.

Friend: Nice, I definitely need some Trance. What about the house and bass side of things? Any new spots I should know about?

Vet: For sure. Check out Stereo Bloom; it’s curated by the Insomniac crew and covers a huge spectrum—house, tech house, bass, and a bunch of up-and-comers. But the real new addition is Bionic Jungle. It’s a smaller, high-energy stage specifically for house artists. Total vibe.

Friend: I love a good hidden gem. Are the art cars still doing their thing on the Speedway?

Vet: Always. They're basically mobile sound systems. Keep an eye out for the BeatBox, Picnic Playtime, and Metaphoenix cars. If you wander far enough, you might even stumble into a secret DJ set.

Friend: Let's talk lineup. I'm looking at Friday, May 15th. Who are the absolute must-sees?

Vet: Friday is stacked. If you want the heavy hitters, you've got Porter Robinson doing a DJ set, Charlotte de Witte, and Fisher. For the Trance fam, Paul van Dyk, Cosmic Gate, and Darude are all playing.

Friend: Wait, is Adventure Club playing a standard set?

Vet: Actually, they're doing a Throwback Set on Friday, which should be legendary. 

Friend: I'm feeling some bass music too. Who's hitting that side of the spectrum on Friday?

Vet: You've got a ton of options. Ray Volpe, Wooli, Kai Wachi, and RIOT are all on the Friday roster. Plus, ATLiens and Deathpact if you want that darker sound.

Friend: What about the underground or tech house side for Friday?

Vet: Peggy Gou is a huge draw, obviously. Also look for MEDUZA, Eli Brown, and the Walker & Royce B2B VNSSA set. If you're into the deeper stuff, Robert Hood is playing too.

Friend: Any weird B2Bs or unique collabs I should track?

Vet: Definitely check out Max Dean B2B Luke Dean, or the salute B2B Chloé Caillet set. Oh, and Tsu Nami is doing "Club Tsu," which should be a complete riot.

Vet: That's the EDC way! Just make sure you hit kineticFIELD and the other mains, but don't sleep on those specialty stages. See you in the dust!
