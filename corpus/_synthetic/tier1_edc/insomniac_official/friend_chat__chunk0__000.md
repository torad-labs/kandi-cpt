Friend: I’m finally doing it! I'm hitting EDC Las Vegas in 2026, but I have no idea where to start. When exactly is it happening?

Vet: Let's gooo! Mark your calendar for Friday, May 15th through Sunday, May 17th. It's held at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway.

Friend: Nice. Do I need to be there early on Friday?

Vet: Definitely. The opening ceremony happens at Cosmic Meadow at 5:00pm on Friday. The rest of the grounds generally open from 7:00pm to 5:30am each night.

Friend: Got it. I'm a bit nervous about the scale of it. How many stages are we talking about?

Vet: There are nine main stages, plus a bunch of art cars and smaller setups. If you want the massive headliners, you'll spend a lot of time at kineticFIELD—it can hold like 70,000 people.

Friend: I'm mostly into the heavier stuff, though. Where do I go for that?

Vet: For the dubstep and trap fans, Basspod is your pilgrimage site. But if you want the really fast, relentless hardstyle and hardcore stuff, you need to head straight to Wasteland.

Friend: What about the moodier side of things? I love deep house and techno.

Vet: That’s where Neon Garden comes in. It's curated by Factory 93, so it's all about that immersive, minimalist techno vibe.

Friend: I've heard the desert is brutal. Any tips for surviving the heat?

Vet: It's no joke. Wear comfortable sneakers—do not wear sandals or you'll regret it. Also, look for the water-droplet symbols on the map; those are the free Electrolit refill stations. Stay hydrated!

Friend: Quick logistics question: can I pop out for a bit to grab food or a break and then go back in?

Vet: Nope. No ins-and-outs allowed. Once you're in for the night, you're in. Just make sure you've got everything you need before you scan your wristband.

Friend: Do I need to bring a wallet full of cash?

Vet: Leave the cash at home. It's a completely cashless event. They take credit, debit, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay, and Android Pay. If you only have cash, you can exchange it for a prepaid card on-site.

Friend: I'm thinking about camping to save money. Is that an option?

Vet: Yeah, you can get a 4-night camping pass for Camp EDC. It's way easier than dealing with Ubers from the city.

Friend: Last thing—I'm bringing a friend who is 19. Is there any part of the festival they can't go to?

Vet: They'll be fine for the general admission areas since the minimum age is 18. However, the VIP Enhanced Experience and the Bottle Service areas are strictly 21-plus.
