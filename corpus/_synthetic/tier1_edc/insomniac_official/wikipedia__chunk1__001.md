# Electric Daisy Carnival 2026

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival produced by Insomniac Events. The 2026 edition is scheduled to take place starting May 15, featuring a variety of themed stages, art installations, and an international lineup of artists.

## Stages and Programming

The festival utilizes several themed environments to categorize different subgenres of electronic music:

*   **dreamstate**: Located at "Quantum Valley," this stage is dedicated to the trance genre, ranging from ethereal melodies to psychedelic trance.
*   **stereoBloom**: Curated by Insomniac Music Group, this stage focuses on a diverse spectrum of sounds, including house, tech house, and bass music, featuring both established artists and emerging talent.
*   **bionicJungle**: A specialized stage focused on house music.

## Art Installations and Mobile Sound

The festival grounds feature various mobile sound systems known as Art Cars. These vehicles roam the Speedway and broadcast diverse forms of dance music, occasionally hosting impromptu DJ sets. Notable examples include the BeatBox Art Car, Picnic Playtime Art Car, and Metaphoenix.
