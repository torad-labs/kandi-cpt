# Strategy: friend_chat
# Audience: a parent helping their kid
# Chunk: 5

Friend: I'm helping my kid get ready for EDC 2026, but I'm a bit nervous. What's the deal with water? Can they just bring a case of bottles?

Vet: Definitely not. You can't bring sealed water in. Tell them to bring an empty CamelBak or a reusable bottle. There are free refill stations by Electrolit all over the place—just look for the water droplet on the map. The water is UV-sterilized, though it might be a bit warm since that's actually easier on the stomach.

Friend: Got it. What about food? Is it just overpriced hot dogs, or can they actually eat well?

Vet: There are tons of stands and even roaming vendors with snacks. They have plenty of vegan and vegetarian options, too. Just a heads up: it’s a completely cashless event, so they’ll need a card, Apple Pay, or Android Pay.

Friend: I'm really worried about the "party" aspect. If their friend is acting too drunk or messed up, when do they stop trying to "walk it off" and actually go to medical?

Vet: This is the most important part. If someone is just tipsy, keep them upright and awake—don't let them lie down, and don't waste time with coffee or cold showers. But if you see vomiting, seizures, pale or bluish skin, or if they're hallucinating or unresponsive, go straight to medical. If their pupils aren't reacting to light or their breathing is weird, that's an immediate escalation.

Friend: That's scary. Should they just stay in one big group?

Vet: Absolutely. Use the buddy system. I always tell people to pick a concrete meeting point—like the entrance to kineticFIELD or a specific landmark—because it's so easy to get separated. They should never leave the show unless the whole crew is accounted for.

Friend: What if they forget something essential, like flip-flops or eye drops? Is there a store there?

Vet: Yeah, the Insomniac General Shop is a lifesaver. They sell the basics like ring pops and flip-flops. If they want the cool gear, the Insomniac Shop has the 2026 apparel and artist collections.

Friend: My kid's phone will definitely die. Is there a way to charge it without lugging around a massive power bank?

Vet: They can rent lockers! They have unlimited access and a universal charger built right in. If they don't want a locker, there are charging stations scattered around the venue.

Friend: And if they lose their wallet or phone, where do they even go?

Vet: Just head to the Info Booths. They're all over the map and handle the Lost & Found services during and between the show days.

Friend: Last question—is there any alcohol restriction I should warn them about?

Vet: Just the law. They have to be 21+ with a valid ID to hit the bars. Just remind them that binge drinking is the most dangerous way to do it—the faster you drink, the higher the blood alcohol hits. Safety first!
