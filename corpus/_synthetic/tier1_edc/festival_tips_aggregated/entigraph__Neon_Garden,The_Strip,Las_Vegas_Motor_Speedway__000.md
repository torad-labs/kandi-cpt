EDC Las Vegas 2026 takes place at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. For attendees staying on The Strip, the EDC shuttle provides transportation to and from the venue via pickups at 10 specified hotels. This shuttle service is accessed using a pre-purchased shuttle bracelet, which serves as a guaranteed ride back to the hotel.

Within the festival grounds, attendees navigate various areas, including the Neon Garden and the Wasteland. Due to unreliable or nonexistent cell service at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, visitors are advised to establish fixed meeting spots and scheduled check-in times every 1-2 hours.
