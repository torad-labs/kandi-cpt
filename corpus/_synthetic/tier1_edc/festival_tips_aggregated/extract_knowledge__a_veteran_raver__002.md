### Venue and Navigation
Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is hosted at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. The festival footprint spans the infield and the perimeter of the NASCAR track. The primary entrance leads directly to Kinetic Field, the main stage characterized by a large mascot face/owl. Other stages radiate from this central point.

The venue's layout includes several distinct stages:
*   **Kinetic Field:** Main stage with iconic mascot visuals.
*   **Cosmic Meadow:** Open-grounds stage utilized for the opening ceremony.
*   **Circuit Grounds:** Defined by LED walls and pyrotechnic fire plumes.
*   **Neon Garden:** A darker, enclosed environment.
*   **Wasteland:** A post-apocalyptic themed area.

Fixed navigational landmarks include the raised bleachers (which indicate the venue exit), the lockers area, merchant stores, and info booths. The Ferris wheel and carnival rides are located in "Downtown EDC" between the main stages. The "Daisy" art installation is mobile and is not recommended as a meeting point.

### Logistics and Gear
Due to the desert environment, temperatures fluctuate significantly, often dropping to the 50s Fahrenheit by 04:00. Recommended attire includes layered clothing, broken-in footwear, and UV protection (hats, shades, and sunscreen) for post-dawn exposure.

Essential utility items for the Speedway environment include:
*   **Health and Hygiene:** High-fidelity earplugs, wet wipes, anti-chafe lubricants (e.g., BodyGlide), and sealed lip balm to counteract wind desiccation.
*   **Foot Care:** Gel insoles, blister pads, bandages, and spare socks.
*   **Hardware:** Power banks, hand fans for high-density crowds at Kinetic Field, and phone cases with wrist lanyards.
*   **Documentation:** Official ID and a separate photocopy of said ID.

### Operational Strategies
Effective attendance requires pre-festival electrolyte loading and a structured pacing strategy. Veteran recommendations suggest a sober first night and an exit time of 04:30 or earlier to avoid peak congestion. 

Communication is hindered by poor cellular service; therefore, attendees are advised to utilize the Insomniac app for maps and set times, and to establish fixed meeting points. Ground Control staff (identifiable by purple shirts) provide directional assistance, while medical services are free and confidential. Social customs include the exchange of "kandi" (beaded bracelets) and the use of totems as visual markers for groups.
