**EDC Las Vegas 2026: Essentials & Navigation**

**Packing List:** Broken-in sneakers, blister pads, bandages, gel insoles, extra socks, hi-fi earplugs, wet wipes, sunscreen, sealed ChapStick, hat/shades, layered clothing (nights drop to 50s F), festival-sized fanny pack/daybag, power bank, hand fan, anti-chafe product (BodyGlide, Squirrel's Nut Butter), electrolyte loading, cash, ID photocopy, phone case with wrist lanyard, optional totems/kandi.

**Wayfinding (Las Vegas Motor Speedway):**
*   **kineticFIELD:** Main stage; giant face/owl mascot.
*   **cosmicMEADOW:** Open-grounds; opening ceremony site.
*   **circuitGROUNDS:** LED walls and fire plumes.
*   **neonGARDEN:** Darker, enclosed.
*   **wasteLAND:** Post-apocalyptic aesthetic.
*   **Other areas:** Downtown EDC (Ferris wheel/rides) sits between main stages.
*   **Reference Points:** Exit via raised bleachers. Use Lockers, merchant store, info booths, or stage entrances for meetings; avoid the mobile Daisy installation.

**Pro Tips:**
*   **Logistics:** Use Insomniac app for maps/sets. Seek Ground Control (purple shirts) or free Medical services.
*   **Strategy:** Prioritize sets; avoid seeing everything. Leave by 4:30am. Plan for poor cell service.
*   **Health:** Prioritize hydration/electrolytes and earplugs.
*   **Culture:** Kandi trading for friendship; sunrise sets (ideally night 2) are recommended.
