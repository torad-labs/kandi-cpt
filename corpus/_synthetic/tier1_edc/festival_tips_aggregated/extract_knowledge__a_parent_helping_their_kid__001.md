### Communication and Coordination
Cellular service at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway is unreliable, often resulting in significant delays for SMS messages and inconsistent functionality for location-sharing apps like Snapchat. Attendees are advised to establish a fixed, unmistakable meeting point for the duration of the event. The locker area is recommended due to its lighting and contained layout. Scheduled check-ins should occur every one to two hours.

### Transportation
The EDC shuttle service provides transport between the venue and ten designated hotels on the Las Vegas Strip. Access requires a pre-purchased shuttle bracelet, which serves as the guaranteed return transport. To avoid peak congestion and shuttle wait times of one to two hours, departures are recommended before 4:30 AM.

### Venue Navigation and Amenities
The Kinetic Field hosts "sunrise sets," typically featuring artists such as Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren. Due to Monday morning traffic in Las Vegas, Night 2 is the preferred window for these sets over Night 3. Regarding sanitation, restroom lines are shortest at the rear of the porta-potty arrays. Because provided toilet paper and hand sanitizer typically deplete by the midpoint of the first night, guests should bring their own supplies and wet wipes.

### Social Conduct and Etiquette
The event follows a code of conduct known as PLUR (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect). Common social practices include the trading of "kandi" (beaded bracelets) and the complimenting of costumes. Attendees are expected to maintain crowd flow by avoiding sitting in walkways and monitoring the well-being of other guests.
