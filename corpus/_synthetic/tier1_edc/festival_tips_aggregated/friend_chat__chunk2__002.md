Friend: I'm finally doing EDC 2026! I'm hyped but low-key terrified of the logistics. What's the deal with the shoes? Can I just buy a cute new pair for the outfits?

Vet: Absolutely not. That is the #1 mistake people make. You need broken-in sneakers you've worn for miles. If you wear new shoes, you're dead by midnight. Also, pack blister pads, gel insoles, and extra socks in your bag. Trust me.

Friend: Good call. I'll stick to my old kicks. What else should I pack for the desert? I know it's hot, but is that it?

Vet: It's a trap! The sun is brutal post-dawn, so bring sunscreen, a hat, and shades. But by 4am, it drops into the 50s, so bring layered clothing. Also, bring a sealed ChapStick—the Nevada wind literally eats your lips alive. And definitely grab some BodyGlide or Squirrel's Nut Butter for your thighs and underarms, or the chafing will ruin your entire weekend.

Friend: Yikes, I didn't think about the cold or the chafing. Anything else for the "survival kit"?

Vet: High-fidelity earplugs are a must. Also, a power bank, wet wipes, and a hand fan—you'll need that when the crowd peaks at kineticFIELD. For your gear, use a festival-approved small fanny pack and a phone case with a wrist lanyard so you don't drop your phone in the dirt. Oh, and load up on electrolytes the day before you go.

Friend: Got it. Now, the map. I've seen it's at the Speedway, but how do I actually find my way around without getting lost?

Vet: The layout is basically the infield and outside of the NASCAR track. When you enter, kineticFIELD is straight ahead—it's the iconic main stage with the giant face. From there, the other stages fan out. If you get totally turned around, look for the raised bleachers; that's your North Star for finding the exit.

Friend: Okay, so kineticFIELD is the landmark. What about the other stages? How do I tell them apart?

Vet: They all have a vibe. circuitGrounds has the massive LED walls and fire plumes. neonGarden is darker and more enclosed, and wasteland looks like a post-apocalyptic movie. cosmicMeadow is the open-grounds spot where the opening ceremony happens.

Friend: If I lose my group, should I just tell them to meet me at the Daisy?

Vet: No! Never use the Daisy as a meeting point because it moves through the night. Use the Lockers area, the merchant store, or a specific stage entrance. And remember, cell service is terrible, so have a plan. If you're really stuck, look for the Ground Control crew in the purple shirts—they're awesome.

Friend: I really want to see the fireworks and the sunrise. Where's the best spot for that?

Vet: Head to Downtown EDC, which is between the main stages. The Ferris wheel is the move—time it right and you get a bird's-eye view of the fireworks and the sunrise. I'd suggest committing to the sunrise set on Night 2.

Friend: That sounds amazing. Any final "pro tips" so I don't look like a total rookie?

Vet: Don't try to see every single act; pick your priorities or you'll burn out. I actually recommend staying sober on Night 1 just to pace yourself. Also, bring a photocopy of your ID separate from the real one, some cash for prepaid card exchanges, and some kandi to trade if you want to make friends. Just remember: medical is free and no-questions-asked if you need them, so don't be afraid to ask for help!
