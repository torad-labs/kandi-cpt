**EDC Las Vegas 2026 Veteran Summary**

**Footwear & Physicality:** Wear broken-in sneakers; avoid sandals/heels. Expect 10-20 miles/day on rough concrete/unpaved surfaces. Bring blister pads, bandages, gel insoles, and extra socks. 

**Pacing & Recovery:** Treat the three 10-hour nights as a marathon. Consider sobriety Night 1 for acclimation; prioritize sleep between nights. Recommended travel window: Arrive Thursday, depart Tuesday.

**Health & Safety:** Drink 16–27 oz of fluid per hour to avoid dehydration and hyponatremia. Combine water with electrolytes (Powerade, Gatorade, Pedialyte, electrolyte tablets, or free Electrolit sticks). Use free water stations (water-droplet symbol) with empty CamelBaks or Insomniac bottles. Emergency bottled water is available at first aid tents. Use high-fidelity earplugs (Etymotic, EARasers, Loop) to prevent tinnitus.

**Logistics:** With 240+ artists across 9 main stages—kineticFIELD, cosmicMEADOW, circuitGROUNDS, neonGARDEN, bassPOD, wasteLAND, quantumVALLEY, stereoBLOOM, bionicJUNGLE—and 8 secondary venues, seeing every set is impossible. Prioritize 3-5 must-see sets per night. Use a buddy system with designated meeting points.
