Friend: Yo, I’m finally locking in my crew for EDC 2026. I know you've done this a million times—what are the biggest rookie mistakes I need to make sure we avoid?

Vet: First thing: don't blow your load on Friday. I've seen way too many people go absolutely feral on night one and then spend Saturday and Sunday basically comatose in their hotel. Pace yourself.

Friend: Fair. I'm already planning my fits. I’ve got some killer black streetwear planned for the kineticFIELD vibe. That work?

Vet: Absolute disaster. It’s 95°F in the desert, man. You’ll bake in all black. Go for breathable, layered clothing. Also, for the love of god, do not wear brand new sneakers or heels. Break your shoes in for a month before we hit the Speedway, or you'll be blistered by midnight.

Friend: Noted. I'll stick to my beat-up kicks. What about hydration? Just chug water, right?

Vet: Wrong. Pure water is a trap—you'll flush your salts and bonk hard. You need electrolytes. Bring Liquid IV or something similar. Also, don't skip breakfast when you wake up late morning; your body needs those calories to recover before you head back to circuitGROUNDS.

Friend: I'll stock up on electrolytes. I'm planning to hit like 15 sets a night to get my money's worth. You think that's doable?

Vet: You’ll end up seeing nothing properly. Pick 3 "must-sees" a night—like a headliner at kineticFIELD or a deep dive at neonGARDEN—and let the rest be spontaneous. If you try to sprint between 15 stages, you'll just be stressed and exhausted.

Friend: True. I'll keep the schedule loose. We’re just gonna use our phones to find each other if we get split up, right?

Vet: Huge mistake. Cell service is a lottery at the Speedway. Do not rely on your phone. Also, don't say "let's just meet at the Daisy." Everyone says that, and the Daisy is a nightmare for finding people. Pick a concrete, obscure landmark and stick to it.

Friend: Damn, I didn't think about the service. What else? Any "must-haves" in the bag?

Vet: High-fidelity earplugs. If you forget them, you're risking a week of tinnitus. Also, if you're bringing gear, test your stuff. Grab reagent kits and fentanyl strips from DanceSafe before we leave. It's legal in most states and it's the only way to be safe.

Friend: Got it. We’re planning a massive pre-game in Vegas on Wednesday and Thursday to get hyped. That's cool?

Vet: That's how you burn out before the music even starts. Keep the Vegas strip partying light. Save that energy for the actual festival.

Friend: Last question: what time should we head out after the closing set?

Vet: Don't be the person leaving at 5:30 AM. You'll get slammed in a two-hour shuttle line. Either leave early or settle in and wait it out. Just don't expect a quick exit.
