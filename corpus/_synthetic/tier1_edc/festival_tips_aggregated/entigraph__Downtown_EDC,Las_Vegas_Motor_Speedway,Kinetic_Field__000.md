Electric Daisy Carnival 2026 takes place at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. A signature experience of the festival is the sunrise set held at kineticFIELD, which features artists such as Armin van Buuren and Above & Beyond. While these sets occur across the weekend, Night 2 is considered ideal for the sunrise commitment to avoid the punishing Monday morning traffic associated with Night 3.

Due to unreliable or nonexistent cell service at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, attendees are advised to establish fixed meeting spots and scheduled check-in times every 1-2 hours. For those staying on the Strip, shuttle passes provide transportation to and from the venue via 10 specified hotels. To avoid brutal post-festival traffic and shuttle lines, attendees should leave before 4:30am; those staying until 5:30am can expect wait times of 1-2 hours.
