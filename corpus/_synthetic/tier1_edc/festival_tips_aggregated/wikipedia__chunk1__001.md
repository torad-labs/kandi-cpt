# Electric Daisy Carnival (Attendee Logistics)

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a large-scale electronic dance music festival produced by Insomniac Events. Due to the scale of the venue and the volume of attendees, specific logistical strategies are employed by participants to navigate the environment, manage transportation, and adhere to community norms.

### Overview
The festival is characterized by a culture of inclusivity guided by the philosophy of **PLUR** (Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect). This code of conduct manifests in community behaviors such as the trading of "kandi" (handmade beaded bracelets), the wearing of elaborate costumes, and a general ethos of mutual aid and welcoming strangers.

### Stages and Programming
The festival features multiple thematic stages, including the **kineticField**. A signature element of the event is the "sunrise set," where headlining artists (historically including acts such as Above & Beyond and Armin van Buuren) perform as the sun rises. Attendees frequently schedule these sets for the second night of the festival to avoid the significant increase in Las Vegas traffic associated with Monday morning departures following the final night.

### Logistics and Transportation
#### Shuttle Services
For attendees staying within the Las Vegas Strip, Insomniac provides a dedicated shuttle service. 
* **Access:** Shuttle passes are purchased in advance and verified via a wristband.
* **Operations:** Shuttles operate continuously throughout the event, with pickup and drop-off points at ten designated hotels along the Strip.
* **Departure:** Peak congestion occurs after 4:30 AM. Attendees departing after the festival concludes at 5:30 AM typically encounter wait times of one to two hours for shuttle boarding.

#### Communication and Navigation
Cellular service at the venue is frequently unreliable or nonexistent, often resulting in delayed SMS delivery and inconsistent functionality of location-sharing applications like Snapchat. To mitigate this, attendees are advised to:
* Establish a fixed, static meeting point (e.g., the locker area) rather than relying on mobile art installations, such as the "Daisy," which may change position.
* Schedule recurring meeting times every one to two hours.
* Maintain a consistent meeting location across all nights of the festival.

#### Sanitation
Restroom facilities consist of large arrays of portable toilets. Shorter wait times are typically found at the rear of these arrays. Because provided toilet paper and hand sanitizer supplies often deplete early in the event, attendees frequently bring personal supplies, including wet wipes.

### Safety and Conduct
Safety protocols emphasize the importance of the shuttle wristband as a guaranteed means of returning to lodging. In the event of separation from a group, attendees are encouraged to utilize the welcoming nature of the community. 

Crowd etiquette focuses on maintaining "flow paths" by avoiding sitting in high-traffic walkways. Attendees are encouraged to monitor the well-being of others and provide assistance to those appearing lost or unwell.
