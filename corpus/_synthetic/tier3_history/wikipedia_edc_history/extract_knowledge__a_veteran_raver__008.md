**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) 2026**

Electric Daisy Carnival is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival produced by Insomniac Events. Originally established in 1996 in California, the event has evolved from localized raves into a global franchise with iterations in Las Vegas, Mexico, Orlando, and other international territories.

**Las Vegas Operations**
The flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, is hosted at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. The festival is characterized by high-budget production, including a main stage that has historically cost approximately $3 million to construct. Recent iterations have featured enhanced drone shows (exceeding 600 drones in 2021) and immersive stage layouts. The 2024 event saw an attendance of 525,000 people and featured debut performances by artists such as Four Tet and Peggy Gou.

**Global Expansion and Diversification**
Insomniac has expanded the EDC brand into several regional and themed events:
*   **EDC Mexico:** A recurring international edition established in 2014.
*   **EDC Orlando:** A North American regional event held in Florida.
*   **EDSea:** A festival cruise debut in 2023, with subsequent 2024 lineups featuring Subtronics and Sara Landry.
*   **International Debuts:** The brand has expanded into Asia, including EDC Japan, EDC China (which faced a 2024 cancellation due to Typhoon Bebinica), and the 2025 debut of EDC Thailand.

**Operational History and Safety**
The festival's trajectory has been marked by significant logistical shifts. After conflicts at the Los Angeles Coliseum, the event relocated to Las Vegas in 2011. The organization has faced challenges including extreme weather—resulting in a second-night shutdown in 2012 due to high winds and an evacuation in 2019—as well as pandemic-related cancellations in 2020. To mitigate heat-related risks, Insomniac implemented a date change to May in 2018 and introduced air-conditioned camping options.
