Q: When did Rotella announce his radio show, Night Owl Radio, on SiriusXM's Electric Area?
A: August 2015.

Q: Which station replaced Electric Area in 2018?
A: Diplo's Revolution.

Q: When did Insomniac partner with Dash Radio as its flagship station?
A: May 2019.

Q: In what month and year did Insomniac acquire a controlling stake in Soundslinger, LLC?
A: June 2019.

Q: In what month and year did Insomniac acquire a majority stake in Miami's Club Space nightclub?
A: July 2019.

Q: Under what banner did Insomniac stream previous festival sets on YouTube and Twitch during the COVID-19 pandemic?
A: Insomniac Rewind.

Q: Where were the "Park 'N Rave" drive-in concerts held in late-2020?
A: NOS Events Center in San Bernardino.

Q: Where was the "Electric Mile" drive-through audiovisual experience located?
A: Santa Anita Park.

Q: What are the focus genres for the Lost in Dreams record label and festival announced in February 2021?
A: Future bass and melodic dubstep.

Q: What is the name of the bass music event held at The Vanguard?
A: Abduction.

Q: Who did Insomniac partner with in March 2022 to develop a persistent online world for virtual concerts?
A: Unity Technologies.

Q: When did Soundslinger state that Insomniac was no longer involved in the Okeechobee festival?
A: April 2025.

Q: Which record label was created in May 2014 in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M?
A: Insomniac Records.

Q: Who was the first artist signed to Insomniac Records?
A: Arty.

Q: When was the Insomniac Music Group formed to provide A&R and distribution services?
A: December 2018.

Q: Which Insomniac event is the longest running festival brand and was the first Insomniac public event?
A: Nocturnal Wonderland.

Q: Which Electric Daisy Carnival locations have been discontinued?
A: UK, India, Puerto Rico, and Brazil.
