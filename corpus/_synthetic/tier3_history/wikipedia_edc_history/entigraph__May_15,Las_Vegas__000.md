EDC Las Vegas is the flagship electronic dance music festival organized by the promoter and distributor Insomniac. The event is held annually in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway.

The next scheduled event for EDC Las Vegas is set to take place from May 15 to May 17, 2026, in Las Vegas.
