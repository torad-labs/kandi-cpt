Friend: I'm thinking about hitting up EDC, but I have no idea what to expect. Does it only happen in Vegas?

Vet: Definitely not! While the Las Vegas Motor Speedway is the big one, it's a global brand. Depending on the year, you can find them in Mexico City at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez, Orlando at Tinker Field, or even internationally in places like the UK, India, Japan, and Korea.

Friend: I've heard Vegas is brutal. When is the best time to go?

Vet: You have to be careful. Back in 2016 and 2017, it was held in June, and the heat was dangerous—medical calls actually doubled on opening night in 2017. Because of that, they shifted the schedule starting in 2018 to May so it's relatively cooler.

Friend: That's a huge relief. Is it hard to get in and out of the venue?

Vet: It can be. To fight the traffic congestion, they started opening the gates earlier and introduced on-site camping, including RV parking and rentable campers. Trust me, having a spot to crash is a game changer.

Friend: I saw a clip of Marshmello doing something wild at a show. Was that at EDC?

Vet: Yeah! In 2016, he did this crazy gimmick where he "removed" his helmet, but it was actually Tiësto underneath. He's a staple; he even played the main stage at EDC Mexico in 2018.

Friend: What about the stages? Are they always open?

Vet: Usually, but the weather is the boss. In 2019, high winds forced some stage closures. Some people panicked because the screens told them to "take cover in your car," and they actually left the festival thinking it was over.

Friend: Wait, if they left because of a mistake, could they just go back in?

Vet: Nope. That's the scary part for first-timers: they have a strict no-reentry policy. If you leave the grounds, you aren't getting back in, regardless of the reason.

Friend: Yikes. Are there any other versions of the festival that are shorter or longer?

Vet: For sure. Most of the international ones, like EDC Japan at Zozo Marine Stadium or EDC China in Shanghai, are usually two-day events. But EDC Orlando actually expanded from two days to three days back in 2019.

Friend: If I go to the Vegas one, should I be worried about the crowd size?

Vet: It's massive. In 2018 alone, about 411,400 people attended over the three days. Just keep your group together and stay hydrated so you don't end up as one of the medical calls.

Friend: I'm definitely leaning toward the May dates then. Which stage should I hit first?

Vet: You can't go wrong starting at kineticFIELD. Just remember to keep an eye on the weather and stay inside the gates!
