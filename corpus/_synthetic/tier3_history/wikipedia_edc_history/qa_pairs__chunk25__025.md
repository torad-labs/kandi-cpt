Q: Who is the promoter and organizer of Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) in Las Vegas?
A: Insomniac.

Q: Who founded Insomniac?
A: Pasquale Rotella.

Q: In what year was Insomniac founded?
A: 1993.

Q: Where is the headquarters of Insomniac located?
A: Beverly Hills, California, United States.

Q: Which electronic music events does Insomniac run in addition to EDC?
A: Beyond Wonderland and Nocturnal Wonderland.

Q: Which Insomniac brands are dedicated to hardstyle and trance events?
A: Basscon is for hardstyle events and Dreamstate is for trance events.

Q: Which three Los Angeles venues are managed by Insomniac?
A: Create, Exchange L.A., and Factory 93.

Q: Which Miami venue did Insomniac acquire in 2019?
A: Club Space.

Q: Which Washington, D.C. nightclubs and operator did Insomniac acquire in 2020?
A: Echostage, Soundcheck, and their operator Club Glow.

Q: What is the name of the record label introduced by Insomniac in 2014?
A: Insomniac Records.
