**Insomniac**
Insomniac is a Beverly Hills-based electronic music event promoter and music distributor founded in 1993 by Pasquale Rotella. The organization operates as a private entity specializing in large-scale music festivals and nightclub management. Its flagship event is the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC), held annually in Las Vegas.

**Event Portfolio and Branding**
The company manages a diverse portfolio of genre-specific event brands:
*   **General EDM:** Beyond Wonderland and Nocturnal Wonderland.
*   **Drum and Bass/Dubstep:** Bassrush.
*   **Hardstyle:** Basscon.
*   **Trance:** Dreamstate.
*   **Additional Properties:** Hard and Audiotistic Music Festival.

**Venue Operations and Distribution**
Insomniac operates several nightclubs across the United States. In Los Angeles, it manages Exchange L.A., Factory 93, and Create (via partnership with SBE). Its East Coast acquisitions include Miami's Club Space (2019) and Washington, D.C.'s Echostage and Soundcheck (2020), the latter of which are managed through the subsidiary Club Glow.

The organization's recording operations began in 2014 with the launch of Insomniac Records in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M. In 2018, the company transitioned its A&R and distribution to an in-house model under the Insomniac Music Group.

**Corporate Partnerships**
In June 2013, Insomniac entered a creative partnership with Live Nation to utilize the latter's resources while retaining internal creative control. Additionally, the company partnered with Dick Clark Productions to establish a dance music awards program.
