The Las Vegas Motor Speedway (LVMS), which opened on September 13, 1996, serves as the host venue for the flagship iteration of the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC). The facility transitioned to hosting the festival in 2011 following the event's relocation from Los Angeles.

The venue's infrastructure includes a primary racing surface and a 0.375-mile oval known as the "Bullring." Following a renovation project initiated in 2006, the track's capacity was increased to 141,000, with banking in the turns adjusted to 20 degrees and the racing surface narrowed to 50 feet.
