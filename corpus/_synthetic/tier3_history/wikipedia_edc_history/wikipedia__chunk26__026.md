# Insomniac Events

**Insomniac Events** is an electronic dance music (EDM) event production company and promoter. The organization operates a diverse portfolio of music festivals, nightclub holdings, and digital media ventures.

## Media and Broadcasting
Beginning in August 2015, founder Pasquale Rotella launched *Night Owl Radio* on the SiriusXM channel Electric Area. Following the replacement of Electric Area by *Diplo's Revolution* in 2018, the company launched Insomniac Radio on SiriusXM. In May 2019, Insomniac established Dash Radio as its flagship broadcasting station.

## Corporate Acquisitions and Partnerships
Insomniac has expanded its footprint through strategic acquisitions of venue and festival assets. In July 2019, the company acquired a majority stake in the Miami-based nightclub Club Space. In June 2019, Insomniac acquired a controlling interest in Soundslinger, LLC, the organizing body of the Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival. Following a one-year hiatus, the festival returned in 2020 featuring production elements integrated from other Insomniac brands. In April 2025, Soundslinger announced that Insomniac was no longer involved with the Okeechobee festival.

In March 2022, the company partnered with Unity Technologies to develop a persistent virtual world designed for digital concert experiences.

## Insomniac Music Group
In May 2014, Insomniac launched Insomniac Records in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M, signing Arty as its inaugural artist, followed by Bingo Players and Chris Lake. 

In December 2018, the company formed the Insomniac Music Group to provide A&R and distribution services for its internal imprints and external electronic labels. The group's portfolio includes:
* Basscon Records
* Bassrush Records
* Discovery Project
* Dreamstate Records
* Gud Vibrations
* HARD Recordings
* In / Rotation
* Lost in Dreams
* What To Do

## Event Portfolio

### Active Brands
Insomniac produces a wide array of festival brands, including the long-running Nocturnal Wonderland—the company's first public event—and the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC), which operates in Las Vegas, Mexico, Orlando, and Portugal. Other active brands include:
* Apocalypse: Zombieland
* Audiotistic Music Festival
* Beyond Wonderland
* Boo Seattle
* Countdown New Year’s Eve
* Daytrip
* Dreamstate
* Electric Forest (operated in collaboration with Madison House under Anschutz Entertainment Group)
* Escape Halloween (formerly Escape From Wonderland)
* Forbidden Kingdom
* HARD
* EDSea
* Project GLOW

### New Developments
In February 2021, the company introduced several new brands, including Abduction—a bass music event held at The Vanguard—and Lost in Dreams, a record label and festival focused on melodic dubstep and future bass.

## Pandemic Response and Logistics
During the COVID-19 pandemic, the company shifted to digital and socially distanced formats after the postponement of all in-person festivals. 

* **Digital Programming:** Insomniac launched "Insomniac Rewind" on YouTube and Twitch, streaming archived sets from previous festivals alongside virtual live events.
* **Alternative Venues:** In late 2020, the company implemented a residency of "Park 'N Rave" drive-in concerts at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino.
* **Audiovisual Experiences:** The "Electric Mile" was established at Santa Anita Park, featuring a drive-through experience with themed "worlds" based on the company's primary festival brands.
