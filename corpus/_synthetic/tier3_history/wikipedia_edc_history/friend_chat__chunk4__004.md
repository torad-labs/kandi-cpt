Friend: I'm trying to dig into some old EDC history. For the 2016 Las Vegas run, do you remember when it happened and where?

Vet: Yeah, that was June 17–19 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. It was a wild year—I remember the big gimmick where Marshmello took off his helmet and it was actually Tiësto underneath.

Friend: No way! Did they do other festivals that year too?

Vet: Tons. EDC Mexico hit the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez on Feb 27–28, EDC New York was at Citi Field on May 14–15, and the UK event was in Milton Keynes on July 9. They also did Orlando at Tinker Field on Nov 4–5 and India at the Buddh International Circuit on Nov 12–13.

Friend: What about 2017? Did it stay at the Speedway in Vegas?

Vet: It did, but it was brutal. Over 135,000 people showed up opening night, and the heat was insane. Medical calls doubled from the year before, and there was a tragic death involving MDMA and TFMPP combined with the heat. On the brighter side, Mexico City returned to Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez on Feb 25–26 and Orlando stayed at Tinker Field for Nov 10–11. Sadly, EDC UK was cancelled that year.

Friend: I heard they changed the dates for Vegas in 2018 to avoid the heat. Is that true?

Vet: Exactly. They moved it to May 18–20 to keep things cooler. They also started on-site camping with RVs and rentable campers, plus earlier opening times to kill the traffic. It worked—about 411,400 people attended over those three days.

Friend: Were there any other cool locations in 2018?

Vet: Definitely. They debuted EDC China in Shanghai at the International Music Park on April 29–30. They also had EDC Japan at Zozo Marine Stadium and Makuhari Beach Park on May 12–13, though heavy rains messed with the equipment. Mexico City was also planned for Feb 24–25.

Friend: I remember hearing something about a chaotic second day in Vegas during 2019. What happened?

Vet: Total mess. High winds forced some stage closures at the Speedway on May 18. The screens told people to "take cover in your car," so a bunch of people thought the whole festival was cancelled and left. Because of the strict no-reentry policy, they couldn't get back in once they realized it was just a partial closure. Plus, the cops made 65 arrests over the weekend.

Friend: That sounds stressful. Did any other cities expand in 2019?

Vet: Yeah, EDC Orlando actually bumped up to 3 days instead of the usual 2, staying over at Tinker Field. Mexico City returned to Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez on Feb 23–24, and Japan hit Chiba on May 11–12. They also added EDC Korea at Seoul Land on August 31 and September 1.

Friend: If I were to go back and look at the stage layouts from those years, which one is the "main" one?

Vet: You're looking for kineticFIELD. That's the heart of the festival. Whether it's the Speedway or the international spots, that's where the biggest names always land.
