Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a global series of electronic dance music festivals. The event originated in 1991 in Chino, California, with an initial attendance of 3,000–3,500. Following a period of rotating venues in California and expansion into Colorado, Texas, and Puerto Rico, the festival established a permanent presence in Las Vegas, Nevada, starting in 2011.

The 2026 schedule includes three planned events. In Asia, EDC Thailand is scheduled for January 16–18 at the Boat Avenue Lakefront in Phuket. In North America, EDC Las Vegas will take place from May 15–17 at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Additionally, the series will expand to South America with EDC Colombia, planned for October 10–11 at the Estadio Atanasio Girardot in Medellín.
