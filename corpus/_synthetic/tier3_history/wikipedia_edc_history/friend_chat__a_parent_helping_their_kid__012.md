Friend: My kid is dying to go to EDC 2026 and I'm trying to help them plan, but I'm totally lost. Where does this even happen?

Vet: It's a massive production! The main event is in Las Vegas. You'll want to make sure they're ready for the scale of it—it's basically a city of music.

Friend: I've heard about different "areas" or stages. Is it just one big party or is it split up?

Vet: It's split into themed stages. The big one everyone wants to see is kineticFIELD; that's where the absolute headliners play. If your kid likes the heavier stuff, they'll spend their time at circuitGROUNDS.

Friend: What about the more "underground" or house music vibes? I don't want them missing the cool spots.

Vet: For that, they need to head to neonGARDENS or the bass-heavy spots. Actually, if they're into the real warehouse vibe, they should keep an eye on Factory 93—that's Insomniac's underground brand.

Friend: Okay, but for a first-timer, who is the "must-see" for 2026?

Vet: You can't miss the closing set at kineticFIELD on Sunday night. It's always the biggest production of the weekend. I'm betting on the top-billed mainstage act to close it out with a massive fireworks display.

Friend: Is there any other way to get into the mood before the trip? Like a radio station or something?

Vet: Definitely. Tell them to check out Insomniac Radio or Night Owl Radio on SiriusXM. It's a great way to discover which artists they actually want to track down during the festival.

Friend: Are there other events around that time? My kid mentioned something about "Zombieland."

Vet: Yeah, if they're still in the mood for more in June 2026, Apocalypse: Zombieland is happening June 19th and 20th at the Queen Mary Waterfront. It's a totally different vibe—more spooky and themed.

Friend: That sounds intense. Is there anything a bit more "daytime" and relaxed?

Vet: For sure. Day Trip Festival is right there too, June 21st and 22nd, also at the Queen Mary Waterfront. It's much more chill than the madness of EDC.

Friend: This is a lot of info. Any last pro-tip for a parent?

Vet: Just tell them to stay hydrated and wear comfortable shoes. Between kineticFIELD and the other stages, they'll be walking miles!
