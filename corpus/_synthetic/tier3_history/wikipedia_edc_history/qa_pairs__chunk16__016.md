Q: On what date did the complex hold its first races on a 1/2 mile dirt oval?
A: May 16, 1971.

Q: Which organization sanctioned the first races on the 1/2 mile dirt oval?
A: Southern Nevada Off Road Enthusiasts (SNORE).

Q: In August 1971, what length buggy course did SNORE open?
A: 0.750 mi (1.207 km).

Q: What was the name of the rival track bid announced by Larry Horten in December 1971?
A: Las Vegas Raceway Park.

Q: On what date did the Clark County board reject Larry Horten's plan for the Las Vegas Raceway Park?
A: December 21, 1971.

Q: When did the Las Vegas International Speedrome's drag strip hold its first races?
A: February 26, 1972.

Q: Which event took place on the first day of the NHRA Open Drag Championships at the Speedrome?
A: The first races on the 0.250 mi (0.402 km) drag strip.

Q: What was the length of the road course for which the International Motorsports Association scheduled races in May 1972?
A: 1.600 mi (2.575 km).

Q: Which organization signed a 15-year agreement to run the drag strip in September 1972?
A: West Charleston Lions Club of Las Vegas.

Q: When did the track serve as the starting point for the Mint 400?
A: 1975.
