"Alright, follow me. I’ve been coming to the Speedway since 2016, so I know these shortcuts. 

We’re starting here at **kineticFIELD**. Look up at the main stage—it’s wild to think that back in 2016, this is where Marshmello did that whole gimmick where he took off his helmet to reveal Tiësto. Now, we’re heading east toward **cosmicMEADOW**. As we walk, keep the merchant area on your left and those massive carnival rides on your right. 

Once we clear the rides and hit the open stretch, we’re moving toward the camping grounds. See those RVs and campers over there? They didn't have that until 2018. Before that, the June heat was brutal—I remember 2017 was a nightmare with the medical calls and heat stroke. That’s why they shifted the dates to May; it's way more manageable now.

Keep walking straight past the food vendors, and we’ll hit the transition zone toward **circuitGROUNDS**. As we pass the perimeter, look toward the far edge of the Speedway. It reminds me of 2019—I remember standing right about here when those high winds hit. The screens told everyone to 'take cover in your car,' and a huge chunk of the crowd actually left the festival thinking it was over. Because of the strict no-re-entry policy, they never got back in. Absolute tragedy.

Now, we’re looping back around toward **unity** (the smaller stages). If you look toward the horizon, it’s crazy to think how global this thing got. While we're here in Vegas, the family is everywhere—from the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez for EDC Mexico to the Zozo Marine Stadium in Japan. I actually went to Japan back in 2018, but the heavy rains absolutely wrecked the equipment.

Let’s cut through this path to get back to the center. We'll pass the security checkpoints on our right—just a heads up, they’re still strict. I remember the police made 65 arrests back in 2019, including a bunch of felonies. Stay hydrated, keep your wristband tight, and let's get back to the music."
