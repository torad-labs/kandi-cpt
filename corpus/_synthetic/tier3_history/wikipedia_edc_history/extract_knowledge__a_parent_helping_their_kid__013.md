The source text describes the Las Vegas Motor Speedway as a 1.500-mile tri-oval intermediate speedway located in Las Vegas, Nevada. Owned by Speedway Motorsports, LLC (SMI) and managed by Patrick Lindsey, the venue has a capacity of 80,000 as of 2023.
