Friend: I’m finally doing it! I bought a ticket for EDC 2026. I have no idea what I'm doing though. Where do I even start?

Vet: Let’s gooo! First thing: it's a marathon, not a sprint. You're going to be walking miles. Wear comfortable shoes and remember that the Las Vegas heat is no joke, even at night.

Friend: I keep seeing weird names like "kineticFIELD." Is that a specific area or just a vibe?

Vet: It's a stage! kineticFIELD is basically the heart of the festival. If you want the quintessential EDC experience with the massive main stage production, that’s where you’ll spend most of your Friday night.

Friend: Got it. Are there other stages I should prioritize so I don't get lost?

Vet: Definitely. You have to hit circuitGROUNDS for the heavy hitters and bass, and if you want something more melodic or house-focused, head over to luminousFIELD. Just download the app the second it drops so you can track the set times.

Friend: I've seen some other festivals listed online like "White Wonderland" or "Project Z." Are those part of EDC?

Vet: Nah, those are separate events. If you like the vibe of EDC, you might check out White Wonderland for a winter rave or Project Z, but EDC is the "big boss" of them all. It's a completely different scale.

Friend: What about the food and water situation? Do I just buy whatever is there?

Vet: Bring a hydration pack! You can refill it for free at the water stations. For food, there are tons of vendors, but it's pricey. Pro tip: eat a massive meal before you enter the gates.

Friend: I saw a movie called "Under the Electric Sky." Should I watch that to prepare?

Vet: Honestly, yeah. It gives you a great sense of the scale and the "Electric Sky" atmosphere. It’ll hype you up and show you how wild the visuals get.

Friend: I'm a bit nervous about the crowds. Is it just one giant mosh pit?

Vet: Not at all. It's a huge community. If you feel overwhelmed, just wander away from the main stages toward the art installations. It's way more chill there and great for photos.

Friend: Any last piece of advice for a rookie?

Vet: Just go with the flow. You can't see every single artist, so don't stress if you miss a set. Just follow the lights, stay hydrated, and I'll see you under the electric sky!
