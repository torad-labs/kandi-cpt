Q: How many seats were removed from the third and fourth turns of the track in 2015?
A: 15,000 seats.

Q: What was the approximate seating capacity of the track after the 2017 decrease?
A: Around 80,000 seats.

Q: Who succeeded Powell as the general manager of LVMS following the 2025 Pennzoil 400?
A: Patrick Lindsey.

Q: Which two annual NASCAR Cup Series race weekends are hosted by the track?
A: The spring Pennzoil 400 and the fall South Point 400.

Q: Which two races are part of the NASCAR O'Reilly Auto Parts Series hosted at the track?
A: The spring LiUNA! 300 and the fall Focused Health 302.

Q: Which NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series race did the track formerly host?
A: The Ecosave 200.

Q: On what date did the 2004 Champ Car World Series race occur at the track?
A: September 25.

Q: What caused the cancellation of the 2011 IZOD IndyCar World Championship and the end of normal human-driven IndyCar races at the track?
A: A lap 11 crash that took the life of Dan Wheldon.

Q: How much of a bounty did IndyCar Series CEO Randy Bernard offer to any non-IndyCar driver who could win the 2011 IZOD IndyCar World Championship?
A: $5 million.

Q: What is the name of the driverless vehicle event that returned IndyCar to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in 2022?
A: The Indy Autonomous Challenge.
