Q: Who founded the promoter Insomniac?
A: Pasquale Rotella.

Q: In which city is the headquarters of Insomniac located?
A: Beverly Hills, California.

Q: What is Insomniac's flagship electronic music festival in Las Vegas?
A: Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC).

Q: Which brands does Insomniac use for its hardstyle and trance events?
A: Basscon is used for hardstyle events and Dreamstate is used for trance events.

Q: Which three Los Angeles venues are managed by Insomniac?
A: Create, Exchange L.A., and Factory 93.

Q: Which Miami nightclub did Insomniac acquire in 2019?
A: Club Space.

Q: Which Washington, D.C. nightclubs and operator did Insomniac acquire in 2020?
A: Insomniac acquired Echostage and Soundcheck nightclubs, along with their operator Club Glow.

Q: What is the name of the co-branded EDM record label introduced by Insomniac in 2014?
A: Insomniac Records.

Q: In what year was Insomniac founded?
A: 1993.

Q: Which company did Insomniac enter into a "creative partnership" with in June 2013 to access resources while maintaining creative control?
A: Live Nation.
