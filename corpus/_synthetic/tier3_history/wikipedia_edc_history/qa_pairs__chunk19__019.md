Q: In what month and year did the city council consider revoking Rodriguez's lease?
A: March 1988.

Q: What reasons did the city council give for considering the revocation of Rodriguez's lease?
A: Accusations of not paying rent and selling food without proper sales tax permits.

Q: Until what date was Rodriguez able to operate the track after the city approved terminating the lease?
A: December 1990.

Q: On what date was the sale of the land to a private operator approved?
A: November 17.

Q: What condition was placed on the buyer of the track when it was put up for sale?
A: The buyer must maintain the track for 10 years.

Q: Who purchased the track complex in May 1989 for $1,070,000?
A: Richie Clyne.

Q: What was the track complex renamed to following the purchase by Richie Clyne?
A: Las Vegas International Raceway.

Q: Who convinced Richie Clyne not to turn the track area into an industrial park?
A: Bob Bahre and Tom Wheatcroft.

Q: What was the name of the first races held under Richie Clyne's leadership?
A: The Nissan 400.

Q: Who took over a lease from Richie Clyne to run the track complex in 1993?
A: David Juberg and Craig Graham.

Q: What did David Juberg rename the complex in 1993?
A: Las Vegas Speedway Park.

Q: From whom did David Juberg and Craig Graham take over the master lease to the track in May 1993?
A: Tom Villardi.
