# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival** (commonly abbreviated as **EDC**) is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals organized by the promoter and distributor Insomniac. The flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, is held annually in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway and is the largest EDM festival in North America as of 2024.

## Overview
EDC is characterized by a combination of electronic music performances and large-scale carnival themes. The event features a diverse range of genres, including house, techno, drum and bass, and dubstep. Beyond musical performances, the festival integrates three-dimensional superstructures, LED-lit flora and fauna, glow-in-the-dark environments, interactive art installations, free-roaming carnival performers, and amusement rides. In 2017, the event received the "Festival of the Year" award at the Electronic Music Awards.

## History
The origins of the event date back to 1986, when Stephen Hauptfuhr began organizing underground parties. Hauptfuhr hosted the first Electric Daisy Carnival in 1991 in Chino, California, which attracted between 3,000 and 3,500 attendees. Following this initial success, subsequent editions were held in 1992 and 1995. The event was later adopted and expanded by Insomniac starting in 1997.

## Stages and Lineup
The festival features a lineup of electronic dance music producers and DJs. The Las Vegas flagship event utilizes eight distinct stages, each dedicated to specific genres of EDM. The largest of these, kineticField, has a capacity of approximately 70,000 attendees.

## Logistics and Expansion
### Event Growth
EDC Las Vegas has expanded in duration and scale over time, evolving into a two-day event in 2009 and a three-day event in 2011. In 2018, the festival introduced an on-site camping experience. To modernize accessibility, ticket purchasing was expanded to mobile applications in 2019.

### Global Presence
While based in Las Vegas, the EDC brand has expanded internationally. Current annual events are held in Orlando, Mexico City, and Suzhou. Past iterations have taken place in various global locations, including Puerto Rico, the United Kingdom, Brazil, Japan, India, and Portugal.

### Operations
The scale of the production involves a staff of over 5,000 personnel. The festival grounds incorporate 18 carnival rides and four Ferris wheels. In 2024, the Las Vegas event recorded a total attendance of 525,000 over its three-day duration.
