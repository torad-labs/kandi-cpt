Friend: I’m trying to get the lay of the land for my coverage of EDC 2026. Is the venue still that same track in the desert?

Vet: Yep, it’s still at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. It’s been the flagship home since 2011 after they had to bail on LA due to those massive safety and underage drug controversies.

Friend: Right, I remember reading about that. Since it's a racing hub, does the track actually do much else, or is it just a giant parking lot for festivals now?

Vet: Oh, it’s way more active. They’ve got the World of Outlaws Sprint Car Series on the half-mile dirt track—though that ran in stints from '96 to '06 and then again from '09 to '19. Even next year in 2025, High Limit Racing is slated to hit the venue during the NASCAR Cup weekend.

Friend: That’s wild. Does it ever host anything... air-based?

Vet: Actually, yeah. They tried the Red Bull Air Race World Championship. 2014 was a total bust—weather killed the race and they just used qualifying speeds for results. They finally nailed it in 2015, but by 2016, high winds just wiped the whole event off the schedule.

Friend: Sounds like the wind is a bit of a nightmare there. What about non-racing stuff? Any other big events?

Vet: It’s a bit of a catch-all. They did the World Long Drive Championship back in 2013, and since 2023, they've been hosting the FoodieLand Night Market. They even did a Faith Lutheran High School graduation there in 2020 because of the pandemic.

Friend: Wait, so you can actually drive there if you aren't a pro?

Vet: Totally. Since 2012, this guy Enrico Bertaggia has run the Dream Racing program where you can take exotics around the road course. There's also the "Driver's Edge" education course if you want to actually learn how to handle a car.

Friend: I saw something about AI racing too. Was that there?

Vet: Yeah, Roborace Season Beta used the outer road course back in 2020. It's a pretty versatile complex.

Friend: Back to EDC—since you've been to so many, what's the move for 2026? Give me a specific "must-see" claim.

Vet: Look, if you want the full experience, you start your night at kineticFIELD for the heavy hitters, but I'm calling it now: the closing set at CIRCUIT on Sunday is going to be the peak of the weekend. You can't miss the production there.
