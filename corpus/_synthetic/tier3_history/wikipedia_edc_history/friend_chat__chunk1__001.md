Friend: I'm trying to get into the history of it all before EDC 2026. Who actually started the whole thing?

Vet: It goes back to 1995. Philip Blaine made a deal to grant the name "Electric Daisy Carnival" to Pasquale Rotella, the CEO of Insomniac. Before the first official EDC, those two actually put together the legendary Organic '96 event.

Friend: Nice. So when did the first actual EDC happen? Was it always this massive?

Vet: Not even close! The debut was in 1997 at the Shrine Expo Hall in LA. A few years later, in 1999, they moved it to Lake Dolores Waterpark in Newberry Springs.

Friend: I heard they had some trouble with venues in the early 2000s. What happened?

Vet: Yeah, in 2000 they hit the International Agri-Center in Tulare. They pulled in 24,000 people, but the noise complaints were so bad the venue cancelled their contract immediately.

Friend: That's wild. When did they start doing the multiple stage thing?

Vet: That started in 2001 at Hansen Dam. Instead of just one spot, they had the Merry Go Round, Fun House, Clown Alley, the Confusin' & Amuzin' Mirror Maze, Bassrush Arena, and Cosmic Healing Temple. That was also the year they launched the first EDC Texas at Thunderhill Raceway Park.

Friend: Did Texas work out well?

Vet: It was rocky. In 2002, the Travis County Exposition Center cancelled the event just days before it was supposed to happen. They ended up doing a make-up show with a different lineup a week later for anyone who didn't want a refund.

Friend: So where did they settle for a while after that?

Vet: From 2003 to 2006, they stayed put at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino. Then they jumped over to the LA Memorial Coliseum in 2007.

Friend: I remember seeing something about them expanding to other states around 2008.

Vet: Exactly. In 2008, they did the LA Memorial Coliseum again on June 28, but they also launched EDC Colorado on June 14 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds.

Friend: How did it transition into the massive multi-day beast it is now?

Vet: The big shift happened in 2009. They announced a two-day festival at the LA Memorial Coliseum and the southern half of Exposition Park. Friday had about 55,000 people and Saturday jumped to 99,000.

Friend: Did they do any other international or regional spots back then?

Vet: Yeah, 2009 was a big year. They did Colorado again on June 13—headlined by Infected Mushroom and Paul Van Dyk—and then they launched the first EDC Puerto Rico at the Arena Fairgrounds in San Juan on August 14.
