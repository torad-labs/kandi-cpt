# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival** (EDC) is a large-scale electronic dance music (EDM) festival produced by Insomniac Events. Founded by Pasquale Rotella, the event has evolved from a localized gathering in California to a global brand with multiple international iterations.

## History and Origins
The origins of the festival date back to 1995, when Philip Blaine granted Pasquale Rotella, CEO of Insomniac Events, the rights to use the name "Electric Daisy Carnival." Prior to the official debut of the festival, Blaine and Rotella organized a precursor event known as Organic '96.

The inaugural Electric Daisy Carnival debuted in 1997 at the Shrine Expo Hall in Los Angeles, California. In 1999, the event was hosted at the Lake Dolores Waterpark in Newberry Springs, California.

## Evolution of Venues and Expansion
Between 2000 and 2009, the festival experienced significant growth and venue transitions:

*   **2000–2002:** In 2000, the festival was held at the International Agri-Center in Tulare, California. Despite attracting 24,000 attendees, noise complaints led the venue to cancel future contracts. In 2001, the event moved to Hansen Dam in Southern California. In 2002, the California edition was hosted at Queen Mary Events Park in Long Beach.
*   **2003–2008:** From 2003 to 2006, the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino served as the primary venue. In 2007 and 2008, the festival relocated to the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum and Exposition Park.
*   **2009:** The 2009 event utilized the entire southern half of Exposition Park and the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum.

### Regional and International Expansion
Insomniac expanded the EDC brand beyond Southern California starting in 2001:
*   **Texas:** The first EDC Texas took place in 2001 at Thunderhill Raceway Park in Austin. A 2002 event at the Travis County Exposition Center was cancelled shortly before the date, though a make-up show with a revised lineup was held a week later.
*   **Colorado:** An EDC Colorado edition was launched in 2008 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora, returning in 2009.
*   **Puerto Rico:** The first EDC Puerto Rico was held on August 14, 2009, at the Arena Fairgrounds in San Juan.

## Stages and Production
The festival transitioned to a multi-stage format in 2001 at Hansen Dam. Early thematic stages included:
*   merryGoRound
*   funHouse
*   clownAlley
*   confusinAndAmuzinMirrorMaze
*   bassrushArena
*   cosmicHealingTemple

## Logistics and Attendance
Attendance grew rapidly during the late 2000s. In 2008, Insomniac announced plans for a two-day festival format. The subsequent 2009 event saw approximately 55,000 attendees on Friday and approximately 99,000 attendees on Saturday.

## Lineup
The festival has featured a wide array of electronic artists. The 2009 Colorado edition was headlined by Paul Van Dyk and Infected Mushroom, among others.
