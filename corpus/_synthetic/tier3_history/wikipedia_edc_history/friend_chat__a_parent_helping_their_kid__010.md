Friend: Hey! My kid is begging me to help them get tickets for EDC 2026. I'm a bit overwhelmed—who actually runs this thing?

Vet: It’s all Insomniac. They are the powerhouse behind it. They’ve grown huge over the years, even starting their own record label, Insomniac Records, back in 2014 with artists like Arty and Chris Lake to find new talent.

Friend: Okay, so they just do EDC?

Vet: Not at all. They have a whole portfolio. They do Beyond Wonderland, Nocturnal Wonderland—which is actually their oldest brand—and Escape Halloween. They even have a huge music group called Insomniac Music Group that handles labels like Basscon and Dreamstate.

Friend: That's a lot of events. Is EDC the only one I should know about for 2026?

Vet: For a first-timer, definitely. Just a heads up: they used to do EDC in places like India and Brazil, but those are discontinued. For 2026, you'll want to look at Las Vegas, Mexico, Orlando, or Portugal.

Friend: I heard they do some "virtual" stuff too? Is that a replacement for the real thing?

Vet: Nope, just extras! During the pandemic, they did "Insomniac Rewind" on YouTube and "Park 'N Rave" drive-ins. They even partnered with Unity Technologies to build a persistent online world for concerts, but nothing beats the actual festival.

Friend: Speaking of the actual festival, what's the vibe? Where do the kids actually hang out?

Vet: It's all about the stages. You'll see them talking about kineticFIELD for that classic vibe, or maybe the bass-heavy sets at Lost in Dreams—that's a newer brand they launched for melodic dubstep.

Friend: I saw something about a festival called Okeechobee. Is that part of this too?

Vet: It used to be! Insomniac had a controlling stake in it through a company called Soundslinger for a few years, but as of April 2025, they aren't involved with Okeechobee anymore.

Friend: Got it. Any other "must-knows" for a parent helping with the planning?

Vet: Just that they are everywhere. They’ve got a huge presence on Dash Radio and used to have a big thing with SiriusXM. If your kid is listening to the "Insomniac Radio" stream, they're basically prepping for the event. Just keep an eye on the Las Vegas dates for 2026!
