**Origins and Early Development**
The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) brand was established in 1995 through an agreement between Philip Blaine and Pasquale Rotella, the CEO of Insomniac Events. Following this agreement, the two partners produced the Organic '96 event. Insomniac Events debuted the first Electric Daisy Carnival in 1997 at the Shrine Expo Hall in Los Angeles, California. In 1999, the event was hosted at Lake Dolores Waterpark in Newberry Springs, California.

**Expansion and Venue Transitions (2000–2006)**
In 2000, the festival took place at the International Agri-Center in Tulare, California, drawing 24,000 attendees. Due to noise complaints, the venue cancelled future contracts. The 2001 event moved to Hansen Dam in Southern California and introduced a multi-stage format featuring the Merry Go Round, the Fun House, Clown Alley, the Confusin' & Amuzin' Mirror Maze, Bassrush Arena, and the Cosmic Healing Temple. 

Regional expansion began in 2001 with the first EDC Texas at Thunderhill Raceway Park in Austin. While a 2002 Texas event at the Travis County Exposition Center was cancelled shortly before the date, a make-up show with a modified lineup was held one week later. The 2002 California event was hosted at the Queen Mary Events Park in Long Beach. From 2003 to 2006, the festival was held at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino, California.

**Growth and Multi-City Events (2007–2009)**
In 2007 and 2008, the California festival was hosted at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum in Exposition Park; the 2008 date occurred on June 28. In 2008, the event expanded to Colorado, held on June 14 at the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora.

In 2009, the California event utilized the southern half of Exposition Park and the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum. The Colorado edition returned to the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds on June 13, featuring headliners Paul Van Dyk and Infected Mushroom. Additionally, the first EDC Puerto Rico was held on August 14, 2009, at the Arena Fairgrounds in San Juan.
