# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac Events. Since its inception in 1991, the event has expanded from a local California gathering into a global brand with iterations across North America, Europe, Asia, and South America.

## Overview
The festival originated in 1991 in Chino, California, with an initial attendance of approximately 3,000 to 3,500 participants. Following a period of sporadic organization in the 1990s, the event grew in scale throughout the early 2000s, transitioning through various venues including the Shrine Auditorium in Los Angeles (1997), Lake Dolores Waterpark (1999), and the International Agri-Center in Tulare (2000), where attendance reached 24,000.

## Stages and Expansion
The event has evolved from a single-location festival into a global franchise. Between 2003 and 2006, the event was hosted annually at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino. Beginning in 2007, the brand expanded into regional "EDC" editions, including EDC Los Angeles, EDC Colorado, EDC Dallas, and EDC Puerto Rico.

In 2011, the flagship event moved to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in North Las Vegas, Nevada. This location saw significant growth in attendance, rising from 230,000 in 2011 to a peak of 525,000 in 2023 and 2024. Other major permanent or recurring editions include:
* **EDC Mexico:** Hosted at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City.
* **EDC Orlando:** Hosted at Tinker Field in Orlando, Florida.
* **EDC China:** Hosted in cities including Shanghai and Suzhou.

The brand has also experimented with cruise-based festivals through the **EDSea** series aboard the *Norwegian Joy*.

## Lineup and Global Reach
The festival has expanded its international footprint through several one-off or recurring events:
* **Europe:** EDC London (2013), EDC UK (2014–2015), and EDC Portugal (2022).
* **Asia:** EDC India (2016), EDC Japan (2018–2019), EDC Korea (2019, 2021), and planned events in Thailand (2025–2026).
* **Americas:** EDC Brasil (2015) and planned events in Colombia (2026).

## Logistics and Venues
The event utilizes large-scale infrastructure to accommodate high capacities. Notable venues have included MetLife Stadium for EDC New York, the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, and various international racing circuits. The transition to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway allowed the festival to scale its production and attendance significantly compared to the earlier San Bernardino and Los Angeles iterations.

## Safety and Disruptions
The event has faced several cancellations due to external factors:
* **Pandemic:** The 2020 EDC Las Vegas event was cancelled due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
* **Weather:** The 2024 EDC China event was cancelled following Typhoon Bebinca.
* **Organizational:** The UK edition was cancelled in 2017.
