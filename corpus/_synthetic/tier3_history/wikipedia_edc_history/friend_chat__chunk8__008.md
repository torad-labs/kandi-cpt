Friend: I’m finally doing it—I'm hitting up EDC 2026! I've never been to the Las Vegas one. Since you're basically a pro, can you give me the rundown?

Vet: Oh man, you're in for it! It's an absolute beast of a festival. Just be ready—the scale is insane. In 2024 alone, they had 525,000 people show up. It’s basically a neon city in the desert.

Friend: That sounds overwhelming. I've heard there are a ton of stages. Which ones are the "must-sees"?

Vet: You have to hit kineticFIELD first. That's the main stage, and it's legendary—they once spent $3 million just on the construction for one of them. If you want something different, check out circuitGROUNDS or bassPOD. Each one has its own entire vibe and immersive art.

Friend: I'm a huge fan of the underground stuff. Is there a spot for that, or is it all just big-room mainstage hits?

Vet: Definitely not all mainstage. You'll want to spend time at the neonGARDEN or the cosmosFIELD. They curate those for the deeper, more experimental sounds. You can literally spend an entire day just wandering those and still miss something.

Friend: I'm starting to plan my schedule. Do you know if certain artists always stick to specific days or stages?

Vet: It changes every year, but generally, the massive headliners take over kineticFIELD on Friday and Saturday nights. For 2026, expect the biggest names to close out those nights with those crazy drone shows and fireworks they're famous for.

Friend: Speaking of the "experience," is it actually as wild as it looks on Instagram?

Vet: Even wilder. It’s not just the music; it’s the "Electric Daisy" legacy. It started way back in 1997 in California, and it's evolved into this global thing with events in Mexico, Orlando, and even a cruise called EDSea. The Las Vegas Motor Speedway is just the crown jewel.

Friend: I'm a bit nervous about the heat and the crowds. Any "pro tips" for surviving the weekend?

Vet: Hydration is everything. They've actually moved the dates to May over the years specifically to avoid the worst of the Vegas heat. Also, wear comfortable shoes. You’ll be walking miles between stages like circuitGROUNDS and bassPOD.

Friend: One last thing—I saw something about "surprise performances." Does that actually happen?

Vet: All the time. That's the magic of Insomniac. In 2024, they had a bunch of surprise sets that weren't even on the official lineup. My advice? Keep your eyes on the screens and be ready to sprint to a different stage the second a secret guest is announced!
