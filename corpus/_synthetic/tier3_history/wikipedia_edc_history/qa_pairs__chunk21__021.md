Q: On what date did the Las Vegas Motor Speedway officially open to the public?
A: September 13, 1996.

Q: Who served as the general manager of Las Vegas Motor Speedway from 1998 to 2025?
A: Chris Powell.

Q: For approximately how much did Bruton Smith announce plans to buy LVMS and its complex in December 1998?
A: $215 million.

Q: What name was given to the modified 0.375 mi (0.604 km) oval in 2000?
A: The Bullring.

Q: What was the goal for the total capacity increase during the 2006 renovation project announced by Chris Powell?
A: 141,000.

Q: To what degree was the banking in the turns changed during the 2006 renovation?
A: From 12 to 20 degrees.

Q: In what year did the Las Vegas Motor Speedway win the rights to host the flagship branch of Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)?
A: 2011.

Q: Which IndyCar driver died during the 11th lap of the 2011 IZOD IndyCar World Championship at the track?
A: Dan Wheldon.

Q: When was the drag strip expanded to four lanes to accommodate four cars racing at once?
A: 2017.

Q: Who has served as the general manager of the track since 2025?
A: Patrick Lindsey.
