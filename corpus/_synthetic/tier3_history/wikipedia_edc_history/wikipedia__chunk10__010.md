# Insomniac Events

**Insomniac Events** is an American promoter of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals and nightclubs. The organization operates a diverse portfolio of event brands and music labels, specializing in large-scale audiovisual productions.

## Media and Broadcasting
Insomniac has expanded its presence in digital and satellite radio through several strategic partnerships. In August 2015, Pasquale Rotella launched *Night Owl Radio* on the SiriusXM channel Electric Area. Following the replacement of Electric Area by Diplo's *Revolution* in 2018, the company launched Insomniac Radio on SiriusXM. In May 2019, Insomniac established Dash Radio as its flagship broadcasting station.

## Corporate Acquisitions and Partnerships
Between 2019 and 2025, Insomniac engaged in several acquisitions to expand its physical footprint:
* **Club Space:** In July 2019, Insomniac acquired a majority stake in the Miami-based nightclub.
* **Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival:** In June 2019, Insomniac acquired a controlling stake in Soundslinger, LLC. Following a one-year hiatus, the festival returned in 2020 featuring production elements integrated from other Insomniac brands. In April 2025, Soundslinger announced that Insomniac was no longer involved with the festival.
* **Unity Technologies:** In March 2022, the company partnered with Unity to develop a persistent virtual world for online concerts.

## Insomniac Music Group
In May 2014, Insomniac launched Insomniac Records in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M, signing artists such as Arty, Bingo Players, and Chris Lake. In December 2018, the organization formed the Insomniac Music Group to provide A&R and distribution services for its internal imprints.

The group's label portfolio includes:
* Basscon Records
* Bassrush Records
* Discovery Project
* Dreamstate Records
* Gud Vibrations
* HARD Recordings
* In/Rotation
* Lost in Dreams (launched February 2021)
* What To Do

## Event Portfolio
Insomniac manages a wide array of festival brands. Nocturnal Wonderland is the company's longest-running brand and was its first public event.

### Active Brands
* **Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC):** Operates in Las Vegas, Mexico, Orlando, and Portugal. Previous editions in the UK, India, Puerto Rico, and Brazil have been discontinued.
* **Electric Forest:** Produced in collaboration with Madison House under Anschutz Entertainment Group.
* **Other Major Events:** Beyond Wonderland, Dreamstate, Escape Halloween (formerly Escape From Wonderland), HARD, Countdown New Year’s Eve, and Daytrip.
* **Specialized Events:** Apocalypse: Zombieland, Audiotistic Music Festival, Boo Seattle, Forbidden Kingdom, EDSea, Project GLOW, and Abduction (a bass music event held at The Vanguard).

## Pandemic Response and Logistics
During the COVID-19 pandemic, Insomniac transitioned from in-person festivals to digital and socially distanced formats:
* **Digital Programming:** The company launched "Insomniac Rewind," a series of archived festival sets streamed via YouTube and Twitch, alongside virtual festivals.
* **Park 'N Rave:** A residency of drive-in concerts held at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino.
* **Electric Mile:** A drive-through audiovisual experience at Santa Anita Park, consisting of themed "worlds" based on the company's primary festival brands.
