**Insomniac and Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)**

Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is the flagship electronic music festival organized by Insomniac, an American event promoter and music distributor founded in 1993 by Pasquale Rotella. Headquartered in Beverly Hills, California, Insomniac operates as a private entity specializing in electronic dance music (EDM) events and venue management.

The company manages a diverse portfolio of genre-specific brands, including Dreamstate (trance), Basscon (hardstyle), and Bassrush (drum and bass and dubstep). Other major festival properties include Beyond Wonderland and Nocturnal Wonderland.

Insomniac's operational infrastructure extends to nightclub management across several U.S. cities. In Los Angeles, the company operates Exchange L.A., Factory 93, and Create (via partnership with SBE). Its East Coast holdings include Miami's Club Space (acquired 2019) and Washington, D.C.'s Echostage and Soundcheck (acquired 2020 via Club Glow).

The organization maintains a vertical integration of music production through the Insomniac Music Group. Originally launched in 2014 as Insomniac Records in partnership with Interscope Geffen A&M, the company brought A&R and distribution operations in-house in 2018.

Strategically, Insomniac maintains a creative partnership with Live Nation to leverage corporate resources while retaining creative control over event production.
