Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is associated with a network of electronic music events and festivals. These include regional events such as Crush—which operates in Southern California, San Francisco, Phoenix, and Dallas—as well as other gatherings including Bay Nites, Together as One, White Wonderland, Middlelands, Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival, We Are NRG, Holy Ship!, and Project Z.

The event's history and atmosphere have been documented through various media releases. Audio records include the *Electric Daisy Carnival* album (2000) and *Electric Daisy Carnival, Vol. 1* (2010). Visual documentation of the festival is featured in the 2011 film *Electric Daisy Carnival Experience* and the 2013 film *Under the Electric Sky*.
