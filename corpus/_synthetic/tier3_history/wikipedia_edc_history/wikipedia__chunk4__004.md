# Electric Daisy Carnival (2016–2019)

The Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac Events. Between 2016 and 2019, the brand expanded its global footprint, holding flagship events in the United States and several international locations, including Mexico, the United Kingdom, India, Japan, China, and South Korea.

## Overview of Events

### Las Vegas
The flagship event took place annually at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. In 2016, the festival was held from June 17–19. In 2017, opening night saw over 135,000 attendees. For the 2018 edition, the dates were moved from June to May 18–20 to provide cooler weather conditions for attendees. This event saw an estimated total attendance of 411,400 over three days. The 2019 event took place from May 17–19.

### Regional and International Editions
*   **EDC Mexico:** Held annually at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City. Events occurred from February 27–28, 2016; February 25–26, 2017; February 24–25, 2018; and February 23–24, 2019.
*   **EDC Orlando:** Hosted at Tinker Field in Orlando, Florida. The event took place November 4–5, 2016, and November 10–11, 2017. In 2018, it was held November 9–10. By 2019, the festival duration was expanded from two days to three.
*   **EDC New York:** Held at Citi Field in Queens, New York, from May 14–15, 2016.
*   **EDC UK:** Held in Milton Keynes, England, on July 9, 2016. The 2017 event, also scheduled for Milton Keynes, was cancelled.
*   **EDC India:** Held at the Buddh International Circuit in New Delhi from November 12–13, 2016.
*   **EDC Japan:** Held in Chiba at Zozo Marine Stadium and Makuhari Beach Park. The 2018 event (May 12–13) experienced equipment issues due to heavy rain. The 2019 event took place May 11–12.
*   **EDC China:** Debuted at the Shanghai International Music Park on April 29–30, 2018.
*   **EDC Korea:** Held at Seoul Land in Gwacheon, South Korea, on August 31 and September 1, 2019.

## Lineup and Performances
The festivals featured various high-profile EDM artists. During the 2016 Las Vegas event, DJ Marshmello performed a staged gimmick in which he removed his helmet to reveal DJ Tiësto. Marshmello also performed on the mainStage of EDC Mexico 2018.

## Logistics and Operations
To address infrastructure and traffic challenges, Insomniac implemented several changes at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway starting in 2018:
*   **Camping:** The introduction of on-site camping, including RV parking and rentable campers.
*   **Scheduling:** Earlier opening times were established to reduce vehicle congestion during entry.

## Safety and Incidents

### Health and Medical
During the 2017 Las Vegas event, there was a significant increase in heat-related medical incidents, with medical calls on opening night doubling compared to the previous year. One 34-year-old male fatality was reported; the cause was attributed to the ingestion of MDMA and TFMPP, exacerbated by heat exposure.
