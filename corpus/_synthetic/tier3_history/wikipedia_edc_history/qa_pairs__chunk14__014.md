Q: What is the address of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: 7000 North Las Vegas Boulevard, Las Vegas, Nevada.

Q: What is the capacity of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: 80,000.

Q: Who is the owner of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway as of 1999?
A: Speedway Motorsports.

Q: On what date did the Las Vegas Motor Speedway track complex open?
A: 16 May 1971.

Q: What was the construction cost for the main track at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: US$72 million.

Q: What are the lengths of the Long, Medium, and Short Road Courses at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: The Long Road Course is 2.499 mi (4.022 km), the Medium Road Course is 2.150 mi (3.460 km), and the Short Road Course is 1.938 mi (3.119 km).

Q: What is the length and surface of the Tri-Oval at Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: The Tri-Oval is 1.500 mi (2.414 km) and has an asphalt surface.

Q: What is the banking degree for the turns, frontstretch, and backstretch of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway Tri-Oval?
A: The turns have 20° banking, and the frontstretch and backstretch both have 9° banking.
