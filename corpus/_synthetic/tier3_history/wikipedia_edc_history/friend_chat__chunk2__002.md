Friend: Yo! I'm thinking about hitting EDC 2026. Since you've been to so many, do you know how the whole thing started? Was it always in Vegas?

Vet: Nah, it actually started in LA! But it got really messy. One of the LA events had about 185,000 people, and while some tickets were $60, others paid over $100. It turned into a disaster—kids under 16 got in, a stampede sent over 100 people to the hospital, and a 15-year-old named Sasha Rodriguez died from MDMA.

Friend: Damn, that's intense. Did the city just shut them down?

Vet: Pretty much. LA suspended all their 2010 events and any future ones until they fixed security. They had to mandate that attendees be 18+ and hire actual on-site doctors.

Friend: So where did they go after that?

Vet: They did a bunch of smaller regional shows in 2010. They hit Aurora at the Arapahoe County Fairground for EDC Colorado on June 12, Fair Park for EDC Dallas on June 19 (about 11,000 people), and then San Juan at the Estadio Sixto Escobar for EDC Puerto Rico on August 28.

Friend: Is that why they moved the big one to Vegas?

Vet: Exactly. Because of all that LA controversy, they moved the flagship to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway in 2011. It was huge—June 24–26, and about 230,000 people showed up over the three days. That's where you'll see the massive setups like kineticFIELD.

Friend: Did they keep doing the other cities too?

Vet: Yeah, 2011 was a busy year. EDC Orlando happened May 27–28 at Tinker Field; they had 12k people Friday and 20k Saturday. They also went back to Aurora for EDC Colorado on June 11.

Friend: What about Dallas? Did they stay there?

Vet: Not for long. They did EDC Dallas on June 18 at Fair Park with 25,000 people, but it was brutal. Temps hit over 110 degrees, dozens of people ended up in the hospital, and there was another death. That was the final nail in the coffin for Dallas.

Friend: Man, the weather is no joke. Did they do Puerto Rico again?

Vet: They did! August 27, 2011, back at the Estadio Sixto Escobar in San Juan.

Friend: It sounds like a wild ride to get to where they are now. Who's a good example of the talent they bring in for the big Vegas stages?

Vet: Oh, plenty. For example, back in 2016, Michael Calfan played a set at EDC Las Vegas. If you're going in 2026, just make sure you stay hydrated and stick to the 18+ rules!
