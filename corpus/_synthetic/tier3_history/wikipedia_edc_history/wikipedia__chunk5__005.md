# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC)** is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac Events. The events are characterized by large-scale art installations, pyrotechnics, and a diverse lineup of electronic music artists.

## Event History

### EDC Mexico
The 2020 edition of EDC Mexico was held from February 28 to March 2 at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City.

### EDC Las Vegas
The 2020 festival was originally scheduled for May 15–17 but was postponed to October 2–4 due to the COVID-19 pandemic. In August 2020, the October dates were officially cancelled. To coincide with the original May dates, a virtual festival was streamed via Twitch and YouTube. Insomniac Events CEO Pasquale Rotella noted that the 2020 planning phase involved utilizing additional areas of the Las Vegas Motor Speedway following the removal of various RV hookups.

The 2021 event was initially scheduled for May 21–23. However, it was rescheduled to October 22–24 after Clark County implemented a reopening plan requiring 60% of residents to be vaccinated before lifting restrictions on large-scale gatherings.

### EDC Thailand
EDC Thailand debuted from January 17–19, 2025, at the Boat Avenue Lakefront in Phuket.

### EDC Orlando
In June 2024, Insomniac Events released the lineup for EDC Orlando, featuring a combination of established and emerging electronic dance music artists.

## Production and Logistics

### Stage Design and Crowd Management
For the 2024 season, organizers implemented a reconfiguration of stage placements. These changes were designed to increase the size of dance floors and optimize crowd flow throughout the venue. Notable stages include the kineticBloom mainstage.

### Safety and Incidents
During the inaugural EDC Thailand in 2025, two foreign tourists died in incidents suspected to be drug-related. Additionally, three individuals were arrested for the possession or use of illegal substances.
