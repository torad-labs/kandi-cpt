Friend: My kid is dying to go to EDC 2026, but I have no idea what I'm getting into. Is this just a new trend, or does this thing have some history?

Vet: Oh, it’s a legacy! It all started back in 1995 when Philip Blaine gave Pasquale Rotella—the CEO of Insomniac—the rights to use the name. Before the first official EDC, they actually put on this legendary event called Organic '96.

Friend: So when did the actual "Carnival" start?

Vet: The debut was in 1997 at the Shrine Expo Hall in LA. It was way smaller back then. By 1999, they were doing things at the Lake Dolores Waterpark in Newberry Springs.

Friend: It sounds like they moved around a lot in the early days. Was it always that huge?

Vet: Not at all. In 2000, they were at the International Agri-Center in Tulare. They had 24,000 people, but the noise complaints were so bad the venue actually cancelled their contract!

Friend: Yikes. Did they just give up after that?

Vet: Definitely not. 2001 was a huge turning point at Hansen Dam. That's when they first expanded to multiple stages. They had the Merry Go Round, the Fun House, Clown Alley, the Confusin' & Amuzin' Mirror Maze, Bassrush Arena, and the Cosmic Healing Temple. They even launched the first EDC Texas in Austin that same year.

Friend: I think I heard something about a venue disaster in Texas?

Vet: Yeah, in 2002. They planned a second Texas show at the Travis County Exposition Center, but the venue cancelled just days before it started. They ended up doing a make-up show with a different lineup a week later for anyone who didn't want a refund.

Friend: Wow. So where did they settle down for a bit?

Vet: They spent a few years at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino from 2003 to 2006, and then they moved to the LA Memorial Coliseum in 2007.

Friend: When did it actually turn into the massive city-sized event it is now?

Vet: The growth exploded around 2008 and 2009. In 2008, they announced a two-day festival in California. By 2009, they were using the entire southern half of Exposition Park in LA. That's also when they expanded globally, hitting San Juan for the first EDC Puerto Rico in August 2009.

Friend: Since you're the expert, if I'm helping my kid plan for 2026, what's the "vibe" for the stages?

Vet: You have to tell them to hit the kineticFIELD for those big melodic moments and definitely check out circuitGROUNDS for the harder hits. If they want the full experience, they need to spend a good amount of time at the neon-soaked neonGARDENS.

Friend: Any specific artists they should look out for?

Vet: For 2026, I’m calling it now: expect a massive headliner set on Friday night at the kineticFIELD. If they want a wilder Saturday, they should look for the heavy hitters at circuitGROUNDS. It's all about the spectacle!
