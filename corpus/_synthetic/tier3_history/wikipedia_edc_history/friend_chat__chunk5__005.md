Friend: Wait, did they do anything at all in 2020?

Vet: Yeah, they did a virtual festival streamed on Twitch and YouTube during those original May dates so people could still vibe from home.

Friend: That sounds rough. Did the pandemic affect the Vegas side of things in 2021 too?

Vet: Big time. It was supposed to be May 21–23, but Clark County required 60% of residents to be vaccinated before allowing huge crowds. Pasquale Rotella basically said they couldn't risk it since the vaccination rate was out of their control. They ended up pushing the event to October 22–24.

Friend: I heard they've been changing the layout of the grounds. Is that true?

Vet: Definitely. In 2024, they actually shifted a few stages around specifically to create larger dance floors and fix the crowd flow. If you're heading to a kineticFIELD or other major stage in 2026, you'll definitely notice more breathing room than in the old days.

Vet: Those are great for catching a mix of legends and rising stars. For example, the June 2024 EDC Orlando lineup was a perfect blend of well-known headliners and emerging artists.
