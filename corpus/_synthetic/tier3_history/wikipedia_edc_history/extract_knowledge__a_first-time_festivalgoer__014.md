**Venue Overview**
The Las Vegas Motor Speedway (LVMS), owned by Speedway Motorsports since 1999, is a racing complex located at 7000 North Las Vegas Boulevard, Las Vegas, Nevada. The facility first broke ground on February 24, 1971, and officially opened on May 16, 1971. The venue has a maximum capacity of 80,000 people.

**Track Configurations**
The primary Tri-Oval track, established in 1996, is an asphalt surface measuring 1.500 miles (2.414 km). It features 20° banking in the turns and 9° banking on both the frontstretch and backstretch. The complex also includes a 0.375-mile Bullring Oval and a road course complex consisting of three distinct lengths: a Long Road Course (2.499 miles), a Medium Road Course (2.150 miles), and a Short Road Course (1.938 miles).

**Event Programming**
The venue hosts multiple major racing series. Current recurring events include the NASCAR Cup Series (Pennzoil 400 and South Point 400) and the NASCAR O'Reilly Auto Parts Series (The LiUNA! and Focused Health 302). Additionally, the facility is scheduled to host Formula D in 2026. Former tenants include the IndyCar Series, the American Le Mans Series, and the Indy Autonomous Challenge (2022–2025).
