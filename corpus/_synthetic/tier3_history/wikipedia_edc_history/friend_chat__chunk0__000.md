Friend: Hey! I'm thinking about finally hitting up EDC 2026. Do you know when it actually happens?

Vet: Definitely do it! EDC Las Vegas 2026 is locked in for May 15th through the 17th. 

Friend: Nice. I've heard it's huge, but like... how huge?

Vet: It's massive. It's actually the largest EDM festival in North America. To give you an idea, back in 2024, they had 525,000 people show up over the three days.

Friend: That sounds overwhelming. Where is it even held?

Vet: It's at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. If you want to make it easier, you can do the on-site camping; they added that back in 2018 so you don't have to commute every day.

Friend: Got it. What's the vibe like? Is it just DJs on a stage?

Vet: Not even close. It's a full-on carnival. We're talking 18 carnival rides, four Ferris wheels, and these wild 3D superstructures and LED plants everywhere. There are performers just roaming around the grounds too.

Friend: That sounds crazy. I'm more into the music side—what genres do they actually play?

Vet: Everything. They cover house, techno, dubstep, drum and bass, and trance. There are eight different stages in total, each catering to different genres.

Friend: Which stage is the "main" one?

Vet: That would be kineticFIELD. It's the biggest one and can hold about 70,000 people. That's where you'll find the absolute headliners.

Friend: Is this a new thing, or has it been around for a while?

Vet: It's actually quite old! The very first one was back in 1991 in Chino, California, started by Stephen Hauptfuhr. Insomniac took over the reins later, and they've grown it from a tiny party into the beast it is now.

Friend: Does it only happen in Vegas?

Vet: Nope, though Vegas is the flagship. They currently run annual events in Orlando, Mexico City, and Suzhou, China. They've even done shows in places like Brazil, Japan, and the UK in the past.

Friend: I'm sold. How do I get tickets?

Vet: Just use the mobile apps. They expanded ticket access to apps back in 2019 to make it way more convenient. Just be ready to move fast!
