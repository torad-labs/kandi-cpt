Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) is an electronic dance music (EDM) festival organized by the promoter and distributor Insomniac. The event features various electronic genres, including house, techno, drum and bass, dubstep, and trance. In addition to musical performances by DJs and producers, the festival incorporates three-dimensional superstructures, LED-lit flora and fauna, interactive art installations, carnival rides, and free-roaming performers.

The flagship event, EDC Las Vegas, is held annually in May at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway. As of 2024, it is the largest EDM festival in North America, recording an attendance of 525,000 people over three days. The Las Vegas site features eight genre-specific stages, including the Kinetic Field, which has a capacity of 70,000. The infrastructure includes 18 carnival rides, four Ferris wheels, and a staff exceeding 5,000 employees. On-site camping was introduced to the Las Vegas event in 2018.

Historically, the event originated from underground parties started by Stephen Hauptfuhr in 1986, with the first official EDC held in Chino, California, in 1991. Under Insomniac's organization since 1997, the festival expanded from a two-day event in 2009 to a three-day event in 2011.

While the festival has previously operated in locations such as New Delhi, India; São Paulo, Brazil; and Milton Keynes, England, current annual events are held in Las Vegas, Orlando, Mexico City, Medellín, Suzhou, Phuket, and Seoul. The next scheduled iteration of EDC Las Vegas will take place from May 15 to May 17, 2026.
