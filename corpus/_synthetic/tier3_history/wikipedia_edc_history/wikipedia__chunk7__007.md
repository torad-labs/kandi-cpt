# Electric Daisy Carnival

**Electric Daisy Carnival** (EDC) is a series of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals produced by Insomniac Events. 

## Philanthropy
Through the Insomniac Care initiative, a percentage of ticket sales from each flagship festival is donated to national charities and community organizations. Since 2011, the organization has donated over two million dollars to various causes. 

EDC Las Vegas has previously collaborated with the fundraising platform Surreal to solicit donations from attendees via sweepstakes for experiences, such as helicopter rides and interactions with performing DJs. Beneficiary organizations have included:
* Rock the Vote
* Families for Effective Autism Treatment
* Injured Police Officers Fund
* Boys and Girls Club of Southern Nevada
* Nevada Childhood Cancer Foundation
* The Las Vegas Academy for the Arts

## Media
The 2013 edition of EDC Las Vegas was the subject of the documentary film *Under the Electric Sky*, which premiered at the 2014 Sundance Film Festival. The film documents the culture and experiences of the festival's attendees.

## Recognition and Awards
Electric Daisy Carnival has received multiple nominations and rankings from industry publications and award bodies.

### International Dance Music Awards
The festival received consecutive nominations for "Best Music Event" between 2010 and 2016. Following a hiatus of the ceremony in 2017, the event returned in 2018 with winners selected by the Winter Music Conference. EDC was nominated for "Best Festival" in 2019 and 2020.

### DJ Magazine
The festival has consistently ranked in *DJ Magazine's* "Top 100 Festivals" list in recent years:
* **2022:** 3rd place
* **2023:** 4th place
* **2024:** 2nd place
