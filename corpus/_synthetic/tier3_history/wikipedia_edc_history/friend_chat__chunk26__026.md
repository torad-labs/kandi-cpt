Friend: I'm starting to plan for EDC 2026! Since you've been to a million of these, do you think it'll be similar to the Vegas setup?

Vet: Oh, for sure. You've gotta prepare for the classic layout. I'm betting my life savings that we'll see the heavy hitters on kineticFIELD on Friday and the melodic stuff over at circuitGROUNDS on Saturday.

Friend: Nice. I keep hearing about "Insomniac" as a company, though. Are they just the promoters or do they actually make music too?

Vet: They're huge. They actually have the Insomniac Music Group for A&R and distribution. They've got a ton of imprints like Basscon Records, Dreamstate Records, and Gud Vibrations. They even started Insomniac Records back in 2014 with Interscope—Arty was actually their first signee.

Friend: That's wild. Do they run other festivals too, or just EDC?

Vet: Tonnes of them. Beyond Wonderland, Nocturnal Wonderland (which is actually their oldest brand), Escape Halloween, and Countdown. They even do some stuff like Forbidden Kingdom and Project GLOW.

Friend: Wait, I thought I saw something about them owning Okeechobee?

Vet: They did for a while—they bought a controlling stake in Soundslinger back in 2019—but Soundslinger actually announced in April 2025 that Insomniac isn't involved with Okeechobee anymore.

Friend: Got it. What about clubs? Do they own any of the spots in Miami?

Vet: Yeah, they took a majority stake in Club Space in Miami back in July 2019. They're basically everywhere.

Friend: I saw some weird videos of "drive-in" raves a few years back. Was that them?

Vet: Totally. During the pandemic, they did "Insomniac Rewind" on Twitch and YouTube, and then they launched those "Park 'N Rave" shows at the NOS Events Center and that "Electric Mile" thing at Santa Anita Park.

Friend: Do they do any VR stuff? I'm seeing more "metaverse" concerts lately.

Vet: They're on it. In March 2022, they partnered with Unity Technologies to build a persistent online world for virtual concerts. They're really trying to merge the digital and physical scenes.

Friend: Last question—I keep seeing "Lost in Dreams" on posters. What is that?

Vet: That's a newer brand they launched in February 2021. It's both a label and a festival specifically for future bass and melodic dubstep. If you like that sound, you'll definitely see them represented at EDC 2026.
