Q: In what year did the Electric Daisy Carnival move its flagship location to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: 2011.

Q: From which city did the Electric Daisy Carnival move when it relocated to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: Los Angeles.

Q: Why did the Electric Daisy Carnival move its flagship location from Los Angeles to the Las Vegas Motor Speedway?
A: Controversies of rampant underage drug use in Los Angeles, which led to the injuries of numerous people and the death of a 15-year-old.

Q: How often has the Las Vegas Motor Speedway held the Electric Daisy Carnival since 2011?
A: Annually.
