List of Pokémon with released Hidden Abilities (Generation VII)

  • Deutsch
  • Italiano

Please discuss it on the talk page for this article.
This article is incomplete.
Please feel free to edit this article to add missing information and complete it.
Reason: Game sups to indicate whether they can be obtained in Sun and Moon or Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon

This is a list of Pokémon that includes all Pokémon that have Hidden Abilities in Generation VII.

While Metapod, Flygon, and other similar Pokémon do not have a Hidden Ability that differs from the regular Ability, at least one of their evolutionary relatives do have one; therefore, they are included on this list. For details on how to obtain Pokémon with their Hidden Ability in Generation VI, see List of Pokémon with released Hidden Abilities (Generation VI).
